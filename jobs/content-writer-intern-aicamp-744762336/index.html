Job Title: Content Writer

Location: Remote

Reporting to: Marketing Manager

Duration: 6 month

Stipend ₹5,000 - ₹10,000 per month

Role Overview

As a Content Writer at AICamp, you will be responsible for creating high-quality, in-depth organic content that drives engagement, educates our audience, and contributes to our content marketing goals. Your work will focus on writing well-researched articles, tutorials, use cases, comparison articles, and alternative content for both Bottom-of-Funnel (BOFU) and Middle-of-Funnel (MOFU) stages. This content will play a crucial role in improving AICamp’s SEO and organic reach.

Key Responsibilities

Content Creation

  • Write in-depth, well-researched articles that provide valuable insights and solutions, addressing the needs of our target audience.
  • Develop comparison articles, use case tutorials, and alternative solution pieces for both BOFU and MOFU content that drive decision-making and engagement.
  • Focus on crafting high-quality organic content that aligns with SEO best practices and enhances AICamp’s online presence.

SEO-Optimized Content

  • Ensure all written content is optimized for SEO, using targeted keywords and search trends to improve rankings and organic traffic.

Content Strategy Alignment

  • Collaborate with the marketing team to ensure content aligns with broader strategies, target audience needs, and company objectives.

BOFU And MOFU Content Development

  • Create compelling BOFU and MOFU content, focusing on addressing the pain points of potential customers and guiding them toward conversion.

Content Planning And Execution

  • Plan and write content in line with content calendars, ensuring deadlines and quality standards are met consistently.

Performance Tracking & Reporting

  • Track the performance of content through analytics tools, providing insights and recommendations for continuous improvement.

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • Proven experience in writing high-quality, long-form content for websites, blogs, and other digital channels.
  • Strong research skills, with the ability to create in-depth, well-supported articles on a variety of topics.
  • Knowledge of SEO best practices and how to implement them in content.
  • Ability to create content for different stages of the customer journey, especially BOFU and MOFU.
  • Excellent written communication skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently and meet deadlines consistently.

About AICamp AICamp is a small, fast-moving startup building Agentic AI Workspaces — helping teams create and deploy internal AI agents.

We care less about hype and more about how AI is actually used in day-to-day work. At AICamp, you’ll work directly with the founders, ship features fast, and learn how AI products are built, tested, and adopted — all in a remote, collaborative setup.
