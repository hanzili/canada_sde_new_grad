About Taskify AI

Full Description

We are hiring motivated individuals for a flexible 100% remote role that involves supporting day-to-day tasks, ensuring smooth operations, and contributing to digital, content, research, data, and AI-related workflows.

Flexible Hours | Weekly Pay | No Experience Needed | Work From Anywhere

  • Location: Remote (US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland)
  • Compensation: Earn between $5,000 - $8,000 per month

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist with everyday tasks based on project needs (content, data, research, analysis, operations, AI review, documentation, or coordination).
  • Review, organise, and update information with accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Communicate effectively with the team and provide timely progress updates.

  • Analytical mindset with attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Eagerness to learn, adapt, and contribute to team goals.

Why Join Us

  • Skill-building opportunities in research, content, and AI-related tasks.
