About the Role

Coordinate sales ops, RFQ prep, reporting.

About Luxe Decor

Growing retailer; cross-functional sales exposure.

Full Description

Luxe Decor Global is growing — and we're looking for a motivated, detail-oriented Junior Sales Associate to join our team. This role sits at the intersection of sales operations, retail execution, and customer programs, with a primary focus on supporting our Home Depot (HD) business. You'll be the connective tissue between our sales team and the rest of the organization — keeping things moving, keeping everyone informed, and making sure nothing falls through the cracks.

What you'll do

Sales support & coordination

  • Serve as the liaison between the sales team and internal departments, surfacing key information quickly and accurately
  • Support the preparation of RFQs and pitches, working closely with cross-functional partners to compile product and pricing details
  • Use internal tools to calculate estimated PDQ/container quantities, run comp shops, and build reverse timelines for Home Depot programs

Retail & HD onboarding

  • Enter and maintain items in HD's Flex Portal and Product Information Management (PIM) system
  • Complete product onboarding templates, including packaging supplier forms and all required HD documentation
  • Support the creation of marketing and transactional materials for new product launches

Orders & inventory

  • Coordinate with logistics to manage the arrival of quarterly promotional goods based on HD communications
  • Follow up with HD to ensure orders are released on time and quantities align with forecasts
  • Manage order exceptions and submit monthly discrepancy disputes through the HD portal

Reporting & administration

  • Send weekly HD sales reports using internal reporting tools
  • Maintain and update the sales notebook (OneNote) and assist in preparing for meetings
  • Take meeting notes and track action items

Field & samples

  • Coordinate and deliver product samples to the Innovation Centre and other stakeholders
  • Conduct store visits and provide documented photo reports
  • Pick up physical samples as needed (valid driver's license required)

What we're looking for

  • 1–2 years of experience in sales support, retail operations, or a related role (internship experience welcome)
  • Strong organizational skills and a high attention to detail — this role involves a lot of moving pieces
  • Comfortable working with vendor portals, data entry systems, and internal tools
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Proactive, resourceful, and comfortable following up with internal and external stakeholders
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote); experience with retail portals is a plus
  • Valid driver's license and access to a vehicle for occasional sample pickups and store visits

Pay: $45,000.00-$50,000.00 per year

Work Location: In person
