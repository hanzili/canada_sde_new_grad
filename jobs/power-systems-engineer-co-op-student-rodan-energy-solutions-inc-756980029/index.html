About the Role

About Rodan Energy Solutions Inc.

Canada’s Top SME Employer of 2026

Full Description

About Us:

At Rodan Energy, our mission is Making Sustainable, Attainable. As a leading North American energy services company, we provide innovative energy solutions that help clients reduce energy costs and GHG emissions, support electrification, and enhance grid efficiency. With over 20 years of expertise, we’re trusted by North America’s largest energy users, power producers, and utilities.  

Why Join Rodan:

  • Thriving Industry:Join a rapidly growing sector at the forefront of innovation and sustainability, helping shape the future of energy management.

  • Product Market Fit:With a 98% client retention rate and consistent year-over-year growth, Rodan has built a reputation for delivering trusted, long-term partnerships with industry leaders

  • Top Talent & Strong Culture: Collaborate with high-performing, passionate individuals who are committed to excellence, innovation, and sustainability.

What Makes This Role Great:

  • Gain hands-on experience supporting real-world power system engineering projects
  • Work with protection & control systems, revenue metering, power quality, and electrical distribution systems
  • Learn from experienced engineers across Ontario and Alberta
  • Build valuable technical and professional skills in a collaborative environment
  • Gain exposure to the Ontario energy market and utility sector
  • Benefit from an in-office environment designed to support mentorship, learning, and professional growth

Key Responsibilities:

  • Assist in the design of protection and control, revenue metering, monitoring, power quality, and related power system engineering projects
  • Support the design team in developing medium- and high-voltage metering, protection, and control systems
  • Prepare drawings, schematics, and diagrams for electrical distribution, metering, and communications systems
  • Support engineering calculations related to metering and protection system configurations
  • Assist with the review and development of vendor equipment specifications
  • Provide technical support to internal teams and, where appropriate, external stakeholders
  • Support technical proposals and solutions engineering activities
  • Develop an understanding of Ontario's electricity market, utility operations, and industry best practices
  • Other duties as required

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's degree program in Electrical Engineering, Power Systems Engineering, or a related discipline
  • Registered in a recognized co-op program
  • Foundational knowledge of power systems, electrical safety principles, grounding, and industry standards
  • Experience or exposure to tools such as Microsoft Excel, AutoCAD, Mathcad, MATLAB, ETAP, or other engineering software is considered an asset
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Excellent problem-solving and analytical abilities
  • Self-motivated, detail-oriented, organized, and eager to learn
  • Strong customer service mindset and ability to work effectively in a team environment

Term Details, Compensation & Work Location

  • Term: September 2026 – December 2026 (4-month co-op term)
  • Compensation: $23.00 - $26.00 per hour, based on year of study, relevant experience, and internal equity
  • Schedule: 40-hour work week
  • Location: Mississauga, ON
  • Work Arrangement: 5 days per week in-office
  • Vacancy Status: This posting is for an existing Fall 2026 co-op opportunity

Life at Rodan – Our Culture:
At Rodan, we believe a great workplace goes beyond the work itself. Recognized as Canada’s Top Small & Medium Employer of 2026, we’ve built a culture rooted in collaboration, celebration, and giving back. Here’s what you can look forward to when you join the team:

  • Celebrations Throughout the Year: We mark occasions and holidays all year long with team events including holiday parties, team dinners, bowling nights, go-karting, golf tournaments, and more.
  • Learning & Growth: Lunch and learns, crossword challenges, and education-funding programs to keep you sharp and growing in your career.
  • Giving Back: Our Employee Initiatives Committee (EIC) organizes food drives and fundraising for local charities throughout the year – and everyone is welcome to get involved.

Hiring Process
Rodan Energy's hiring process is designed to identify candidates who are the right fit — technically, culturally, and professionally. As part of our process, candidates should be aware of the following:

  • Background Check: As part of our hiring process, the successful candidate will be required to complete a background check, which may include a criminal record check and credential verification. Professional references will also be collected as part of the process.
  • Assignment: Shortlisted candidates may be asked to complete a practical assignment. This is an opportunity to demonstrate your strategic thinking, creativity, and approach to real-world challenges relevant to the role.

We respect your time and will provide clear instructions and reasonable timelines for any assignments requested.
