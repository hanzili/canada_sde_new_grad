Job Description The Institutional Equities division of TD Securities provides equity sales, trading, research, underwriting and distribution from offices in Canada, the U.S. and the UK.

As an Associate in Equity Research, you will have an opportunity to work directly with an equity analyst on the daily publication of company and industry research reports. This role is for an Equity Research Associate that will work alongside a senior analyst covering the Engineering & Construction (E&C) sector. The successful candidate will be a key contributor to the production of financial models, published research, and the day-to-day analytical work that supports our coverage.

Responsibilities

  • Build, maintain, and enhance detailed financial models (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, DCF/SOTP, and segment-level and project-backlog models) for E&C and Industrials companies.
  • Analyze sector-specific drivers including backlog and book-to-bill trends, project margins and execution risk, infrastructure spending cycles, and public/private funding dynamics.
  • Draft and contribute to initiation reports, earnings previews/reviews, sector notes, and thematic research.
  • Conduct company and peer analysis, valuation benchmarking, and competitive positioning assessments across the coverage universe.
  • Monitor company news flow, contract awards, regulatory developments, and macro indicators relevant to sector; prepare summaries and flag actionable insights to senior analyst.
  • Support the senior analyst during earnings season, investor marketing, non-deal roadshows, and industry conferences.
  • Engage with institutional sales and trading teams to communicate investment theses and respond to client inquiries.
  • Continuously develop sector expertise and progress toward greater analytical ownership and independent contributions over time.

Qualifications

  • Ability to work independently under strict deadlines, with an attention to detail and output orientation
  • Ability to research a topic from the ground up
  • Strong work ethic, ability to take initiative and balance multiple tasks
  • Superior business writing skills, presentation skills and interpersonal skills
  • Excellent spreadsheet, quantitative and financial analysis skills
  • Demonstrated interest in capital markets – comfortable with finance theory
  • Service orientation and professional attitude – presentable to both internal and external clients
  • Flexibility and ability to invest long hours as needed/required by business due to the nature of the role
  • We welcome applications from candidates across a range of experience levels. While relevant experience in equity research and/or familiarity with the E&C or broader Industrials space, is preferred, it is not required.
  • The candidate must possess exceptional aptitude. Similarly, professional designations (e.g., CFA, CPA, P.Eng.) are viewed favorably but are not a prerequisite. If you are motivated, analytically rigorous, and eager to contribute, we encourage you to apply.
  • Minimum Undergrad degree in a relevant finance or industry field

Who We Are TD Securities offers a wide range of capital markets products and services to corporate, government, and institutional clients who choose us for our innovation, execution, and experience. With more than 6,500 professionals operating out of 40 cities across the globe, we strive to make every interaction, product and experience remarkably human and refreshingly simple. Our services include underwriting and distributing new issues, providing trusted advice and industry-leading insight, extending access to global markets, and delivering integrated transaction banking solutions. In 2023, we acquired Cowen Inc., offering our clients access to a premier U.S. equities business and highly-diverse equity research franchise, while growing our strong, diversified investment bank.

Together, we are reimagining what banking can be for our clients, colleagues and communities.
