About Klohn Crippen Berger

Full Description

As a Junior Civil Engineer-in-Training with a strong interest in either water resources, geotechnical engineering, or structural engineering, you will have the opportunity to work on small to large scale civil engineering projects from concept through construction and commissioning. You will expand your academic training and professional practice by working along-side senior engineers & environmental scientists on civil engineering projects in western Canada.

You will be part of our award-winning Infrastructure group and advance your career in our One Team culture that builds strong, collaborative and focused teams who enjoy themselves throughout project delivery. We value a winning attitude, a hunger for learning and innovation, adaptability and flexibility, respectful engagement with our team and our client representatives, and a sense of fun.

This Role Will Give You The Opportunity To

  • Work on a variety of projects in the infrastructure, mining, and oil sands markets for our key clients.
  • Types of projects include: storm water management systems for municipalities and large mining clients; municipal engineering, stormwater and wastewater networks; geohazard assessment and repair; structural design of water retention structures; dam safety reviews; flood protection works; irrigation systems; and general civil engineering projects.
  • Broaden your professional practice by working in a range of engineering disciplines while maintaining your focus on civil engineering.
  • Develop your project management skills by working with experienced and skilled senior project managers on sustainable projects.
  • Based in Calgary with opportunities to work throughout western Canada, the United States, Europe, south America, Australia, south-east Asia, and Peru.
  • Work in a dynamic company culture with a diverse, hands-on, small, and hard-working office team that supports career development and diversification.

If you are recent graduate or have up to 2 years of experience and are registered or are eligible to register as an Engineer-In-Training in Canada, have a Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Civil Engineering, we want to hear from you! We Value Your

  • Strong initiative, innovation, and commitment to technical excellence as well as your enthusiasm and drive toward long-term success.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills including experience in contributing to proposals, technical reports, and general project correspondence.
  • Exceptional team spirit, interpersonal skills, and your ability to build long-term, successful relationships with our clients and project teams.
  • Exceptional time management and organizational skills.

Along With Continued Career Development, KCB Offers

  • Hybrid home-office work opportunities.
  • Competitive wages with bonus potential.
  • Vacation policy that aligns to your experience.
  • Paid volunteer time, overtime, and sick days.
  • Professional registration support.
  • Retirement planning and flexibility through group RRSP options.
  • Flexible health benefits, including social and mental well-being initiatives.
  • Commitment to global Environmental, Social, and Governance standards.

About Us Klohn Crippen Berger Ltd. (KCB) is a multi-disciplinary consulting firm with five major business units providing engineering and environmental services in the mining, hydropower, transportation, ports, infrastructure, geotechnical, oil & gas, environmental, and water resources sectors. We offer a full range of services from our offices in North America, Australia, South America, and Europe.

From our offices in Calgary, Edmonton, Saskatoon, York (UK), Naas (Ireland), Sweden and Dominican Republic our EMIE Group works on challenging projects in the Water, Power and Transportation, Oil Sands, Mining, and Oil and Gas market sectors.

KCB is a platinum member of Canada’s Best Managed companies. We are committed to employment equity and encourage applications from all qualified career seekers. We offer professional career development tracks and salaries commensurate with experience and an attractive benefits package.

Are you up to the challenge? If this sounds like the perfect opportunity for you, simply apply on-line with your résumé.

We thank all candidates for applying, however only short-listed candidates will be contacted.

KCB is an award-winning multidisciplinary engineering, geoscience, and environmental consulting firm grounded in Passion, Integrity, and Excellence. With teams across North America, South America, Australasia, and Europe we collaborate globally to solve complex challenges.
