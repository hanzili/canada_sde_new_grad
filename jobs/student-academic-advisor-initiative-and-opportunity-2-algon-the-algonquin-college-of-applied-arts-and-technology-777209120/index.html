Job Description:

Reporting to the Manager, Enrolment Services and Student Success, the incumbent is the primary contact for a diverse population of full-time and part-time students and fulfills multiple responsibilities related to specialized progression supports and guidance while meeting and exceeding student success and retention objectives. The incumbent is required to coordinate and maintain a portfolio of student programs that includes case management processes, analyzing detailed academic records, and communicating a wide variety of information to help facilitate learner success.

In providing high-quality client service, the incumbent builds relationships with learners through focused outreach and communication supports, as well as student appointments.  The incumbent utilizes technology-enabled applications to help plan, develop and deliver focused and self-directed progression and registration services, which relate to learner retention and success. 

Working with an integrated team of Student Success Specialists(s), Registration and Records Officers, Financial Aid and Student Awards Officers, the incumbent supports learners throughout the student lifecycle, from registration to graduation.

Employing a variety of college technology systems and early alerts to monitor, track and create reports the incumbent determines specific retention supports and contributes to the strategic enrolment management process. Maintaining a personalized and continuous focus on the learner experience is central to the role. This position is responsible for identifying process improvements and recommending new initiatives and strategies.

Required Qualifications:

  • Minimum three (3) year diploma or degree in Business, Education, Human Resources, Marketing, Public Relations, Social Sciences, Career Development, or any other program with a Cooperative Education component. Equivalent combination of relevant education and work experience may be accepted in place of formal education.
  • Minimum of three (3) years experience in providing client service and/or general advising for a diverse clientele preferably in an educational setting.

This position requires regular on-site work at Algonquin College’s Woodroffe (Ottawa) Campus.
