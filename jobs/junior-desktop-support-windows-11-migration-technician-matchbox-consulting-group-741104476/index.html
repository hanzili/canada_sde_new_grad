Full Description

MatchBox Consulting Group is currently seeking a Junior Desktop Support & Windows 11 Migration Technician for a 3-month contract role (with the possibility of extension) with a client of ours located downtown Vancouver. This is an on-site part time role 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) -no work Monday and Friday. 22.5 hours total per week. Looking For a Vancouver-based Candidate With Skills/experience In

  • Windows 10 and Windows 11 administration and support
  • Laptop deployment and imaging
  • Device provisioning and configuration
  • Hardware troubleshooting (laptops, peripherals, docking stations)
  • Software installation and troubleshooting
  • IT ticketing systems – Asana)
  • Experience with Microsoft Intune and Autopilot (preferred)
  • Basic networking troubleshooting (Wi-Fi, VPN, printers)
  • Data backup and migration procedures
  • Experience supporting Apple macOS devices and MacBook deployments
  • Apple device deployment and management (preferred)
  • Scheduling and coordinating user appointments
  • Managing multiple users and upgrade schedules simultaneously
  • Can work on-site downtown Vancouver Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday (no work Monday and Friday)

For more current job opportunities, Follow MatchBox at linkedin.com/company/matchbox-recruitment/
