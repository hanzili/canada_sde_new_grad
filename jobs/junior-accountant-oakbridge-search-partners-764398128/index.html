Full Description

About the Company

Our client is a growing Canadian mining and resource development company focused on advancing high-quality exploration and development projects. As the organization continues to grow, they are seeking a detail-oriented and proactive Junior-Intermediate Corporate Accountant to support the day-to-day accounting operations and help ensure accurate financial reporting and compliance.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys working in a collaborative, entrepreneurial environment where they can take ownership of the accounting function and contribute across multiple areas of the business.

Key Responsibilities

Accounting & Financial Reporting

  • Perform monthly bank reconciliations using QuickBooks.
  • Prepare and assist with monthly and quarterly government remittances and reporting, including PD7A, HST, Employer Health Tax (EHT), Workers' Compensation, and other statutory requirements.
  • Maintain payroll records and support payroll administration.
  • Process cheques and electronic payments.
  • Manage the full accounts payable cycle, including invoice processing, payment scheduling, and vendor communications.
  • Maintain accurate, complete, and up-to-date financial records.
  • Generate financial reports using QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop.
  • Maintain and improve accounting process documentation and vendor records.
  • Establish and maintain organized digital and physical filing systems.
  • Track financial and operational KPIs to support monthly reporting and management analysis.
  • Assist with month-end close activities and other accounting functions as required.
  • Support additional accounting and finance projects as assigned.

Additional Responsibilities

  • Perform general administrative duties, including organizing and maintaining company documentation.
  • Handle sensitive and confidential financial information with discretion.
  • Provide ad hoc financial analysis and reporting to support business decision-making.
  • Effectively manage multiple priorities while working independently with minimal supervision.
  • Participate in process improvements to enhance efficiency and accuracy.
  • Occasional travel to operating sites may be required.

Qualifications

  • Post-secondary education in Accounting, Finance, or a related discipline.
  • 2–5+ years of full-cycle accounting experience.
  • Proficiency with QuickBooks Online and/or QuickBooks Desktop.
  • Strong understanding of Canadian payroll, HST, and government remittance requirements.
  • Experience managing accounts payable, reconciliations, and month-end processes.
  • Excellent organizational skills with exceptional attention to detail.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, evolving environment.
  • Experience within mining, natural resources, construction, or other project-based industries is considered an asset.
