Sales Associate – Luxury Furniture & Design

Studio B Home is a luxury furniture and design showroom representing some of the world’s leading furniture and lifestyle brands. We work with homeowners, designers, architects, and developers to create exceptional residential and commercial spaces through personalized service and high-end design solutions.

We are currently looking for an experienced Sales Associate to join our team.

Responsibilities

• Develop and maintain strong client relationships

• Assist clients with furniture and design selections

• Manage the sales process from initial inquiry to final delivery

• Prepare quotes and follow up on leads and opportunities

• Coordinate with vendors, suppliers, and internal teams

• Maintain strong product knowledge across luxury furniture and design collections

• Support showroom presentation and overall client experience

Qualifications

• Prior sales experience required

• Experience in high-end or luxury sales required

• Furniture or interior design experience is a strong asset

• Strong communication and relationship-building skills

• Professional, organized, and detail-oriented

• Ability to work in a fast-paced client-focused environment

Compensation

• 45,000-55,000 plus commission based on experience

Please send your resume and relevant experience to hrmgr@studiobhome.com
