Full time – Junior Fundraiser & Government Relations Assistant

Canada Powered by Women (CPW) | Calgary, AB (Hybrid)

About Canada Powered by Women

Canada Powered by Women champions the informed, unignorable voices of Canadian women. Established in 2019 as a non-partisan non-profit, we equip and empower women to understand, articulate, and influence sound energy policy. We believe in a Canada where these vital perspectives shape a thriving economy and sustainable energy future for all. We create opportunities for women to join the national energy conversation, ensuring their insights influence public perception, policymakers, and government in an advocacy context. United by the power of common-ground solutions, we’re focused on a stronger, more competitive, and prosperous Canada.

The opportunity

Canada Powered by Women (CPW) is looking for a full-time Junior Fundraiser & Government Relations

Assistant. We’re seeking a motivated early-career professional to support fundraising and advocacy initiatives aligned with our mission to amplify women’s voices in Canada’s energy policy landscape.

This role supports donor engagement, sponsorship coordination, and advocacy activities while working collaboratively across marketing, communications, events, and external partners. The position reports to the CEO with day-to-day oversight from the Senior Executive Assistant & Operations Specialist.

Key responsibilities

Fundraising & sponsorship (80%)

Donor stewardship & fundraising support

• Support donor communications, sponsorship proposals, presentations, and reporting.

• Research prospective sponsors and funding opportunities; maintain accurate tracking systems.

• Draft donor correspondence, reports, and fundraising materials.

• Coordinate donor and stakeholder meetings, including follow-ups and action items.

Sponsorship fulfillment & event coordination

• Track sponsorship agreements and ensure all commitments are delivered accurately and on time.

• Maintain fulfillment trackers and provide status updates to leadership.

• Coordinate sponsor activations and National Tour engagements with internal teams and partners.

Salesforce & reporting

• Maintain accurate records of donors, sponsors, and stakeholders in Salesforce.

• Track donations, pipelines, and engagement activities.

• Prepare monthly reports, dashboards, and performance updates.

Campaigns, events & communications

• Support fundraising campaigns, email outreach, and stakeholder communications.

• Help coordinate logistics for events, advocacy activities, and campaign launches.

• Develop event materials, invitations, and sponsor recognition assets.

Administrative support

• Assist the CEO with scheduling, briefing materials, and project coordination.

• Liaise with Board members, partners, and external stakeholders.

• Support merchandise coordination and operational initiatives.

Government relations & advocacy (20%)

• Support advocacy campaigns, events, and stakeholder engagement initiatives.

• Coordinate outreach and logistics for meetings with government and community stakeholders.

• Maintain stakeholder tracking systems and support external relations activities.

Qualifications

• Post-secondary education in public policy, political science, nonprofit management, communications, business or related field.

• Strong communication (written and oral), research, organization and attention to detail.

• Proficiency in Microsoft 365 applications (specifically Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams).

• Strong interpersonal skills and a collaborative, proactive mindset with willingness to learn and be part of a team.

• High level of professionalism and confidentiality.

• Commitment to the values of economic prosperity and sustainability.

• Belief in the CPW vision and mission, alignment with its core values

Preferred experience

• 1-2 years’ work experience.

• Volunteer or work experience in a nonprofit, advocacy, or public policy setting.

• Experience using Salesforce or CRM systems, Monday.com, and Canva is an asset.

• Experience supporting fundraising efforts, campaigns, proposal writing /

writing, and distilling policy.

• Familiarity with Canadian government institutions, public policy processes and

stakeholder engagement practices is an asset.

• Knowledge of Canada’s energy sector and related issues is an asset.

• Bilingualism (English/French) is an asset.

Additional expectations

• Full-time role (~37.5 hours/week)

• Calgary based only, hybrid work model.

• Occasional travel required to support national events, advocacy initiatives and stakeholder engagement activities.

• Flexibility to attend occasional evening or weekend events as required.

Why join CPW

• Be part of a nationally visible platform at the intersection of energy,

economy, policy, and public dialogue.

• Help shape and deliver high-impact events, campaigns, and engagements with

women and senior business and government leaders across Canada.

• Work alongside a committed and influential leadership team.

• Play a central role in amplifying forward-looking national dialogues that help

shape prosperity for Canadians.
