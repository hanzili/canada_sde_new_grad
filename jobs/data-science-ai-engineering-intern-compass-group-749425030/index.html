About the Role

Build data-driven reports; onboard clients; craft case studies

About Compass Group

Global food services leader; data-driven client solutions.

Full Description

As a Data Science/ AI Engineering Intern you will be responsible for client-facing data engagements with Compass Digital’s Data Products and Services. This includes building case studies that illustrate value proposition; generating reports and insights for clients; and onboarding new clients onto our platforms.

Now, if you were to come on board as one of our Data Science/ AI Interns we’d ask you to do the following for us:

  • Build data-driven narratives and case studies to help sell new clients
  • Pull data and ad-hoc service reports for clients
  • Onboard new clients and provide training to help them maximize the value they get out of our platforms

Think you have what it takes to be our Data Science / AI Intern? We’re committed to hiring the best talent for the role. Here’s how we’ll know you’ll be successful in the role.

  • Bachelor's degree in a technical field, such as Computer Science or Engineering
  • 2+ years of experience in a sales engineering or technical sales role
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Client-facing experience
  • Experience in a analyst role (preferably within CPG)
  • Experience with SQL
  • Experience with an enterprise BI tool (PowerBI / Looker / Tableau / Etc. )
  • Python experience
  • CPG Experience
  • Embrace customer-centricity
  • Technical experience with analysis reporting
  • The ability to quickly become a subject matter expert (Within CPG food distribution space)
