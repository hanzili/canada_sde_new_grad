About the Role

Assist library patrons and support services

About University of Niagara Falls Canada

Public university library supporting student success

Full Description

UNF is part of Global University Systems (GUS), an international network of private higher-education institutions.

Located in downtown Niagara Falls, UNF’s technology-centred approach puts students first, offering a cutting-edge learning framework that is adapted as technology changes and new processes emerge. Students learn from industry leaders and highly experienced faculty while gaining experience through work-integrated learning opportunities in a variety of interdisciplinary programs designed to address the widening digital skills gap.

With a strong focus on high-quality, digital-focused education, the university continues to broaden its program offerings to meet emerging needs in the global economy while maintaining its inclusive, student-centred learning environment.

About the position

Location: Niagara Falls, Ontario, Canada

Work arrangement: On-campus, part-time student contract

Reports To: University Librarian

Reason for vacancy: Replacement

Expected hiring salary range: $17.60 per hour. *This part-time, term contract student position is open and reserved for current UNF students only.

Primary Purpose:

Under the direction of the Senior Library Assistant, the Student Library Associates work as official representatives of the library and play a pivotal role in supporting UNF students. Student Library Associates provide frontline student support, and work with a high level of responsibility.

Specific Responsibilities:

Library Services Desk

  • Assist students with basic research help questions, such as how to use the library website to search for resources, guides, and other information to support their library research
  • Provide immediate support on how to use library workstations, and how to use printer
  • Refer complex research questions to a librarian, via booking form
  • Provide directions to campus areas, such as classrooms, lounges, and student support areas
  • Collect and record data (door counts and reference questions) while maintaining confidentiality.
  • Maintain the cleanliness of library in general
  • Provide reference assistance via LibChat during scheduled shift

Library Tours

  • Conduct library tours to visiting students and community members upon request.

Event Support

  • Occasionally assist with on-campus library events upon request

Administration & Other

  • Collaborate closely with Library colleagues to provide excellent support to students
  • Be available for evening and weekend work.
  • Perform other tasks as assigned, contributing to the overall success of the Library and UNF.

Position Requirements:

  • Enrolled as a student within the University of Niagara Falls Canada during the entire duration of the contract.
  • Preferred that at start date, student has completed at least one school term with UNF.
  • Must be available for a minimum of 1 shift per week (minimum 3 hour shift), may consist of opening, mid-day, evening and/or weekend shift(s).
  • Students have not worked 2 consecutive student contracts with UNF .
  • Must have a cumulative average of 70% or greater.
  • Students who are actively participating in a capstone project or internship as part of their academic program are not eligible to work in a student staff role during these periods.
  • Experience in a customer service role is required.
  • Experience in academic library is considered an asset.

Competencies:

  • Excellent oral and written communication skills.
  • Demonstration of critical thinking skills.
  • Demonstrated ability to work independently and as a member of a team
  • Demonstrated reliability and high work ethic
  • Ability to follow library processes and practices with high attention to detail
