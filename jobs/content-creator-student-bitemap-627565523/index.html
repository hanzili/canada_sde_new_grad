About BiteMap

Niche video platform connecting creators and diners

Full Description

Company Description

BiteMap is a dynamic short-form video platform that bridges the gap between local food creators and nearby diners. By providing geo-tagged, actionable food experiences, BiteMap fosters connections between food enthusiasts and culinary experts. The platform is dedicated to celebrating local flavors and food culture, creating immersive and interactive experiences for its users. BiteMap is redefining how food lovers discover and engage with the world of gastronomy.

Role Description

This is a part-time, hybrid role for a Content Creator based in Toronto, ON, with flexible opportunities for remote work. The Content Creator will be responsible for developing engaging short-form video content, capturing vibrant food experiences, and crafting creative stories to connect with BiteMap's audience. Daily tasks include conceptualizing, filming, editing, and publishing video content, collaborating with local food creators, and ensuring brand consistency across all published media. The role also includes staying updated on food trends to enhance content relevance and appeal.

Qualifications

  • MUST BE A CONTENT CREATOR / FILM STUDENT
  • Feel free to attach Tiktok/Instagram/portfolio
  • Proficiency in Video Creation, Editing, and Content Production
  • Experience in Content Strategy, Creative Writing, and Storytelling
  • Knowledge of Social Media Platforms, Trends, and Digital Marketing
  • Strong Research and Planning Skills
  • Excellent Communication, Collaboration, and Time Management Skills
  • Familiarity with local food culture and trends is a plus
