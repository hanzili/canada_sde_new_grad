About Progressive Ford

Full Description

Progressive Auto Group is a growing automotive organization committed to delivering exceptional customer experiences across our dealerships throughout Southwestern Ontario.

We are seeking a motivated, detail-oriented, and hands-on Junior Controller to join the team at Progressive Ford in Wallaceburg.

Reporting to the Group Controller, this role plays a key part in the day-to-day accounting operations of the dealership while developing the skills and experience required to grow into a Controller position. The successful candidate will work closely with dealership leadership and the accounting team to ensure accurate financial reporting, strong financial controls, and efficient dealership operations.

This position is based in Wallaceburg, with occasional travel to our Sarnia dealerships for training, collaboration, and operational support.

This is an excellent opportunity for an accounting professional looking to take the next step in their career within a progressive and growing automotive group.

Key Responsibilities

General Accounting

Maintain and reconcile the dealership general ledger

Post new and used vehicle deals accurately and on time

Complete monthly bank reconciliations

Assist with month-end and year-end close processes

Prepare monthly OEM financial statements

Ensure accounting policies, procedures, and internal controls are consistently followed

Accounts Payable

Enter vendor invoices and bills of sale

Process accounts payable payments and cheque runs

Reconcile supplier statements

Pay and track recurring expenses including utilities, credit cards, and other operating expenses

Accounts Receivable

Process customer payments and maintain accurate receivable records

Issue monthly customer statements

Monitor outstanding accounts and assist with collections as required

Reconcile customer and manufacturer receivable accounts

Banking & Cash Management

Process and reconcile EFTs, wire payments, and electronic deposits

Assist with dealership banking activities and cash controls

Process lien payouts and OEM-related financial transactions

Operational Support

Post daily sales and service tills

Ensure timely and accurate posting of dealership transactions

Support month-end reporting and financial analysis

Assist with process improvements and special accounting projects

Work collaboratively with dealership leadership to support operational performance

Provide backup support across accounting functions as required

Qualifications

Accounting diploma or degree preferred

1–3 years of accounting experience preferred (automotive dealership experience is an asset)

Strong understanding of accounting principles

Experience with dealership management systems considered an advantage

Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills

Ability to prioritize work and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment

Strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset

Self-motivated with a desire to learn, take ownership, and grow within the organization

Compensation & Benefits

Compensation Range: $45,000–$65,000 annually (based on experience)

Health and dental benefits

Ongoing training and professional development

Mentorship from experienced dealership accounting leaders

Career advancement opportunities within Progressive Auto Group

Why Join Progressive Auto Group?

At Progressive Auto Group, we believe in developing great people. Through mentorship, collaboration, and ongoing professional development, we provide opportunities for team members to build rewarding, long-term careers in automotive accounting and finance.

If you're looking to grow your accounting career in a supportive, team-oriented environment where your work makes a meaningful impact, we'd love to hear from you.
