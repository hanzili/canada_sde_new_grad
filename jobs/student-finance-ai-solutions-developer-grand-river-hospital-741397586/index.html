About the Role

About Grand River Hospital

Healthcare network using AI to improve operations

Full Description

Home to seven regional programs and comprehensive health-care services, Waterloo Regional Health Network (WRHN, pronounced wren) is committed to meeting the current and emerging needs in Waterloo-Wellington and beyond.

WRHN is redefining the health-care experience through collaboration and innovation, addressing barriers to access, advancing care delivery, and setting new standards in compassionate, empowered community-driven health care.

At WRHN, every patient is at the centre of everything we do as we strive to improve lives, inspire healing, and build healthier, stronger communities. By listening to patients and partners, we strive to connect communities to the right care, at the right place, and at the right time.

Position LOCATION : AI Solutions Developer (Co-op) September to December 2026

Hybrid

Hours Of Work Nonunion 37hours/week

Job Summary As a Healthcare AI Solutions Developer, you will work closely with finance, teams to identify opportunities where Microsoft Copilot and other AI technologies can streamline processes, improve decision-making, and enhance productivity. You will help design and implement practical AI solutions, automate repetitive tasks, develop reporting tools, and support the adoption of AI capabilities across the organization.

This role offers a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of finance, healthcare, technology, and innovation while gaining hands-on experience applying emerging AI tools in a large and complex healthcare environment. This is an opportunity to make a meaningful impact on healthcare operations and patient care.

Key Responsibilities

  • Identify and evaluate opportunities to improve workflows through AI and automation
  • Develop and implement solutions using Microsoft Copilot, Power Platform, and related technologies
  • Create automated reports, dashboards, and productivity tools
  • Analyze existing business processes and recommend efficiency improvements
  • Prototype and test AI-powered solutions to address operational challenges
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to understand business needs and translate them into technical solutions
  • Support AI literacy and user adoption through training materials, demonstrations, and knowledge-sharing sessions
  • Research emerging AI technologies and recommend innovative use cases relevant to healthcare operations

Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Systems Design Engineering, Data Science, or a related program
  • Strong interest in artificial intelligence, automation, and digital transformation
  • Experience with programming languages such as Python, Java, C++, or JavaScript
  • Familiarity with Microsoft Copilot, Power Platform, APIs, or automation tools is an asset
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Excellent communication and collaboration abilities
  • Self-motivated with a desire to learn and experiment with emerging technologies
