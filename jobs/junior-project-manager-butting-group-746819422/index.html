About the Role

About BUTTING Group

Family-owned global steel processor, strong manufacturing heritage

Full Description

BUTTING Canada Ltd.

As a family-owned business with more than 245 years of history and over 2,500 employees worldwide, the BUTTING Group is one of Europe's leading stainless steel processors. Our customers benefit from the combined strengths of our individual companies, creating added value for our partners. Within the BUTTING Group, our Sales Office in Calgary, AB, is the connecting link to the North American market. BUTTING Canada supplies our North American customers with welded pipes, machined and fabricated components, pressure vessels and storage tanks manufactured in our plants in Germany, USA and China.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the execution, procurement, monitoring, and successful delivery of projects on time and within budget while managing customer communication, reporting, and project closure
  • Coordinate sourcing and purchasing activities with multiple vendors, manage supplier and subcontractor contracts, monitor fabrication progress, support inspections, and optimize logistics, supply chain operations, and transportation routes
  • Collaborate closely with engineering, production, and quality assurance teams to ensure seamless project execution and compliance with customer specifications, project requirements, applicable standards (ASME, API, AWS, ISO), and regulatory requirements
  • Assess and develop supplier relationships to ensure quality, reliability, and long-term collaboration while maintaining strong partnerships with all relevant stakeholders
  • Support the Sales Team with cost estimation for capital projects and provide customers with detailed project updates, cunduct meetings, and provide technical coordination throughout project execution
  • Identify, assess, and mitigate project risks to ensure successful delivery while maintaining quality, schedule, and budget objectives

Qualifications

  • Technical expertise in pipe spool fabrication and skid assemblies
  • Excellent organizational and problem-solving skills
  • Willing to travel internationally
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Experience reading and interpreting design, engineering and construction drawings, as well as knowledge of welding processes and code compliance, is considered an asset
  • Fluent in English, German is considered an asset

Benefits

  • Extended medical, dental and vision coverage
  • AD&D, life and travel insurance
  • Paid sick days
  • Opportunities for professional growth and career development
  • Additional paid vacation days
  • Flexible schedule
  • On-Site parking
  • Performance bonus based on Management's discretion

Join BUTTING Canada as a Junior Project Manager and become part of an international, customer-focused team working in a modern office environment with flexible work arrangements. We value our employees and are committed to supporting your professional growth and well-being through a competitive compensation package and comprehensive benefits.
