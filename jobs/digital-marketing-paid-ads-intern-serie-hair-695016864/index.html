About the Role

About Serie Hair

Remote digital marketing internship in e-commerce

Full Description

Job Summary
We are seeking a motivated Digital Marketing & Paid Ads Intern to join our dynamic marketing team. This paid internship offers an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in digital marketing strategies, paid advertising campaigns, and analytics. The ideal candidate will be eager to learn about SEO, Google Ads, social media advertising, and e-commerce marketing while supporting our ongoing marketing initiatives. This role is perfect for individuals passionate about digital marketing and looking to develop practical skills in a fast-paced environment.

Responsibilities

  • Assisting with Meta Ads campaigns (Instagram & Facebook)
  • Supporting TikTok Ads and content promotion strategies
  • Researching Pinterest trends and Pinterest Ads opportunities
  • Assisting with Google Ads campaigns and keyword research
  • Monitoring campaign performance and reporting insights
  • Brainstorming creative ideas for ads and content
  • Helping organize marketing assets and content calendars
  • Supporting social media posting and engagement
  • Assisting with influencer and creator campaign coordination

Requirements:

  • Interest in digital marketing, social media, or e-commerce
  • Familiarity with platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, and Google
  • Strong organizational and communication skills
  • Creative mindset with attention to detail
  • Comfortable working independently in a remote environment
  • Bonus if you have experience with Canva, Shopify, Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads, or Google Analytics

Additional information:

  • Remote position
  • 3-month internship contract
  • Flexible schedule
  • 1–2 virtual meetings per week

This opportunity is designed for someone looking to build real-world experience and strengthen their portfolio in digital marketing and paid media.

Work Location: Remote
