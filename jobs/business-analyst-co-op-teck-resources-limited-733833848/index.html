About our Vancouver Office

Located in the heart of downtown Vancouver, between the Pacific Ocean and the Coast Mountains, Teck’s Corporate Office sits in one of Canada’s most culturally diverse cities.

Surrounded by world-renowned nature and globally inspired cuisine, the office brings together many of Teck’s corporate functions - all working toward Teck’s purpose of providing the essential resources the world relies on.

Application Deadline: We will review applications on a rolling-basis

Duration: 12 months

Positions Available: 1

Start Date:September 14, 2026

For this exciting position, we are specifically targeting students who have a background in Business Management, Information Systems, Marketing, Commercial Studies, Economics, or Engineering. Candidates are required to maintain enrollment in a Canadian post-secondary institution throughout the co-op term. They must intend to continue their studies for at least one academic term after it finishes. If you are not currently located near the work location, you must be willing to commute or temporarily relocate for the duration of the term. A valid study permit and studentship letter will be required where applicable.

The Business Analyst Co-Op will work closely with the Senior Business Advisor to improve performance reporting, metric tracking, and process improvements aligned with our Management Operating System. This role is ideal for someone strategic by nature, analytically inclined, diligent, and passionate about the convergence of business and technology. You will gain exposure to real-world scenarios around strategy, planning, and performance in a multi-national corporation and develop and present consulting-level presentations, while leveraging your practical programming capabilities to streamline analytical tasks.

The role will also be supporting the Teck Digital Systems’ (TDS) Strategy, Planning & Performance department in facilitating planning routines for TDS’ leadership team and engagement with relevant TDS partners. The individual is encouraged to collaborate with TDS business partners, including organizational change management, finance, human resources, communications, and risk management. The individual will ensure clear, concise communication by demonstrating communication skills and supporting in-service buildout objectives within the Performance group.

Don’t miss out on this opening to be part of one of Canada’s leading mining companies and join our team!

Key Responsibilities

  • Be a courageous safety leader, adhere to and sponsor safety and environmental rules and procedures
  • Contribute to the build and development of strategic processes in an agile environment, ensuring TDS and general SPP departmental objectives are met
  • Create detailed reporting presentations and documentation for TDS partners
  • Engage with stakeholders to ensure comprehensive and accurate reflections of TDS service areas’ performance
  • Develop communications strategies to transform business needs to drive performance improvement
  • Support in the service build-out process for the Performance Team by demonstrating business strategy frameworks
  • Build and maintain comprehensive documentation for performance management processes and procedures

Qualifications

  • Pursuing a degree in Business Administration / Commerce, Economics, Information Technology, Marketing, or Engineering
  • Expert in analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and identifying and analyzing large volumes of information
  • Passion for the intersection of business and technology, with a practical understanding of programming concepts
  • Demonstrable ability to compose polished, consulting-level presentation slides
  • Superb communication skills, both verbal and written, with the ability to clearly communicate data-driven insights
  • Outstanding attention to detail and strong organizational skills to balance multiple priorities within a fast-paced setting
  • Self-motivated, with a proven ability to learn new technologies and methodologies on the job
  • Familiarity with performance management frameworks, key performance indicators (KPIs), or business operating systems is a plus
  • Case Competition experience is strongly preferred
  • Experience with process mapping or business process improvement is an asset
  • Experience in Business Process Analysis and Development is an asset
  • Experience with Corporate Communication and Change Management is an asset
  • Proficiency in Spanish is an asset
