Job title : Junior Mining Engineer

Type of position :Régulier

Work location :Sudbury

Your future role on our team As an Engineer you will be reporting to the Team Lead for Mining Engineering. In your role you will be assisting on support mandates for mining operations and on mining studies, both in Canada and internationally. You will be assigned to both open pit and underground mining projects and will be given the opportunity to collaborate with the engineers and geologists in offices across Canada.

With us, you’ll get the opportunity to...

  • Import block models
  • Open pit optimization and cut-off grade analysis
  • Open pit design
  • Underground mining method selection
  • Underground stope optimization and mine design
  • Mine planning and production scheduling
  • Equipment fleet calculations
  • Capital and operating cost calculations
  • Write and contribute to study reports
  • Work in collaboration with our geology and drill and blast teams
  • Liaise with clients regarding technical matters and be able to professionally present results

Do your qualities and values match our corporate culture?

  • Results-driven
  • Thirst to learn and excel
  • Willingness to work together while having fun
  • Constantly seeking sustainable and agile solutions
  • Caring mindset that puts people first
  • Rigour and ethical

Overview Of Certifications And Job Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in mining engineering
  • Experience working at a mining operation
  • 2 to 5 years of experience relevant to the position
  • Member of the PEO
  • Availability to take on fly-in/fly-out (FIFO) assignments
  • Proficiency with mine design software, Minesight and Deswik are preferred
  • Asset : Bilingual (English and French)

At BBA, You Get Many Benefits

  • Access to many experts and coaching
  • A collaborative team that shares its ideas and knowledge
  • Opportunity for site work: an amazing career accelerator!
  • Interesting growth opportunities to match your ambitions
  • Custom training and development plans
  • A caring environment where everyone’s ideas are listened to and there is no perception of hierarchy

BBA is committed to promoting diversity and employment equity. We thank you for your interest in our firm. Please note that only those candidates selected will be contacted.
