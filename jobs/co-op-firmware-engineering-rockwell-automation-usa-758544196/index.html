Job Description

Rockwell Automation is the largest company in the world dedicated to industrial automation and information. Here, we connect the imaginations of people with the potential of technology to make the world more intelligent, more connected, and more productive.

The Firmware Engineering intern will perform project-oriented work under the direction of firmware development engineers that are focused on developing firmware for implementing features to control variable frequency drives (VFDs).

This is 12 month co-op and is full-time on-site at our Cambridge, ON office and does not provide relocation assistance.

Your Responsibilities:

  • Design and develop firmware.
  • Product testing and certification.
  • Coding activities (C/C++ and/or MATLAB/Simulink).
  • Maintain and develop test setups.
  • Maintain firmware metrics.
  • Document firmware applications and components.
  • Research into new technologies.

The Essentials - You Will Have:

  • Must be pursuing a bachelor's or advanced degree from an accredited college or university.
  • Legal authorization to work in Canada is required. We will not sponsor individuals for employment visas, now or in the future for this job opening.

The Preferred - You Might Also Have:

  • Must be working towards a Bachelor of Science degree in Mechatronics, Engineering or Computer Science from an accredited college or university.
  • Previous internship or co-op experience.
  • Experience with C/C++ or MATLAB/Simulink.
  • Exposure to oscilloscopes, multimeters and other hardware tools.
  • Experience with motor controls
  • Knowledge of PLC programming and Control Systems.

This position is part of a job family. Experience will be the determining factor for position level and compensation.

For this role, the Hourly Compensation is from $20 - $32/hour. Actual pay will be based on factors such as skills, knowledge, education, and experience.
