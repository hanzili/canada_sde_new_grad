About CBCL Limited

Civil/structural engineering firm with national reach

Full Description

We have an exciting opportunity for a Junior Structural Engineer to join our New Brunswick Structural Team in our Fredericton, NB office.

Do you like to work in a team environment to solve challenging structural problems? We have an exciting opportunity for a Junior Engineer to be part of our Structural Engineering practice. The successful candidate will support a variety of projects within the Industrial, Electrical Utility, Water and Waste Water, Material Handling and Heavy Civil structures side of the practice while gaining hands-on experience and mentorship from experienced professionals.

The successful candidate will have excellent communication skills, be a natural problem solver, have strong attention to detail, and be eager to learn and contribute to a collaborative team environment. Familiarity with industry standard software such as ETABS, SAP 2000, S-Frame, Lusas and Revit would be considered an asset.

Your Key Responsibilities:

  • Support structural engineering design tasks under the guidance of experienced engineers;
  • Assist with design calculations, analysis, drawings, reports, and technical documentation;
  • Contribute to the preparation of technical specifications and construction documents;
  • Coordinate with engineers, technologists, and other project team members to support project delivery;
  • Participate in site visits, field reviews, meetings, and data collection as required;
  • Assist with construction contract administration tasks under supervision;
  • Review and apply applicable codes, standards, and project requirements;
  • Develop technical skills through mentorship, training, and hands-on project experience; and
  • Communicate effectively with internal project teams, clients, and contractors.

Your Capabilities and Credentials:

  • Undergraduate degree in Civil Engineering.
  • Previous co-op, internship, or related experience in structural engineering or consulting would be considered an asset.
  • Registered or eligible to register as an Engineer with APEGNB.
  • An interest in structural design for industrial, electrical utility, water and wastewater, material handling, heavy civil, and building projects.
  • Familiarity with Revit and AutoCAD.
  • Detail oriented, organized, hardworking, with a team player attitude.
  • Ability to balance professional commitments to meet project deadlines.
  • Strong verbal and written communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Valid driver’s license and be able to travel to job sites within Atlantic Canada.

What we can offer you:

  • A competitive compensation package effective on your first day of employment:

  • Comprehensive health and dental benefits

  • CAAT Defined Benefit Pension Plan with employer contributions

  • Generous vacation allowance commensurate with experience

  • Maternity or Parental leave top-up

  • Health spending account
  • Wellness incentive
  • Membership dues reimbursement
  • Education and lifelong learning subsidization
  • Community volunteering incentive
  • Employee Family Assistance Program
  • Public transit subsidy

In addition to the above benefits, we offer:

  • Flexible work hours with occasional work-from-home opportunities, supporting both your professional and personal commitments.
  • Diverse team with a wide range of experience and expertise.
  • Exposure to a variety of projects.
  • Support to develop skills and pursue projects related to your own interests.
  • Collaboration between multiple disciplines and levels of expertise.
  • Mentoring opportunities to foster your personal and professional development.
  • Accessible and central offices in Atlantic Canada and Ottawa.
  • Access to training opportunities in areas ranging from technical development to leadership skills.
  • Competitive salary: $65,000 - $85,000 annually, depending on experience.

To meet pay transparency requirements, compensation ranges are included for roles in locations where required by law.
