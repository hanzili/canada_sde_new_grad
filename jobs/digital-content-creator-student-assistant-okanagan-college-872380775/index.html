Every course you create, every student you support and every connection you build, it all matters.

Be a part of a vibrant campus community, where the work you do every day truly makes a difference.

Your Opportunity

In accordance with Letter of Understanding #1 – Student Employees, and under the direct supervision of the Digital Marketing Specialist in College Relations, the Student assists the department in digital storytelling and promotional activities featuring OC students, faculty, and staff.

Functions and Duties

The Student will assist the supervisor by performing the following duties:

  1. Produce high-quality, engaging content (posts, videos, stories, reels, photos) that captures the OC student experience and showcases Okanagan College’s values of inclusivity, accessibility, and hands-on learning.
  2. Work with a variety of digital formats and platforms, ensuring consistency in messaging and tone.
  3. Manage and grow OC’s presence on social media platforms. Develop strategies to increase engagement and connect with OC students and prospective students through digital platforms.
  4. Collaborate with the College Relations team to ensure that content aligns with the broader marketing goals of Okanagan College.
  5. Develop and maintain a content calendar that aligns with the College’s key events and campaigns. Support the OC brand by ensuring all content reflects the College’s identity and values.
  6. Engage with students, faculty, and alumni to gather content ideas and identify student success stories. Attend events and activities on campus to capture content and promote the vibrant OC community. Be a representative for the student body and ensure that content resonates with peers.

Education and Experience

Enrolled in a post-secondary program at Okanagan College. Prior work experience is an asset.

Skills and Abilities

  • Strong writing and verbal communication skills, with experience in content creation (videography, photography).
  • Basic knowledge of digital editing tools (e.g., Canva, CapCut, or similar platforms) is an asset.
  • Passion for storytelling and a keen eye for creating visually appealing content.
  • Familiarity with social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook, etc.) and trends in digital marketing.
  • Ability to create engaging, shareable content tailored to different audiences.
  • Experience or interest in developing social media strategies to drive engagement.
  • Strong time management and project management abilities, with the ability to meet deadlines and juggle multiple tasks.
  • Detail-oriented, proactive, and able to work both independently and in a team environment.
  • Enthusiastic about building connections and working with diverse groups within the OC community.
  • Comfortable being on camera and interviewing and interacting with students, faculty, and staff to capture authentic stories.

Our Values

  • Students first: Creating meaningful life-long educational opportunities of the highest quality for alumni, current, and future students is at the heart of everything we do.
  • Community: We are one College. We enthusiastically embrace the opportunity to work with, learn from, and support everyone we encounter collaboratively and collegially.
  • Respect: We welcome, embrace, and celebrate that which make us unique in creating an environment in which every individual is valued, affirming our commitment to human dignity for all.
  • Courage: We are courageous in our actions in areas that contribute to positive change in our communities and beyond. We have the courage to be vulnerable. We are truthful, sincere, and act ethically with honesty and fairness.
  • Relationships: We steward meaningful relationships built on a foundation of reciprocity. We are compassionate, empathic, and care for the holistic well-being of students, employees, community members, and the land.
  • Distinction: We choose activities in which we can achieve excellence and positively impact society.
