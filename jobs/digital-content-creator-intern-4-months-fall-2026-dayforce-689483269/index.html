About the Role

Create short-form video content and edit clips

About Dayforce

Global HCM leader; strong marketing content exposure

Full Description

Dayforce, a global leader in Human Capital Management (HCM) with headquarters in Toronto, Ontario, and Minneapolis, Minnesota, operates across North America, EMEA, and APJ regions. Our Cloud HCM platform, recognized for its unified database and continuous calculation engine, enhances efficiency, productivity, and compliance for global workforces. We are committed to "Making Work Life Better™" for employees, customers, and partners worldwide. As an Intern at Dayforce, you’ll gain real-world experience with meaningful responsibilities, access to development programs, and the chance to collaborate globally, bringing new ideas and optimizing processes across multiple business units. Job Title: Digital Content Creator Intern

Location : Virtual

Duration: Fall 2026 – 4 months (September - December 2026)

Availability : *Full-time availability of 37.5 – 40 hours weekly is required to be eligible for this opportunity. Benefits For Students

  • Experience working for one of the fastest growing Human Capital Management technology companies in the world Access to Dayforce’s development programs and resources
  • Ability to work with as well as create relationships with members across the globe
  • Autonomy to bring forth new ideas and optimize existing structures within the organization
  • Meaningful responsibilities, enabling you to obtain ‘real-world’ experience
  • Dynamic hands-on exposure to multiple business-units and stakeholders across Dayforce

About The Opportunity Join the Dayforce Creative Studio to help create engaging short-form video and social content that amplifies our annual customer conference Dayforce Discover, customer stories, product innovation, and key marketing moments. This role is ideal for a creative, social-first storyteller who is excited about video production, digital marketing, and live event coverage.

What You’ll Get To Do

  • Capture and create short-form video content for YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, Instagram Reels, and other social platforms
  • Edit keynote highlights, customer testimonials, executive soundbites, and behind-the-scenes content into engaging clips
  • Collaborate with Brand, Social Media, Events, and Product Marketing teams on content planning and storytelling
  • Assist with content calendars, video publishing, and caption writing
  • Help identify and implement trends and best practices in short-form social media content

Skills And Experience We Value

  • Interest in video content creation, social media marketing, or digital storytelling
  • Experience using video editing tools such as CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, Canva, or similar platforms
  • Familiarity with YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and LinkedIn video trends
  • Strong communication, creativity, and collaboration skills
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple projects
  • Currently pursuing a degree or diploma in Marketing, Communications, Media, Digital Content, Film, Journalism, or a related field

What Would Really Make You Stand Out

  • Experience creating content for personal, school, or brand social media channels
  • Photography, videography, or motion graphics experience
  • Basic knowledge of social media analytics and engagement metrics
  • Comfort interviewing people or capturing content in live event settings
  • YouTube, LinkedIn, or Instagram content examples showcasing editing and storytelling skills

We understand that no matter where you are, choosing a new employer is always one of life’s most stressful events. We encourage you to check us out at Dayforce.com or follow us on LinkedIn to learn more about how we are impacting the world of work for our customers, employees and you!

Fraudulent Recruiting Beware of fraudulent recruiting. Legitimate Dayforce contacts will use an @dayforce.com or @Ceridian.com email address. We do not request money, checks, equipment orders, or sensitive personal data during the recruitment process. If you have been asked for any of the above, or believe you have been contacted by someone posing as a Dayforce employee, please refer to our fraudulent recruiting statement found here: https://www.dayforce.com/be-aware-of-recruiting-fraud
