Full Description

Program Overview:

Fraser Region Aboriginal Friendship Centre Association is looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Junior Bookkeeper to join our finance team. As a Junior Bookkeeper, you will play a crucial role in maintaining accurate financial records and supporting various accounting functions. This is an excellent opportunity for a student who is looking to kick-start their career in accounting and gain valuable experience in a dynamic and growing organization.

Responsibilities:

– Record financial transactions accurately and timely into the accounting system.

– Assist with accounts payable and accounts receivable processes, including processing invoices, preparing payment vouchers, and tracking outstanding payments.

– Help maintain the general ledger by recording journal entries, categorizing expenses, and updating account balances.

– Support the tracking and reconciliation of company expenses, including reviewing expense reports and verifying receipts.

– Assist in the preparation of financial reports, such as balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements.

– Provide administrative support for payroll-related processes after training/probation period.

– Maintain organized physical or digital filing systems for financial documents.

– Collaborate effectively with team members, supervisors, and external stakeholders to address inquiries and contribute to the overall financial operations of the organization.

– Assist with filing, printing, and other administrative duties as required.

Requirements:

– Bachelor’s degree/Diploma in accounting, finance, or a related field (or currently pursuing a degree in these fields) would be an asset.

– Experience with Sage/Quickbooks and other Accounting software is an asset.

– Strong attention to detail and a high level of accuracy in data entry and record keeping.

– Knowledge of basic accounting principles and procedures.

– Proficiency in using Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Word, Outlook).

– Excellent organizational and time management skills, with the ability to prioritize tasks and meet deadlines.

– Effective communication skills, both written and verbal.

– Ability to work collaboratively as part of a team and take initiative when necessary.

– Prior experience or internships in accounting or bookkeeping is a plus, but not required.

– Demonstrated focus and ability to work independently to complete work within deadlines

– Ability to maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive financial and employee information with discretion.
