About Green 2 Pack Inc

Green 2 Pack Inc is a growing manufacturer of food packaging products located in Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu, Quebec. We manufacture plastic food containers, paper cups, paper plates, paper bags, take-out packaging and other food packaging products for customers across Canada.

We are looking for a motivated Manufacturing Engineering Apprentice to join our production team. This position is ideal for candidates who are interested in manufacturing engineering, production equipment optimization, and industrial automation. Comprehensive on-the-job training will be provided.

Responsibilities

  • Assist with the setup, adjustment, and optimization of production equipment
  • Monitor machine performance and production efficiency
  • Support production to ensure stable and continuous machine operation
  • Troubleshoot equipment and process issues to minimize production downtime
  • Assist with commissioning new production equipment
  • Optimize machine parameters to improve product quality and productivity
  • Work closely with production and quality teams
  • Participate in continuous improvement projects
  • Maintain production records and technical documentation
  • Follow all workplace health and safety procedures

Qualifications

  • Diploma or education in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Mechatronics, Automation or a related field is an asset
  • Strong mechanical aptitude and willingness to learn
  • Basic understanding of manufacturing processes and industrial equipment
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Comfortable working in a manufacturing environment
  • Reliable, responsible and team-oriented
  • French required
  • English is an asset
  • Shift work may be required

What We Offer

  • Comprehensive on-the-job training
  • Stable full-time employment
  • Competitive salary
  • Health and dental benefits
  • Paid vacation
  • Career development opportunities
  • Friendly and professional working environment

Work Schedule

  • Monday to Friday
  • Day Shift
  • Shift work may be required
  • Overtime may be required during peak production periods
