Start Date: Immediate Term Length: 4, 8 or 12 months At Gibson, we believe our competitive advantage lies in the strength and talents of our employees. The Role This exciting Co-op opportunity is designed for a student who is looking to gasin hands-on experience in a terminal laboratory environment. As a Lab Co-op Student, you will work with experienced lab personnel to support sample handling, routine testing, equipment care, documentation, and safe day-to-day lab operations. This role provides practical exposure to industrial laboratory work while helping you build technical skills, confidence, and an understanding of how accurate lab data supports terminal operations and product quality.

What You’ll Do

  • Assists with routine laboratory testing and analysis of terminal products under supervision and in accordance with approved procedures.
  • Assists with sample receiving, preparation, labelling, documentation, retention, and disposal as required.
  • Collects or assists with collecting samples within the terminal as required, following applicable safety and site procedures.
  • Maintains accurate records of testing, observations, and assigned lab activities.
  • Assists with routine housekeeping, inventory checks, and organization of laboratory supplies and consumables.
  • Assists with preventative maintenance activities by completing basic cleaning, setup, and documentation tasks as directed.
  • Follows safe work procedures and contributes to a safe and organized lab environment in accordance with Gibson Safety and Environmental policies.
  • Demonstrates a personal commitment to Environment, Health & Safety (EH&S) by participating in applicable EH&S programs, reporting hazards, and following all Company EH&S policies and procedures.
  • May assist with special projects, data entry, procedure reviews, or other duties as assigned.

What You Bring To The Role

  • Currently enrolled in a chemistry, laboratory technology, chemical technology, environmental science, engineering technology, or related post-secondary co-op program.
  • Ability to work 12-hour day shifts on a regular rotation, including weekends, with some 8-hour shifts scheduled based on lab needs.
  • Strong oral and written communication skills, including the ability to communicate clearly by radio when required.
  • Valid Class 5 Driver’s License, or ability to obtain or meet site requirements, as required for site sampling duties.
  • Interest in laboratory operations, petroleum testing, quality control, or industrial laboratory environments.
  • Basic understanding of chemistry, laboratory safety, sample handling, and accurate record keeping.
  • Experience with laboratory instruments, analytical testing, or gas chromatography would be considered an asset but is not required.
  • Strong attention to detail with the ability to follow written procedures and ask questions when clarification is needed.
  • Demonstrates proficiency with MS Outlook, Excel, and Word.

Working Conditions

  • Work is performed in a laboratory and terminal environment with exposure to routine laboratory chemicals, petroleum products, field sampling areas, and active operational areas.
  • Worker must wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) as required by the company's safety policies and as required for weather conditions.
  • This job is subject to background checks as outlined in Gibson Energy’s Background Checks policy. Background checks may include: pre-employment drug & alcohol testing, pre-employment functional fitness testing, reference checks, education verification, driver's license verification and a criminal history check.

About The Company Gibson Energy Inc. (“Gibson” or the “Company”) is a diversified North American energy infrastructure company headquartered in Calgary, Alberta. They have significant terminal assets, including 25 million barrels of storage and over 300 miles (500 kilometres) of crude pipelines across North America. The company has been providing market access to the oil and gas industry for over 70 years and is publicly traded on the Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX:GEI) with a market capitalization of approximately $4 billion.

Gibson Energy Operates Through Two Main Segments

  • Infrastructure: This includes terminals, marine loading, rail loading and unloading facilities, gathering pipelines, a diluent recovery unit, and a crude oil processing facility. Key facilities within this segment include the Hardisty, Edmonton, and Ingleside Terminals, as well as gathering pipelines in the United States and a crude oil processing facility in Moose Jaw, Saskatchewan.
  • Marketing: This involves the purchasing, selling, storing, and optimizing of hydrocarbon products.

Gibson Energy has a strong presence in the United States, due to the acquisition of the Gateway Terminal in Ingleside, Texas. This terminal is a world-class liquids terminal and export facility with very large crude carrier (VLCC) capabilities and direct pipeline connections to key basins like the Permian and Eagle Ford. The acquisition strengthens Gibson's cash flow with over 95% of the revenue under take-or-pay contracts with high-quality counterparties.
