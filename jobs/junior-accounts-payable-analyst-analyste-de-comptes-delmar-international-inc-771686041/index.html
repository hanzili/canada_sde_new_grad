Full Description

Mandate:
Reporting to the Accounts Payable Manager, the Analyst is responsible for managing a portfolio of Accounts Payable to meet or exceed corporate objectives within established policies. The analyst will be responsible for:

• Verifying invoices and preparing high volume of payments
• Reconciling statements with invoices
• Filing and any other day to day duties as assigned
• High volume of invoices to process

Requirements:
• Minimum 1 year of experience working in accounts payable or degree in Accounting field
• CEGEP or College Diploma considered an asset
• Bilingual (English and French)

What You Offer:

• Great analytical and organizational skills required
• Strong communication and listening skills
• Ability to work effectively in a diverse work group
