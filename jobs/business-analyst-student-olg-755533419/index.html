About OLG

Full Description

Job Description Business Analyst Student Location: Sault Ste. Marie (Onsite)

Duration: Fall (September – December 2026)

Type: Student Full Time

Range: $18.50 - $28.50

We are ready to take this game to the next level and need someone passionate about supporting the team through planning and execution of business requirements with an emphasis on User Acceptance Testing as part of the Deployable User Acceptance Testing Team.

YOUR ROLE IN THE GAME Reporting to the Sr Digital Business Analysis Manager, you will be empowered to:

  • Developing an understanding of business processes
  • Supporting UAT, operational readiness, change management, and post-implementation tasks
  • Assisting in identifying the technology, expertise, and stakeholders needed to pass acceptance criteria
  • Conduct manual test cases across a variety of products and solutions as part of a team
  • Coordinating with internal stakeholders, Subject Matter Experts, and vendors
  • Supporting business deliverables such as user stories, test plans and test reports

Education & Experience WHAT YOU BRING TO THE GAME

  • Currently enrolled in a post-secondary program with a focus on business administration, computer science, finance, or marketing discipline
  • Sound interest in analysis, including data collection and cleaning
  • Comfortable working as part of a versatile team
  • The passion and hunger to learn, high attention to detail, and strong time-management and organizational skills to manage multiple deadlines and priorities
  • Work collaboratively within an agile environment Work collaboratively within an agile environment

Benefits Of Joining The Winning Team

  • Growth Focus: Become your best self with access to new opportunities and personalized support programs.
  • Comprehensive Learning: 24/7 access to robust online learning programs.
  • Equity, diversity, and inclusion: Core to OLG’s culture, empowering employees to bring their authentic selves and make a meaningful impact across Ontario.

To learn more about our organization and other opportunities visit our career site.
