About the Role

Engage customers, drive sales, merchandise displays

About Sleep Country

Canadian sleep retailer investing in employee development

Full Description

At Silk & Snow, we believe our home is our sanctuary. So, when you purchase mattresses, bedding, bath, or furniture with us, you can rest easy knowing that it’s made with the care your home deserves.

As aSales Associate you provide an unparallel shopping experience for customers looking to elevate their home decor, bedroom aesthetics, and sleep.

Location: 1133 Yonge St, Toronto, ON M4T 2Y7

In this current open role you will:

  • Engage with customers to identify their needs and wants, promote our quality products and service offerings.
  • Ensure our stores are always visually appealing through product presentation, replenishment and store housekeeping.
  • Work together as part of the sales team to exceed goals and expectations.
  • Participate in visual directives in accordance with company product launches, including but not limited to setting displays, merchandising products, and maintaining pricing signage.
  • Be the face of our combined values, with a focus on customer, wellness, transparency and sustainability.
  • Be responsible for ensuring that the story behind our brand gets communicated to our customers
  • Create and curate a comfortable environment for customers to experience our products and learn about our brand.
  • Perform other duties as assigned by management.
  • Comply with all company policies and procedures.
  • Have fun and promote creativity.

The Qualifications and Experience we like to see:

  • Ideally have 1- 3 years of experience in Sales, Retail and/or Customer Service, this is considered an asset and not mandatory as we will invest in the right person.
  • Passion for aesthetics and experience with home style and décor, design and visual merchandising would be an asset.
  • A positive, optimistic attitude and a strong customer focus.
  • Be ambitious and willing to commit to continuous education and learning.
  • Full time position; available to work any days, evenings and weekends.
  • Proven ability to exceed selling goals.
  • Critical thinking skills and the ability to tackle problems on your own.
  • You are driven to constantly improve yourself and the experience you manage.

Why members of our Sales Team love working at Silk & Snow:

  • This position offers a base pay salary of $20,800.00 plus competitive commission as well as monetary bonuses for meeting monthly targets. There are no limits to your earning potential!
  • Diverse and inclusive work environment.
  • We promote 100% leadership roles internally.
  • We will invest in you and provide extensive sales training on our products and services.
  • Full medical, and dental benefits.
  • Tuition Reimbursement Program that covers professional AND personal development.
  • Maternity/Parental leave top up benefits.

Additional Information

Our recruitment process incorporates various screening technologies, which may include AI, to screen for employability or suitability. Human recruiters review all applications.
