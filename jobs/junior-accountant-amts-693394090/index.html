About the Role

About AMTS

Private multi-entity group with broad accounting exposure

Full Description

About the Opportunity

Join a privately held group of companies with a strong, established presence across HVAC product distribution, real estate, and importing. Our core distribution company generates $35M+ in annual revenue, and you'll support the full accounting function across all entities making this a uniquely broad and hands-on role for someone looking to grow.

About the Companies

You'll be supporting accounting across a family of related businesses:

  • HVAC Distributor - distribution of HVAC products to wholesalers (Prophet 21 ERP)/ (AccountEdge)
  • Property Holding Companies - Ongoing construction and rental revenue (AccountEdge)

The Role

This is a hands-on, in-office position focused on the day-to-day accounting activities, which will lead to broader responsibilities over time.

Core Responsibilities

  • Process A/P invoices and ensure timely payments, capturing purchase discounts with major U.S. fund suppliers
  • Monitor and reconcile 13+ bank accounts daily; communicate daily EFT receipts
  • Post and track tenant rents at month-start; record loans throughout the month
  • Manage U.S. foreign exchange purchases
  • Perform monthly bank reconciliations
  • Prepare and file tax provision payments, WSIB reporting, and HST returns (monthly and quarterly across entities)
  • Run month-end inventory valuation; reconcile GL to subledger for inventory, A/R, and A/P
  • Maintain asset files and balance sheet account working files

Growth Responsibilities

  • Learn Prophet 21 for accounting system troubleshooting
  • Take over ADP biweekly payroll, AccountEdge payroll, and monthly commission and subcontractor payments
  • Administer the Employee Benefits program
  • Support year-end reporting and account analysis preparation for the external accounting firm

What We're Looking For

  • Post-secondary education in Accounting or Finance
  • 1–3 years of hands-on accounting experience (AP/GL preferred)
  • Experience with accounting software such as AccountEdge, Prophet 21, or similar ERP
  • Exposure to multi-entity accounting environments an asset
  • Strong attention to detail; comfortable with high transaction volumes and recurring deadlines
  • Experience with foreign currency (U.S. funds) an asset
  • Self-motivated with attention to detail

Why Join Us?

  • Rare breadth of exposure distribution, real estate, and importing all in one role
  • Stable, long-tenured business with a clear path to more responsibility
  • Learn payroll, benefits, and year-end processes to round out your skill set
