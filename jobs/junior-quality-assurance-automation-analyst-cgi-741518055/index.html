Full Description

Position Description

This role is hybrid and requires working from our client's office in downtown Toronto a minimum of 2 days per week (subject to change).

We are seeking a Junior QA Analyst to join our Quality Assurance team. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience with Selenium automation using Java and possess a strong understanding of software testing methodologies. This role involves developing automated test scripts, executing regression and functional testing, identifying defects, and working closely with development teams to ensure high-quality software delivery.

Candidates with Banking or Salesforce experience will be given preference.

Your future duties and responsibilities

  • Develop, maintain, and execute automated test scripts using Selenium WebDriver and Java.
  • Perform functional, regression, integration, and smoke testing.
  • Design and maintain automation frameworks for web applications.
  • Identify, document, and track software defects using defect management tools.
  • Collaborate with developers, business analysts, and product owners to understand requirements.
  • Participate in sprint planning, backlog grooming, and Agile ceremonies.
  • Execute manual testing when automation is not feasible.
  • Maintain test cases and testing documentation.
  • Support CI/CD testing initiatives.
  • Participate in production validation and release testing.

Required Qualifications To Be Successful In This Role

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related discipline.
  • 1–3 years of QA Automation experience.
  • Strong experience with Selenium WebDriver.
  • Good programming skills in Java.
  • Understanding of SDLC and STLC.
  • Experience with regression and functional testing.
  • Familiarity with defect management tools such as Jira or Azure DevOps.
  • Knowledge of Agile/Scrum methodologies.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Banking or Financial Services experience.
  • Salesforce testing experience.
  • API testing using Postman or REST Assured.
  • Git and Jenkins experience.
  • Basic SQL knowledge.
  • Exposure to BDD/Cucumber.
  • Experience working in CI/CD environments.
