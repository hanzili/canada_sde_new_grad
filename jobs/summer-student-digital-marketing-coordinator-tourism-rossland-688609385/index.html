Full Description

Tourism Rossland is now accepting applications for the full-time position of Summer Student - Digital Marketing Coordinator. We are looking for an outgoing, enthusiastic student with digital media experience–including photography and videography. This 12-week position is based on 35 hours per week with a flexible schedule.

Start Date: June 15th 2026

Wage: $23/ hour

Contract: up to 12 weeks with a possibility to extend

The Government of Canada is partially funding this job through the Canada Summer Jobs program. To apply for this job, you must:

  • Be aged between 15 and 30 years old on the start date of the job;
  • Have a valid Social Insurance Number (SIN); and
  • Be a Canadian citizen, a permanent resident of Canada, or a person who has been granted refugee status in Canada.
  • International students and other temporary residents of Canada are not eligible.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:

  • Create, post and manage content and updates on Tourism Rossland’s digital media channels (Instagram, Facebook)
  • Support the development of marketing projects and marketing collateral for print and web
  • Produce and coordinate the development of new photo and video assets
  • Manage and organize all creative assets in Dropbox & SmugMug
  • Assist in any other duties that may be assigned by the Marketing Manager or Executive Director

Required Skills:

  • Knowledge of tourism, hospitality and marketing
  • Skilled in all digital media platforms with the ability to create engaging content highlighting the region
  • Excellent verbal, written and online communication skills
  • General office skills, including basic computer literacy
  • Experience with photo and/or video production
  • Self-starter and able to work well solo or with a small team and be goal oriented
  • High degree of sensitivity in dealing with the Public, Board and Stakeholders
  • Able to work independently, show initiative and meet deadlines
  • Able to travel and work flexible hours as required

If this position suits you, please submit your resume and a cover letter explaining us why you are the perfect candidate to melissa@tourismrossland.com before Mon June 1st. Please use the following subject line: “Summer Student – Digital Marketing Coordinator”. We thank all applicants, however only those selected for an interview will be contacted.

Pay: From $23.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Casual dress
  • Flexible schedule

Work Location: Hybrid remote in Rossland, BC
