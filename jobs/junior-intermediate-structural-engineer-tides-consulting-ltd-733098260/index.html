About TIDES Consulting Ltd.

Full Description

Our growing team has an immediate opening for a Junior Structural Engineer. Our projects include residential, commercial and industrial projects.

· Eligible to work in Canada, Permenant residence, or Canadian citizen

. Registered EIT with APEGBC or similar organization

· Bachelor Degree or Master’s degree in Civil Engineering

· Coursework in structural analysis, timber design, concrete design, steel design, dynamic analysis

· Knowledge of BC engineering practice and regulations

. Familiarity with EGBC requirements, guidelines and standards

· Proficient with Canadian Building and Material codes

· Experienced in residential, commercial and industrial projects.

· Able to communicate with client, contractors and fellow team members in an effective manner

· Able to write reports and letters at a high level.

· Organized and have good planning skills

· Experience with AutoCAD and Revit is preferred

· Experience with structural analysis software such as SAP2000, Etabs, SAFE

· Proficient with spreadsheet software and/or Mathcad

· Ability to multi task and meet tight deadlines

· Highly motivated

· Have valid Driving license and his own Vehicle

· Able to carry out site inspections as required

We offer a Competitive Compensation, Permanent Job Category, Extended Health Benefits, Dental Benefits, RRSP Benefits, Paid Time off, and Yearly Bonus.

You will be part of a small, expertise-driven team where our commitment to employees, clients and producing a quality product is our top priority.

Please email your resume, cover letter, your availability and expected salary. Please submit only PDF files.

We thank all applicants in advance for their interest in our current opening. However, only applicants selected for an interview will be contacted.
