Full Description

Job Overview

We are seeking a full-time experienced Sales Associate to join our dynamic retail team. This role is ideal for motivated individuals with a passion for customer service and technology sales. The successful candidate will be responsible for engaging customers, demonstrating products, and driving sales through effective communication and upselling strategies. Multilingual abilities and technical knowledge are highly valued to enhance customer interactions and expand our reach. This position offers an exciting opportunity to grow within a fast-paced retail environment while delivering exceptional service.

Duties

  • Greet customers warmly and provide excellent customer service throughout their shopping experience
  • Conduct product demonstrations, especially for technology-related items, to educate and engage customers
  • Assist customers with product selection, answering questions about features and benefits
  • Perform merchandising duties to ensure attractive product displays and organized shelves
  • Operate POS systems accurately for sales transactions, including cash handling and processing payments
  • Upsell complementary products to maximize sales opportunities
  • Manage inventory levels, restock shelves, and maintain a clean, organized store environment
  • Handle customer inquiries via phone or in person with professional phone etiquette
  • Maintain organizational skills to ensure smooth daily operations and meet sales goals
  • Support team members with cashiering duties, basic math calculations, and time management tasks as needed

Qualifications

  • Proven experience in retail sales, preferably in technology or related fields
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to explain technical products clearly and effectively
  • Bilingual or multilingual abilities are highly desirable to serve diverse customer bases
  • Familiarity with merchandising, POS systems, cash handling, and basic math skills required
  • Excellent organizational skills and time management capabilities to prioritize tasks efficiently
  • Demonstrated customer service excellence with professional phone etiquette
  • Ability to perform product demos confidently and engage customers through upselling techniques
  • Previous experience in technical sales or product demonstrations is a plus
  • Ability to work full-time hours with flexibility to adapt to store schedules

This role offers an engaging work environment where your sales expertise can thrive while providing valuable service to our customers.

Pay: From $80,000.00 per year

Benefits:

  • On-site parking
  • Store discount

Work Location: In person
