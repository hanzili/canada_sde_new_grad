Junior Data Engineer

Develop your talent, work with the best in the business

Have you finished your studies in Computer or Data Science (or are you about to graduate), and do you want to take your first steps in data engineering? At Blenddata, you get the chance to make an immediate impact and develop yourself at lightning speed in an enthusiastic and close-knit team.

What does a Junior Data Engineer do at Blenddata?

As a Junior Data Engineer, you will collaborate on data-driven solutions for clients such as Rabobank, De Lage Landen and Damen Shipyards. You will build modular data pipelines that unlock data from different systems, prepare them and prepare them for analysis. Together with experienced colleagues, you will not only learn the technology, but also how to really help customers.

Together with your colleagues, you work as a knowledge partner for our customers. In a close-knit team, you will learn from experienced engineers while building modular data pipelines that process data efficiently. You play an active role in the team, mastering new technologies and adding value to projects that are future-proof and scalable.
