YDC (YDCPro) is a North American based technology consulting and talent solutions firm with HQ in Toronto, Canada, helping organizations accelerate digital transformation. Since 2016, it has focused on artificial intelligence, data analytics, cloud computing, and application development, delivering tailored solutions that improve efficiency and business performance.

We are looking for a motivated fresh graduate or junior QA candidate with foundational knowledge of both automation and manual testing. The candidate should be eager to learn, take ownership, and grow within the team.

Depending on performance and demonstrated capability, this opportunity may lead to a full-time role as a Tester or Software Engineer.

Ideal Candidate must have

  • 4 to 5 years of experience in QA, Software Testing, or SDET roles .
  • Strong experience with Test automation tools such as Playwright or Selenium
  • Experience with manual, integration, regression, end-to-end, API, and UI testing .
  • Familiarity with Agile/Scrum environments, Jira/Xray, CI/CD pipelines, and source control tools
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and collaboration skills.

We are seeking an experienced Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) to help ensure the quality, reliability, and performance of our software products.

  • This role combines software testing expertise with automation development and requires close collaboration with Product, Development, and Systems teams throughout the software development lifecycle.
  • The ideal candidate will have experience with manual and automated testing, test framework development, workflow-driven applications, API/UI testing, and modern quality engineering practices, including AI-assisted testing.
  • The SDET will play a key role in driving product quality, improving test coverage, supporting release readiness, and contributing to continuous improvement initiatives.

Nice to have

Experience with

  • AI-driven testing,
  • Gherkin scripting, and
  • workflow-based enterprise applications is highly desirable

This is an excellent opportunity for a quality-focused professional who enjoys building robust testing solutions, improving software reliability, and partnering with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality products.
