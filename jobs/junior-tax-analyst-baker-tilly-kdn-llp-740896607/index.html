We are looking for a Junior Tax Analyst to join our team—bring your ambition and shape what comes next.

Be part of something bigger. Build with us today — Now. For tomorrow.

At Baker Tilly, we care about the impact you make. That’s why we’re invested in your growth from day one—because the steps you take today define your tomorrow.

We are looking for a Junior Tax Analyst  to join our team—a role where your expertise will drive meaningful outcomes and your ambition will help shape what comes next. Here, you’ll collaborate with a dynamic team of professionals, united by a shared commitment to making a difference—for our clients, our communities, and one another.

Your role:

As a Junior Tax Analyst, you will have the opportunity to work with senior team members on a variety of tax files, assisting clients, and provide support to the tax team.
The successful candidate will assist in preparing tax returns, conducting research, and providing support for our clients' tax needs while gaining valuable experience in a professional accounting environment. 

  • Assisting with the preparation of various Canadian income tax returns (T1, T2, T3) and tax election forms.
  • Contribute to client satisfaction by providing clients with quality, timely and responsive services and work products.
  • Demonstrate an understanding of increasingly complex tax concepts.
  • Discuss moderately complex tax issues with the engagement team and client management.
  • Keep informed of current tax developments and effectively apply tax knowledge to client situations. Thoroughly and accurately analyze information.
  • Prepare practical and effective approaches to the client's tax situation and arrive at appropriate conclusions.
  • Preparing corporate tax returns.
  • Share tax planning ideas with others.
  • Participate in and contribute to achieving team goals.
  • Assist in the development of recommendations and implementation plans and assist in making quality decisions in complex areas.

What you bring to the table:

  • Accounting degree. E.g. BBA, BCOMM, or CPA designation.
  • Entry level technical knowledge and skills.
  • Knowledge of Caseware/Caseview and TaxPrep would be an asset.
  • Interpersonal and relationship building skills.
  • Team player with a positive can-do approach.
  • Attention to detail.
  • Creative problem-solving ability and critical thinking
  • Seeks and researches solutions or answers.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Experience in delivery of quality client service.
  • A general knowledge of firm's products and services.
  • A general understanding of industry and market trends including opportunities, global and local business initiatives and stakeholder/competitor interests.
  • A desire to dig deep into work, learn all they can and have fun!

What's in it for you?

  • Banked overtime program (say hello to summer Fridays off
  • Pre-approved CPA employer
  • Financial and mentorship support to obtain CPA designation
  • 3 weeks paid vacation to start moving to 4 weeks once CPA designation is received
  • Challenging and varied assignments
  • Ongoing training and support for professional development
  • Competitive compensation
  • Employer paid benefits package including comprehensive health and dental starting from day one
  • Enhanced mental health benefits
  • A fully-stocked fridge with drinks as well as coffee and tea throughout the year - don't forget the snacks and fun during busy season
  • Wellness subsidy
  • RRSP matching program
  • A leadership team committed to your success
  • A professional, fun and friendly working environment
