Full Description

Position Overview

NABI is hiring a Digital Marketing Specialist to support content creation and frameworks for social media, and promotion of programs and events. This role focuses on helping share NABI’s story and increasing awareness of opportunities for entrepreneurs.

About NABI

NABI is a community-based business incubator that supports entrepreneurs through programming, mentorship, and connections. We help individuals start and grow businesses in a supportive environment.

The Opportunity This position is funded through the Canada Summer Jobs (CSJ) program. To be eligible, applicants must:

• Be between 15 and 30 years of age at the start of employment

• Be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or person with refugee protection under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act

• Be legally entitled to work in Canada (International students are not eligible for this position)

What You’ll Do

• Help create a social media and marketing framework

• Create simple graphics and visuals for social media and events

• Curate content for newsletter and social media

• Draft and schedule posts (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook)

• Assist with newsletters and website updates

• Support storytelling through entrepreneur features and highlights

What You’ll Gain

• Hands-on marketing and content creation experience

• Exposure to branding and communications

• Experience working with small businesses and entrepreneurs

• Portfolio-building opportunities

Who This Role is For

You might be a good fit if you:

• Enjoy creating content and have an eye for clean, engaging visuals and messaging

• Are comfortable using tools like Canva or similar design platforms

• Are familiar with social media platforms and understand how to write posts that engage an audience

• Can work independently and follow through on tasks like drafting, scheduling, and updating content

• Pay attention to detail and care about consistency in branding and presentation

• Are interested in storytelling, marketing, or helping share the work of entrepreneurs

• Are open to feedback and willing to iterate and improve your work over time

JOB TYPE: Full-time, Temporary (8-10 week term) DURATION: 35 Hours/week, 8-10 weeks WORK LOCATION: Hybrid 40% remote, 60% on-site at NABI Building, St. Albert COMPENSATION: $17/Hour START DATE: Between June 22 and July 6

Application Instructions

Please review the Canada Summer Jobs (CSJ) eligibility criteria outlined above before applying.

To apply, please submit your resume along with a short response (max 1 page total) addressing the following:

  1. Tell us a bit about yourself and why you’re interested in this role
  2. Share an example of something you’ve worked on (school, work, or personal project) that relates to this position
  3. Confirm that you meet the Canada Summer Jobs eligibility requirements

Application deadline is 11:59pm, Sunday June 14th, however we reserve the right to begin the interview process before the deadline. We thank all applicants for their interest; however, only those selected for an interview will be contacted.

Please forward cover letter and resume, with position title in the subject line to: info@nabi.ca
