About the Role

Post journal entries, reconcile GL, support month-end close

About TFG Financial

Regional equipment finance leader; CPA-friendly growth opportunities

Full Description

  • Job type – Permanent, full time
  • Location – Vancouver, BC – Hybrid (2 days/week in office)
  • Working hours – Standard business hours PST

Your Talent, Your Terms | Results, Recognition and Rewards What’s The Job The Junior Accountant supports day-to-day accounting operations, including account reconciliations, journal entries, payment processing, and month-end activities. This role helps ensure accurate financial records and timely reporting.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Prepare and reconcile general ledger accounts, ensuring supporting schedules and working papers are accurate, complete, and properly maintained.
  • Post journal entries, including accruals, depreciation, prepaid expenses, intercompany transactions, and other month-end adjustments, with appropriate supporting documentation.
  • Perform monthly bank reconciliations and investigate and resolve reconciling items on a timely basis.
  • Maintain and update account schedules, reconciliations, and financial reporting workbooks to support accurate reporting.
  • Support period-end close activities by completing assigned tasks within established deadlines.
  • Process daily demand runs and records related transactions in the accounting system accurately and timely.
  • Process semi-monthly Rental PAP transactions and ensure supporting entries are properly recorded.
  • Perform bank, intercompany, and other balance sheet account reconciliations, identifying and resolving discrepancies as needed.
  • Assist with audit requests and provide supporting documentation for internal and external reporting requirements.
  • Contribute to process improvements and help maintain effective internal controls and accounting procedures.
  • Provide backup and vacation coverage for other accounting team members.
  • Assist with ad hoc projects and other duties as assigned.

What You Bring

  • 1–2 years of accounting experience in a high-volume, transactional environment.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Commerce, or a related field.
  • Enrolled in CPA PEP or in progress with CPA PREP program.
  • Strong attention to detail with a high degree of accuracy and commitment to quality work.
  • Solid analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to investigate and resolve discrepancies independently.
  • Well-organized with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
  • Self-motivated and proactive, with a strong sense of ownership, accountability, and follow-through.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work effectively across departments.
  • Adaptable and willing to take on new responsibilities as business needs evolve.
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and other Microsoft Office applications.
  • Experience with ERP systems, particularly Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, is an asset.
  • Previous experience in the financial services industry is an asset.

Why Join TFG TFG’s culture is results, recognition and rewards based, with a focus on achieving results in a high performing team environment.

As part of the Travelers Financial Group of Companies, TFG Financial Corporation is one of the largest independent commercial equipment finance companies in Canada. We provide financing and leasing for a variety of revenue-producing assets such as trucks, trailers, light to heavy construction equipment, and manufacturing and material handling equipment. Our mission is to be our customer’s first choice by making equipment financing easy while providing a wide range of financing solutions.
