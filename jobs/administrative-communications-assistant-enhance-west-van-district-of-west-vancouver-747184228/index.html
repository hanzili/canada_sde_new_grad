ADMINISTRATIVE & COMMUNICATIONS ASSISTANT - ENHANCE WEST VAN

(Permanent* Part-Time)*** Located on the traditional, ancestral and unceded lands of the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh Nations, West Vancouver is a vibrant community on the North Shore and home to parks, beaches, and to sports, recreation and arts amenities. Our dedicated staff deliver services, operate facilities and run the programs that enhance the well-being of our community. We are seeking employees with a passion for public service who share our commitment to creating a complete, inclusive and livable community.

Responsibilities The West Vancouver Community Services Department, in partnership with Enhance West Van, has an opening for a permanent part-time Administrative and Communications Assistant. This role combines executive administrative support with digital marketing and communications, helping to support the day-to-day operations and public engagement initiatives of Enhance West Van. Reporting to the Executive Director of Enhance West Van, this position will be responsible for:

  • Providing general administrative support including the preparation of reports, presentations, statistics, correspondence, Board packages, agendas, and meeting minutes;
  • Assisting in the coordination of meetings and taking minutes;
  • Managing digital communications, social media, related analytics and online marketing;
  • Supporting the Executive Director in projects and event planning as required;
  • Organizing office functions including mail distribution, scheduling office supplies, filing and maintaining records;
  • Providing communications to internal and external stakeholders.

The preferred candidate will possess: excellent multi-tasking abilities; the ability to establish and maintain effective working relationships; the ability to exercise independent judgment, initiative, tact, and diplomacy; sound knowledge of software applications; and knowledge of non-profit organizations and its relationships with members and community residents, local government, and the private sector.

The successful candidate has completed courses in administration support, communications or marketing and possesses at least three years’ experience in an office environment, or a combination of education and comprehensive related experience. A BC Driver’s Licence valid for the Province of British Columbia is required.

The successful candidate will be required to supply an acceptable Police Information Check and a copy of their credentials.

Position Status: Exempt – Permanent Part-Time

Hours of Work: 21 hours per week. Hours and days of work may vary and must be available for day and evening shifts.

Rate of Pay: $39,800 - $44,800 per year (2025 rates)

Closing Date: 11:59 p.m on Sunday, July 12, 2026
