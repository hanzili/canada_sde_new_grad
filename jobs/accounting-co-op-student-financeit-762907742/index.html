Full Description

About the role:

Financeit is looking to add an Accounting Co-op Student to our Finance team! We're looking for a student who understands the basics of day-to-day accounting and is eager to build real, hands-on experience. Advanced Excel skills are essential, along with strong attention to detail and the ability to prioritize what matters.

The right person is curious, thrives in a fast-paced environment, and enjoys finding smarter, more efficient ways to tackle problems. If you love to learn and want to see how accounting works inside a consumer lending company, this is a great opportunity to grow with us.

Fall 2026 Co-op term : 4 month or 8 month co-op available (September start)

Working hours : Monday - Friday from 9:00 am - 5:00 pm

Working location : In office 2-3 days a week at 8 Spadina Ave, Suite 2400, Toronto, ON M5V 0S8

What you'll do:

  • Post daily journal entries
  • Reconcile bank accounts and loan portfolios
  • Support the preparation of lender reporting
  • Assist with the full-cycle accounts payable process
  • Help with month-end close, including accruals, financial reporting and ad hoc analysis
  • Maintain the capital assets ledger and continuity schedule
  • Assist with the annual audit
  • Take on additional tasks or special projects focused on efficiency and cost savings

Requirements

  • Currently enrolled in a 3rd, or 4th year university program
  • Strong Excel skills (VLOOKUP, pivot tables, SUMIF)
  • Working toward or planning to pursue a CPA designation
  • Solid understanding of GAAP and day-to-day accounting principles
  • Experience in audit, treasury, or compliance is an asset
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills
  • Takes initiative, is organized, accountable, and always looking for more efficient ways to get work done
  • General understanding of lending and credit
  • Experience in accounting within financial services or e-commerce is considered an asset

Benefits

Winner of Canada's Most Admired Corporate Culture in 2018 and 2021, we offer more than just the basics. Take advantage of:

  • An award-winning culture with a collaborative & inclusive team
  • Compensation is $22/hr.
  • Committed to offering hybrid workplace options
  • Virtual events such as Lunch & Learns, company parties, fun team activities and charity initiatives
  • Career learning and development programs
