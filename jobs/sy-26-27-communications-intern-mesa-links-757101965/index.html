About MESA Links

Growing NYC charter network focused on education equity

Full Description

MESA Links is a growing network of high-performing charter schools serving the diverse communities of Brooklyn. Our flagship campus, Math, Engineering, and Science Academy (MESA) Charter High School , opened its doors in Bushwick in August 2013 and now serves 500 students in grades 9–12. Since graduating our first class of seniors in 2017, MESA Bushwick has maintained a 94% cumulative graduation rate , reflecting our students' strong commitment to academic success.

Education Building on the success of our flagship school, MESA Links is expanding to meet the growing demand for high-quality education in Brooklyn and is projected to double in size over the next 5 years:

  • MESA South Brooklyn launched this year with its inaugural 9th grade class of 130 students, offering a rigorous college-preparatory program that mirrors the excellence of our flagship campus.
  • MESA East New York will launch its high school campus in SY 26-27, bringing the same high-quality experience to students in the East New York & Cypress Hill communities.
  • Our MESA Alumni Lab supports 125 young adults (ages 18–24) from across NYC, helping them re-engage with their education and career pathways through small-group workshops and interventions.

To support this expansion, we are seeking a part-time Communications Intern . You will be a critical partner to the Director of External Affairs & Communications as you support the full scope of the department's work: storytelling, enrollment communications, community engagement, donor and board-facing materials, and the day-to-day execution that keeps a growing network's brand consistent and visible. The ideal candidate is a strong writer and communicator who is organized, proactive, and passionate about educational equity. They are comfortable working across campuses and with diverse communities, including multilingual and immigrant families . They take initiative, produce clean work without heavy oversight, and understand that communications at a growing school network requires both strategic thinking and getting things done.
