Full Description

The Student Research Assistant - Acoustic Voice Analyst will support research activities in the Music Cognition Lab through the analysis of acoustic voice data (song and speech) using PRAAT software. This position involves processing and analyzing recorded voice data for experimental research projects and contributing to the organization and management of research datasets. The ideal candidate has experience with acoustic analysis, strong attention to detail, and a background in music theory.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Analyze acoustic voice data using PRAAT software
  • Process and organize research data for experimental studies
  • Assist with maintaining accurate records of analyses and datasets
  • Collaborate with research team members to ensure consistency and quality of data analysis
  • Contribute to research discussions and project-related tasks as required

QUALIFICATIONS:

  • Must be a current UPEI student
  • Experience using PRAAT for acoustic voice analysis
  • Background in music theory
  • Strong analytical and organizational skills
  • Ability to work independently and manage assigned tasks effectively
  • Experience in music cognition research, psycholinguistics, or related areas is an asset
  • Excellent attention to detail and accuracy
  • Strong problem-solving and analytical abilities
  • Effective communication and time-management skills
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality and research integrity
  • Self-motivated with a commitment to high-quality work

Priority will be given to University of Prince Edward Island students in accordance with Recruitment, Selection, Hiring and Employment Policies and the Procedures manual.
