About the Role

Gather requirements, model data, support Agile delivery

About Teckhorizon Inc

Tech-focused firm; healthcare interoperability focus

Full Description

The Business Analyst will support digital transformation and enterprise system implementation initiatives by gathering, analyzing, documenting, and validating business and data requirements. The role requires close collaboration with business stakeholders, product owners, and technical teams to ensure successful delivery of solutions aligned with business needs.

Responsibilities

  • Collaborate with business stakeholders, Product Owners, and technical teams to gather, analyze, and document business and data requirements.
  • Translate business requirements into clear user stories, use cases, and functional specifications.
  • Support Agile delivery teams by managing and refining backlog items using Azure DevOps.
  • Facilitate requirements-gathering activities including workshops, interviews, and stakeholder discussions.
  • Perform data analysis, source-to-target mapping, and data validation activities.
  • Work closely with development and technical teams to clarify, communicate, and validate requirements.
  • Support FHIR resource modeling and healthcare data transformation initiatives.
  • Create and maintain project documentation, process flows, business rules, and data mappings.
  • Assist with API testing and validation using Postman and JSON payloads.
  • Monitor project progress, identify issues, and provide regular updates to stakeholders and project teams.
  • Support business process improvement and digital transformation initiatives.

Must-Have Qualifications

  • Hands-on experience working with FHIR standards, including resource modeling and FHIR APIs.
  • Experience with Agile methodologies and working within Agile delivery teams.
  • Experience using Azure DevOps for user story creation, backlog management, and requirements tracking.
  • Strong experience translating business and data requirements into well-defined user stories and use cases.
  • Experience working closely with technical teams to communicate, clarify, and validate requirements.
  • Experience with Databricks and writing SQL queries.
  • Experience in data analysis, data mapping, and source-to-target mapping.
  • Experience transforming relational database source systems into FHIR-compliant structures.
  • Experience creating and working with JSON files.
  • Experience using Postman for API testing and validation.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills.

Nice-to-Have Qualifications

  • Knowledge of HL7/FHIR implementation guides and healthcare interoperability standards.
  • Experience supporting enterprise digital transformation or large-scale system implementation projects.
  • Experience with business process modeling, workflow analysis, and process improvement initiatives.
  • Familiarity with conceptual and logical data modeling techniques.
  • Knowledge of cloud-based solutions and system integrations within the Microsoft ecosystem.
  • Experience with Microsoft Power Platform.
  • Understanding of Systems Development Life Cycle (SDLC) methodologies.
  • Experience using CASE tools or other requirements management tools.
  • Knowledge of distributed systems and internet-based application design methodologies.
  • Understanding of project planning, estimation, scheduling, and delivery practices.
  • Knowledge of Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) standards and accessibility requirements.
  • Ability to serve as a proxy Product Owner when required and support Agile team decision-making.
