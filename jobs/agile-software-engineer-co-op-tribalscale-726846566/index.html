About the Role

Build full-stack features, collaborate, ship client apps

About TribalScale

Canada-based AI-driven software consultancy with client projects

Full Description

  • Build full-stack applications using modern technologies (frontend, backend, APIs)
  • Work on real client projects across a variety of industries
  • Collaborate with engineers through pair programming and AI-assisted development
  • Use AI tools (Copilot, Claude, etc.) to:
  • Generate and refine code
  • Debug and iterate faster
  • Explore alternative technical approaches
  • Design and build agentic workflows where AI systems:
  • Execute multi-step tasks
  • Interact with APIs and external tools
  • Support product functionality and internal workflows
  • Integrate LLM-powered features such as:
  • Intelligent assistants
  • Automated workflows
  • Context-aware user experiences
  • Contribute to the design of scalable, maintainable systems that combine traditional software engineering with AI-driven logic and orchestration
  • Participate in Agile ceremonies including standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
  • Work on real client products from your first day instead of internal training projects
  • Gain hands-on experience building software in an AI-native, agent-driven environment
  • Learn how modern engineering teams use AI to accelerate development and problem-solving
  • Work alongside experienced engineers, product leaders, and founders
  • Develop practical skills in AI agents, automation, and LLM-powered applications
  • Gain exposure to how leading companies are adopting AI and agentic systems in production
  • Join a high-performance, collaborative team with strong mentorship opportunities
  • Build skills that are shaping the future of software development
  • Ideal for students who enjoy side projects, hackathons, and learning new technologies quickly
  • Opportunity to accelerate technical growth faster than a traditional co-op placement
  • Learn how to design systems where AI can reason, act, and automate tasks, not just generate content
  • Work on meaningful projects that impact real users and businesses
  • Strong fit for candidates who are curious, proactive, and excited about the future of AI-powered engineering

Benefits

  • Health Insurance: Health, dental, vision, life insurance
  • Professional Development: Weekly Lunch & Learns, subsidies on courses and workshops to help you upskill
  • Conferences & Events: Constant opportunity to attend conferences and events, tickets on us
  • Socials: Friday socials, group hangouts, themed parties, quarterly events!
  • Flexible Work Schedule: Ability to fit work into your personal schedule
  • Parental Leave: Paid parental leave for moms and dads
  • Fitness Perk: Subsidized corporate membership of Class Pass offering an array of classes; Goodlife corporate gym membership which offers 30% discount as well as sign-up fees waived
  • Mental Health Perk: Subsidized online therapy sessions and resources with Inkblot
  • Long Weekend Perk: The option for extra long weekends to spend with your family & friends
  • Take your birthday off: Go ahead, celebrate!
  • Inward Strong Partnership: TribalScale has sponsored an Inward Strong platform membership for all our Tribe members
  • Vet Perks: 4 Virtual Vet Appointments annually for your Furry Friends- You seek more than a job; you seek a calling—a chance to make a lasting impact on the digital landscape
  • You thrive in challenging environments where innovation is key. Your passion for technology is matched only by your drive to redefine its limits
