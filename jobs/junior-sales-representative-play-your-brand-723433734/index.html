About the Role

About Play Your Brand

Global video production; training-focused sales internship

Full Description

Play Your Brand Canada is looking for motivated Junior Sales Representative Interns to join our growing sales team.

Play is a global video production company that connects brands, agencies, and marketing teams with vetted filmmakers around the world. We help companies create high-quality branded content, campaign videos, social content, interviews, and event coverage with reliable local production support.

This is a great opportunity for students or early-career professionals who want hands-on experience in sales, marketing, media, and creative production.

You’ll learn how to find potential clients, build prospect lists, support outreach campaigns, use HubSpot, and understand the B2B sales process from prospecting to closing.

What you’ll do

  • Research brands, agencies, marketing teams, and potential clients
  • Build and organize prospect lists
  • Support email and LinkedIn outreach campaigns
  • Track leads and follow-ups in HubSpot
  • Help prepare sales materials and client follow-up messages
  • Join sales training sessions and team check-ins
  • Learn how creative production services are pitched to brands and agencies

What we’re looking for

  • Interest in sales, marketing, advertising, media, or creative production
  • Strong written communication skills
  • Organized, proactive, and comfortable working remotely
  • Comfortable doing online research and outreach
  • Willingness to learn and receive feedback
  • Experience with HubSpot, LinkedIn, Excel, or Google Workspace is an asset, but not required
  • Previous sales experience is a plus, but not necessary

What you’ll gain

  • Hands-on B2B sales experience
  • Training in lead generation and outreach
  • Experience using HubSpot and CRM workflows
  • Exposure to the creative production and advertising industries
  • Mentorship from an international production company
  • Opportunity to grow with the company based on performance

Compensation

This is a commission-based internship/entry-level sales role. Selected candidates will receive training, mentorship, and support from the Play Your Brand team.
