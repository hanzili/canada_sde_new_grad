About Us:

Welcome to Lactalis Canada – where we take pride in being recognized as one of 2025 GTA's Top Employers! As well as Forbes Canada, Top Canadian Employers for 2025! Our journey revolves around enhancing the lives of Canadians through a rich variety of dairy delights, including some of Canadian’s favourite dairy brands such as like Black Diamond Cheese, Lactantia Milk, Astro Yogurt, Balderson Cheese, and Cheestrings. In fact, we’re proud to say our products grace the shelves of an incredible 94% of Canadian households!

With a 140-year legacy driven by innovation and unwavering quality, we're seeking like-minded individuals to join us in shaping our next chapter. Our flexible hybrid work model accommodates 40% remote work, adapting to the evolving needs of our workforce. Step into a collaborative hub with our revamped office, and enjoy free parking, endless coffee, delicious Lactalis snacks, an on-site gym, and more!

Lactalis Student Program:

At Lactalis, we are committed to cultivating talent and providing enriching opportunities that ignite passion and foster growth in our future leaders. Our student program is designed to nurture your potential by providing you with enriching work experience in a dynamic and innovative environment.

Lactalis Canada's student program encourages curiosity, creativity, professional development, and personal growth. Not only will you develop your expertise through hands-on experiences, but you will also join a thriving network of Lactalis Students across Canada. We are invested in your long-term success and aim to equip you with skills and experiences that will fuel your future!

About the opportunity:

With oversight of both internal and external communications, Lactalis Canada’s Corporate Communications team plays a key role in shaping the company’s voice and reputation. As a Corporate Communications Intern, you will gain hands-on experience in a wide range of areas including employee engagement, event management, multimedia content creation, social media, media relations, community relations and strategic storytelling. This internship offers the opportunity to work cross-functionally and build your portfolio with real-world communications projects while receiving mentorship from various members of the team.

The attributes we are looking for:

  • Content Development : Draft, edit, and proofread communications materials, including announcements, social media posts, press releases, and presentations.
  • Digital Library: Manage a new project including the organization, tagging and categorization of assets within a new Digital Asset Management System (DAMS) to improve searchability and user access.
  • Internal Communications: Assist with employee communications including drafting content and managing Lactalis Canada’s internal communications platforms (Viva Engage and digital screens).
  • Media & Public Relations Support: Monitor media coverage, compile media reports and briefing documents.
  • Event Support : Assist in planning and execution of corporate events, town halls, and employee engagement activities including the upcoming Leadership Exchange.
  • Digital & Social Media : Help monitor, track, and create content for corporate social media channels, ensuring alignment with brand voice and messaging.
  • Administrative Support: Provide day-to-day assistance to the Corporate Communications team on various projects and reporting needs.

What you will bring:

  • Currently enrolled in a program in Communications, Public Relations or a related field.
  • Strong oral, writing, editing, and storytelling skills.
  • Detail-oriented with excellent organizational abilities.
  • Knowledge of social media platforms and digital communications trends.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, PowerPoint, Excel), Canva and ability to quickly learn new tools (e.g. DAMS, Viva Engage)
  • Collaborative, creative, and proactive team player.
  • Professional, adaptable with ability to manage multiple priorities in order to meet deadlines and get the job done.
  • Ability to work independently and as an effective team player

What Lactalis will offer:

Lactalis Canada believes in rewarding its students with the following student package:

  • A competitive salary
  • 8% vacation pay
  • Health & dental benefits starting day 1 (including coverage for vision, mental health practitioners, physiotherapy, chiropractic care, massage therapy, naturopathy, and more!)
  • A paid volunteer day to give back to your community

  • Social events with other students
  • Opportunities to learn about different functions within our company
  • Potential for future full time permanent opportunities!
