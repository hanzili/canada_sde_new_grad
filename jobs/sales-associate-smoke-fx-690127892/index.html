About SMOKE FX

Small local vape shop focused on customer service

Full Description

Overview
Smoke FX is seeking a part-time sales associate to join our team at our Kanata location. The ideal candidate will have a passion for customer service and a strong ability to engage with customers to drive sales. As a Sales Associate, you will play a key role in providing exceptional service, promoting products, and ensuring a positive shopping experience for all customers. This position requires excellent communication skills and the ability to work in a fast-paced retail environment.

Responsibilities

  • Engage with customers to understand their needs and provide tailored product recommendations.
  • Maintain knowledge of current promotions, policies regarding payment and exchanges, and security practices.
  • Assist in inventory management by restocking shelves, organizing merchandise, and conducting stock checks.
  • Collaborate with team members to achieve sales goals and maintain a positive work environment.

Requirements

  • Previous experience in sales or customer service is preferred.
  • Familiarity with vaping products is preferred.
  • Must be able to work flexible hours, including evenings and weekends as needed

Pay: $17.60 per hour

Work Location: In person
