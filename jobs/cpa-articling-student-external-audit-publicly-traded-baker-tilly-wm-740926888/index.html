We are looking for a CPA Articling Student – External Audit, Publicly Traded Companies to join our team in September 2026 —a role where your expertise will drive meaningful outcomes and your ambition will help shape what comes next. Here, you’ll collaborate with a dynamic team of professionals, united by a shared commitment to making a difference—for our clients, our communities, and one another.

Your role:

  • Assist in executing audit engagements for public and private companies under IFRS, including performing assigned audit procedures and supporting fieldwork activities;
  • Communicate progress and findings to senior team members, assisting managers and partners in the execution of audit engagements;
  • Build and maintain effective working relationships with clients and team members;
  • Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment;
  • Deliver quality work while developing technical accounting knowledge under IFRS;
  • Support senior team members and contribute to a collaborative team environment;
  • Other duties as assigned.

What you bring to the table:

  • Academic achievement – completed or near completion of an undergraduate degree and CPA pre-requisite courses
  • Plans to pursue a CPA designation
  • Strong client service skills
  • Excellent interpersonal relations and demonstrated ability to work with others effectively in teams
  • Superior verbal and written communication skills
  • Strong problem solving and analytical skills

What’s in it for you? 

  • Career growth opportunities
  • Comprehensive training to cover all aspects of your development
  • On-the-job learning
  • A manager mentor and a senior buddy to support you throughout your time with us
  • A top notch CFE prep program with great results year after year
  • Competitive total compensation package including extended health care, dental, and group life insurance benefits
  • Vacation (3+ weeks/year)
  • Health and Wellness Spending Account
  • Continuing Professional Development courses are provided to maintain your designation as well as numerous informal training and development opportunities. The Firm will also reimburse you for approved professional development fees and membership dues.
  • Reimbursement for CPA PEP module fees and expenses
  • Secondment opportunities with Baker Tilly International to Australia and New Zealand
  • An open-door culture promoting interaction with all staff levels, including partners
  • Coffee, tea, and drinks throughout the year
  • Overtime meal and parking reimbursements
  • A smart casual dress code (client/day specific)
  • Client referral & employee referral bonuses
  • Many entertaining social events throughout the year
