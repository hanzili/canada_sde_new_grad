About the Role

Create social content, update listings, support campaigns

About Tundra Designs

Small Vancouver handmade goods brand, seasonal markets

Full Description

Tundra Designs is a Vancouver-based small business that designs and creates handcrafted wood products, including souvenirs, home décor, and custom gifts. We sell online and through seasonal events, including the Vancouver Christmas Market.

This role is ideal for a student looking for meaningful work experience while maintaining a full course load. Hours are flexible and can be scheduled around classes, exams, and extracurricular commitments.

Location: Remote initially (now until mid-November), with in-person work at the Vancouver Christmas Market (mid-November - end of December)

Hours: Approximately 5 hours per week initially, with increased hours (16 - 20 hours, more if desired) starting mid-November through end of December.

What You'll Do

  • Create and schedule social media content
  • Update website content and product listings
  • Write product descriptions and marketing copy
  • Assist with email marketing campaigns
  • Conduct market and competitor research
  • Support seasonal promotions and marketing initiatives
  • Represent Tundra Designs at the Vancouver Christmas Market
  • Engage with customers and provide exceptional customer service
  • Help customers select products and gift ideas
  • Process sales transactions and contribute to sales goals
  • Assist with special projects as needed

What We're Looking For

  • Current student in Business, Marketing, Communications, Commerce, or a related field
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Friendly, confident, and comfortable engaging with customers
  • Strong customer service and sales aptitude
  • Self-motivated and able to work independently
  • Organized and detail-oriented
  • Eligible to work in Canada and meet the eligibility requirements of available student employment funding programs (Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or protected person)
  • Successful candidates MUST be available to work in person at the Vancouver Christmas Market between mid-November and December 23 (end date can be adjusted if you need to go home early for the holidays).

Bonus:

  • Previous retail, hospitality, customer service, or sales experience
  • Experience with Canva, Shopify, social media, photography, or graphic design

What we offer:

  • Flexible schedule - you choose when you work
  • Remote work until mid-November
  • Increased hours during the holiday season
  • Direct mentorship from the business owner
  • Real responsibility and meaningful business impact
  • Experience at one of Vancouver's largest seasonal events (staff pass + 3 free tickets + discounts)

The ideal candidate enjoys both creative marketing work and interacting with customers face-to-face. Strong sales and customer service skills are essential, particularly during the Vancouver Christmas Market, where customer engagement and sales will be a major focus of the role.

How to apply:

  • Send your resume (no cover letter required) to info@tundra-designs.com.
  • Optional: Instead of a cover letter, you're welcome to send a short video (30–60 seconds) introducing yourself. Since this role involves customer-facing sales at the Vancouver Christmas Market, we'd love to get a sense of your personality and communication style.
  • The video can be informal and about anything you'd like. It's completely optional - we just find it more personal than a cover letter and much harder for ChatGPT to tell us what makes you interesting.

Pay: $20.00-$23.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Casual dress
  • Flexible schedule
  • Work from home

Work Location: Hybrid remote in Vancouver, BC
