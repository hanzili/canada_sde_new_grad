Full Description

Brand: True Wireless Canada
Location: 6500 Roblin Blvd, Winnipeg, MB

Join True Wireless Canada, an Authorized Dealer of Rogers. We are proud to be recognized as 2023 Best Place to Work by HR Director Magazine.

Have you ever wanted a role where you can use your web development skills to build real tools for a growing business? Look no more, you’ve found it at TRUE WIRELESS CA!

As a Junior Full Stack Web Developer, you will help us build, improve, and maintain our company websites, internal tools, dashboards, inventory systems, automation workflows, and online platforms. We are looking for someone hands-on who understands web coding, can solve problems, and can make things work in a real business environment.

This role is ideal for someone who is practical, tech-savvy, and excited to work with real business needs across telecom, retail, repairs, accessories, certified pre-owned devices, and wholesale operations.

What’s in it for you?

Generous discounts — 50% off your phone bill and 10% off cost price for accessories
Career growth opportunities — grow your technical, business, and leadership skills
Hands-on experience building systems used daily by our stores and team
Opportunity to work on real business projects, not just theory
Fast-moving environment where good ideas are welcomed

Your typical day:

Build, update, and maintain company websites and landing pages
Create internal tools for inventory, sales, wholesale, reporting, and daily operations
Work with APIs, forms, databases, dashboards, and web integrations
Fix bugs, troubleshoot issues, and improve existing systems
Help automate repetitive business tasks
Improve user experience, website speed, and overall system performance
Work closely with management to turn business ideas into working tools
Use your problem-solving skills to find practical solutions on the fly

Who you are:

You understand web coding and enjoy making things work
Self-motivated with an ability to problem solve independently
A good communicator with strong attention to detail
You are an enthusiastic learner with a desire for continual growth
You are comfortable working in a fast-paced business environment
You can take an idea and help turn it into a working website, tool, or system
You are practical, reliable, and willing to learn new technologies

Skills we are looking for:

Good knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
Experience with React, Next.js, Node.js, or similar tools
Basic understanding of databases
Comfortable using GitHub or similar version control tools
Ability to work with APIs and web integrations
Basic understanding of website hosting, deployment, and troubleshooting

Bonus skills:

WordPress or Shopify experience
CRM, POS, or inventory system knowledge
Automation tools
Basic UI/UX design understanding
AI tools and prompt-based development
Telecom, retail, phone repair, or e-commerce experience

TRUE WIRELESS CA is an equal-opportunity employer and we are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive environment. At TRUE WIRELESS CA, we celebrate differences, and we invite you to be a part of our team.

Then join us now!
