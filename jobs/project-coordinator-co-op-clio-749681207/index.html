About the Role

Coordinate cross-functional projects, track progress, draft reports

About Clio

Canadian legal tech leader with strong PMO culture

Full Description

Clio is the global leader in legal AI technology, empowering legal professionals and law firms of every size to work smarter, faster, and more securely.

We are transforming the legal experience for all by bettering the lives of legal professionals while increasing access to justice.

Summary: We are currently seeking a new Project Coordinator, Co-op to join our Project Management Office team in Burnaby.

Applicants should be available for an 8-month co-op period from September 2026 - May 2027. We will be accepting applications from January to March.

*What your team does:* The Project Management Office (PMO) team leads high impact, cross-functional initiatives aimed at growing and transforming the business. We are involved in all aspects of a project life cycle including supporting the strategic vision, managing execution, leading change management strategies, reporting strategies, and long-term success. In collaboration with the other strategic teams (Strategic Planning, Customer Insights, and Data Insights) we are the gears that reliably keep projects running on time and keep the business growing.

*What you’ll work on:*

  • Work on the Project Management Office team and gain access and visibility into Clio’s largest projects and long term business strategy.
  • Partner with functional leaders to understand project targets and help achieve business goals.

  • Partner with Project Managers on major cross-functional projects and lead smaller initiatives independently. This work could include:

  • Meeting with project teams to check on progress and status of project work - ensuring individuals and teams complete project tasks on time

  • Tracking a variety of moving projects pieces; such as action items, meeting minutes, decisions, timelines, and risks
  • Drafting and maintaining project documents and spreadsheets
  • Maintaining project tracking tools like Asana
  • Preparing regular project status reports
  • Help develop project management best practices at Clio.
  • Contribute to project playbooks and develop project templates
  • Maintain the Business Operations and project management wiki
  • Additional administrative support as required

*What you have:*

  • A learning mindset and an enthusiasm for trying new things
  • Ability to work collaboratively and lead a large cross-functional project team
  • Strategic mindset (Critical thinking, ability to see the big picture, ability to ask strategic questions, proactive problem solver)
  • Excellent writing and clear communication skills
  • Experienced with gantt charts, spreadsheet management, or project management tools (Excel, Google Sheets, Asana)
  • Ability to facilitate meetings while taking highly detailed meeting notes

*Serious bonus points if you:*

  • Have good stakeholder relationship management skills
  • Have a ingenuity and the ability to figure things out independently
  • Have a keen eye for details and the ability to spot gaps and risks
  • Have a high level of understanding and empathy
  • Have a data mindset (general data literacy and the ability to interpret dashboards)

This role is a backfill for an existing position.

What you will find here: Compensation is one of the main components of Clio’s Total Rewards Program. We have developed a series of programs and processes to ensure we are creating fair and competitive pay practices that form the foundation of our human and high-performing culture.

Some highlights of our Total Rewards program include:

  • Hybrid work environment, with expectation for local Clions (Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, Dublin and Sydney) to be in office minimum twice per week.
  • 4% vacation pay for hours worked.

The expected new hire base pay for this role is $46,000 CAD.
