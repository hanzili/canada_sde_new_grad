Full Description

Join the ride! Position : Sales Associate

Dealership : Grande Prairie Nissan

Location : Grande Prairie, AB

Classification : Full-Time, Immediate Hire

Earning Potential: $60,000.00 – $160,000.00+ Annually

Ready to shift your career into high gear? Grande Prairie Nissan , a proud part of the AutoCanada dealership network, is currently looking for a driven, high-performing Sales Professional who is motivated by results and excited to grow their earning potential. If you thrive in a customer-focused environment, enjoy building relationships, and bring a proactive, goal-oriented mindset, this opportunity offers the platform to succeed. You’ll have access to strong inventory, ongoing training, and a steady flow of customer traffic — giving you the support and resources needed to build a rewarding and financially successful career.

AutoCanada is the largest Canadian owned and publicly traded automotive dealer group operating in Canada and theUS. We are innovative, data-driven and our commitment to our Team Members, customers and operational excellence fuels our success.

Why High Performers Succeed Here

  • Uncapped Earning Potential: A competitive commission structure (up to 45%), plus tiered volume bonuses, rewards performance and consistently high producers.
  • Career Growth: As part of AutoCanada—the largest publicly traded automotive dealership group in Canada and the U.S.—you’ll have access to clear advancement paths across a national network.
  • Strong Benefits & Perks: A comprehensive health benefits package, employee vehicle purchase and service programs, paid vacation and flex time, and company-wide appreciation events designed to recognize and support your contribution.

Your Role On The Showroom Floor As an Automotive Sales Professional at Grande Prairie Nissan, you will represent the dealership as a trusted advisor, helping customers navigate one of their most important purchases with confidence. You’ll focus on delivering a positive, informative, and relationship-driven experience from first contact through to delivery.

In Your Day-to-day, You Will

  • Engage with customers in person, online, and over the phone to understand their needs and match them with the right new or pre-owned vehicle
  • Conduct engaging vehicle demonstrations, test drives, and walkthroughs that highlight features and benefits clearly and confidently
  • Work collaboratively to structure fair, mutually beneficial deals that align customer expectations with dealership objectives
  • Actively grow and manage a strong sales pipeline using CRM tools, with a focus on repeat business, referrals, and long-term relationships

What You Bring To The Table (The Must-Haves)

  • Experience: 3+ years in a customer-facing role (retail, sales, marketing, or hospitality). Automotive sales experience is an asset, but a consistent track record of sales performance and customer success is what matters most.
  • Drive: A competitive, self-motivated mindset with the resilience and focus to consistently achieve and exceed monthly sales targets.
  • Communication: Strong listening skills and clear, confident communication that builds trust and rapport quickly with customers.
  • Flexibility: Availability to work a variety of shifts, including days, evenings, and weekends, to accommodate customer needs.
  • The Essentials: Must be 18+ with a valid CA-issued Driver’s License, a clean and acceptable Driver’s Abstract, and the ability to obtain a valid AMVIC license.

The Perks

  • Competitive Commission up to 45%, with tiered volume bonuses for top performers
  • Health Benefits Package
  • Employee Vehicle Purchase & Service Plans
  • Employee and Family Assistance Programs
  • Paid holiday and flex time for full time AutoCanada employees
  • Company-wide appreciation events and contests throughout the calendar year
  • Professional development and the opportunity to grow your career

And those are just the basics.
