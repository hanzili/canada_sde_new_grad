About The Wawanesa Mutual Insurance Company

Founded in 1896, The Wawanesa Mutual Insurance Company is one of Canada’s largest mutual insurers, fully owned by its more than 1.8 million members. Headquartered in Winnipeg, Wawanesa is the parent company of WSI™, a specialty insurer delivering tailored solutions built around unique needs; Wawanesa Life, a provider of life insurance products; and Western Financial Group, a leading national distributor of personal and commercial insurance.

Wawanesa is committed to helping communities thrive in a changing world. The company invests more than $4 million annually in charitable initiatives, including $2.5 million each year to support people and communities on the front lines of climate change. Learn more at wawanesa.com.

We are currently seeking dedicated, driven, and enthusiastic individuals who thrive in a dynamic environment, embrace change, and are eager to gain diverse experience while advancing their careers as part of a growing team.

Job Overview

The Enterprise Architecture team helps shape how Wawanesa uses technology. We're looking for an AI Developer co-op with hands-on agentic AI experience to help us build internal tools and try out new AI technologies. You'll work closely with our architects on real projects.

This is a student term position, with a start date of January 11, 2027 and end date of April 23, 2027. The deadline to apply for this role is Friday, October 16, 2026.

Job Responsibilities

  • Help build internal tools and AI agents for the architecture team.
  • Build proof-of-concepts, which are small projects to test whether a new AI tool or framework works for us.
  • Help build our Technology Radar, a tool that tracks the technologies we care about and shows which ones we use, which ones we're testing and which ones we're watching.
  • Write simple documentation and share what you find with the team.

Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering or a related program.
  • Hands-on experience building with LLMs or AI agents, through coursework, personal projects or hackathons.
  • Familiarity with at least one agent framework or approach, such as LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, Semantic Kernel or MCP (Model Context Protocol).
  • Programming experience in Python.
  • Basic understanding of prompting, tool calling and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
  • Curious, willing to learn and comfortable asking questions.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.

Assets

  • Experience building a simple web app or user interface.
  • Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS or Azure), or AI services like Amazon Bedrock or Azure AI Foundry.
  • Experience with Git or GitHub.
  • A portfolio or GitHub project that shows something you've built with AI.
