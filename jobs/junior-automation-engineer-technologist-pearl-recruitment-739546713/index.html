Full Description

Job Title: Junior Automation Engineer / Technologist

Job Type: Full-time

Workplace Type: Hybrid

Experience Level: Entry level

Location: Calgary, AB

About the Company

Our client is a full-service EPCM consulting and project delivery firm supporting projects across the oil and gas and industrial sectors. The organization is known for practical project execution, lean team structures, technical depth, and strong client outcomes.

The firm delivers multidisciplinary engineering solutions for projects ranging from approximately $500,000 to $50 million.

About the Role

Our client is seeking a Junior Automation Engineer / Technologist to support the design, programming, configuration, testing, and commissioning of industrial control systems.

This role will work closely with senior automation personnel, project managers, engineers, vendors, contractors, and field teams to support automation projects from initial design through commissioning and start-up.

The successful candidate will assist with control-system documentation, PLC/SCADA/DCS/HMI configuration, equipment selection, testing, field implementation, and project closeout activities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist with the preparation of functional design requirements, design specifications, control narratives, shutdown keys, I/O lists, system architecture drawings, and network diagrams.
  • Support the development and maintenance of automation engineering drawings and technical documentation.
  • Participate in P&ID reviews, HAZOP/PHA sessions, and other multidisciplinary design reviews.
  • Assist with material take-offs, bills of materials, equipment specifications, and procurement documentation.
  • Review vendor drawings, equipment information, and technical submissions under the guidance of senior personnel.
  • Support the programming, configuration, and testing of PLC, SCADA, DCS, and HMI systems.
  • Assist with the configuration and testing of industrial networks and communication equipment.
  • Participate in internal testing, FAT, SWAT, SAT, commissioning, start-up, and field troubleshooting activities.
  • Coordinate assigned automation tasks with instrumentation, electrical, mechanical, process, and other engineering disciplines.
  • Maintain organized project records and support the preparation of testing and commissioning documentation.
  • Ensure assigned deliverables comply with project requirements, company standards, and applicable safety and regulatory requirements.

Core Competencies

  • Up to 3 years of experience in industrial automation, controls, instrumentation, engineering design, or industrial project delivery.
  • Exposure to PLC, SCADA, DCS, or HMI programming and configuration through employment, co-op placements, internships, or academic projects.
  • Familiarity with Allen-Bradley/Rockwell, Schneider Electric, Siemens, Emerson, Honeywell, ABB, Yokogawa, or similar automation platforms is considered an asset.
  • Basic understanding of P&IDs, industrial instrumentation, control-system architecture, I/O, and communication networks.
  • Familiarity with oil and gas, industrial, utility, or EPCM project environments is considered an asset.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office and automation or engineering software relevant to the role.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong analytical, organizational, problem-solving, and time-management skills.
  • Ability to follow technical direction, manage assigned priorities, and work effectively within a multidisciplinary project team.
  • Willingness to participate in field, testing, commissioning, and start-up activities as required.

Education and Experience

  • Engineering degree or Technology diploma in automation, electrical, instrumentation, controls, or a related field from a recognized institution.
  • Eligibility for registration with APEGA or certification with ASET is considered an asset.
  • Previous co-op, internship, field, industrial, or EPCM experience is considered an asset.
