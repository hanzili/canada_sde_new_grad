Full Description

Job Description We have a student opportunity!

If you are looking for hands-on, real-world experience in a supportive, diverse, and fast-paced environment, JDI IT is the challenge for you.  A work term here as a Business Analyst will provide you with an experience that will stretch your abilities, build your curiosity, and grow your resume in a way that is unique in the Maritimes.  Focusing on the goal of adding value through information technology, you will learn how to ask the right questions, build professional relationships, and drive solutions. You will be exposed to technical and business processes across various functional groups throughout different lines of businesses.

All successful applicants must meet requirements for Canadian Controlled Goods Program (CGP), Canadian Government Security clearance, and U.S. International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR). Responsibilities

  • Get experience in translating business problems and opportunities into technical solutions through working in a team environment acting as the liaison between the business and development
  • Assist in gathering business requirements for technical solutions
  • Support communications and reporting to internal teams and various business areas
  • Exposure to various business areas and their respective responsibilities, processes, etc
  • Job shadowing opportunities and establishment of mentor relationships
  • Successful application of business analyst tools, such as business process mapping, project charters, requirement gathering, etc
  • Training and experience in multiple industry technologies, including but not limited to: Tableau, Sharepoint, Clarity, and Azure DevOps

Qualifications

  • Current enrollment in an accredited university co-op program, business or computer science preferred
  • Proficiency with the Microsoft Suite of tools (i.e., Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Visio, etc.)
  • Desire to problem solve and work with others to map out processes to pinpoint improvements
  • Proficiency in written and verbal English communication
  • Curiosity, drive for excellence and deep interest in technology

About The Team The belief that what people experience matters more than technology is part of our foundational core. We also believe that our ability as a team grows exponentially when we share success. These centering values – some of our non-negotiables - guide our decisions and inform how we show up daily. Join a team that aims to simplify the path forward and inspire commitment.

With over 380 employees centralized in Saint John, Fredericton, Dieppe and Halifax, we offer the opportunity to create real value by helping people and businesses work smarter.  JDI IT supports the entire J.D. Irving Limited group of companies which means we offer opportunities and experiences across a wide breadth of IT areas, businesses and industries. As a member of the JDI IT team, you will be a key player in enabling the business to win by overcoming obstacles and “getting it done”!

We believe in the power of diverse teams and invest in continuous professional growth. We are seeking naturally curious change makers looking to join a collaborative and motivated team. Come help JDI IT Raise the Bar!
