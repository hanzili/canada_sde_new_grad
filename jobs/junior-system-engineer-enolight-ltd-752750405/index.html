About Enolight Ltd

Full Description

Overview

EnoPower is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Junior System Engineer to join our growing Research & Development team. This is an excellent opportunity for a recent engineering graduate who is passionate about hardware, software, and emerging energy technologies.

In this multidisciplinary role, you will work closely with our Hardware R&D, Software Development, and Product Strategy teams to support the development of next-generation energy storage and power management systems. Your responsibilities will include component research, hardware assembly, laboratory testing, technical documentation, and maintaining alignment between engineering activities and the company’s product roadmap.

A key focus of this role is producing high-quality engineering documentation that supports Canada’s Scientific Research and Experimental Development (SR&ED) program while helping ensure our hardware, software, and business strategy remain synchronized throughout product development.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist with hardware assembly, prototype setup, and laboratory testing.
  • Research and evaluate hybrid inverters, batteries, BMS, communication devices, sensors, and other electrical components.
  • Participate in testing hybrid inverters, battery systems, Modbus communication, failover performance, and system integration.
  • Record, organize, and analyze laboratory testing results.
  • Maintain detailed engineering documentation, technical journals, test reports, and meeting notes to support SR&ED claims.
  • Build and maintain an organized internal engineering knowledge base.
  • Coordinate communication between the Hardware R&D, Software Development, and Product Strategy teams.
  • Help ensure engineering activities remain aligned with the company’s product roadmap and business plan.
  • Identify documentation gaps, technical inconsistencies, and opportunities for process improvement.
  • Assist with internal technical quality control to ensure engineering work remains consistent with the intended system architecture.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Systems Design Engineering, or a closely related discipline.
  • Candidates with an advanced diploma in Engineering Technology and strong technical aptitude are also encouraged to apply.
  • Basic understanding of power electronics, AC/DC electrical systems, and battery technologies.
  • Familiarity with Linux, Python, and embedded systems.
  • Familiarity with communication protocols such as Modbus RTU/TCP, CAN Bus, or REST APIs is an asset.
  • Excellent technical writing and documentation skills.
  • Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to work effectively across multidisciplinary teams.
  • Able to work full-time from our Markham office.

What We Offer

  • A collaborative and innovative engineering environment.
  • Direct mentorship from experienced systems engineers and software developers.
  • Hands-on experience with commercial battery energy storage systems, hybrid inverters, IoT, and embedded technologies.
  • Opportunities to contribute to real commercial products from concept through deployment.
  • Competitive compensation based on qualifications and experience.
  • Health and dental insurance.
  • Initial 6-month full-time contract with strong potential for extension or permanent employment.

Professional Development

This position is designed as a career development opportunity for a recent engineering graduate interested in multidisciplinary product development. High-performing candidates will have the opportunity to grow into Systems Engineering, Technical Product Management, or R&D leadership roles.

EnoPower actively supports professional growth through mentorship, hands-on engineering experience, and exposure to hardware development, software integration, system architecture, and commercial product development.
