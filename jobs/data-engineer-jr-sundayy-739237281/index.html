About Sundayy

Citylitics, Toronto-based AI-driven infra data company

Full Description

About The Company Citylitics Inc. is a leading provider of predictive intelligence solutions focused on infrastructure investments across North America. The company specializes in transforming vast amounts of data from millions of documents related to cities, utilities, and public agencies into actionable market signals and high-value sales intelligence. Citylitics serves a diverse client base including infrastructure vendors, engineering firms, and construction companies, enabling them to identify and pre-position for major infrastructure opportunities at an earlier stage. The platform leverages advanced AI agents, proprietary signals graphs, and human-in-the-loop workflows to deliver nuanced, reliable insights seamlessly integrated with existing sales and data management systems. With a mission to improve infrastructure investment processes, Citylitics aims to ensure critical needs are addressed proactively, resulting in stronger, more resilient communities. The company fosters a results-driven, collaborative environment where innovation, teamwork, and continuous improvement are prioritized, making it a dynamic place to grow and contribute to meaningful industry advancements.

About The Role We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Junior Data Engineer to join our expanding team. In this role, you will be instrumental in designing, building, and maintaining mission-critical data pipelines that support our customer-facing solutions. Your work will directly impact how we deliver real-time market signals and insights to our clients, enabling them to make informed decisions on infrastructure investments. The ideal candidate will have a solid understanding of data engineering best practices, including data modeling, orchestration, and data warehousing. You will work closely with cross-functional teams such as Data, Product, and Customer Success to develop bespoke solutions, optimize data workflows, and ensure the robustness and scalability of our data infrastructure. This hybrid position is based out of our Toronto office, requiring a minimum of three days in-office per week. If you are passionate about data, eager to learn, and excited to work on impactful projects within a cutting-edge AI-driven environment, we encourage you to apply.

Qualifications

  • Proficiency in Python and SQL
  • Minimum of 2+ years experience building and maintaining data pipelines using Airflow or Cloud Composer
  • Strong understanding of data warehousing principles and experience with BigQuery
  • Knowledge of ETL/ELT processes, data modeling, and building reliable, idempotent pipelines
  • Experience with Git and cloud platforms, preferably Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
  • Experience with building and deploying interactive dashboards or data applications (e.g., Dash on Cloud Run) is a plus
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and proactive issue resolution abilities
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills to work effectively with analysts and stakeholders
  • Enthusiasm for leveraging AI tools like Claude to enhance productivity

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and deploy high-performance, reliable data pipelines using Airflow and BigQuery
  • Collaborate with data analysts and stakeholders to define data requirements and create efficient data models
  • Optimize query performance and pipeline efficiency for maximum scalability and responsiveness
  • Serve as the dedicated data engineering resource for custom solutions, translating client requirements into technical implementations
  • Participate in testing, deployment, and ongoing monitoring of data workflows
  • Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives by suggesting new technologies, tools, and best practices
  • Leverage AI tools to accelerate development, debugging, and documentation processes
  • Assist in maintaining and enhancing existing data infrastructure and workflows
  • Perform other related duties as assigned to support team objectives

Benefits

  • Opportunity to make a tangible impact on infrastructure development and societal well-being
  • Work within a disruptive, data-driven platform at the forefront of AI and infrastructure markets
  • Fast-paced environment with minimal bureaucracy, encouraging rapid learning and growth
  • Access to advanced AI tools and a comprehensive data universe to boost productivity
  • Deep investment in professional development through coaching, mentorship, and skill-building initiatives
  • Internal promotion pathways to support career advancement
  • Collaborative team culture that values support, celebration of wins, and shared success
