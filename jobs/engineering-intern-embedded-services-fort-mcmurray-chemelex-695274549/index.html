About the Role

Design and analyze EHT systems; produce docs

About Chemelex

Global engineering firm delivering electrical safety solutions

Full Description

Chemelex is a global leader in electric thermal and sensing solutions, protecting the world’s critical processes, places and people. With over 50 years of innovation and a commitment to excellence, we develop solutions that ensure safety, reliability, and efficiency in diverse environments – from industrial plants and data centers to people’s homes. We deliver future-ready technologies, advanced engineering capabilities and local expertise backed by global standards. Our offering includes a leading portfolio from our trusted brands: Raychem, Tracer, Nuheat and Pyrotenax.

We’re looking for people who put their innovation to work to advance our success – and their own. Join an organization that ensures a more secure world through connecting and protecting our customers with inventive electrical solutions.

What You Will Experience In This Position

  • Provides engineering design and analysis of EHT (Electrical Heat Trace) Systems within assigned project scope of work. This includes conceptual studies, layouts, specification development, and engineering drawings.
  • Build Construction Work Packages (CWP’s) and Quality Work Packages, complete with all applicable content such as Bill Of Materials (BOM’s), Test sheets, Engineered Drawings.
  • Analyze project progress and, work with project manager to adjust scope, timelines and cost
  • Prepare requisitions as the need arises for the project teams
  • Preparing material documentation for customers including EHT controllers, data specifications, and any necessary customer documents as per project specifications
  • Analyze and communicate with the supply and procurement team priorities, expediting and shipping within the project
  • Expedite any materials within supply chain and develop schedules
  • Ensure documentation is maintained through the project duration
  • Change order support including development, quantity tracking and collection, and document preparation.

You Have

  • Obtaining a degree in Electrical or Mechanical Engineering or related Engineering degree; from an accredited post-secondary institution
  • Knowledge of the engineering, maintenance and/or operations experience at a petrochemical facility or industrial site. Previous EHT experience is a plus
  • Ability to adapt to multiple projects and priorities
  • Strong interpersonal skills
  • Ability to prioritize priorities based on deadlines
  • Good Microsoft Office, other Database experience can be a plus
  • Willingness to learn how to retrieve and work with data
  • Possess solid time management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills

At Chemelex We protect places of progress through electric excellence.

Pioneering plants, sprawling data centers, busy transport hubs, and your own home. These are the places that bring progress to the world, and the places that we protect with time-tested technologies, advanced engineering, and a suite of services. Because here, excellence is everything.

Our Dimensions of Excellence reflect the core values of Chemelex. Together, they inspire our decisions and guide our actions – defining excellence in our work for colleagues, customers and communities.
