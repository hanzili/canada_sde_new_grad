About the Role

Plan and execute campaigns, create content, analyze results

About Calian Group

Canadian tech services leader with diverse industries

Full Description

Position Overview We are looking for an enthusiastic Marketing Assistant Co-op student to join the Calian marketing team for a 4- or 8-month term. In this role, you will support the planning, development, and execution of marketing content and campaigns across multiple divisions while contributing to process improvement initiatives using AI-enabled tools and marketing technologies. You will gain exposure to a variety of marketing functions and work closely with a collaborative team in a fast-paced environment.

Responsibilities

  • Support the planning and coordination of sponsorship events, webinars, and virtual events.
  • Assist with the development and execution of demand generation campaigns.
  • Collaborate with the marketing team to create and manage content calendars.
  • Help define, track, and report on campaign KPIs and outcomes.
  • Assist in creating and editing written, video, and photo content while ensuring alignment with brand standards and editorial guidelines.
  • Use AI-assisted tools to support content creation, research, ideation, summarization, and workflow improvements.
  • Review and refine AI-generated content for quality, accuracy, consistency, and brand alignment.
  • Support website content updates and page creation within WordPress CMS.
  • Assist with campaign and email activities using marketing tools, including HubSpot.
  • Support the organization and maintenance of marketing assets, templates, and campaign materials.
  • Identify opportunities to improve efficiency through AI-assisted workflows, templates, and automation.
  • Monitor campaign performance, analyze results, and contribute recommendations for optimization.
  • Support a variety of marketing initiatives, including event preparation, merchandise research, webinars, and other team projects as required.

Qualifications

  • Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Advertising, Business, Communications, or a related field.
  • Strong interest in digital marketing, marketing technology, and emerging AI tools.
  • Excellent project management and organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Strong copywriting, editing, research, and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience with Microsoft Office, including Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
  • Familiarity with AI tools such as Copilot, ChatGPT, or similar platforms.
  • Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment and adapt to changing priorities.
  • Experience with HubSpot, CMS platforms, or marketing automation tools is considered an asset.
  • Proficiency with Canva or similar design tools is considered an asset.
  • Bilingualism in English and French is considered an asset.

Additional Requirements

  • Demonstrated curiosity and willingness to explore new ideas and technologies.
  • Interest in identifying process improvements and contributing fresh perspectives.
  • Commitment to responsible AI use, including privacy, confidentiality, accuracy, and human review.

Compensation $22.50/hour
