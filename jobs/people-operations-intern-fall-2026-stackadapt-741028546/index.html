We are looking for a People Operations Intern to provide administrative support across our Talent and People Operations team. Reporting to the Manager, Global Mobility , you will play a key role in keeping our team organized, our data accurate, and our hiring process running smoothly. This is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys the details—you’ll balance core coordination tasks with projects aimed at making our internal processes more efficient and user-friendly.

StackAdapt is a remote-first company. We are open to candidates located anywhere in Canada for this position and encourage those from all backgrounds to apply.

What you’ll be doing:

  • Provide broad administrative support across the employee lifecycle, including interview scheduling, managing talent workflows in the ATS (Greenhouse), onboarding in the HRIS, submitting tickets and other administrative support.
  • Monitor the People Operations, People Technology and Global Mobility mailboxes, responding to general employee questions and routing requests as needed.
  • Prepare standard employment documents, such as verification letters, and maintain organized digital files for the team.
  • Support the tracking of company policy updates and assist with the basic administrative tasks required for work authorizations.
  • Perform regular data entry and routine audits within the HRIS (UKG) to ensure all employee information remains accurate and consistent.
  • System updates across the People Technology stack.
  • Other duties as assigned

What You’ll Bring to the Table:

  • Must be currently enrolled in a post-secondary program, with a strong preference for Human Resources or a related field.
  • Strong written and verbal communication, ability to take meeting notes and help draft step-by-step guides for team tasks.
  • Proficiency in Excel/Google Sheets skills (pivots, VLOOKUPs) is advantageous.
  • Demonstrates a high degree of personal integrity and an understanding of the importance of keeping employee information confidential.
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy for compliance-heavy tasks, data audits and reporting.
  • Comfortable working independently and taking initiative.
  • An interest in learning how systems work together and a willingness to learn how to build automated forms and workflows.
