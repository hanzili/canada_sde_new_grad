Full Description

Azure Group is looking to hire a part time Junior Environmental Scientist to join our team, please email samantha@azuregroup.ca if you are interested in applying. The role includes the following:

Responsibilities

Conducting site visits and collecting soil & groundwater samples, drilling supervision, writing Phase I/One & II/Two ESA and other related work, etc.

Qualifications

-Completion of an undergraduate degree in Environmental Science, or other Engineering discipline

-Minimum of 1 year experience conducting environmental site assessments and related field work

-Valid G2 or G license and suitable car for site visits, Canadian Citizenship or Permanent Residence required.
