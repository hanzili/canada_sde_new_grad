About GFL Environmental

Leading environmental services firm with process focus

Full Description

The Opportunity

As a Junior Business Analyst in the Treasury & Shared Services – Business Process & Special Projects team, you will support the Manager of Cash Operations in driving efficiency and standardization for the Cash Application department. This is a highly fluid role where you will work under the guidance of the Manager of Business Processes & Special Projects to provide Business Analyst support on small to large projects.

Responsibilities

  • Utilize process mapping techniques to document current-state and future-state processes and systems.
  • Coordinate workshop sessions with appropriate groups to gather feedback and alignment on proposed processes and initiatives.
  • Document requirements with acceptance criteria to facilitate internal and external development.
  • Draft and maintain preliminary training, SOP, and/or communications materials to educate internal cash application departments on upcoming changes.
  • Partake in User Acceptance Testing (UAT) for process or system changes, and identify and document gaps.
  • Support projects and M&A activity by representing the Cash Application stream and providing input based on developed knowledge.
  • Provide audit support as required.
  • Perform other duties as assigned.

Required Qualifications

  • Strong critical thinking and analytical skills with the ability to analyze complex processes and data to identify opportunities.
  • Proficiency with Excel or Google Sheets, and PowerPoint.
  • University degree in Business, Finance, or a similar field.
  • Excellent organizational, communication, and time management skills.
  • Ability to foster collaborative decision-making to inform future process improvements and initiatives.

Preferred Attributes

  • Experience as a Business Analyst within the finance field.
  • Finance and/or Order-to-Cash (O2C) expertise.
  • Experience with process mapping tools such as Lucidchart or Visio.
