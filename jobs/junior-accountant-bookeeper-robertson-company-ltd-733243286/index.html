About the job

Job Title:

Junior Accountant / Bookkeeper

Location:

Toronto, ON (Remote- 1 day a week onsite)

Base Salary:

$70K–$90K CAD (dependent on experience)

The Client:

Our client is a growing and dynamic organization with a lean finance team supporting continued expansion. The company offers a collaborative environment where employees are given the opportunity to grow internally, take on increasing responsibility, and contribute directly to business success. With additional planned hiring in the coming months, this is an exciting time to join a scaling organization.

This is an opportunity to join the finance team in a hands-on accounting role, supporting monthly reporting, audit activities, bookkeeping functions, and day-to-day accounting operations. The successful candidate will work closely with finance leadership and play an important role in maintaining accurate financial records while helping support future growth initiatives.

This role is ideal for an experienced bookkeeper, junior to intermediate accountant, or an individual actively pursuing their CPA designation who is looking to gain broader exposure in a growing business environment.

What you will achieve in this role:

• Prepare monthly and quarterly financial reports

• Assist with month-end and year-end close processes

• Maintain accurate general ledger records and reconciliations

• Support external audit processes and provide required documentation

• Assist with accounts payable, accounts receivable, payroll, and transactional accounting tasks

• Ensure accuracy and completeness of financial data

• Help improve accounting processes and internal controls

• Support budgeting and forecasting activities as needed

• Work closely with finance leadership on reporting priorities

• Contribute to a high-performing and collaborative small team environment

• Take ownership of a variety of accounting responsibilities and assist wherever needed

• Assist with ad hoc finance and accounting projects as the company grows

What you must bring to this role:

• 3+ years of accounting, bookkeeping, or finance experience in an industry environment

• Experience preparing monthly financial statements and reporting packages

• Strong proficiency with QuickBooks (required)

• Experience with full-cycle bookkeeping, account reconciliations, and month-end activities

• Audit support or audit coordination experience considered an asset

• CPA designation not required; candidates actively pursuing their CPA are encouraged to apply

• Strong attention to detail and high level of accuracy

• Ability to manage priorities in a fast-paced environment

• Excellent communication and organizational skills

• Comfortable working independently in a small, lean team setting

• Demonstrated willingness to take ownership and support multiple areas of the business

• Spanish language skills considered a nice to have

Only those who are qualified will be contacted.

This posting is for an existing vacancy and is intended to fill a current open position.

Additional Information

Background screening may be required as part of the onboarding process. The type of screening required will vary based on the position and client requirements.

We use AI technology as part of our application review process to assist in screening and assessment. All applications are also reviewed by our recruitment team.

How to Apply

If you are a motivated accounting professional looking to join a growing company with strong internal advancement opportunities, please submit your resume outlining your qualifications and relevant experience.

Robertson is an equal opportunity employer committed to diversity and inclusion. We welcome applications from all qualified individuals regardless of race, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, or any other protected status.
