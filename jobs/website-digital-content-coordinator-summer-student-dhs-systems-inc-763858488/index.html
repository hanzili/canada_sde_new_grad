Full Description

About DHS Systems Inc.

DHS Systems Inc. designs, installs, and services luxury outdoor systems for high-end residential properties — including fire features, pool and spa heating, gas infrastructure, and premium outdoor upgrades.

We operate at a high level of precision, organization, and client experience. Our work is clean, detailed, and built to last — and we are now building a digital presence that reflects that standard.

Role Overview

This role is focused on building and organizing our digital presence.

You will take real project content and turn it into:

  • Website pages
  • Project case studies
  • Structured, professional content assets

This is not just posting — this is about building something that drives real business growth.

Key ResponsibilitiesWebsite Development & Content Build

  • Create and update service pages (fire features, heating, infrastructure, etc.)
  • Build clean, well-structured project pages using real job content
  • Maintain strong formatting, layout, and user experience across the website
  • Improve calls-to-action and overall flow for client inquiries

Content Organization & Asset Management

  • Organize all incoming photos/videos into a clean, searchable system
  • Maintain a structured content library for long-term use
  • Identify high-value projects and flag them for full case studies

Writing & Content Development

  • Write clear, professional drafts for:
  • Website pages
  • Project summaries
  • Service descriptions
  • Maintain a consistent, premium tone aligned with our brand

Basic SEO & Performance Support

  • Implement on-page SEO fundamentals:
  • Page titles
  • Meta descriptions
  • Headings and structure
  • Support Google Business Profile updates with project content

Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled in post-secondary (marketing, business, communications, or similar)
  • Strong attention to detail and organization skills
  • Comfortable with website platforms (WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, etc.)
  • Strong writing ability (clear, structured, professional)
  • Able to take direction and execute consistently

What You’ll Gain

  • Real-world experience building a business website
  • A portfolio of published project pages and service content
  • Exposure to a high-end residential construction environment
  • Structured mentorship and clear expectations
