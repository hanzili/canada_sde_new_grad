WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

We’re hiring a Quality Assurance Associate to maintain Quality Standards in our production and compounding processes.

This is an on-site role based out of our Eastside Manufacturing Facility in Etobicoke.

PAY RANGE

The anticipated base salary for this position is $45,000 to $50,000.

Exact salary depends on several factors such as experience, skills, education, and budget. Salary range may vary based on geographic location.

WHAT YOU’D BE DOING IN THIS ROLE

  • Issue Master Batch Packaging Records (MBPR), Compounding Batch Records (CBR), and logbooks as needed.
  • Document batch details, verify reconciliations, and review MBPR and CBR for accuracy and compliance.
  • Prepare Packaging Assembly Instructions for Fill & Pack operations in Microsoft Dynamics 365 (D365).
  • Process Quality Orders in D365 for each production order.
  • Perform daily weighing scale verification checks.
  • Conduct random kettle cleanliness checks for routine batching processes, new product development (NPD), and existing product development (EPD).
  • Perform in-process checks during batching (e.g., temperature, speeds, weights, timing).
  • Verify documents generated from the Pre-weigh and Compounding areas.
  • Verify machine parameters used during the batching process.
  • Conduct required in-process checks on packaging lines and kettles as per quality standards and specifications.

SKILLS AND QUALIFICATIONS NEEDED TO GET THE JOB DONE

  • Minimum of 2 years’ experience in Quality Assurance, Quality Control, or Regulatory Affairs within a manufacturing environment.
  • Solid understanding of Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and Good Documentation Practices (GDP).
  • Demonstrated ability to work independently with minimal supervision.
  • Proficiency in key software platforms, including Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP ERP, and Intelex eQMS.
  • Strong organizational skills, with exceptional time management and attention to detail.
