About Captain Handy Inc

Full Description

Overview
We are seeking a motivated Junior Project Manager to join our dynamic landscaping construction management team. This entry-level role offers an excellent opportunity to develop foundational skills in project coordination, planning, and execution within the landscaping and construction industry. The ideal candidate will possess a strong interest in outdoor construction processes, excellent organizational abilities, and a desire to grow professionally in a collaborative environment. This position provides hands-on experience with industry-leading landscaping management software and tools, supporting the successful delivery of projects from inception to completion.

Duties

  • Assist in developing and reviewing project plans, schedules, and budgets
  • Support project teams by coordinating activities across various departments
  • Read and interpret blueprints, permits, and technical drawings to facilitate accurate project execution.
  • Utilize construction estimating techniques to assist in preparing cost projections and resource allocation.
  • Prepare and manage project documentation, including reports, change orders, and progress updates.
  • Collaborate with team members to ensure adherence to project timelines, quality standards, and safety protocols.
  • Support site visits and inspections to monitor progress and resolve issues promptly.
  • Maintain effective time management practices to prioritize tasks efficiently across multiple projects.

Qualifications

  • Familiarity with blueprint reading, schematics, and technical drawings.
  • Experience or training in landscaping management
  • Knowledge of landscaping construction processes and construction management principles.
  • Strong organizational skills with excellent time management abilities.
  • Ability to work collaboratively within a team environment while managing multiple priorities effectively.
  • Basic understanding of construction safety standards and site protocols.
  • Excellent communication skills—both written and verbal—are essential for coordinating with team members. This position offers a valuable platform for emerging professionals eager to build a career in landscaping and construction project management while gaining practical experience with industry-standard tools and techniques.
