Full Description

About Mansteel Rebar

Mansteel Rebar Ltd. is a leading fabricator, installer, and distributor of concrete reinforcing steel serving commercial, residential, infrastructure, and industrial construction projects across Ontario. Our Richmond Hill facility operates in a fast-paced environment where accuracy, safety, and quality control are essential.

At Mansteel Rebar, we believe every team member can make a difference. We invest in our employees, foster collaboration, and strive to provide an exceptional experience for our customers from order placement to delivery and installation.

Position Summary

We are seeking a detail-oriented and motivated Accounting Clerk to join our team on a full-time temporary basis. This role is ideal for a student, recent graduate, or individual seeking practical accounting experience in a professional office environment.

The successful candidate will support day-to-day accounting operations through invoice processing, data entry, document review, account verification, and Excel-based reporting. This position requires strong attention to detail, accuracy, and organizational skills, with a primary focus on invoice matching, verification, and maintaining financial records.

Please note: Mansteel Rebar Ltd. does not require Canadian work experience as part of its hiring criteria.

Key Responsibilities

  • Process and enter vendor invoices into the accounting system.
  • Review and verify invoices for accuracy and completeness.
  • Match invoices to purchase orders, receipts, and supporting documentation.
  • Verify account information and identify discrepancies.
  • Perform data entry with a high degree of accuracy.
  • Organize and maintain electronic and physical accounting files.
  • Assist with month-end documentation and reporting.
  • Support the Accounting Department with general administrative duties.
  • Maintain confidentiality of financial and employee information.
  • Other duties as assigned.

Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled in or recently completed a diploma or degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • Strong Microsoft Excel skills, including data entry, sorting, filtering, and basic formulas.
  • Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Ability to manage confidential information professionally.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team environment.
  • Previous office, administrative, or accounting experience is considered an asset but is not required.

AI Disclosure

Applicants should be aware that Mansteel Rebar Ltd. may use automated systems or artificial intelligence tools to support resume screening and recruitment decision-making processes.

Compensation & Benefits

  • $20.00 – $24.00 per hour, based on education, skills, and experience.
  • Full-time schedule (40 hours per week).
  • Temporary contract position with potential for extension based on business needs.
  • Hands-on experience in accounting, accounts payable, and financial administration.
  • Opportunity to gain experience within the construction and manufacturing industry.
  • Supportive team environment and on-the-job training.
