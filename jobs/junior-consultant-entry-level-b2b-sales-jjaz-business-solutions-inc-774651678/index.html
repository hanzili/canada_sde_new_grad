About the Role

Build relationships, assess tech, present client solutions

About JJAZ Business Solutions inc.

Growing consultancy delivering tech-enabled marketing solutions

Full Description

Company Overview
JJAZ Business Solutions helps businesses evaluate and improve the technology, marketing systems and essential services they use to operate.

Through our clients and provider relationships, consultants gain exposure to established technology companies and recognizable products used by businesses across Canada.

We work across several areas, including payment technology, telecommunications, software, CRM systems, marketing, websites, energy and other essential business services.

This gives consultants the opportunity to learn about multiple areas of business rather than being limited to one product or industry.

About the Role
As a Junior Technology and Marketing Consultant, you will gain hands-on experience working directly with Calgary business owners.

You will learn how different businesses operate, how they make purchasing decisions, how they attract and manage customers, and how technology can help them reduce costs, improve efficiency and support growth.

This is practical experience that can support a future career in sales, marketing, technology, business development, management or entrepreneurship.

What You’ll Do

  • Build professional relationships with local business owners
  • Learn about their operations, goals and current challenges
  • Evaluate existing technology, marketing systems and business services
  • Identify potential savings, improvements and growth opportunities
  • Present suitable solutions through JJAZ and its provider relationships
  • Coordinate quotes, applications and customer onboarding
  • Maintain long-term relationships with clients
  • Introduce additional solutions as clients’ needs evolve
  • Build and manage your own portfolio of business customers
  • Participate in ongoing training and professional development

Why Join Us?

  • Career Growth: Fast-track your career with opportunities for rapid advancement into senior roles.
  • Training & Development: Access ongoing training to sharpen your skills and achieve your potential.
  • Impactful Work: Make a real difference by helping businesses grow and succeed.
  • Dynamic Team: Work alongside motivated and supportive team members.

What We’re Looking For

  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • A genuine interest in marketing, sales, business, or technology.
  • Self-motivated with a drive to succeed and grow.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively.
  • A valid driver’s license and access to a reliable vehicle (required).
  • No prior experience required—just a willingness to learn and excel.

How to Apply
If you’re ready to launch your career in marketing and sales, we want to hear from you! Submit your resume today.

Join us in empowering businesses to save, grow, and succeed!
