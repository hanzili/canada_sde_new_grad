Full Description

At C-CORE, we are committed to investing in people and creating a dynamic and rewarding employment experience. We are seeking a Junior Mechanical Engineer to join our Oceans & Energy Team. This position is based in St. John’s, NL.   

If you have a passion for complex design solutions, hands-on fabrication, and contributing to cutting-edge R&D projects from concept to commissioning, this opportunity may be for you. Are you enthusiastic about engineering, attentive to detail, and motivated to support a diverse team of professionals in a dynamic environment? If so, we want you on our team!  

About C-CORE

C-CORE conducts R&D and provides research-based advisory services, delivering innovative engineering and technology solutions to national and international clients. For over 50 years, we have built a world-class team of professionals in Remote Sensing and Oceans & Energy. We are global leaders in Earth Observation and geospatial intelligence, with our flagship platform Coresight driving major contracts in defense, environmental monitoring, and advanced R&D.   

Position Overview

As a Junior Mechanical Engineer , you will be responsible for contributing to and completing complex design solutions by applying principles of mechanical, structural, hydraulic, and electrical engineering. You will operate within a collaborative team environment, requiring effective communication with engineers, technicians, and stakeholders to ensure the safe and efficient design, fabrication, and operation of equipment. Additionally, this role provides vital engineering support across the entire organization whenever hardware fabrication is required.   

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide mechanical engineering support (including design, fabrication, installation, testing, commissioning, and operations) as required for a variety of projects across the organization.
  • Update and maintain 3D CAD models of assemblies and individual components, and generate detailed 2D manufacturing drawings.
  • Research and identify potential vendors for Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) parts and raw materials, and assist in preparing technical specifications.
  • Conduct literature and state-of-the-art technology reviews to support component design, and assist in evaluating concepts and prototypes through calculations or physical testing.
  • Draft technical documentation, including assembly instructions, user manuals, test plans, and maintenance procedures.
  • Take part in client meetings, contribute to project reporting, and effectively communicate project challenges, risks, and schedule status.

Desired Qualifications

  • Bachelor of Engineering degree required.
  • PEGNL Certification (Engineer in Training).
  • 1 year of experience in mechanical, structural, electrical, or instrumentation systems engineering, including developing designs, fabricating, installation, and operations.
  • Working knowledge of mechanical engineering, hydraulics, fluid flow, structural analysis, and machine component design.
  • Familiarity with industry-standard design software applications.
  • Hands-on ability to complete laboratory and shop work, including fabrication, mechanical and electrical assembly, commissioning, and test execution.
  • General knowledge of geotechnical engineering, electrical engineering, instrumentation design and use, as well as data acquisition.
  • Excellent problem-solving, time management, and stakeholder communication skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in standard office environments, laboratories, fabrication shops, and field locations.

Why Join Us?

C-CORE offers competitive salaries and benefits, including attractive vacation entitlements and comprehensive medical and dental plans. We support professional development and personal growth. Here’s what makes this role special:   

  • Impact : Your work directly ensures the safe and efficient design, fabrication, and operation of mission-critical equipment across C-CORE.
  • Innovation : Support diverse engineering needs by completing R&D tasks and applying state-of-the-art technology to practical problems.
  • Collaboration : Work in a highly collaborative environment alongside engineers, technicians, and external stakeholders.
  • Growth Opportunities : Gain hands-on, end-to-end experience—from 3D CAD modeling and desktop design to equipment mobilization, field testing, and shop fabrication.
