As a Junior Asphalt Laboratory Technician in our Construction Services, Materials Engineering and Laboratory Group based in London, Ontario , you'll contribute to asphalt materials testing, quality assurance, and laboratory operations while developing your technical expertise alongside experienced engineering and laboratory professionals.

Your Impact

  • Conduct asphalt laboratory testing in accordance with ASTM, LS, AASHTO, and applicable MTO standards.
  • Support the generation of accurate and reliable test data used for engineering analysis and client reporting.
  • Maintain required certifications and participate in proficiency testing programs.
  • Assist with calibration, maintenance, and housekeeping activities to ensure a safe and efficient laboratory environment.
  • Support quality management processes by identifying and reporting potential non-conformances.
  • Manage the receiving, logging, handling, storage, shipping, and disposal of materials and samples.
  • Collaborate with engineers, technologists, technicians, and clients to deliver high-quality laboratory services.
  • Develop technical knowledge and hands-on experience while contributing to infrastructure projects that strengthen communities across Ontario.

The Skills That Set You Apart

  • Post-secondary education in Civil Engineering Technology, Civil Engineering Technician, or a related technical discipline.
  • Current CCIL Type B Asphalt Testing Certification (Marshall and/or Superpave), or working toward certification.
  • 1 to 3 years of experience with CCIL-certified Marshall and/or Superpave asphalt testing methods.
  • Working knowledge of MTO, ASTM, AASHTO, and related laboratory testing standards.
  • Experience performing laboratory testing, data entry, and technical report preparation.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including Excel, Word, and Outlook.
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Demonstrated commitment to health, safety, quality, and teamwork.
