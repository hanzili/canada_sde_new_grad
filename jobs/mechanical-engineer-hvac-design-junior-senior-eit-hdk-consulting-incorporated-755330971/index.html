About the Role

Assist HVAC/plumbing/fire design; prepare drawings.

About HDK Consulting Incorporated

Growing building systems consultancy; diverse commercial projects.

Full Description

Summary:

  • Assist lead mechanical engineer with aspects of projects including HVAC, Plumbing and Fire protection design for commercial buildings.
  • Manage project tasks that are assigned to you by the lead mechanical engineer
  • Assist with preparation of construction documents (drawings and specifications).
  • Write specifications, prepare schedules and assists with technical reports and control schedules, budgets.
  • Perform onsite reviews, contract administration
  • Attend meeting with clients, other consultants and contractors
  • Prepare design calculations with assistance from lead mechanical engineer.
  • Assist with condition assessments on existing building systems, prepare written reports

Qualifications: 0 – 4 years of relevant experience in the design of building Mechanical systems including HVAC, Plumbing and Fire Protection required. Experience must be in building consulting engineering.

  • Registration as a C.E.T., Ctech with CTTAM or EIT/MIT with EGM.
  • Experience in AutoCAD and MS Office required.
  • Experience in Revit is not required but valued.
  • Ability to obtain Government of Canada CISD Security Clearance
  • Travel required; 1-3 days per month on average with overnight stays in other provinces.
  • Driver’s License preferred
  • Good initiative, attentive to details, ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

Salary commensurate with experience, HDK consulting offers a full health and dental package.

Please send your resume along with a cover letter via indeed/this job posting.

We appreciate your interest in this opportunity, however only those selected for an interview will be contacted.

About HDK Consulting Incorporated: HDK Consulting Incorporated is a growing Mechanical and Electrical Engineering firm. We specialize in Mechanical, Electrical, Communications and Security Systems Engineering for commercial buildings. Our partners and network of professionals have extensive experience in both small and large scale projects located across the world. These projects span all sectors of the industry including Office/Retail, Hospitality, Healthcare, Institutional, Science and Technology, and National Defence Projects.
