About Brandt Group of Companies

Regina-based diversified manufacturing and service corporation.

Full Description

Brandt Corporate Services is seeking a self-motivated and disciplined Junior Copywriter to join our team in Regina, SK. This position is responsible for developing creative copy and content that connects with audiences across all marketing channels.

Duties and Responsibilities

  • Support the marketing team and subject matter experts through brainstorming sessions, contributing fresh ideas and helping develop content strategies across a range of topics for online platforms
  • Assist with stakeholder interviews to gather insights and help shape them into compelling content
  • Write copy for campaigns targeting internal and external audiences across channels including social media, email marketing, online display ads, website content, teaser videos, blog posts, e-newsletters, and more
  • Produce, maintain, and proofread marketing content for multiple applications under the guidance of senior team members
  • Follow the company style guide and editorial standards to consistently reinforce brand voice
  • Other copywriting as required
