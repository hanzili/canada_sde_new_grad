Full Description

WE'RE HIRING: SLEEP CONSULTANT (Sales) Ready to Make More Money, Work Better Hours, and Actually Enjoy Your Team?

Modern Mattress is growing, and we're looking for our next Sleep Consultant to join one of Saskatchewan's fastest-growing local companies.

If you've ever worked as a server, bartender, or waitress, keep reading...

Honestly? You might already have exactly what we're looking for.

Why?

Because great servers know how to:

  • Build relationships
  • Read people
  • Ask great questions
  • Deliver incredible customer experiences
  • Thrive in fast-paced environments
  • Earn their pay through performance

The difference?

  • You can make significantly more money here without the late nights, split shifts, and constant weekend closings.

What You'll Love About Working at Modern Mattress:

Uncapped Commission Potential
Your earning potential is in your hands. The harder and smarter you work, the more you earn. There is no ceiling.

Retail Hours That Don't Suck
Say goodbye to midnight closes and unpredictable restaurant schedules. Enjoy consistent retail hours that allow for an actual life outside of work.

An Incredible Team Culture
We work hard, laugh hard, celebrate wins, and support each other. No drama. No toxic environments. Just great people chasing big goals together.

Real Opportunities for Growth
We're growing, and we love promoting from within. Many of our leaders started on the sales floor.

Employee Benefits Package
Because taking care of our team matters.

Employee Purchase Program
Get exclusive discounts on products designed to help you sleep, recover, and feel your best.

Full Training Provided
No mattress experience? No problem. We'll teach you everything you need to know about sleep, products, and how to succeed.

Who We're Looking For:

You might be a perfect fit if you are:

  • Personable and outgoing
  • Competitive and motivated
  • Passionate about helping people
  • A strong communicator
  • Driven to earn based on performance
  • Someone who loves being part of a winning team

At Modern Mattress, we don't just sell mattresses.

We help Saskatchewan sleep better.

And we're looking for people who want to build a career while making a real difference in people's lives.

Think you've got what it takes?

Apply today and come see why so many people who leave hospitality never look back.
