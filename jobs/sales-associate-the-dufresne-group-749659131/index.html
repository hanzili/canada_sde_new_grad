About the Role

About The Dufresne Group

Largest Ashley licensee in Canada; strong growth

Full Description

Join the Ashley Canada Team – Sales Associates Abbotsford, BC At The Dufresne Group, we don’t just sell furniture, we help people create homes they love. As the largest Ashley licensee in Canada, with locations from B.C. to Ontario, we’re proud to Make Life Better for our guests and our communities.

We’re growing and hiring Sales Associates for our Abbotsford store located at 1425 Sumas Way, Unit 106. If you're looking for a rewarding career where you can make an impact, grow professionally, and be recognized for your success, this may be the perfect fit.

What You’ll Love About This Role

  • Strong earning potential: Full-Time $54,000–$80,000+ (based on performance)
  • Career growth: Clear path to higher earnings and advancement
  • Perks: Employee team members discounts, benefits (FT), educational support
  • Training: Industry-leading programs + ongoing coaching
  • Culture: Supportive, collaborative, and fun team environment

What You’ll Do

  • Guide guests in finding the right products for their homes
  • Meet or exceed sales goals
  • Stay on top of product knowledge, trends, and competitor offerings
  • Build relationships through calls, online leads, and in-store traffic
  • Keep organized and follow through on all our guests’ needs

What You Bring

  • High school diploma
  • 2+ years of sales experience (retail or commissioned preferred)
  • A confident, results-driven sales approach
  • Strong communication and organizational skills
  • Comfortable using MS Office and learning new systems
  • Flexibility to work days, evenings, weekends
  • Ability to lift up to 30 lbs and stay active on the sales floor

If you’re guest-focused, motivated by results, and ready to grow your career— we’d love to meet you.
