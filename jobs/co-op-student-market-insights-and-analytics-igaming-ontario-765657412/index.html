About the Role

About iGaming Ontario

Ontario government agency overseeing regulated iGaming market

Full Description

Position Term: Fall 2026 Term

Salary: $23.00/per hour

Location: Head Office, Toronto

iGaming Ontario is an agency of the Government of Ontario responsible for conducting and managing the Province’s regulated internet gaming (iGaming) market. Established in July 2021, iGaming Ontario works with private gaming operators and government stakeholders to deliver a safe, sustainable, and healthy market that operates in the public interest.

iGaming Ontario is excited to offer students valuable learning opportunities through our Student Program. If you are enrolled in a co-op program, a student position with iGaming Ontario will provide meaningful hands-on experience, professional development opportunities, and exposure to Ontario’s dynamic and evolving iGaming sector.

Departmental Summary

The Market Strategy and Insights team delivers analytics, industry intelligence and market research solutions to inform strategic and policy decisions and support business critical enterprise programs.

Work Description

Supporting the Responsible Gaming and Agency Compliance function, the student may have the opportunity to work on some or all of the following activities:

  • Support technical user acceptance testing (UAT) for a data platform modernization project.
  • Assist in managing and responding to operator inquiries.
  • Contribute to the development of automated data quality validation system for existing data collection pipelines.
  • Support the development of a centralized system for data and information storage within the department.
  • Conduct online research for market report generation.
  • Support design and development of PowerBI Dashboards.

How do I qualify?

  • Currently enrolled in a post-secondary program in Data Analytics, Economics, Statistics, Computer Science, Data Science, Data Engineering, or a related field.
  • Hands-on experiencing coding in SQL and Python.
  • Hands-on experience building PowerBI dashboards.
  • Solid understanding of database concepts and relational data.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Critical thinker possessing sound judgement, a business mindset, and ability to conceptualize, evaluate, and problem-solve.

Mandatory

You must meet all eligibility criteria by the first day of employment:

1. Co-op Student Status: Enrolled as a full-time post-secondary student in a co-operative education program at a recognized institution.

2. Work Environment: You must be living in the province of Ontario during the term of employment and you are required to work in office 5 days a week.

3. Work Status: Eligible to work in Canada.

All applications must include the following:

  • Resume
  • Cover Letter
  • Copy of your most recent GPA transcript
  • Proof of enrollment for the September 2026 and/or 2027 school year

The successful candidate must be eligible to work in Canada and will be subject to a criminal background check.

iGaming Ontario is an equal opportunity employer that encourages all qualified candidates to apply.

As an organization shaping the future of igaming in Ontario and beyond, our success is built on curiosity, open dialogue, and freedom of thought. We strive to foster a culture where every team member feels empowered to challenge assumptions, explore new ideas, and create opportunities for innovation in our sector. We invite you to share how your own unique background, expertise and ideas would enrich our team.

Disability related accommodation is available upon request during the recruitment process.
