Full Description

Job Description What is the opportunity? This is a unique opportunity to join a brand-new, fast-paced team at the ground level. The Global Security’s Strategic Insights and Enablement team is building a product that gives leadership real-time visibility into strategic delivery and outcomes—automating manual processes and enabling AI-driven digital transformation across Global Security.

As the Junior Full Stack Developer (Global Security), you'll work in an Agile manner to deliver websites and automations.

What will you do?

  • Create, design, analyze, develop, and debug full-stack software applications and responsive web interfaces to meet business requirements, ensuring high-quality and timely delivery
  • Design and develop user-facing web applications using modern frameworks (React, Vue.js, or Angular) that deliver exceptional user experiences and integrate seamlessly with backend APIs and databases
  • Support real-time insights and automation capabilities across the Global Security organization. Work with RESTful web services and backend infrastructure for secure integrations.
  • Build and optimize data pipelines, query databases, and establish data flows from various sources. Work with both SQL and NoSQL databases
  • Deploy and maintain applications on OCP (OpenShift) cloud platforms, containerize applications, and integrate with existing infrastructure
  • Work cross-functionally with business units to gather requirements, support integration projects, and participate in planning sessions

What do you need to succeed? Must-have

  • 2+ years of experience with full-stack development combining Python backend and modern front-end frameworks
  • Proficiency with frontend frameworks such as React, Vue.js, or Angular
  • Experience designing and implementing RESTful web services
  • Strong experience with OCP (OpenShift), Azure, or AWS
  • Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases
  • Experience with GitHub Actions

Nice-to-have

  • Exposure to Generative AI, LLMs, NLP or Data Science exposure
  • Solid understanding of cybersecurity practices

What’s in it for you? We thrive on the challenge to be our best, progressive thinking to keep growing, and working together to deliver trusted advice to help our clients thrive and communities prosper. We care about each other, reaching our potential, making a difference to our communities, and achieving success that is mutual.

  • A comprehensive Total Rewards Program including bonuses and flexible benefits, competitive compensation, commissions, and stock where applicable
  • Leaders who support your development through coaching and managing opportunities
  • Ability to make a difference and lasting impact
  • Work in a dynamic, collaborative, progressive, and high-performing team
  • Flexible work/life balance options
  • Opportunities to do challenging work
  • Opportunities to take on progressively greater accountabilities
  • Access to a variety of job opportunities across business
