Full Description

About Tavendi

Tavendi is a consumer-facing, AI-powered mobile app designed to eliminate the friction of peer-to-peer selling. With just a single photo, our proprietary AI engine evaluates real-time market value, writes optimized descriptions, and instantly cross-posts items to global marketplaces. We’ve recently rolled out a major brand evolution and are expanding our user acquisition pipelines across North America.

The Opportunity

We are looking for a creative, high-energy Digital Growth & Content Creator Intern for the Fall 2026 cohort. Skip the corporate busywork—in this role, you will gain hands-on startup experience by owning our consumer marketing channels, driving mobile app downloads, and leading our local student and creator outreach.

📋 Key Responsibilities

  • Short-Form Content Production: Script, film, edit, and deploy high-retention video content for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
  • Creator & Campus Outreach: Identify, pitch, and secure strategic partnerships with Montreal campus organizations, student creators, and local micro-influencers.
  • Social Media Management: Plan and schedule content calendars across all organic channels to build an active, engaged community around mobile reselling.
  • Copywriting: Craft punchy marketing text, app store optimizations, and promotional push angles that match our brand identity.

🎯 What We Are Looking For

  • Creative Video DNA: A strong, proven interest in short-form video creation, algorithm trends, and mobile editing workflows. (Links to a portfolio or active social channels are highly valued!)
  • Excellent Communication: Exceptional copywriting and pitch skills—you know how to turn raw attention into app store clicks.
  • Proactive Mindset: Highly organized, self-motivated, and excited to move fast in an early-stage consumer tech environment.

💰 Compensation & Benefits

  • Salary: $18.00 per hour.
  • Term: Fall 2026 (4-month duration, with potential for extension).
  • Work Style: Hybrid (Flexible blend of remote work and collaborative on-site sessions at our Montreal office close to Snowdon metro).
  • Perks: Deep mentorship directly under an Entrepreneur in Residence, massive portfolio-building ownership, and a casual, high-growth tech environment.

⚙️ Our Hiring Process

We value your time and move fast. Our process consists of:

  1. Resume & Portfolio Review: We read every single application (especially your video links).
  2. The Virtual Coffee (30 Mins): A casual Google Meet call with our team to talk about your creative style and alignment with Tavendi.
  3. The Offer: No endless rounds—if it's a match, we lock it in.

🔒 Applicant Privacy Practice

At Tavendi, we take your data privacy seriously. All personal information and portfolio materials submitted during the application process are collected solely for recruitment purposes, handled securely in compliance with local regulations, and will never be shared with third parties.

  • Note: Applicants must be currently enrolled students to satisfy local workspace credit alignments. To apply, please submit your resume and a link to your creative work/portfolio.
