Full Description

About the role

Hybrid role, 1 day per week in our downtown office. Only candidates located in the Lower Mainland will be considered. 

This is a new, dedicated position created to address Traction Complete's growing backlog of internal automation projects. This role focuses exclusively on building AI-powered tools and automations for internal teams. The Junior AI Automation Developer will report directly to the CTO.

You'll work across every department — marketing, customer success, product, sales, etc. — to discover pain points, gather requirements, and build solutions that make teams more effective. Think of it as running your own internal product shop: you'll prioritize projects by ROI, manage stakeholder expectations, and ship working applications.

This is fully AI-driven development. You won't spend time coding manually; you'll prompt AI systems, orchestrate agents, and use tools like Claude, Cursor, and Playwright to build, test, and ship at a pace that would normally require a much larger team. You'll need the technical understanding to review and correct AI-generated code, but the emphasis is on directing AI rather than writing every line yourself.

This is a standalone role. You'll operate as a one-person team, owning projects end-to-end from discovery through delivery, with support from department heads and the IT team for security reviews.

Who We Are

At Traction Complete, we empower our customers with great technology. Built on the Salesforce platform, we enable some of the world's largest companies, like Zoom, to deliver exceptional services and achieve their missions. At our core, we are driven by a strong desire to support and uplift our clients.

Traction Complete has been honored as one of Forbes' Top Startups and a Great Place to Work. We take immense pride in our product development, outstanding customer service, and the strong relationships we build with our partners and clients.

Community is at the heart of everything we do. We’re seeking individuals who are eager to join a dynamic team of engaged professionals who view business as more than just a means to generate revenue. We’re passionate about making a positive impact on our customers' lives through innovative solutions and genuine care.

As part of our Traction team, you'll experience a strong sense of camaraderie. We support one another through challenges, celebrate each other's successes, and grow together in an environment where collaboration and shared achievements drive our collective success.

If you're looking for a workplace where your contributions are genuinely valued and your growth is supported by a team that truly cheers you on, you've landed  on the right page.

What you'll be doing

  • Discover and document automation needs across departments through stakeholder conversations
  • Partner with department leaders to understand workflows, gather requirements, and turn ideas into practical solutions
  • Prioritize projects based on ROI and time investment
  • Design and build AI-powered internal tools, workflows, and agents using modern AI platforms such as Claude, Cursor, and other emerging technologies
  • Work closely with IT to ensure applications meet security, data handling, authentication, and deployment requirements
  • Perform QA testing on built applications, including automated testing via tools like Playwright
  • Keep stakeholders informed on progress, timelines, and tradeoffs throughout the project lifecycle
  • Own and complete proof-of-concept projects that are currently in limbo
  • Create clear documentation so others can understand, maintain, and build upon your work
  • Focus on one project at a time through to completion, even when competing requests come in

What You Bring

Our team comes from a variety of backgrounds, and we're excited to meet people from non-traditional paths. Typically, people are successful in this role when they have:

  1. Computer Science degree or equivalent related education and/or practical experience
  2. 1-2 years of experience in a similar role
  3. Programming knowledge and ability to code (Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, or similar)
  4. Technical understanding to review and correct AI-generated code
  5. Hands-on experience with AI-assisted development tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code)
  6. Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with confidence engaging stakeholders across the business
  7. Strong organizational and self-management skills, with the ability to manage priorities and timelines
  8. Entrepreneurial mindset — focused on business impact, not just writing code
  9. Ability to ask useful questions, gather requirements, and run discovery conversations with non-technical stakeholders
  10. Basic understanding of secure development practices, including authentication, secrets handling, and access control

Nice to have

  • Product management certificate or training
  • Design skills
  • Salesforce experience or certification
  • Prior experience working in a company environment and building cross-functional relationships

Early impact (first 90 days)
Within your first three months, you should expect to take at least one AI-powered application from idea to internal release, working closely with stakeholders and the IT team throughout discovery, build, and security review.
