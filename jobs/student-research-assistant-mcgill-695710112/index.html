Hiring Unit:

Department of Psychology

Position Summary:

  • Assist with the design and development of the research project tools.
  • Assist research team members in creating descriptive analyses of research tools, including sound files.
  • Conduct internet-based search for appropriate research tools.
  • Assist in ordering audio research supplies.
  • Assist users with research equipment operation.
  • Implements pre-established procedures for maintaining research quality

Qualifications:

  • Experience in research or related setting
  • Experience working in teams
  • Experience providing written and/or oral explanations of work to others

Experience and Education:

  • Bachelor's Degree

Before applying, please note that to work at McGill University, you must be both authorized to work in Canada and willing to work in the province of Quebec at the campus where the position is based / located.

McGill University is an English-language university where most teaching and research activities are conducted in the English language, thereby requiring English communication both verbally and in writing.
