About Inkeros

Inkeros: AT&T client services, growth-focused

Full Description

Inkeros is growing, and we’re looking for driven, ambitious individuals to join our team as Outbound Sales Specialists in Burnaby.

In this role, you’ll represent AT&T Business Services , connecting with U.S.-based business clients through inside B2B sales. You’ll start with warm leads and quickly develop the skills to succeed in outbound prospecting—supported by ongoing training and a high-energy team environment.

What you’ll do:

• Connect with business clients to understand their needs and recommend tailored solutions

• Present AT&T products with confidence, clearly explaining value and benefits

• Manage warm leads while building skills in outbound cold outreach

• Build strong client relationships and handle inquiries professionally

• Accurately process sales and maintain customer records

• Participate in daily coaching and ongoing development sessions

What you’ll get:

• Hourly base pay + uncapped commissions

• Benefits after 3-month probation

• Career growth with clear advancement opportunities

• Company travel opportunities

• A supportive, high-performance team culture

What we’re looking for:

• 1–2 years of sales experience preferred (not required)

• Strong communication and interpersonal skills

• Self-motivated, goal-oriented, and confident

• Comfortable working independently and in a team environment

• Basic computer skills and willingness to learn

If you’re looking to build a career in sales with unlimited earning potential and real growth opportunities, we want to hear from you.

Individuals who complete this assessment will only be contacted by our HR Team.
