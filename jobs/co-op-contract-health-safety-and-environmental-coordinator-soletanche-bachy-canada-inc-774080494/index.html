Full Description

CO-OP/Contract Health, Safety & Environmental Coordinator - Western Canada

Co-op/Contract Position | Calgary, AB (Travel within Western Canada Required)

This is not your typical co-op opportunity.

If you're looking for a placement where you'll spend your term observing from the sidelines, this probably isn't the right fit.

If you want to:

• Gain hands-on experience on active construction projects

• Learn from experienced HSE and operational leaders

• Participate in inspections, audits, investigations, and risk assessments

• Help support a strong safety culture built on care, learning, and continuous improvement

• Build a foundation for a successful career in Health, Safety & Environment

...this role is built for that.

Why Soletanche Bachy Canada – We Care

At Soletanche Bachy Canada, safety isn't a rulebook. It's how we care for each other.

We are building a culture where people feel safe to speak up, leaders coach rather than blame, learning is valued alongside compliance, and risks are managed through effective planning, systems, and critical controls.

With more than 120 years of experience in North America and as part of the Soletanche Freyssinet global group, we combine local expertise with international capability while creating an environment where people can learn, contribute, and grow.

What This Job Actually Is

This is an opportunity to become an active contributor to our Health, Safety & Environmental team while gaining real-world experience in the construction industry.

You'll work alongside HSE professionals, Project Managers, Superintendents, Supervisors, and field crews to support the implementation and continuous improvement of our Health, Safety & Environmental programs. You'll gain exposure to project operations, safety systems, inspections, investigations, audits, training, reporting, and risk management.

This role combines field exposure, learning, collaboration, and professional development. No two days are the same.

What Your Day-to-Day Will Look Like

Health, Safety & Environmental Program Support

• Be an active participant in SB Canada's Health and Safety Program and contribute to a positive safety culture

• Support the implementation and continuous improvement of HSE programs and initiatives

• Assist in maintaining HSE documentation and records

• Help ensure consistency in safety documentation across projects and business units

• Support communication of HSE expectations and best practices

Site Inspections, Audits & Assessments

• Participate in inspections, audits, assessments, tours, and workplace reviews

• Assist with hazard identification and verification of control measures

• Support workplace assessments to ensure compliance with legislation and company standards

• Assist in reviewing audit findings and tracking corrective actions

• Visit project sites to support audits, employee engagement, incident reviews, and HSE initiatives

Incident Reporting & Risk Management

• Assist with incident investigations and documentation

• Support the collection, review, and analysis of incident data

• Help identify trends and opportunities for improvement

• Assist with risk assessments and hazard evaluations

• Support the development and implementation of corrective actions

Data Analysis & Reporting

• Perform data entry and maintain accurate HSE records

• Compile information and prepare reports using Excel and other systems

• Assist with tracking key safety performance indicators (KPIs)

• Support monthly reporting and statistical analysis

• Help monitor safety performance across active job sites

Training & Communication

• Assist with organizing and developing training sessions, workshops, and presentations

• Support safety communication initiatives across the organization

• Help create practical resources that improve awareness and engagement

• Collaborate with project teams to communicate safety requirements and expectations

Project & Business Support

• Support contract-specific hazard assessments and project safety plans

• Assist with emergency response planning initiatives

• Provide HSE support during project planning, tender reviews, and contract administration activities

• Support relationships with clients, contractors, suppliers, and regulatory agencies

• Work closely with operational teams to support safe project execution

What You Will Learn

This co-op placement will provide exposure to:

• Construction health and safety management systems

• Incident investigations and corrective action processes

• Risk assessments and hazard identification techniques

• Safety audits, inspections, and compliance activities

• Data analysis and KPI reporting

• Client safety requirements and project-specific safety planning

• Occupational health and safety legislation and industry best practices

• Communication and leadership skills within a construction environment

Why This Role Stands Out

This is more than a co-op position. It's an opportunity to gain meaningful experience with one of Canada's leading deep foundations contractors.

You'll work alongside experienced professionals, contribute to active projects, participate in real HSE initiatives, and develop the knowledge and skills needed to launch a successful career in Health, Safety & Environment.

What Success Looks Like in This Role

• You actively contribute to HSE initiatives and project teams

• You build strong relationships with field and office personnel

• You demonstrate curiosity and a willingness to learn

• You help identify opportunities for improvement

• You support safety programs through accurate reporting and documentation

• You communicate effectively and professionally

• You take initiative and seek opportunities to contribute

• You leave the placement with stronger technical, operational, and leadership skills

Who You Are

We hire for potential, attitude, and willingness to learn. You are someone who is:

• Curious – you enjoy learning and asking questions

• Motivated – you take initiative and look for opportunities to contribute

• Organized – you can manage multiple priorities and maintain accurate records

• Adaptable – you are comfortable in changing environments

• Collaborative – you enjoy working with others and building relationships

• Detail-Oriented – you take pride in producing quality work

• Professional – you communicate effectively with a variety of stakeholders

• Positive – you bring a strong attitude and eagerness to learn

• Safety-Minded – you understand the importance of keeping people safe

Bonus If You're Interested In:

• Health & Safety program development

• Incident investigations and root cause analysis

• Risk assessments and hazard identification

• Data analysis and KPI reporting

• Construction operations and project execution

• Safety communications and training development

• Human & Organizational Performance (HOP)

• Learning and continuous improvement initiatives

Qualifications

• Currently pursuing a Diploma or Degree in Occupational Health & Safety or a related field

• Proficient in Microsoft Office applications including Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and SharePoint

• Strong verbal and written communication skills

• Strong interpersonal and problem-solving abilities

• Ability to identify potential risks and support corrective actions

• Positive attitude and willingness to learn in a collaborative environment

• Interest in HSE inspections, investigations, and program development

• Previous construction, safety, or internship experience is considered an asset

• First Aid, WHMIS, CPR, or other safety certifications are considered an asset

• Familiarity with applicable health and safety regulations is considered an asset

• Valid driver's licence and willingness to travel within the assigned region

What You Should Know Before Applying

• This role is based in Calgary, Alberta

• Travel to project sites will be required

• Some physical site activities may be required

• You'll work closely with project teams, field personnel, clients, and HSE professionals

• You'll have opportunities to take ownership of your development while learning from experienced industry professionals

Our Commitment

We are committed to:

• Providing a safe and supportive environment where you can learn and grow

• Offering meaningful, hands-on experience throughout your co-op term

• Supporting professional development and career growth

• Encouraging continuous learning and innovation

• Building future leaders within our industry
