Location: Mississauga, ON, CAN - 2300 Meadowvale Blvd (Hybrid)

We’re hiring a Sales Enablement & AI Intern to support our Inside and Field Sales teams. Work at the intersection of AI, Salesforce, and Sales — helping teams prioritize opportunities, improve workflows, and turn data into action. If you’re interested in sales, commercial operations, and AI-driven tools, this is a strong opportunity to gain hands-on experience.

Role Summary:

Sales Enablement & AI Intern supporting CRM (Salesforce) and AI tools across Inside and Field Sales. Focused on improving data visibility and consistent use of CRM and AI in day-to-day sales activities.

This internship supports the sales team in adopting and using Salesforce and AI-driven tools to enhance customer engagement, prioritization, and sales productivity. The intern will assist with workflow improvements, data quality, and integration of new tools into daily sales activities. Their work will contribute to more consistent processes, improved pipeline visibility, and stronger use of CRM and AI capabilities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support integration of CRM and AI tools (e.g., Agentforce, Data 360, conversation intelligence) into daily sales activities
  • Maintain Salesforce data and assist with field updates and workflow improvements
  • Support lead and opportunity processes, including updates and basic automation tasks
  • Gather sales feedback to identify workflow gaps and support improvements
  • Document processes and create simple user guides to support CRM and AI use
  • Support the automation and the improvement ofsales workflows

Key Benefits

  • Supports adoption of CRM and AI tools across Inside and Field Sales
  • Improves visibility into leads, opportunities, and sales activity
  • Enhances consistency across the team in how leads, opportunities, and follow-ups are managed in Salesforce
  • Success will be measured through adoption of CRM and AI tools and consistent use of workflows.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Business Analytics, Business Technology Management, Management Information Systems with an interest in sales and commercial operations
  • Basic understanding of sales processes (leads, opportunities, conversion)
  • Interest in CRM (Salesforce) and AI-enabled tools, with willingness to learn and explore new capabilities
  • Basic awareness or exposure to Salesforce (or similar CRM) preferred
  • Comfortable working with data to support analysis and learning
  • Detail-oriented with strong problem-solving skills
  • Effective communicator, able to gather, synthesize, and translate feedback
  • Bilingual (English/French) preferred
  • Sales experience considered an asset
