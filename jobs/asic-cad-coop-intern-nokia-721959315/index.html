Full Description

Job Description Position Title: ASIC CAD Co-op/Intern

Number of Position(s): 1

Duration: 4 Months

Date: Sept - Dec 2026

Location: Hybrid- Ottawa, CA

Education Recommendations Currently enrolled in an Electrical/Computer Engineering program at an accredited institution in Canada.

How You Will Contribute And What You Will Learn You’ll be part of an innovative ASIC design team contributing to the development of leading-edge chips, gaining hands-on experience in design automation, synthesis, and rule-checking in a collaborative environment.

You will

  • Collaborate with a world-class ASIC design team, focusing on Coherent Optics market chips.
  • Develop and implement an efficient ASIC flow, ensuring optimal design processes.
  • Create and optimize scripts for design automation, enhancing productivity.
  • Perform synthesis and rule checking, ensuring design integrity and compliance.
  • Assist in various design tasks as needed, contributing to the overall project success.
  • Engage in knowledge sharing and continuous learning within the team.
  • Maintain effective communication with team members and stakeholders.
  • Stay updated with industry trends and advancements in ASIC design.
  • Support the team in achieving project milestones and goals.

Key Skills And Experience You have

  • Proficiency in Python or other programming/scripting languages, with solid hands-on experience.
  • Strong problem-solving skills with a structured, analytical, and detail-oriented mindset
  • Clear and effective verbal and written communication skills, enabling strong collaboration across teams.
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a dynamic, fast-paced environment and adapt to changing priorities.
  • A genuine passion for technology and motivation to contribute to cutting-edge innovations.

Nice to have

  • Academic or practical experience in ASIC design through coursework, projects, or internships.
  • Familiarity with Synopsys Electronic Design Automation (EDA) tools.
  • Knowledge of Verilog or System Verilog for digital design tasks.
  • Exposure to machine learning concepts or neural networks.
