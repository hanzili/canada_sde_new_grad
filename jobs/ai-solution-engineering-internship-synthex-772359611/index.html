About the Role

Design and deliver production AI workflows for clients

About Synthex

Early-stage AI startup solving enterprise financial workflows

Full Description

About Synthex Building production AI for financial services

Synthex is building AI-native enterprise software for financial services, with multiple enterprise clients in production and a first funding round closed. We are past the experiment phase — processing regulated financial data in live, audited systems, with clear expansion ambitions across Europe, Africa, and North America.

This is a rare moment to join early, where your work will shape the platform, standards, and culture of a high-growth company.

The role

What you'll be doing

We are seeking two hands-on solution engineering interns to sit at the intersection of our platform and our clients. Your job is to turn messy, real-world financial workflows into reliable AI solutions built on Synthex.

This is a chance to accelerate faster than usual by working directly on production deployments. You will scope real problems with real clients, design and build the solution on our platform, and watch it go live in audited systems.

Key contributions

Solution design & delivery

Translate client requirements into concrete AI workflows. Design end-to-end solutions on the Synthex platform, configure node-based workflow engines, and own delivery from first prototype to production.

Applied agentic & LLM systems

Compose agentic and multi-step workflows for client use cases — tool calling, retrieval (RAG), and prompting strategies that prioritise determinism and structured outputs validated by strict schemas.

Financial document intelligence

Build solutions that handle challenging financial documents (PDFs, Excel, emails) and complex, imperfect, real-world data and edge cases.

Client-facing engineering

Work alongside clients to validate outputs, iterate quickly on feedback, and harden solutions until they are accurate, auditable, and trusted in production.

Who we're looking for

You are enrolled in a Master's program (or recently graduated), strong in computer science fundamentals, and deeply curious about AI systems beyond surface-level usage. You enjoy solving open-ended problems, communicate clearly, and like seeing your work create direct, measurable client value. You are comfortable learning independently and asking good questions. English (advanced) is required.

Big pluses

  • Personal or non-academic technical projects.
  • Experience with backend systems, APIs, or ML tooling.
  • Comfort working directly with stakeholders and translating fuzzy requirements into working solutions.
  • Interest in system design and real-world constraints.

Culture & benefits

  • Full remote (Europe). We optimise for autonomy and focus; ownership matters more than hours.
  • High-context environment where you are trusted with meaningful responsibility early.
  • Mentorship from experienced engineers and founders.
  • Steep learning curve and exposure to real production AI systems and real clients.
  • Quarterly team retreats.

Why Synthex

  • Build AI solutions that go beyond demos and notebooks.
  • Learn how enterprise AI platforms are built and delivered end to end.
  • Join a company with serious technical and growth ambitions.
  • Shape solutions, and the platform behind them, while they are still evolving.
