About the Role

Develop AI-enabled workflows for sales ops

About Semtech Semtechcareers

Global tech firm pursuing AI-powered workflow automation

Full Description

Location: ​Richmond, BC​ 

Work Arrangement: Hybrid, 4 days on site (Monday to Thursday)  

Job Summary: 

​​Semtech is building the next generation of AI-powered commercial workflows across its global sales organization. This 8-month co-op is a hands-on, production-oriented role embedded within Sales Operations. You will work directly with the Senior Director of Global Sales Operations to design, test, and document AI-enabled workflows that improve efficiency, consistency, and insight across real sales processes. This is not a research role — your work will be used by sellers, distributors, and sales leadership. The ideal candidate is curious, operationally minded, and has a genuine interest in applying AI to solve business problems.  

​ 

​This role is best suited for students who enjoy figuring out how things work and making them work better. You don’t need to be a software engineer — but you do need to be comfortable with data, curious about AI, and motivated by operational impact. If you’ve ever built a workflow to automate something in your own life, used AI tools to solve a real problem, or found yourself thinking ‘there has to be a better way to do this’ — this is for you.  

​​ 

Responsibilities: 

​​AI Workflow Development & Testing 

  • ​Research, design, and build AI-enabled workflow automations targeting repeatable sales operations tasks (e.g., account research, CRM data enrichment, meeting prep, follow-up summaries)

  • ​Test and validate workflows across edge cases to ensure accuracy, reliability, and repeatability before deployment

  • ​Configure AI-assisted summarization, data processing, and exception-based logic steps within approved operational frameworks

  • ​Support prompt engineering and iteration for tools including Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Vinton AI

  • ​Document automation logic, inputs, outputs, and governance notes so solutions can be maintained and reused

​Sales Process & Data Analysis 

  • ​Map existing sales workflows to identify friction points, manual steps, and automation candidates

  • ​Analyze operational and CRM data to surface trends, gaps, and opportunities for AI-assisted improvement

  • ​Assist in building dashboards and reports to track AI tool adoption, usage patterns, and productivity impact

  • ​Support data preparation, cleaning, and validation to ensure high-quality inputs for AI workflows

​Collaboration & Communication 

  • ​Work cross-functionally with Sales Enablement, Salesforce Admins, and commercial team members to gather requirements and validate solutions

  • ​Present findings, workflow designs, and test results to the Sales Operations and AI Ambassador teams and relevant stakeholders

  • ​Contribute to documentation, knowledge base articles, and user guides for deployed AI workflows

  • ​Participate in the AI Ambassador program and support broader AI adoption initiatives across the commercial team

​ 

​What You’ll Gain: 

  • ​Direct exposure to how AI is applied within a real enterprise sales environment — not academic simulations

  • ​Hands-on experience with leading AI tools: Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Vinton AI, and Salesforce-integrated AI agents

  • ​Practical skills in workflow design, operational analytics, and stakeholder communication

  • ​Experience that translates directly into careers in Sales Ops, Revenue Ops, AI Strategy, or Digital Transformation

  • ​Mentorship from a Sales Operations Leader with active responsibility for AI adoption across a global commercial team

​​ 

Minimum Qualifications: 

  • ​​Currently enrolled in a post-secondary program in Business, Business Analytics, Information Systems, Computer Science, or a related field

  • ​Demonstrated interest in AI tools and workflow automation — through coursework, personal projects, or hands-on experimentation

  • ​Basic understanding of sales processes and commercial operations (e.g., lead management, pipeline, CRM usage)

  • ​Strong analytical thinking with the ability to break down operational problems and propose structured solutions

  • ​Proficiency in Microsoft 365 (Excel, SharePoint, Teams); comfort working in data-rich environments

  • ​Clear written and verbal communication skills; able to document technical logic for non-technical audiences

  • ​Ability to work independently with minimal direction and navigate ambiguity

​​ 

Desired Qualifications 

  • ​​Hands-on experience with generative AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, or equivalent) in an applied or academic context

  • ​Programming experience in Python, particularly for data manipulation, automation scripting, or API interaction

  • ​Familiarity with Salesforce CRM — even basic navigation or coursework (Trailhead counts)

  • ​Experience with data visualization tools such as Power BI or Tableau

  • ​Exposure to prompt engineering, LLM-based workflows, or no-code/low-code automation platforms (Zapier, Power Automate, n8n, or similar)

  • ​Knowledge of SQL or experience querying structured datasets

  • ​Prior co-op or internship experience in a business operations, analytics, or technology role

​​
