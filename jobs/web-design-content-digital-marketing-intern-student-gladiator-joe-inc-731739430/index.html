About Gladiator Joe

Gladiator Joe is a Montreal-based manufacturing company specializing in custom sheet metal fabrication, engineering, product development, e-commerce, and industrial manufacturing.

We are looking for a creative and motivated student to help improve our online presence and showcase our products, projects, and capabilities.

Responsibilities

The successful candidate will assist with:

Website Development & Content

  • Improving website content and layout
  • Creating new pages and product descriptions
  • Updating photos, graphics, and content
  • Assisting with SEO and website optimization
  • Researching competitor websites and industry trends

Photography & Videography

  • Taking professional photos of products, manufacturing processes, and completed projects
  • Recording videos for marketing and social media
  • Editing photos and videos for promotional use
  • Creating visual content for the company website

Digital Marketing

  • Assisting with social media content creation
  • Creating marketing materials and promotional content
  • Supporting Amazon and e-commerce marketing initiatives
  • Conducting market and competitor research

Content Creation

  • Writing blog articles and company news updates
  • Creating customer success stories and project showcases
  • Helping develop brochures, presentations, and sales materials

  • WordPress or website management
  • Photography and video editing

  • Hands-on responsibility for company marketing initiatives
  • Portfolio-building opportunities
