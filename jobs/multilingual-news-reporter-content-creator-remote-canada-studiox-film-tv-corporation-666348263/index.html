About the Role

Research, write, translate, publish multilingual news content

About StudioX Film & TV Corporation

Multilingual national news network; onboarding training required

Full Description

About Us

StudioX News is a fast-growing, multilingual digital media network serving diverse communities across Canada. With 34 language platforms including Punjabi, Hindi, Urdu, French, Chinese, Spanish, and more, we are building one of Canada’s most inclusive and scalable news ecosystems.

Position Overview

We are hiring Multilingual News Reporters / Content Creators for all our language platforms. This role is open across Canada as a remote position. However, selected candidates must attend an initial in-person training and onboarding program at our head office in Surrey, BC before transitioning to remote work.

Key Responsibilities

  • Research, write, and publish news articles for your assigned language platform
  • Cover local, national, and international news relevant to your community
  • Translate/adapt content for multilingual audiences
  • Create digital content (articles, short videos, social posts)
  • Maintain accuracy, credibility, and editorial standards
  • Collaborate with central editorial and AI-assisted publishing systems

Languages We Are Hiring For

We are actively hiring across all platforms, including:
English, Punjabi, Hindi, Urdu, French, Arabic, Chinese, Tagalog, Tamil, Bengali, Gujarati, Korean, Vietnamese, Spanish, Marathi, Malayalam, Japanese, Farsi, Amharic, Somali, Polish, Ukrainian, Portuguese, Indonesian, Swahili, Nepali, Tigrinya, Thai, Sinhala, Romanian, Cree, Inuktitut, Ojibwe, Cantonese

Requirements

  • Strong writing and communication skills in at least one target language
  • Basic understanding of journalism, media, or content creation
  • Ability to work independently in a remote environment
  • Willingness to travel to Surrey, BC for initial training
  • Passion for community storytelling and multicultural content

Important Condition (Must Read)

  • This is a Canada-wide remote role ONLY after completing initial training at our Surrey, BC head office
  • Candidates applying from anywhere in Canada must be able to attend this onboarding
  • For candidates based in or near Surrey, BC: role follows the same training requirement

What We Offer

  • Remote work flexibility after onboarding
  • Opportunity to be part of a national multilingual media network
  • Fast growth and leadership opportunities
  • Exposure to AI-powered journalism workflows
  • Portfolio-building and byline credits across platforms
