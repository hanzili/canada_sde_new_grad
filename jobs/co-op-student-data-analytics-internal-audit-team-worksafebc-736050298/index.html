Overview

The Internal Audit department is looking for 2 (two) Data Analytics co-op students who are passionate about applying their knowledge and skills in data analytics, automation, and artificial intelligence to enhance the quality, impact, and relevance of internal audit in a rapidly changing business landscape. You will work closely with data and innovation teams to integrate emerging technologies into our audit engagements and processes under the supervision of the Audit and Analytics Lead.

This role is for a 4-month long work term starting August 31, 2026.

How you’ll make a difference: You’ll help ensure WorkSafeBC is accomplishing its objectives by evaluating and improving the effectiveness of risk management, control, and governance processes.

Where you’ll work

At WorkSafeBC, we offer a hybrid work model that combines working remotely, and in our offices based on the operational needs of the position.

In this role, you will work primarily from our Richmond Office with some flexibility to work from your home in B.C.

What you'll do

On Day 1 in this role, you will get a chance to meet your manager and get acquainted with your team. You will spend time setting up administrative and technological essentials, and completing onboarding tasks, including security and payroll setup.

During your first week you will engage in a comprehensive onboarding program designed to equip you for success, including becoming more familiar with internal audit. You will be partnering and meeting with your manager to discuss your goals and align them with the team’s vision. Additionally, you will learn how our department contributes to the overall business strategy and supports the organization’s mandate. You will also work through the onboarding program alongside your manager for added guidance and support.

This role is ideal for an individual who is eager to step beyond their comfort zone. We are looking for someone with a forward-thinking mindset, strong motivation, and a passion for innovation. The right candidate will possess analytical and critical thinking skills, enabling them to propose ideas that enhance various processes and initiatives to execute audit engagements more efficiently and effectively.

Your daily responsibilities may include the following:

  • Extract and transform data from a variety of sources using tools, such as SQL, Python, Power BI
  • Analyze entire datasets to identify risks, anomalies, trends, and potential control failures, and support continuous monitoring
  • Automate repetitive audit tasks to enhance productivity (e.g., data preparation, repeatable tests, and draft workpaper/report outputs) by building and maintaining reproducible scripts, prompts, and templates in line with established documentation and quality standards
  • Provide data visualization through dashboards to provide deeper and actionable insights
  • Collaborate with other WorkSafeBC departments to implement artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies to enhance audit efficiency and effectiveness
  • Share learnings and provide hands-on support to Internal Audit team members when using analytics and automation tools (e.g., explaining outputs, documenting steps, and helping with tool adoption)
  • Perform other related duties as required

Is this a good fit for you?

We’re looking for someone who:

  • Communicates effectively, both in writing and verbally
  • Enjoys problem-solving and analyzing data (unstructured and structured) to deliver actionable insights
  • Demonstrates tact and discretion when handling confidential data
  • Effectively collaborates and interacts with a range of stakeholders
  • Demonstrates teamwork and organization skills, and is detail-oriented
  • Is curious, receptive to learning and willing to ask questions

Your background and experience

  • Currently enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s degree in business analytics, data science, or related discipline
  • Working knowledge of data analytics and visualization tools, such as SQL, Python, Power BI, and Tableau.
  • Exposure to artificial intelligence and advanced data analytics and visualization tools (e.g., large language models, machine learning, automation, or prompt-based tools) through coursework, projects, and/or co-op/internship experience
  • Exposure to internal audit, risk management and internal control
  • We’ll consider equivalent combinations of education and experience.

Who we are

At WorkSafeBC, we promote safe and healthy workplaces across British Columbia. We partner with workers and employers to save lives and prevent injury, disease, and disability. When work-related injuries or diseases occur, we provide compensation and support injured workers in their recovery, rehabilitation, and safe return to work. We’re honoured to serve the 2.49 million workers and 263,000 registered employers in our province.

What’s it like to work at WorkSafeBC?

It’s challenging, stimulating, and rewarding. Our positions offer diversity and opportunities for professional growth. Every day, the work we do impacts people and changes lives. What we do is important, and so are the people we do it for.

Our ability to make a difference relies on building a team with a rich variety of skills, knowledge, backgrounds, abilities, and experiences that reflects the diversity of the people we serve. We are committed to fostering a welcoming, inclusive, and supportive work culture where everyone can contribute as their best, authentic self.
