About Clear Health Solutions

Full Description

Company Overview

Clear Health Solutions is dedicated to supporting families across Canada by providing personalized intervention services that enable children and youth to thrive within their homes and communities. Our mission is rooted in fostering growth, development, and independence for the individuals we serve.
Summary

We are seeking a Junior Program Advisor to join our team in Canada. Based in, this role is vital in helping us deliver impactful support to families, ensuring children and youth can remain in their homes and communities. If you are passionate about community service and making a difference, we want to hear from you.
Responsibilities

  • Assist in developing and implementing family support programs tailored to community needs.
  • Engage with families through effective communication and support strategies.
  • Support the recruitment and onboarding of new team members.
  • Conduct educational sessions and workshops to empower families.
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to ensure comprehensive support.
  • Maintain accurate records of program activities and outcomes.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives within the organization.
  • Qualifications
  • Excellent public speaking skills to effectively communicate with families and team members.
  • Experience or familiarity with Ellucian systems is a plus.
  • Proven recruiting experience or knowledge of hiring processes.
  • Strong teaching abilities with a passion for education and community development.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a collaborative team.
  • Compassionate attitude with a commitment to supporting families.
  • Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail.
  • Bilingual abilities are considered an asset but are not mandatory.
  • Call-To-Action

Join us at Clear Health Solutions and be part of a dedicated team making meaningful impacts in families' lives. If you’re ready to contribute your skills to a compassionate organization, we encourage you to apply today—your journey toward making a difference starts here!

Pay: From $21.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Casual dress
  • Extended health care
  • Mileage reimbursement
  • On-site parking

Work Location: In person
