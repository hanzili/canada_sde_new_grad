Full Description

Position: IP Routing App Engineer Co-op/Intern
Number of Position(s): 2
Duration: 4 months+
Date: Sep – Dec 2026
Location: On-site in Ottawa, Ontario

EDUCATIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS

Currently a candidate for bachelor’s or master's degree in network technology, Computer/Software/Electrical/Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, or related field with an accredited school in Canada. 

  • Acquire a deep understanding of IP/MPLS networks
  • Obtain hands-on experience on the industry-leading 7750 Service Routers and 7250/7220 IXR Data Center Routers
  • Assist team members with the physical deployment and cabling of network topologies
  • Develop enhanced techniques in the troubleshooting and reproduction of real-world issues

  • Experience teamwork in a fast-paced environment and get exposure to Customer Issue Management

  • An interest in working with the industry-leading 7x50 series of carrier-grade service routers, in learning how IP/MPLS networks are built, and in how the Internet works

  • Critical thinking, troubleshooting and problem-solving skills
  • Attention to details and excellent communication skills to provide written and verbal explanations to customers and internal Nokia teams
  • Good organizational and time management skills with the ability to prioritize between tasks

It would be nice if you also had:

  • Possess a certification in the Nokia Service Routing Certification (SRC) Program
  • Knowledge of IP/MPLS and routing protocols such as OSPF, IS-IS, BGP, etc.
  • Experience in IP network troubleshooting techniques
  • Experience working in a Linux/Unix environment, including automation via Bash/Shell scripting and Python programming
