Full Description

Junior Laboratory Technician (R&D, high-pressure systems)

·      3-month, part-time contractual position with potential for extension

·      Location:  Edmonton, Alberta, Canada (on-site)

·      Compensation: $22.50+/hr, depending on skills and experience

About the Role

SCFCan Inc. is an Edmonton-based startup focused on supercritical fluid processes for the recovery of valuable compounds from natural and industrial feedstocks. We operate a small-scale R&D laboratory using high-pressure systems to explore and optimize these processes.

We are hiring a Junior Laboratory Technician to support our team. This position is designed for individuals early in their technical career who are interested in gaining hands-on experience with specialized laboratory and high-pressure equipment in a supervised environment.

Key Responsibilities

·      Assist in the operation of bench-scale high-pressure equipment for supercritical fluid processes

·      Assist with troubleshooting equipment and process issues

·      Assist with maintenance, adjustments, and minor modifications of lab equipment

·      Perform end-of-run cleanup, waste handling, and lab organization

·      Prepare and analyze experimental samples

·      Maintain clear and accurate experimental records and documentation

·      Update and maintain standard operating procedures and safety documentation

Education and Experience

·      Background in laboratory work through education, research, and/or employment

·      No prior experience with high-pressure process equipment is required but would be an advantage

·      Completion of a laboratory technician/technologist or related program is considered an asset

Candidates with non-traditional or diverse career pathways who have the required aptitude and interest are welcome to apply.

Skills

·      Willingness and ability to learn operation of high-pressure equipment in a supervised setting

·      Willingness and ability to learn operation of analytical instrumentation

·      Mechanical aptitude and ability to troubleshoot laboratory equipment and experimental processes

·      Strong attention to detail and ability to follow standard operating procedures

·      Commitment to safe laboratory practices

·      Ability to learn quickly and apply training

·      Strong organizational skills and ability to maintain accurate experimental records and ensure a clean, organized workspace

·      Proficiency with spreadsheets and word processing software for documentation and reporting

What You Will Learn

·      Safe operation of a high-pressure supercritical fluid extraction system

·      Hands-on experimental design and execution in an R&D environment

·      Sample preparation, processing, and documentation workflows

·      Practical troubleshooting of laboratory and experimental equipment

Additional Information

This is a hands-on experimental role in a startup environment. Candidates who are adaptable, safety-conscious, and motivated to learn will do well. If you are interested in this role but unsure whether you meet every qualification, we encourage you to apply. We also encourage applications from individuals of diverse backgrounds, including those traditionally underrepresented in the STEM fields.

Interested? Email careers@scfcan.ca, including your resume and a cover letter highlighting your fit for the position. We will review applications and interview suitable candidates on an ongoing basis. This job posting will remain open until the position is filled.
