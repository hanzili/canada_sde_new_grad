About Kensington

Global travel experiences provider with diverse catalog

Full Description

Kensington delivers the world’s most personal travel experiences. We bring to life each client’s desire to travel in a way that’s tailored specifically for them, in the company of local private guides who ensure that they enjoy the authentic best of their destination. The result is a unique journey rich in memories that last a lifetime!

We achieve this because of our extraordinary people, passionate experts with deep knowledge of their destinations. They are what enables us to deliver remarkable experiences in over 120 countries worldwide, and across each of our businesses – Private Touring, Cruises, Villas, Yachts, Expeditions, Ultraluxe Land, Private Jets, and Air.

To learn more about Kensington, visit kensingtontours.com.

About the Role

We are seeking a detail-oriented and analytical intern to support our Product Management and Merchandising teams. This role will focus on the Global Catalogue/Product Information Management (PIM) initiative and broader product operations projects. The successful candidate will work with large datasets, leverage AI tools to improve efficiency, and contribute to the organization and optimization of product information at scale.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the Global Catalogue/PIM initiative by structuring, mapping, and maintaining large volumes of product data.
  • Use AI tools to assist with auto-tagging, categorization, and attribution of product records against established taxonomy standards.
  • Conduct competitor research and audits, collecting, organizing, and analyzing data to identify trends and opportunities.
  • Assist with product merchandising activities, including data validation, quality assurance, and content organization.
  • Support recurring merchandising tasks and ad hoc product operations projects as required.
  • Identify opportunities to improve processes through automation and AI-enabled workflows.

Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled in or recently graduated from a relevant program such as Business, Commerce, Marketing, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Tourism, or a related field.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Highly organized with strong attention to detail.
  • Comfortable working with large datasets and spreadsheets.
  • Interest in product management, merchandising, e-commerce, or data operations.
  • Familiarity with AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar) and enthusiasm for applying them to business processes.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Excel and/or Google Sheets.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and work independently in a fast-paced environment.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with data management, taxonomy, content management systems, or product information systems.
  • Exposure to data analysis, research, or process improvement projects.
  • Interest in travel, e-commerce, or digital marketplaces.

What You'll Gain

  • Hands-on experience with large-scale product catalogue and PIM initiatives.
  • Practical experience using AI tools in a business environment.
  • Exposure to product management, merchandising, and data operations.
  • Opportunity to work on impactful projects and contribute to process improvements across the business.
