About Eclipse Automation

Full Description

Job Title: Software Developer Co-op

Location: Cambridge, ON | On-site

Job Type: Full-time

Compensation: $27.00 - $29.00 / hr

Get to Know Us:
Eclipse Automation delivers cutting-edge custom automated manufacturing solutions across multiple industries. We combine advanced automation expertise with digital innovation to create smart, efficient, and sustainable manufacturing systems. Our global network includes facilities in Canada, the United States and Hungary ensuring regional expertise and global strength.

The Position:
The Software Developer Co-op will support the Development & User Acceptance Testing (UAT) of our cutting-edge Omniverse extensions, connectors, and applications. The incumbent will work with advanced technology in the areas of digital twins, AI, and custom automation, contributing to the digital transformation of manufacturing processes. The Software Developer Co-op will work with internal stakeholders to deliver the Eclipse Vision and the Eclipse Entrepreneurial Culture.

What You’ll Be Doing:

  • Perform user acceptance testing (UAT) for Omniverse extension, connectors, and applications
  • Learn and use Azure DevOps to provide feedback on tasks, create bug reports, track progress and communicate with the development team
  • Participate in daily sprint sessions, providing input and feedback, and addressing clarifying questions from developers.
  • Aid in development of Omniverse extensions as required (Python, C++, .USD).
  • Conduct basic troubleshooting of Omniverse extensions and connectors
  • Work requires professional written and verbal communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work in a team environment and actively participate in group meetings safely
  • Work requires willingness to work a flexible schedule including weekends and or travel as required in varying timelines
  • Ensure scheduled milestones are met and notify appropriate departments if milestones are not met
  • Adhere to the Eclipse Project Operating Policy
  • Maintain good corporate citizen role in support of the Eclipse corporate Vision
  • Ensure that all business activities are performed with the highest ethical standards and in compliance with the Eclipse Automation Inc. Conduct Code
  • Foster the development of a culture reflective of the core values of the business

What We’re Looking For:

  • Successful enrolment in a post-secondary degree/diploma, preferably in the Computer Science, Engineering or related program.
  • Familiarity with Python, knowledge of Universal Scene Description (.USD) &NVIDIA Omniverse is a plus
  • Familiarity with DCC Software, CAD, and/or spatial computing
  • Familiarity with or willingness to learn Azure DevOps for development pipelines, task management and bug tracking.
  • Ability to work within the fast-paced environment, and proven ability to multitask
  • Team player and able to coordinate activities with other departments
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills.
  • Students should be enrolled in a post-secondary academic program requiring a co-op/work term.

What We Offer:

  • Hands-on experience working for one of the largest and fastest growing automation companies on the globe
  • Access to Eclipse’s network of industry professionals who will support and coach you
  • Variety - our Co-ops love that every day is different
  • Opportunities to work on projects for highly recognized brands
  • Free snacks
  • Fun events and celebrations throughout the year
  • Potential full-time work after you graduate

Why Join Us:
Here at Eclipse Automation, creation and innovation are at our core, and our culture reflects that. Fast paced and continuously fostering growth, we encourage the entrepreneurial spirit that our teams embody, and leverage each of their strengths to do better for our people, our clients, and our communities.

Our teams are made up of innovators, thinkers, and doers, with the know-how and expertise to exceed expectations, and the relentless spirit to create and reinvent. Our people are what enable us to live up to our name – to eclipse all others, inspiring and creating outcomes that change the world.

Eclipse Automation is committed to creating an accessible and inclusive work environment. It is our policy to recruit and select applicants solely on the basis of their qualifications. We are an equal opportunity employer that hires talent regardless of age, race, creed, colour, religion, national origin, ancestry, marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, disability, or any other status or condition protected by applicable law.
