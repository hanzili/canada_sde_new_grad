About Genesys

Full Description

Be the one building AI-powered experiences where they matter most

At Genesys, we help organizations create better customer experiences through AI-powered experience orchestration. Our platform connects people, systems, data and AI to help organizations deliver more personalized service, improve operational efficiency and build stronger customer relationships.

Help build, support and operate technology used by more than 8,000 organizations in over 100 countries – moving AI from possibility to production in real-world enterprise environments every day.

The Workforce Engagement Management (WEM) QA Team is looking for an enthusiastic individual that is dedicated to learning and contributing to continued innovation in areas that provide solutions in our testing framework to maintain the quality of our ever-growing cloud-native products. You will have an opportunity to develop high quality automation tests for both our UI and backend microservices. It will be a fast-paced environment that will focus on continuous change, innovation and experimentation.

As a part of the intern momentum in our Canada region, you will be a key member of our team, learning about many aspects of agile testing methodologies and continuous delivery (CD) including design, implementation, testing, bug fixing and test automation. There is no limit to what you can accomplish in the internship.

Responsibilities: 

In this role, the primary responsibilities will include (but are not limited to):

  • Design and develop high-quality automated tests

  • Explore innovative technologies to support continuous improvement

  • Sustain quality by demonstrating ownership in your work

  • Learning how Gamification can engage employees and ideating test coverage use cases

  • Regularly collaborate in person at the Toronto office

Qualifications:

  • Actively enrolled in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or related disciplines
  • Able to commit to a 12mo Co-op Program

  • Show passion within school and/or personal projects

  • Self-motivated, pro-active, dynamic team player with a positive attitude that enjoys collaborating with others

  • Willingness to learn and pick up new technologies

  • Solid communication skills

  • Experience with software engineering in Java (or similar OOP languages)

  • Understands that QA is an integral part of development, not an activity at the end of the process.

We use the following technology stack:

  • Java, Kotlin, Python, RESTful API, Jenkins, Git, Bitbucket, New Relic, JQL (for JIRA), Confluence, Sumo Logic,  Selenium, Playwright, TestNG, Maven, AWS
