Full Description

**At Beedie, we foster talent, encourage innovation, and reward success.

We’re always looking for like-minded people to join our team and share our passion for doing the best work of our lives. Our approach is simple: we work hard, we have fun, and we make a difference. And, we’ve been recognized as a top employer provincially and nationally. Our people are the heart and soul of our organization. If you are passionate, hardworking, ambitious, and want to be part of a dynamic and growing company that will take your career forward, join us.

At Beedie, we believe co-op students are an important part of our team. As a Finance Co-op Student, you'll gain hands-on experience in financial reporting, corporate accounting, and period-end reporting activities while contributing to a variety of projects. You'll work alongside experienced professionals, including a large team of designated accountants, who are committed to providing mentorship and guidance throughout your term. The objective of the finance team is to accurately capture, report, and analyze financial and non-financial information to provide the business with exceptional decision support. This is an excellent opportunity to develop practical skills, build your professional network, and gain exposure to how finance supports strategic business decisions.

This opportunity is available to students seeking an 8-month co-op term beginning in September 2026 .

Key Responsibilities

  • Recording daily deposits and cash transactions into the accounting system

  • Preparation of routine and ad-hoc journal entries

  • Preparation and analysis of bank reconciliations including the investigating and resolving reconciling items

  • Assisting with the month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close processes, including preparing supporting schedules, reconciliations, and working papers

  • Assisting in compiling financial reporting packages, including assisting in preparation of financial statement files and working papers

  • Supporting various ad-hoc accounting projects

  • Performing clerical and administrative duties to support the finance function

Desired Skills and Experience

  • Currently enrolled in a university degree or college diploma program in accounting or a related field

  • Computer Skills: Microsoft Office experience with intermediate Excel skills

  • Effective organizational and time management skills

  • Effective written and verbal communication skills

  • Strong work ethic, attention to detail, and commitment to accuracy

  • Proactive self-starter who takes ownership of assigned responsibilities and follows through on commitments

  • Ability to prioritize work and manage multiple tasks as part of a team or independently

  • Team player who wants to help and contribute to a team environment

  • Comfortable and confident in asking questions, seeking guidance, and feedback
