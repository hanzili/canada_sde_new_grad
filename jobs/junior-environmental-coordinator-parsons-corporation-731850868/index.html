Job Description Parsons is looking for an amazingly talented Junior Environmental Coordinator to join our team! In this role you will get to support our Project Managers, Field Technicians and Qualified Persons by applying technical and soft skills on environmental database and data management, project management, and field programs of the environmental industry, which will serve as a steppingstone in advancing their career.

What You'll Be Doing The position is entry-level and requires active learning, engagement, and multi-tasking in a fast-paced consulting environment. In this position, the right candidate will learn the technical and soft skills, environmental database and data management, project management, and field programs of the environmental industry, which will serve as a steppingstone in advancing their career. Our team is looking for the right personality and attitude: eager to learn and communicate, up for challenges, curious, self-driven and motivated.

The Junior Environmental Coordinator will work on projects such as Contaminated Land Assessments and Management Programs (Phase I and II ESAs), Remediations, Excess Soil management, and other environmental management plans for infrastructure projects.

  • Processing, uploading, quality checking and presenting environmental data in reports, tables, figures and dashboards by working with a sql database EQuIS to support environmental investigations and its geographical, numerical, monitoring and chemical data.
  • Using ArcGIS Pro to create routine and custom figures for environmental site assessments.
  • Managing environmental, chemical, hydrogeological, geographical and opensource data within ArcGIS Pro.
  • Completing utility locates through OneCall.
  • Completing Phase I Searches.
  • Drafting borehole logs.
  • Formatting and finalizing technical letters, memorandums, reports and health and safety plans using Word and excel.
  • Managing end-user platforms (i.e. SharePoint) and mobile applications (i.e. Appenate, Field Maps) for digital collection of environmental data.
  • Being in the forefront of innovative digital solutions to improve data collection and visualization, and project delivery.
  • Proactively asking questions and calling external parties including cities, subcontractors and vendors to problem-solve.
  • Supporting office administrative tasks like managing laboratory deliveries.
  • Working in project teams of 45 people including field personnel, project coordinators, project managers, draftspersons and technical staff using interactive, critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

What Required Skills You'll Bring

  • University degree in environmental science or engineering, earth sciences, or other related programs.
  • Experience with data management and queries, coding languages and/or AI applications.
  • Working knowledge of ArcGIS Pro.
  • Working knowledge with Microsoft products Word, Excel, Powerpoint, PowerBI, Outlook, and Teams. Skills in using styles and cross-referencing in Word and Excel formulas.
  • Relevant academic, software and work experience may also be considered as equivalent for the right candidate with experience in similar operations.
  • Demonstrated ability to work independently and as a team.
  • Excellent written and oral communications skills and ability to operate self-sufficiently.

What Desired Skills You'll Bring

  • Working knowledge of AutoCAD an asset.
  • Experience working with environmental or scientific databases (e.g., EQuIS, SQL, or similar platforms) is an asset.
  • A valid driver’s license with experience in driving and a clean driving record.
  • Demonstrated presentation skills to present information to varied audiences and advanced writing skills to prepare detailed report/materials of varying complexity.
