Financial Accountant (Trainee) Vernon, British Columbia

Our Financial Accountant (Trainee) is someone with a passion for numbers and a drive to support Tolko’s strategic vision by providing accurate and timely financial analysis and reporting . With a collaborative spirit and attention to detail, they play a vital role in building relationships and supporting business objectives through sound financial practices. If you are ready to take the first step in your accounting career and grow with us, read on to discover more.

We’re seeking a Financial Accountant (Trainee) to join our head office located in Vernon, British Columbia . This position supports the practical experience requirements to become a CPA.   Our head office houses Tolko’s corporate services groups including Sales, Marketing & Logistics, Finance, Accounting & Credit, IT, Procurement and Human Resources.

Our ideal candidate has the following qualifications:

  • Enrolled in or working towards a professional accounting designation (CPA) .
  • Strong understanding of general accounting principles and cost accounting .
  • Experience working with Microsoft Office .
  • Exceptional analytical, interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Detail oriented with strong organizational and time management skills .
  • Proven ability to think logically and solve problems effectively.
  • Collaborative and proactive thinking.
  • Possess a high level of integrity and confidentiality .
  • Experience working with JDE financial systems and/or other financial ERP systems is considered an asset.
  • General knowledge of the forest industry is an asset.

We value diversity at Tolko and know that experience and knowledge come in many forms. Even if you don't have all the qualifications above, please apply. We would love to hear your story and have you grow with us.

In this role, you will:

  • Prepare month end financial statements, journal vouchers, variance analysis, fixed asset and trend reporting on a consistent and accurate basis.
  • Create and maintain f inancial statements and analytical reports as required.
  • Calculate and/or review related accruals and payment vouchers .
  • Analyze assigned balance sheet accounts .
  • Prepare external audit working papers .
  • Prepare ad-hoc analysis and reports in a timely manner in response to queries from interested parties.
  • Prepare government and external reporting requirements (e.g. Statistics Canada and industry surveys) as required.
  • Assist and back up Financial Accountants as needed.
  • Respond to requests and provide analysis/information to support leadership, operations sectors or departments.
  • Build trusted relationships with key business partners.

So, what’s in it for you?

  • You’ll take on an  exciting new challenge  with  supportive team members.
  • You’ll earn a competitive salary based on your skills and experience. The expected base salary range for this position is between $60,000 - $80,000 per year.
  • You will be eligible to participate in Tolko’s  bonus program.
  • Competitive benefits and retirement plans come standard, so you don’t have to choose between your bottom line and wellbeing or your future.
  • Continued development through on the job and classroom training and reimbursement of tuition and membership fees.
  • A positive , innovative , and inclusive work environment.

The North Okanagan Valley includes the communities of Vernon, Armstrong, Lavington, Lumby and Lake Country, British Columbia.  Surrounded by lakes, these communities provide family-friendly living with lots of amenities and beautiful weather.
