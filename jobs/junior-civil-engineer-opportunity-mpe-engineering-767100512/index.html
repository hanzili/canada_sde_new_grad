About the Role

Design and manage water resources projects

About MPE ENGINEERING

Water resources engineering firm with strong client focus

Full Description

  • ID No: 14-1553
  • Location: Sudbury

Description:

The successful candidate will take an important role in design and project management within MPE’s Water Resources team. The position requires a broad range of skills in a wide variety of water resource projects. We are open to applicants across Ontario, but preference will be given to those in Sudbury.

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree from an accredited University in Civil or Environmental Engineering,
  • Registration with PEO, or another provincial regulator, as an Engineer (P.Eng., or E.I.T.),
  • 0 to 4 years of experience in project management, design, hydraulics, and construction contract administration in the following areas of practice is desired:
  • Hydropower development, water management, water control structures, dam safety, flood mitigation, drainage, pipelines, irrigation development and river engineering.
  • Self-motivated, with good leadership, project management and client management experience; and possesses excellent written and verbal communication skills

Responsibilities

  • Management of projects, contract administration, construction management, and design engineering,
  • Prepare planning reports, design drawings, specifications, and contract documents,
  • Perform construction administration and general construction supervision duties,
  • Work closely with our clients to ensure their project objectives are met.

Interested individuals should submit their résumés and cover letters to:

Jeff Olitch, P.Eng.

VP Water Resource Engineering

We thank all applicants in advance for submitting a resume, but only potential interview candidates will be contacted.
