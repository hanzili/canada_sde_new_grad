About E3 Laboratories Inc.

Full Description

Company Description

E3 Laboratories Inc. E3 Laboratories specializes in environmental analytical services, analyzing drinking water, wastewater, sludge, bio-solids, and soil samples.

Located in Niagara-on-the-Lake, we provide services to customers all over Ontario. Our clients range from private, municipal, regional, large industrial and governmental organizations.

Our mission is to deliver rapid and accurate results, at a reasonable cost to our clients.

Job Description

Please note: We are currently only accepting applicants with min. 2 years of experience in a similar position. Please do not apply if you do not meet this requirement.

You will be joining our team of lab technicians to conduct testing for inorganics including but not limited to BOD / HACH / COD / pH / TSS / Oil & Grease.

Your tasks will include:

  • Organizing and preparing samples for testing
  • Performing inorganic laboratory testing
  • Data entry and reporting test results in LIMS
  • Using standard laboratory practices and procedures, QA/QC procedures, and other applicable standards when carrying out your work
  • Assisting fellow technicians as and when needed
  • Ensuring the proper function of your equipment
  • Maintaining a clean and organized workspace

Qualifications

  • Equivalent to 2-year Laboratory Technician College Degree
  • Minimum 2 years of experience in a similar position
  • Excellent organizational skills
  • Good communication skills – ability to understand and execute instructions and read and understand technical instructions
  • Good knowledge of Windows and Microsoft Office
  • Team player with a positive attitude
  • Willingness to learn
  • Must have own transportation

We are currently only accepting applicants with min. 2 years of experience in a similar position. Please do not apply if you do not meet this requirement.
