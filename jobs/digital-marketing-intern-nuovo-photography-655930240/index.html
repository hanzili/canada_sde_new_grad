About the Role

Assist content creation and social media campaigns

About Nuovo Photography

Art photography brand focused on empowerment

Full Description

Nuovo Artistic Studios is the premier destination for all things self-love. A by-women company and one of the largest artistic photography companies in North America, Nuovo is on a mission to empower individuals by fostering confidence and self-expression while celebrating intimacy and individuality.

We are searching for a Digital Marketing Intern to join our fast-growing marketing team. This opportunity is ideal for someone who lives and breathes pop culture, keeps up with the latest social media trends and is eager to build and develop their marketing skills. You will have the opportunity to contribute to campaigns and help bring our brand to life across social media platforms. If you’re creative, organized, and enjoy turning ideas into compelling content, this role is for you.

This Internship will take place from May 4th, 2026 to August 28th, 2026

What You’ll Do:

  • Assist in planning, creating and publishing content across platforms (Instagram, Facebook, Tik Tok, Linkedin, Youtube, and more)
  • Support the development of creative ideas aligned with social media campaigns and brand image
  • Collaborate with the marketing team on filming, editing and writing content
  • Help with maintaining and organizing the social media calendar

Requirements

About You:

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree/diploma in Marketing, Communications, or a related field
  • Strong interest  in social media, content creating and digital marketing
  • Creative mindset, curiosity and attention to detail
  • Eagerness to learn, take initiative, and grow within a collaborate team
  • Experience with social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Tik Tok) and tools like CapCut, Google Suite, Airtable or similar platforms is an asset
  • Fluent in English and French is considered a strong asset

Benefits

What We Offer:

  • Inspiring Work Environment: Immerse yourself in a creative, collaborative company where artistry and empowerment converge.
  • Growth Opportunities: Be part of a fast-growing company committed to nurturing talent and fostering career development.
  • Recognition and Support: A team that values your contributions and celebrates your success.
  • Exclusive Perks: Access to Nuovo’s services and products, ensuring you, too, can experience the power of portraiture. We also provide you with a 50% Family & Friends discount.

About Us:

Nuovo Artistic Studios is not just a photography studio; it’s a movement designed to uplift, empower, and transform. Our mission is to provide every guest with a luxury experience that inspires confidence and celebrates individuality. With studios across North America, we are redefining the art of modern portraiture, one empowered story at a time.

This is your opportunity to join a team dedicated to excellence and artistry. Become part of the Nuovo legacy and make an impact, one unforgettable moment at a time.

This is your moment. Apply today!
