About Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment Partnership

Leading Canadian sports & entertainment organization

Full Description

Powered by Passion. United by Purpose. Build for Impact.

At Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment Partnership (MLSE), we exist to deliver the ultimate fan experience by lifting trophies, spirits, and communities – united as one.

We're more than a workplace. We are a team of passionate people, boldly building the future of sport and entertainment, together. We believe in the power of play, the strength of collaboration, and the energy that comes from showing up with purpose.

From the ice to the pitch, the hardwood to the digital arena, we’re proud to be the driving force behind the Toronto Maple Leafs (NHL), the Toronto Raptors (NBA), Toronto FC (MLS), Toronto Argonauts (CFL) and development teams with the Toronto Marlies (AHL), Raptors 905 (NBA G League), Toronto FC II (MLS NEXT Pro League) and Raptors Uprising Gaming Club, the Toronto Raptors Esports franchise in the NBA 2K League.

We bring these teams – and world-class entertainment – to life at our iconic venues, including Scotiabank Arena, BMO Field, Coca-Cola Coliseum, Ford Performance Centre, BMO Training Ground, and OVO Athletic Centre. Off the field, we serve up elevated dining at e11even, Real Sports, and our signature club spaces like Hot Stove Club, ScotiaClub, and Platinum Club.

Through MLSE Foundation and MLSE LaunchPad, we use the power of sport to help youth facing barriers reach their full potential. Since 2009, we’ve invested more than $45 million into Ontario communities - and we’re just getting started.

This is what it means to be One MLSE: a culture where everyone plays a role, everyone belongs, and everyone contributes to something bigger than themselves.

So, if you're ready to play with purpose, grow with passion, and win as one, we’d love to have you on our team.

** We know that great candidates come from a variety of backgrounds and experiences. Even if you do not meet every qualification listed, we encourage you to apply. Your unique perspective, transferable skills, and lived experience may be exactly what we’re looking for. **

Job Description

This role allows you to be part of the MLSE Corporate Communications Team and support the group’s vast public relations and influencer strategy portfolio.

As a valuable member of the MLSE Communications team, you will assist with public relations and influencer relations for all Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment’s brands (Toronto Maple Leafs, Toronto Raptors, Toronto FC, Toronto Argonauts, MLSE Live, MLSE Foundation, and MLSE LaunchPad) and work internally with all departments on various events and campaigns.

MLSE's Internship term dates are from September 8, 2026 - December 18, 2026

  • Complete daily media monitoring for the Communications team.
  • Provide support at PR events and press conferences for all MLSE brands.
  • Work on communications initiatives for Leafs, Raptors, Toronto FC, Toronto Argonauts, Marlies & Raptors 905.
  • Research and build media and influencer/content creator lists for partnership opportunities.
  • Draft media pitches and influencer/content creator pitches for PR campaigns.
  • Compile coverage reports and ongoing results reports for media and influencer initiatives.
  • Provide event and PR support at community events for each MLSE team.

Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled in a University or College degree, diploma, or certificate in a communications, media, journalism, public relations, or related field.
  • Currently enrolled in a post-secondary co-op or internship program.
  • Thorough knowledge of Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and proficient at learning new software and technology.
  • Understanding of and familiarity with social media.
  • Proficient with Microsoft Office products, including Excel, PowerPoint, and Word.
  • A sound understanding of the sports industry and the Canadian sports landscape is an asset.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills; strong editing and proofreading abilities.
  • Solid time management and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to multitask and work independently with a high degree of accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Excellent organizational skills and the ability to meet deadlines.
  • Able to always exercise diplomacy and tact.
  • Available to work evenings, including weekends and holidays.
