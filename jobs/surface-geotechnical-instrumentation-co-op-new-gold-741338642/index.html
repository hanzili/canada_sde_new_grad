About the Role

Support geotechnical instrumentation installation and data review

About New Gold

All-North American precious metals producer

Full Description

Make an impact at an all-North American precious metals producer. New Gold is now part of Coeur Mining. Visit www.coeur.com to learn more.

Coeur Mining attracts and hires highly skilled individuals. By joining our team, you can look forward to:

  • A stimulating values-based work environment
  • A culture of collaboration and inclusion
  • Inspiring colleagues and approachable leaders
  • Career development opportunities
  • A deeply rooted commitment to responsible mining, health and safety and community engagement
  • A competitive total compensation program
  • A comprehensive benefits package including a retirement savings plan

About Coeur Mining:

Coeur Mining is a growing, all-North American precious metals producer with seven operations: the Las Chispas silver-gold mine and Palmarejo gold-silver complex in Mexico, the Rochester silver-gold mine in Nevada, the Kensington gold mine in Alaska, the Wharf gold mine in South Dakota, the Rainy River gold mine in Ontario, Canada, and the New Afton copper-gold mine in British Columbia, Canada. In addition, Coeur owns the Silvertip silver-zinc-lead exploration project in British Columbia.

Coeur offers a flexible, hybrid work model and a culture built on safety, collaboration, and real opportunities to develop your career for those who want to grow alongside a company that is doing the same.

New Afton

Situated 10 kilometers west of Kamloops, British Columbia, the New Afton mine is an underground gold and copper producing site.

Kamloops is a city of 105,000 people, located 350 kilometers east of Vancouver. The area provides the opportunity to live and work in a vibrant community with its own university and within easy reach of world class ski resorts, championship golf courses, mountain biking trails, popular recreational lakes and award-winning vineyards.

About the Role

Schedule: 5x2, Monday – Friday (8h) or 4x3, Monday - Thurs (10h)

Position Reports To: Dam Safety EIT

Position Overview
We currently have an exciting opportunity for a student to join our New Afton team for an 8-month work term commencing in September 2026. This position will provide support to the Surface Geotechnical team.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prioritizing safety in the planning and execution of all job responsibilities.
  • Support regular collection of surface subsidence instrumentation data.
  • Assist with installation and repairs of new and existing instrumentation.
  • Review monitoring data results.
  • Support development and implementation of new surface geotechnical instrumentation initiatives.
  • Support installation of geotechnical instrumentation.
  • Assist with research and implementation of new monitoring technologies.
  • Gain exposure to block cave gold mining and Owner/Contractor relations

Qualifications

  • Enrolled in a University engineering or technical diploma program. Preference given to candidates enrolled in geotechnical, geological, civil or mining engineering or related technical program.
  • Excellent interpersonal skills.
  • Demonstrated leadership skills.
  • A business focus with an emphasis on safety.
  • Past exposure to tailings or construction environments will be considered an asset.
  • Applicants must have access to personal transportation.

Other Requirements

Candidates will be required to participate in a comprehensive qualification process, including:

  • Successful completion of pre-placement functional screen
  • Provide proof of a valid Class 5 driver’s license
  • Demonstrate eligibility to work in Canada
  • Provide confirmation of valid educational requirements

Compensation

Anticipated salary for this position is $52,000.00 - $68,640.00. This is not a camp operation and therefore the successful candidate will be required to relocate to the Kamloops area or travel and accommodation will be the responsibility of the successful applicant.

To Apply

Interested candidates are encouraged to submit their cover letter and resume outlining their education, experience, and qualifications through our website at www.newgold.com in Word or PDF format no later than 11:59pm on July 9, 2026.
