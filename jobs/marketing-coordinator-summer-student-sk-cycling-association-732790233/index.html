About SK Cycling Association

Full Description

Job Summary
We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Marketing Coordinator- Summer Student to join our dynamic marketing team. This internship provides an excellent opportunity for students interested in digital marketing, e-commerce, and product management to gain hands-on experience. The ideal candidate will assist in developing and executing marketing strategies across various channels, including social media, email campaigns, and website management. This role offers exposure to SEO, Google Analytics, Google AdWords, Facebook Advertising, and other digital marketing tools, fostering skills essential for a future career in marketing.

Responsibilities

  • ■ Maintains the organization’s website to enhance communication, policies, news, events, and program information, etc.

■ Maintains the organization’s social media interests to enhance communication.

■ Generates electronic communications (email, website, social media) for the membership.

■ Facilitates the development and implementation of SCA communication and marketing initiatives.

■ Collaborates with the SCA Manger, Operations & Board of Directors and committees to develop and update SCA programs and services.

■ Organize and maintain an inventory of SCA communications and marketing information.

■ Create and maintain the organization-wide communications calendar.

■ Assess and report on the effectiveness of communication strategies (i.e. Social Media, Monthly & Annual Reports).

Experience

  • · University Degree / Diploma in Administration, Business Management, Non-Profit Management, or related

degree, plus 2+ years’ progressive leadership experience working in a non-profit management role

· Consideration will also be given to candidates without this level of experience who can demonstrate a strong understanding of the sport governance structure, exhibit impeccable financial management and strategic planning skills, and have significant experience dealing with stakeholder engagement

Pay: $19.00 per hour

Work Location: Hybrid remote in Regina, SK S4P 0S4
