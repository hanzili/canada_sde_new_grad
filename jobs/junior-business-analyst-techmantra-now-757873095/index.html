About the Role

Map clinicians to PHIPA types; support onboarding

About TechMantra Now

Healthcare tech firm focused on PHIPA/privacy

Full Description

A Junior Business Analyst is required to support access to eReferral, utilizing the Agency’s pre-existing onboarding methodology. Validation of Health Information Custodian (HIC) and legal status via the Client Information Form will provide the path forward for identification. This resource will be required to category and map clinician end users to the Agency’s pre-approved HIC types and validate the legally responsible person for pre-approval prior to migration to digital health services.

Required Skills:

Understanding of PHIPA principles, including consent, collection, use, disclosure, and safeguards of personal health information; knowledge of the roles of health information custodians and agents under PHIPA; and understanding of the Ontario health care sector and access to the EHR/digital health services in Ontario.

Desired Skills:

  • Knowledge of information management principles, concepts, policies, and practices.
  • Experience with business analysis methods and tools such as business process mapping, business functional modelling, stakeholder engagement, and documentation development.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills with the ability to work effectively in a team environment and meet deadlines.

Evaluation Criteria:

  • Understanding of PHIPA principles: consent, collection, use, disclosure, and safeguards of PHI
  • General understanding of the roles of health information custodians and agents under PHIPA
  • Demonstrated knowledge and experience in conducting requirements gathering, developing documentation, including presentations and briefing notes, and conducting stakeholder engagement
  • Experience with stakeholder engagement, with excellent analytical, problem-solving and decision-making skills; verbal and written communication skills; interpersonal and negotiation skills
