Closing Date August 07, 2026

Salary $22.00

Duration Contract

Job Description Why choose SSMIC The Sault Ste. Marie Innovation Centre (SSMIC) is a nonprofit organization, one of 17 Regional Innovation Centres (RIC) in Ontario, dedicated to economic development and diversification in the science and technology sectors.

As an intern, if you are looking for a place to learn, grow, and make a real impact, SSMIC is more than just an organization; it is a community of passionate and innovative thinkers. Here, you would have the opportunity to collaborate with people who value creativity, fresh ideas, and teamwork. At SSMIC, you can explore your interests, develop your skills, and contribute to projects that have impact. Every challenge is treated as an opportunity to innovate, and ideas are genuinely valued.

Job Summary SSMIC is seeking a motivated Business Research Analyst Intern to join our team. This internship presents an exciting opportunity to gain hands-on experience working in a fast-paced and supportive environment. The Intern will work closely with all teams to assist with research, community engagement, and CRM maintenance.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct research on regional businesses, sector trends, and funding opportunities to support business development outreach, program intake, and client engagement activities.
  • Research emerging technologies including AI to implement into internal client and partnership workflows.
  • Assist in researching, identifying, coordinating, and attending opportunities for organizational visibility within the local business community, including partnerships, sponsorships, award programs, and regional initiatives.
  • Support the maintenance and updating of contact records, program participant data, and outreach tracking within the organization's CRM and internal tools to ensure accuracy and reporting readiness.
  • Assist in with client and community engagements including digital content, and client highlights.

Required Skills

  • A university degree or college diploma in business, information technology, or related field.
  • Aptitude for accurate, highly detailed and analytical skills.
  • Strong research skills.
  • Ability to utilize the Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook)
  • Superior customer service skills, and professional attitude.
  • Excellent presentation, written and oral communication skills.
  • Reliable, independent, and able to work flexible hours with minimal supervision or direction

How To Apply NOTICE TO ALL APPLICANTS Confidentiality of all applicants assured. Only those selected for interviews will be contacted and we thank all other applicants for their interest. The Sault Ste. Marie Innovation Centre welcomes a diverse workforce. As a non-for-profit organization, we are committed to an inclusive, barrier-free recruitment and selection process and workplace. If you require any accommodations, please notify us and we will work with you to meet your needs.

This opportunity is proudly supported by Northern Ontario Heritage Fund Corporation and is funded through the Workforce Development Program. Eligibility requirements of the program can be found here: https://nohfc.ca/en/pages/programs/people-talent-program/workforce-development-stream
