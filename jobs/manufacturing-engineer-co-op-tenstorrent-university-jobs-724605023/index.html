About the Role

Support manufacturing processes, analyze failures, improve quality

About Tenstorrent University Jobs

AI hardware company, hands-on co-op program

Full Description

Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

We are seeking a detail-oriented and results-driven Manufacturing Engineering Co-op to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will support new product introductions, contribute to global manufacturing operations, and help maintain high quality standards across production builds. The ideal candidate brings strong analytical skills, Excel proficiency, and the ability to work onsite to support hands-on manufacturing activities. If you are a motivated and analytical professional with a passion for improving manufacturing and supply chain processes, we encourage you to apply and join our team

This role is onsite, based out of Toronto, ON.

Who You Are

  • Detail-oriented and analytical, with the ability to balance precision and big-picture thinking.
  • A collaborative team player who communicates clearly with engineers, suppliers, and cross-functional partners.
  • Curious and proactive, with a strong interest in manufacturing, quality, and process improvement.
  • Methodical problem-solver who approaches challenges with critical thinking and structure.

What We Need

  • Familiarity with failure analysis concepts, root cause analysis, and basic manufacturing principles.
  • Ability to document processes, data, and findings clearly and accurately.
  • Comfort working with technical tools such as Excel, CAD, or statistical software (e.g., Minitab).
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills to support supplier and internal collaboration.

What You Will Learn

  • How to investigate manufacturing failures and product defects in a real-world production environment.
  • Best practices for data-driven process improvement and quality optimization.
  • How to collaborate with suppliers to resolve quality issues and support operational efficiency.
  • Exposure to engineering project workflows, including new product introductions and process upgrades.

Tenstorrent offers a highly competitive compensation package and benefits, and we are an equal opportunity employer.

This offer of employment is contingent upon the applicant being eligible to access U.S. export-controlled technology. Due to U.S. export laws, including those codified in the U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR), the Company is required to ensure compliance with these laws when transferring technology to nationals of certain countries (such as EAR Country Groups D:1, E1, and E2). These requirements apply to persons located in the U.S. and all countries outside the U.S. As the position offered will have direct and/or indirect access to information, systems, or technologies subject to these laws, the offer may be contingent upon your citizenship/permanent residency status or ability to obtain prior license approval from the U.S. Commerce Department or applicable federal agency. If employment is not possible due to U.S. export laws, any offer of employment will be rescinded.
