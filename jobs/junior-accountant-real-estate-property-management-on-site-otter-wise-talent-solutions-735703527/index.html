Full Description

Intermediate Accountant (CPA or CPA Candidate)

Edmonton, Alberta
$80,000 - $95,000 + Comprehensive Benefits

Build More Than Financial Statements. Build Your Career.

Otter Wise Talent Solutions is proud to partner with a growing and respected Edmonton-based organization in the real estate development and property sector to recruit an Intermediate Accountant.

This opportunity is ideal for an ambitious accounting professional looking to take the next step in their career and gain exposure to complex financial operations, multi-entity accounting, audit coordination, and strategic business initiatives.

Whether you are currently completing your CPA designation or have recently earned it, this role offers the opportunity to build upon your public practice and industry experience while working alongside experienced financial leaders in a collaborative and growth-oriented environment.

About the Opportunity

Reporting to senior financial leadership, the Intermediate Accountant will support full-cycle accounting activities, month-end reporting, financial statement preparation, audit coordination, and financial analysis across multiple entities.

This role is designed for an accounting professional who is eager to expand their technical skills, develop business acumen, and gain valuable experience within a growing organization.

Key ResponsibilitiesFinancial Reporting & Accounting

  • Assist with month-end and year-end close activities
  • Prepare journal entries and account reconciliations
  • Support financial statement preparation and working papers
  • Review and reconcile general ledger accounts
  • Assist with intercompany transactions and reconciliations
  • Support GST filings and regulatory reporting requirements
  • Assist with budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis

Audit & Compliance

  • Support year-end audit preparation and external auditors
  • Prepare audit schedules and supporting documentation
  • Assist with auditor requests and information gathering
  • Ensure financial records are maintained accurately and in accordance with accounting standards

Collaboration & Development

  • Work closely with finance leadership and operational stakeholders
  • Participate in special projects and process improvement initiatives
  • Contribute to continuous improvement within the accounting function
  • Build expertise across financial reporting, analysis, and operational accounting

Process Improvement

  • Identify opportunities to improve accounting processes and reporting efficiency
  • Support system enhancements and process documentation
  • Assist with strengthening internal controls and reporting procedures

Qualifications Required

  • CPA designation or actively pursuing CPA designation
  • Bachelor's Degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related discipline
  • 1–3 years of progressive accounting experience
  • Experience preparing reconciliations, journal entries, and working papers
  • Exposure to month-end close and financial reporting activities
  • Strong Excel skills
  • Experience working with ERP or accounting systems
  • Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines

Preferred

  • Experience supporting external audits
  • some public practice, audit, or assurance experience
  • Exposure to intercompany accounting and multi-entity organizations
  • Experience within real estate, construction, development, or property management environments
  • Exposure to ASPE and IFRS reporting standards
  • Experience with budgeting, forecasting, or financial analysis

Compensation & Benefits

  • Competitive salary
  • Extended Health Care
  • Dental Care
  • Vision Care
  • Life Insurance
  • Paid Time Off
  • Company Events and Team Activities
  • Professional Development Support
  • CPA Development Opportunities
  • Long-Term Career Growth Potential

Why Join?

This is an excellent opportunity for an early-career accounting professional to gain exposure to sophisticated financial operations while working within a growing organization that values development, accountability, and continuous improvement.

You will have the opportunity to learn from experienced leaders, broaden your technical accounting expertise, and build a strong foundation for future advancement within the finance profession.

About Otter Wise Talent Solutions

Otter Wise Talent Solutions is a proudly Indigenous-owned and women-led workforce solutions company operating across Canada and the United States.

We specialize in recruitment, executive search, workforce consulting, Indigenous workforce development, and talent strategy. Our team partners with organizations ranging from growing businesses to large enterprise organizations to build workforce solutions that create lasting impact.

At Otter Wise, we believe people are the foundation of every successful organization. Our approach combines industry expertise, relationship-driven recruiting, and innovative workforce solutions to help employers attract, engage, and retain exceptional talent.
