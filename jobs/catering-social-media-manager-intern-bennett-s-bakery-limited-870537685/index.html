Catering/Social Media Manager Intern

Position Overview

  • Support the management team in the day-to-day operation and continued growth of the company.
  • Gain hands-on experience in retail management, catering, marketing, business development, and strategic planning.
  • Assist in developing systems, policies, and procedures to improve business efficiency and customer service.
  • Support the expansion of lunch catering, supper takeout, and other new business opportunities.
  • Work closely with management and staff to identify operational challenges and opportunities for improvement.

Management & Business Development

  • Assist with the development and implementation of company policies and procedures.
  • Support strategic planning and long-term business development initiatives.
  • Identify opportunities to improve sales, profitability, customer service, and operational efficiency.
  • Assist with developing new products, services, and revenue opportunities.
  • Help monitor business performance and identify areas requiring improvement.
  • Assist management with special projects and business expansion initiatives.

Catering Operations

  • Assist with the launch and development of expanded catering services.
  • Coordinate catering orders from initial customer inquiry through completion.
  • Assist with menu development, pricing, packaging, and presentation.
  • Communicate with customers regarding catering requirements, custom orders, and event details.
  • Coordinate with kitchen, bakery, and retail staff to ensure catering orders are completed accurately and on time.
  • Assist with developing systems and procedures to improve catering efficiency.
  • Support the growth of both lunch and supper catering programs.

Retail Operations

  • Assist with day-to-day retail operations and customer service.
  • Support retail staff and help ensure company standards are consistently maintained.
  • Assist with merchandising, product presentation, inventory organization, and store appearance.
  • Help identify opportunities to improve the customer experience.
  • Assist with scheduling, workflow planning, and operational organization as required.
  • Provide support during busy periods and assist staff with customer-facing duties.

Marketing & Social Media

  • Assist with the development and implementation of marketing strategies.
  • Support social media planning, content creation, photography, and promotional campaigns.
  • Help promote new products, catering services, seasonal offerings, and special events.
  • Assist with website content and promotional material.
  • Monitor customer engagement and help identify effective marketing opportunities.
  • Assist in maintaining a consistent brand image across company locations and platforms.

Policy & Process Development

  • Review existing procedures and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Assist in creating written policies, procedures, checklists, and operational guidelines.
  • Help implement standardized processes across retail and catering operations.
  • Assist with improving communication between management, production, retail, and catering teams.
  • Track results and provide recommendations for further improvements.

Customer Service

  • Provide professional and friendly customer service.
  • Respond to customer inquiries regarding products, catering, and custom orders.
  • Assist with resolving customer concerns and finding appropriate solutions.
  • Build positive relationships with customers and identify opportunities to increase customer satisfaction and sales.

Training & Professional Development

  • Participate in management meetings and business planning discussions.
  • Develop leadership, communication, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
  • Receive mentorship and hands-on training from experienced management.
  • Gain practical experience in multiple areas of business operations.
  • Take on increasing responsibility as knowledge and skills develop.

Qualifications & Skills

  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Strong organizational and time-management abilities.
  • Interest in business management, retail, catering, marketing, or entrepreneurship.
  • Ability to work independently while also working effectively as part of a team.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills.
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced customer-service environment.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines.
  • Proficiency with computers, social media, and common office applications is an asset.
  • Previous retail, food service, catering, hospitality, or supervisory experience is an asset.

Pay: From $23.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Store discount

Work Location: In person
