About the Role

Support community relations and communications

About Suncor

Energy company with charitable foundation and community programs

Full Description

At Suncor, we produce and provide energy. When you join Suncor, you become part of a company that has built a solid foundation for both business and employee success. We are a place where talented people thrive. As part of our team, you play a vital role in delivering energy we all rely on, and you'll make a meaningful impact in the communities where we live and work.

Looking to gain hands-on experience in community engagement, communications, and event planning? Suncor is seeking a motivated student to support our Petro-Canada CareMakers Foundation for an 8–12 month term. In this role, you'll work with internal and external teams and community partners to support charitable giving programs, community initiatives, and stakeholder engagement activities. This is an excellent opportunity to build professional experience while making a meaningful impact in the communities where we live and work.

What we offer students:

  • Meaningful experience: gain valuable work experience with competitive pay to advance your early career journey and explore professional interests
  • Dynamic student community: participate in our engaging student community, where you’ll have the chance to connect with fellow students and build your network
  • Senior leadership exposure: interact with senior leadership and ask questions, discuss career paths, and receive advice
  • Mentorship and networking: peer, formal, and informal mentorships and opportunities to participate in Suncor’s six employee inclusion networks

Requirements:

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's degree program, preferably in Arts, Social Sciences, Communications, Marketing, Business, or a related field
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Experience with social media an asset
  • Ability to build relationships and collaborate with a variety of stakeholders
  • Experience volunteering with or supporting a non-profit or charitable organization is an asset
  • Organized, proactive, and able to manage multiple priorities in a team environment
  • Experience managing events an asset

Responsibilities:

  • Support the administration of the Petro-Canada CareMakers Foundation, a public Foundation dedicated to supporting family caregivers
  • Collaborate with internal and external supporters, communications and stakeholder engagement teams on community initiatives and events
  • Support relationships with charities, non-profit organizations, and other community partners
  • Assist with planning and coordinating events, meetings, and volunteer activities
  • Prepare presentations, communications materials, and program updates
  • Conduct research and provide general project support to the team

Location and other details:

  • This is an office-based role. You will work out of our Calgary head office, located in the Suncor Energy Centre at 150 – 6th Ave S.W.
  • Hours of work are a regular 40-hour work week, Monday to Friday
  • This is a 8-12 month work term starting Sept 2026

Think we are a fit? Apply now! Suncor is committed to providing equal opportunities for employment and building an inclusive, results-oriented and high-performance culture where all members of our team feel safe, valued and respected.
