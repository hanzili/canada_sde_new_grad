About the Role

Assist AI problem framing, analysis, and client deliverables

About IBM

Global tech giant with AI and cloud focus

Full Description

Introduction A career in IBM Consulting is rooted by long term relationships and close collaboration with clients across the globe.

You'll work with visionaries across multiple industries to improve the hybrid cloud and AI journey for the most innovative and valuable companies in the world. Your ability to accelerate impact and make meaningful change for your clients is enabled by our strategic partner ecosystem and our robust technology platforms across the IBM portfolio, including Software and Red Hat.

Curiosity and a constant quest for knowledge serve as the foundation to success in IBM Consulting. In your role, you'll be encouraged to challenge the norm, investigate ideas outside of your role, and come up with creative solutions resulting in groundbreaking impact for a wide network of clients. Our culture of evolution and empathy centers on long-term career growth and development opportunities in an environment that embraces your unique skills and experience.

About Business Unit IBM Consulting is IBM’s consulting and global professional services business, with market leading capabilities in business and technology transformation. With deep expertise in many industries, we offer strategy, experience, technology, and operations services to many of the most innovative and valuable companies in the world. Our people are focused on accelerating our clients’ businesses through the power of collaboration. We believe in the power of technology responsibly used to help people, partners and the planet.

Your Role And Responsibilities Our team is seeking students with strong critical thinking and a passion for solving business problems with AI. We’re looking for individuals who enjoy structuring problems, working in teams, and telling compelling stories with data.

The successful candidate will join a fast-paced consulting team. In this role, you will help plan, coordinate, and support the delivery of AI-driven solutions to enterprise-scale challenges. Working closely with senior team members and under their guidance, you’ll learn how technical teams and business stakeholders come together to deliver clear, measurable outcomes.

As a Consultant Intern, you will contribute to problem framing and solving, project planning, requirements gathering, and the preparation of insights. You’ll observe and support client meetings and workshops alongside senior team members, building your client-facing skills in a supportive setting. You’ll also gain hands-on exposure to cutting edge AI technologies while learning to translate business problems into AI solutions.

Key Responsibilities Include

  • Supporting requirement gathering to understand, document, and frame business problems, business cases, and pain points
  • Leveraging AI tooling to improve internal processes, working models, and deliverables
  • Helping synthesize and communicate insights, recommendations, and outputs to the project team, and to clients alongside senior team members
  • Supporting project planning, timelines, and deliverables across one or more engagements
  • Collaborating with cross-functional teams to support alignment between business needs and technical solutions
  • Helping prepare presentations, reports, and materials that support client deliverables
  • Assisting in identifying opportunities to apply AI and automation to improve business processes
  • Helping track project progress and supporting successful delivery against objectives

You’ll be part of a collaborative environment where diverse perspectives are valued, and where team members are encouraged to take initiative, think critically, and contribute to meaningful outcomes for clients.

This opportunity is for a hybrid position, based out of Toronto, Calgary, or Montreal, and is a 4-month work term commencing in January 2027. All applicants must be eligible to work full-time in Canada and enrolled full-time in an eligible post-secondary program. It is mandatory that applicants return to full-time studies upon completion of the work term.

Candidates pursuing Bachelor’s or Master’s degrees are eligible. Please note that MBA and PhD candidates are not eligible for this opportunity.

Preferred Education Bachelor's Degree

Required Technical And Professional Expertise

  • Currently enrolled full-time in a Bachelor’s or Master’s program in Business, Commerce, Economics, or a related field
  • Ability to understand business needs and challenges and apply a logical, defensible approach to solving them
  • Curiosity and creativity in re-imagining how work is done with cutting edge AI tools
  • Sound judgment in using AI responsibly, including verifying outputs for accuracy and protecting sensitive information
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to clearly present ideas and insights to peers and team members
  • Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate and build positive working relationships
  • Ability to work both independently and collaboratively within cross-functional teams
  • Strong organizational skills, with the ability to manage timelines and deliver work with attention to detail
  • Eagerness to learn quickly, seek out feedback, and act on it
  • Working proficiency with Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel for analysis, report creation, and general productivity
  • All locations require fluency in English. Montreal posting additionally requires fluency in French.

Preferred Technical And Professional Experience

  • Technology focused or technology adjacent coursework, minor, or program, in addition to studies in Business, Commerce, Economics, or similar
  • Familiarity with AI/GenAI concepts (e.g., large language models, prompt design, automation use cases)
  • Exposure to structured problem solving (e.g., hypotheses, issue trees) and a habit of checking your own analysis before sharing it
  • A passion for innovative ideas, coupled with the ability to understand and assimilate different points of view
  • Previous internship, case competition, or project experience in business analysis, consulting, project coordination, or AI
  • Demonstrated interest in data, analytics, AI, or automation through academic coursework, student clubs, or personal projects
  • Exposure to project management or collaboration tools (e.g., Jira, Confluence, Trello)
  • General understanding of how AI and automation can be applied to improve business processes.
