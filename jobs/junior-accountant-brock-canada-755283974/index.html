Full Description

Build your accounting career with a team that will give you real exposure.

Brock Canada is looking for a Junior Accountant to join our Finance team in Nisku. This is a great opportunity for someone who is early in their accounting career and looking to gain hands-on experience across month-end reporting, reconciliations, sales tax filings, intercompany invoicing, audit support, and day-to-day finance operations.
This role is well suited to someone who is detail-oriented, eager to learn, and interested in building a strong foundation in corporate accounting within a fast-paced industrial services environment.

About Brock
The Brock Group is a leading provider of industrial specialty services to clients across Canada, the United States, Latin America, and around the world. In Canada, Brock supports routine maintenance, turnarounds, shutdowns, and industrial capital projects across Western Canada.
Our services include scaffolding and work access, insulation, fireproofing, asbestos abatement, and other specialized industrial services for major producers and project sites.

About the Role
As a Junior Accountant, you will be part of a Finance team that supports accurate financial reporting, account reconciliations, tax filings, intercompany transactions, month-end processes, and audit preparation.
You will gain exposure to a wide range of accounting activities and work closely with internal teams to ensure financial information is accurate, well-supported, and completed on time.

What You’ll Do
In this role, you will:

  • Support month-end and year-end financial reporting activities
  • Prepare and maintain account reconciliations, including bank accounts and prepaid expenses
  • Assist with accruals by gathering information, preparing entries, and ensuring proper supporting documentation is in place
  • Prepare invoices for intercompany transactions
  • Prepare and file GST and PST returns
  • Enter transactions into SAP Business One
  • Support the preparation of year-end review files for external auditors
  • Gather project accruals and provisions information as required
  • Assist with document control and finance-related record keeping
  • Provide support to the Finance department and other internal teams as needed
  • Participate in special projects related to Finance
  • Communicate professionally with employees, internal stakeholders, and external contacts
  • Perform other duties as assigned

What You Bring
The successful candidate will bring:

  • A Business Diploma or Degree, or be working toward one
  • Strong attention to detail and a high level of accuracy
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills
  • A willingness to learn and take on new responsibilities
  • Professional, clear, and concise communication skills
  • Strong computer skills, including proficiency with Excel, Word, Outlook, and PowerPoint
  • The ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • A high level of professionalism, integrity, and confidentiality
  • The ability to meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment

Experience with an accounting system or enterprise resource planning system is considered an asset. Experience with SAP Business One is also considered an asset, but not required.

Why Join Brock?
This is an opportunity to develop your accounting skills in a role that provides meaningful exposure to the full Finance function. You will work with a knowledgeable team, support real business operations, and gain experience in an industrial services environment where accuracy, teamwork, and continuous improvement matter.
If you are motivated, detail-oriented, and ready to grow your accounting career, we would like to hear from you.
