About the Role

Build web apps with ASP.NET, MVC, APIs

About Bombardier

Canadian multinational aerospace leader

Full Description

Innovation is in our DNA… is it in yours? Bombardier is a global leader in aviation, creating innovative and game-changing planes. Our products and services provide world-class experiences that set new standards in passenger comfort, energy efficiency, reliability and safety. We are a global organization focused on working together with a team spirit.

Your boarding pass will include… Several conferences, including:

  • Meet An Executive
  • Women Taking Flight

Learning more about Bombardier, including:

  • Bombardier Products conference
  • Visits of the Bombardier sites
  • Bombardier Academy of Learning

Many social/networking opportunities, including:

  • Volunteering
  • Networking for Success
  • 5 à 7, Potluck, and much more!

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Working on web application development using the ASP.NET framework (4.5+) and Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture
  • Planning and building responsive user interfaces with HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap, JavaScript, and jQuery
  • Organizing and managing data using SQL Server and Entity Framework
  • Developing and integrating REST APIs to enable communication between services
  • Participating in debugging, testing, and optimizing code for performance
  • Collaborating with team members to deliver clean, maintainable, and scalable solutions

How to thrive in this role? During your internship, you will learn:

  • How to use modern development tools and environments
  • How to grow soft skills such as time management, teamwork, and problem-solving
  • How to participate in real-world projects and code reviews

What we’re looking for:

  • You’re familiar with web development and .NET technologies
  • You’re currently enrolled in a program in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field
  • You have a solid understanding of HTML/CSS, JavaScript/jQuery, SQL Server, Entity Framework, and MVC architecture
  • You’re eager to learn full-stack development and continuously improve your skills
  • You speak and write fluently in English and French

Boarding Information:

  • Location: Completion Center
  • Duration: 8 months
  • Flexible workplace-Hybrid

It is important to note that our internship opportunities are open to students only, not new graduates. All our interns may be required to occasionally travel outside of Canada for training/work purpose.
