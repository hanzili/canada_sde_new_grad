About Cosmaroma Inc.

Full Description

Overview
We are seeking a dynamic and customer-focused Retail Sales Associate to join our team. This role offers an exciting opportunity to engage with customers, showcase products, and drive sales in a fast-paced retail environment. The ideal candidate will possess excellent communication skills, a strong understanding of sales techniques, and the ability to manage multiple responsibilities effectively. Experience in outside sales, inside sales, or high-end sales is a plus, along with familiarity with merchandising, product demos, and upselling strategies. Multilingual abilities are welcomed to better serve diverse customer needs. This position emphasizes customer service excellence, organizational skills, and product knowledge to enhance the shopping experience.

Responsibilities

  • Greet customers warmly and assist them in finding products that meet their needs
  • Conduct product demonstrations and provide detailed explanations of features and benefits
  • Engage in upselling and cross-selling to maximize sales opportunities
  • Manage point-of-sale (POS) transactions accurately, including cash handling and cashiering duties
  • Maintain organized merchandise displays and ensure proper merchandising standards
  • Handle customer inquiries professionally and resolve issues efficiently
  • Utilize marketing techniques to promote new products or special offers
  • Assist with inventory management and stock replenishment
  • Support account management activities for repeat customers and loyalty programs
  • Collaborate with team members to achieve sales targets and improve overall store performance

Requirements

  • Proven experience in retail sales, inside sales, or high-end sales environments
  • Strong communication skills in English; multilingual abilities are a plus
  • Familiarity with POS systems, cash handling, and basic math skills for transactions
  • Knowledge of merchandising, product demos, upselling, negotiation, and marketing strategies
  • Organizational skills to manage multiple tasks efficiently in a busy retail setting
  • Ability to work well within a team while also demonstrating independent initiative
  • Customer service orientation with excellent interpersonal skills
  • Basic understanding of renovation or construction industry knowledge is beneficial for certain product lines

Join our team as a Retail Sales Associate to deliver exceptional customer experiences while advancing your career in retail sales. We value proactive individuals who are eager to learn and grow within our organization.

Pay: $19.00-$22.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Casual dress
  • On-site parking
  • Store discount

Work Location: In person
