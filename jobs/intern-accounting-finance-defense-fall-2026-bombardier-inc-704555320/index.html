Full Description

Innovation is in our DNA… is it in yours?
Bombardier is a global leader in aviation, creating innovative and game-changing planes. Our products and services provide world-class experiences that set new standards in passenger comfort, energy efficiency, reliability and safety. We are a global organization focused on working together with a team spirit.

Your boarding pass will include…

Several conferences, including:

  • Meet An Executive
  • Women Taking Flight

Learning more about Bombardier, including:

  • Bombardier Products conference
  • Visits of the Bombardier sites
  • Bombardier Academy of Learning

Many social/networking opportunities, including:

  • Volunteering
  • Networking for Success
  • 5 à 7, Potluck, and much more!

What are your contributions to the team?

Our team, Finance – Bombardier Defense, handles the complete financial governance related to this growing business unit. Pricing, order recording and analysis of each contract, profitability analysis by program, accounting entries and month-end reconciliations, budgeting and strategic planning are just a few examples of responsibilities within our very dynamic team.

During your internship, you will contribute to:

  • Participate in the monthly accounting process
  • Extract current data on sales, costs, inventory, etc
  • Record new contracts in our backlog
  • Prepare financial analyses related to programs
  • Support the preparation of monthly financial statements for Bombardier Defense
  • Analyze results and provide comments on variances
  • Support the accounting of Defense aircraft orders and sales
  • Prepare balance sheet account reconciliations
  • Prepare cost reports by department
  • Apply cash inflows related to payments received
  • Prepare and analyze various management reports
  • Work jointly with the Defense Programs team
  • Verify internal controls and ensure our compliance with the requirements of various auditors (EY, OSC, CASRA)
  • Numerous special mandates.

How to thrive in this role?

  • You are in the process of obtaining a Bachelor's degree in Finance or Accounting and a CPA certification,
  • You have good knowledge of the Microsoft Office suite (Excel in particular) and knowledge of SAP is an asset,
  • You have excellent analytical skills, you enjoy teamwork and you demonstrate rigor, innovation, commitment and perseverance,
  • You are able to work under pressure with short deadlines,
  • You are bilingual (English and French) both written and spoken.

​
Boarding Information:

  • Location: Administrative Centre (CA)
  • Duration: 8 months
  • Flexible workplace-Hybrid

It is important to note that our internship opportunities are open to students only, not new graduates. All our interns may be required to occasionally travel outside of Canada for training/work purpose.
