Full Description

A GOOD INTERNSHIP IS NEVER HANDS OFF. SHARE YOUR PASSION. We believe in creating an environment where our interns really can learn by doing and where they are given their own areas of responsibility right from the start of their time with us. That’s why our experts will treat you as part of the team from day one, encourage you to bring your own ideas to the table – and give you the opportunity to really show what you can do.

BMW Group Canada is offering a well-rounded internship with the focus on realizing data-based sales performance that permeates from headquarter down to each single sales outlet within Canada. This role is a part of an interdisciplinary team with a start-up mentality to innovate within a traditional automotive OEM for premium cars. You are working in an interdisciplinary team of sales, marketing, business development and IT experts, willing to share knowledge and expertise with you. Your help to shape and improve performance within the project is needed and wanted.

What awaits you?

  • Supporting the setup of KPI-oriented / data based reports and analysis for management and enhancing digital transformation are part of your area of responsibility
  • Processing raw data to desired output format
  • Creating data visualizations
  • Driving automation of reports and ensuring availability of accurate data in dashboards
  • Collaborating within our network using agile working structures and processes like JIRA and Confluence.
  • Supporting the analysis regarding industry trending, market potential and competition.
  • You will also be working with team on the bonus program to ensure the monthly reports are published to retailers on time
  • Supporting retailer system project and digital transformation

What should you bring along?

  • Experience within Sales, Marketing, Business Development or IT. Relevant analytics and/or BI related working experience is preferred.
  • Strong knowledge in MS Office, especially PowerPoint and Excel.
  • Knowledge in visualization programs like Qlicksight is an asset.
  • Coding experience in data processing, aggregation and database management is an asset.
  • Analytical thinking and first knowledge in analyzing and visualizing data.
  • Fast learner and willingness to work with Data Analytics Software.
  • Teamwork and communication skills.
  • Openness, motivation and hands-on mentality.

Ready to transform insights into action and strategy into results? Apply now!

Note: Please apply exclusively online via our career portal. Applications via other channels (esp. e-mail) cannot be considered.
