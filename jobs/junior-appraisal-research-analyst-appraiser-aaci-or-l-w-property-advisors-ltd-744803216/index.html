About L.W. Property Advisors Ltd.

Full Description

About Us

L.W. Property Advisors Ltd. is a leading commercial real estate appraisal firm based in Vancouver, British Columbia. We specialize in appraising all commercial real estate asset classes across the province. As our firm continues to grow, we are seeking motivated and detail-oriented professionals to join our team.

Positions Available

We are currently hiring for Junior Research Analyst

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct market research and data analysis on commercial real estate assets
  • Prepare detailed appraisal and research reports
  • Analyze market trends, financial data, and comparable properties
  • Assist with report writing and valuation assignments
  • Meet strict deadlines while maintaining high-quality standards
  • Collaborate with team members and work independently when required

Qualifications

  • Bachelor of Commerce (or related degree) or higher
  • Progress toward or completion of AACI designation (for appraiser roles)
  • Strong research, analytical, and writing skills
  • Excellent attention to detail
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word and Excel)
  • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary ($50,000–$60,000 annually)
  • Extended health benefits
  • Educational credits and professional development support
  • Opportunity to grow within a respected appraisal firm

Pay: $50,000.00-$60,000.00 per year
