Black Diamond Group’s Major Bids department has a full-time position for Jr Project Manager .

Reporting to the Project Manager, Major Projects, the Jr. Project Manager is a hybrid field/office role responsible for supporting the successful delivery of Black Diamond projects. This position is designed as a development role , providing hands‑on exposure to project planning, field execution, subcontractor coordination, and project controls. As competency increases, the Jr. PM will progressively assume greater responsibility, with a clear growth path toward a full Project Manager position.

The Ideal Candidate is a motivated and detail-oriented construction professional with 3–5 years of project experience, who thrives in both field and office environments, enjoys coordinating multiple stakeholders, and is eager to grow into a Project Manager role through hands-on project delivery experience.

Responsibilities Project Support

  • Assist the Project Manager with planning, scheduling, and coordination of project activities.
  • Track project progress, deliverables, and milestones; prepare weekly and monthly reporting packages.
  • Assist with cost tracking, forecasting, and budget updates.
  • Support procurement activities including material tracking, vendor coordination, and expediting.
  • Prepare and maintain project documentation including RFIs, submittals, change orders, and meeting minutes.
  • Assist with contract administration and subcontractor onboarding.
  • Contribute to risk identification, mitigation planning, and issue tracking.
  • Support development and maintenance of project schedules (MS Project or equivalent).

Field Coordination

  • Spend regular time on site to support field execution and understand construction workflows.
  • Work closely with Contractor Superintendents to track daily progress, manpower, equipment, and material needs.
  • Support quality inspections, deficiency tracking, and punch list completion.
  • Monitor subcontractor performance and escalate issues to the Project Manager.
  • Assist with site logistics planning, laydown coordination, and material deliveries.
  • Participate in commissioning and turnover activities as required.

Safety & Compliance

  • Participate in daily toolbox talks and site safety meetings.
  • Ensure subcontractors follow Black Diamond safety requirements and site procedures.
  • Complete safety documentation as required (inspections, hazard IDs, near‑miss reports).
  • Support the Project Manager and HSE team in maintaining a safe work environment.

Client & Stakeholder Interaction

  • Support the Project Manager in client communications and progress updates.
  • Attend project meetings and prepare action logs.
  • Assist in managing expectations, resolving minor issues, and maintaining positive relationships with clients, vendors, and subcontractors.

Qualifications

  • 3-5 years of experience in construction, modular construction, or industrial projects.
  • Strong organizational and communication skills.
  • Proficiency with MS Project, Excel, and project management software.
  • Ability to work in remote environments and travel to project sites as required.
  • Valid driver’s license
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities.
  • Ability to build positive relationships with field teams, subcontractors, and clients.
  • Self‑starting, problem‑solving mindset with a strong drive to learn and proactively take initiative.
  • Comfortable working in both office and field environments.
  • Desire to grow into a Project Manager role and take on increasing responsibility.

Work Environment & Travel Requirements

  • This role requires regular travel to remote project sites.
  • Candidates should expect to be on site for up to three (3) weeks at a time, depending on project schedules and execution requirements.
  • While on site, accommodations, meals, and travel are provided.
  • Work will occur in remote camp environments, modular construction sites, and industrial settings.
