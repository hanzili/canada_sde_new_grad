DEVON SHOES – High Energy Sales Associate (Part-Time & Full-Time)

Locations: Guildford mall ( Surrey)& Lougheed Mall ( Burnaby )

DEVON SHOES is hiring outgoing, confident, high-energy salespeople who love talking to people and making sales.

This is not a stand-around retail job — we want people who can approach customers, start conversations, and confidently sell.

If you’re motivated, social, and want to grow in retail, you’ll thrive here.

* What We Offer:

  • Competitive wages
  • Employee discount on all products
  • Opportunities for growth and promotion
  • Management growth path — top performers can move into leadership roles
  • Opportunity to attend buying shows for those who advance into management
  • Fun, fast-paced, team-oriented environment
  • Learn real sales skills (upselling, styling, customer relationships)
  • A workplace where your effort is noticed and rewarded

* What You’ll Do:

  • Confidently approach and engage every customer
  • Recommend multiple styles and drive sales (upselling & cross-selling)
  • Stay knowledgeable on products and inventory
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and visually appealing store
  • Support team sales goals
  • Build customer relationships and encourage repeat business

* What We’re Looking For:

  • Must be 18+
  • Outgoing, talkative, and confident personality
  • Comfortable speaking to and approaching strangers
  • Strong work ethic and willingness to learn
  • Able to stay productive during both slow and busy periods
  • Able to work at both Metrotown & Lougheed locations (flexibility is a strong asset)
  • Interest in fashion/footwear is a bonus
  • Looking for long-term growth
  • 4 day MINIMUM availability - including Sunday

* Not the right fit if:

  • You’re shy or uncomfortable talking to customers
  • You prefer a quiet or slow-paced job
  • You struggle staying productive during downtime

!! Apply in-store or on Indeed !!
