Full Description

Job Overview

Quality Analytical Services Ltd (QAS) is looking to hire a Trainee Lab Technician to join our team. We are looking for a reliable team player who is interested in science and can demonstrate strong attention to detail. This will be a full time, permanent role, on completion of a successful probationary period.

Responsibilities:

  • Perform routine and non-routine tests using the appropriate technical equipment and instruments
  • Receive or collect, as well as process, samples
  • Book in samples onto the LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System), and prepare & issue reports after checking accuracy
  • Prepare and supervise experiments
  • Perform quality control and calibrate equipment if necessary
  • Maintain equipment in proper working order and maintain a clean work area
  • Practice safe work habits, including complying with all safety, health, and environmental rules and regulations

Qualifications:

  • High school diploma required, with preference for science courses
  • No prior lab experience needed as full training will be provided
  • Computer skills: knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Analytical and numerical skills: ability to perform mathematical calculations (add, subtract, multiply, divide)
  • Strong attention to detail
  • Excellent organizational skills
  • Capable of following written instructions/ procedures
  • Ability to perform physical work such as standing for long periods of time, moving racks of samples around the laboratory etc.
  • Able to obtain a clean criminal record check

QAS is an equal opportunities employer, all candidates meeting the qualification criteria above will be considered. Employees must be willing to comply with random

drug and alcohol testing, due to the nature of our business.

Job Types: Full-time, Permanent

Pay: $16.97-$20.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Dental care
  • Extended health care
  • Life insurance
  • On-site parking
  • Paid time off
  • Vision care
  • Wellness program

Work Location: In person
