About the Role

Assist architectural design tasks under senior designer

About SJ Group

Global design firm with sustainable architecture leadership worldwide

Full Description

As a Summer Architectural Intern , you will work closely under the guidance of a Senior Designer or Architect to gain hands‑on experience in architectural design and project development. This internship is designed for students early in their architectural studies who are looking to build practical skills in conceptual design, space planning, schematic design, and technical drawing. You will support project teams on real project work while learning sustainable design principles, green architecture approaches, and responsible material selection.

Responsibilities

  • Assist in interpreting sketches, drawings, building codes, program requirements, and related project documents
  • Support the project team in preparing design documentation and presentation materials

  • Contribute to design concept development through:

  • Hand sketches

  • Physical models
  • Digital models
  • Diagrams
  • Massing studies

  • Other visual or graphic tools

  • Use BIM technologies (primarily Revit) to develop 3D building models

  • Conduct research on architectural concepts, materials, typologies, precedents, and industry benchmarks
  • Assist with site visits, surveys, and field observations (as applicable)
  • Provide general project and administrative support as needed

Education & Experience

  • Enrolled in the 3rd or 4th year of an accredited architecture program
  • Strong Revit proficiency
  • Working knowledge of AutoCAD (asset)
  • Experience with Rhino, SketchUp, Photoshop, Illustrator, or similar modelling/visualization tools (preferred)
  • Ability to hand sketch and communicate design ideas visually
  • Strong verbal and written communication
  • Effective team collaborator
  • Interest in sustainable and innovative architectural design

Our Firm B+H is a global, award-winning, full-service design firm recognized for excellence in sustainable design, technical expertise and the delivery of complex, large-scale projects. A legacy of 70 years of design innovation and problem solving has endowed us with a depth of knowledge, skill and agility that benefits each client we serve, wherever they are in the world. We are committed to delivering high-performance and sustainable communities, buildings and environments that exceed client expectations, contribute to human well-being, and enhance the health of our planet. We believe in the power of design to elevate people’s lives and we are committed to doing everything necessary to deliver environments that outperform our clients’ expectations, now and in the future.

Collaborating across ten studios in Toronto, Vancouver, Seattle, Shanghai, Hong Kong, Singapore & Ho Chi Minh City, B+H’s architects, interior designers, planners, landscape architects, organizational design specialists, experience designers, researchers, analysts, and brand strategists combine strategic thinking, bold design, and advanced technology with the creative human energy of an interdisciplinary, multi-generational team. B+H delivers strategic consulting and high-value design solutions that shape spaces, communities, and economies. At B+H we push the limits of creativity by acknowledging the humanity in the creative process. We believe in letting things get a little messy while we let our minds explore the bounds of what’s possible. Once we’ve found something truly new, then we reassemble it into the clean, well-executed project our clients expect from us. Join us if you’re willing to try new things, to go bold, and to let the best idea win. If you’re willing to stretch yourself and you want to create something that’s never existed before, we want you.
