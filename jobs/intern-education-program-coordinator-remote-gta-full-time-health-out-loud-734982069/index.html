About the Role

Coordinate education programs; facilitate community learning

About Health Out Loud

Health-focused nonprofit delivering recovery-oriented education programs

Full Description

WE ARE RECRUITING FOR EDUCATION PROGRAM COORDINATORS!

Make a meaningful difference by:

  • Contributing to learning materials for community programs and offering resources for specific demographic groups using a recovery-orientated and strength-based approach
  • Adapting learning outcomes, program plans, language used, resources, and program materials, to be culturally relevant and accessible for specific demographic groups
  • Creating or adapting curricula, practical content, and facilitation manuals for community, educational, and online programs
  • Exploring and facilitating training on virtual facilitation tools and approaches that are engaging, reflective of current best practices and pedagogical approaches
  • Facilitating certificate-based programs, including Canadian Red Cross first-aid, psychological first-aid, and babysitting courses
  • Using relatable platforms (i.e. social media), as a platform for relationship-building, knowledge translation, telling stories, and promoting access to our supports

You may also assist with:

  • Coordinating advisory groups, administrative duties (i.e. logistical communication for programs), evaluation processes, report creation, as well as presenting at public forums and conferences

We're recruiting full-time interns and employees (35 hours/week), at an hourly wage of $17.20–$30.00/hours.

Grow with us!

  • Apply Here: https://healthoutloud.org/intern-epc-apply2025

We’re not just about working hard; we’re about building friendships, pursuing personal wellness goals together, and personal development.

We’re looking for people who really care – about our mission, our work, and the communities we serve.

What are we looking for?

1) FACILITATION AND COMMUNICATION SKILLS

  • Previous experience with program planning, small group facilitation, or facilitating virtually
  • Knowledge of participant-driven pedagogical approaches, which are learner-centred and promote power-sharing
  • Strong writing and oral skills, especially in relation to active listening and conveying complex thoughts in a way that is relatable
  • Ability to nurture and grow relationships, as well as offer peer support

2) PROJECT MANAGEMENT SKILLS

  • Experience coordinating and working collaboratively with multidisciplinary teams and individuals, internally and externally to teams
  • Ability to track progress, create communication systems, and inspire motivation under time sensitive deadlines

3) COMMUNITY EXPERIENCE

  • Ability to understand and assess the needs of diverse community groups, clients, and learners
  • Strong understanding of diversity, equity, and inclusion
  • Previous experience working with vulnerable populations

4) EDUCATION

  • Educational background in teaching, public health, adult education, fitness, and social work, or equivalent relevant life experiences
  • Certification in Wellness Recovery Action Plan (WRAP), Psychological First Aid, Mental Health First Aid, First Aid & CPR, ASSIST or facilitation training are assets
  • Lived experience of mental health and/or addictions
