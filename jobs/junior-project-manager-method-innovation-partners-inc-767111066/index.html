About Method Innovation Partners Inc

Full Description

Our people are our greatest strength. Come and join the team and be part of a great culture while enjoying the pursuit of new products, construction projects and continual growth.

This opportunity is with EXO Construction Group, a division of METHOD Innovation Partners. EXO is a growing Surrey-based construction company offering general contracting, design-build and construction management services to the institutional sector. EXO is notably recognized for its EXO Prefabricated Building System, meeting growth needs with rapidly built, quality, permanent buildings delivered seamlessly. The EXO Building System is currently focused on new schools and school additions in BC, with future growth leading to other sectors and expanded geographic areas.

Our construction services expertise as general contractor, design builder, and construction manager also provides opportunities in traditional construction projects across a broad range of sectors.

If you are an energetic, agile, driven professional who is excited about construction, project management, and delivering quality prefabricated and traditional projects, you might be the perfect fit to join our team as a Junior Project Manager.

OVERVIEW:

The Junior Project Manager is responsible for managing assigned projects or portions of larger projects from preconstruction through construction, commissioning, and closeout under the guidance of senior leadership as required. They are responsible for implementing the project plan, managing day-to-day project activities, and supporting successful project delivery.

The Junior Project Manager works closely with owners, consultants, subcontractors, suppliers, and the internal project team to maintain alignment, resolve issues proactively, and drive the project forward. Through strong planning, communication, and leadership, they are expected to deliver high-quality projects that meet or exceed client expectations while striving to complete the work ahead of schedule and under budget wherever practical.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

Project Planning & Preconstruction:

  • Develop the overall project execution plan, including scope, schedule, budget, procurement strategy, staffing requirements, and key project milestones. Ensure all planning activities align with the contract documents, client expectations, and company objectives.
  • Identify project risks and issues and work with senior leadership to develop mitigation strategies.
  • Coordinate with owners, consultants, authorities having jurisdiction, and internal teams to obtain permits, approvals, and information required to maintain the project schedule.
  • Support estimating and preconstruction activities by providing constructability input, scheduling assistance, and coordination.

Budget and Cost Management:

  • Monitor project budgets, cash flow, cost reports, and productivity, escalating significant variances to senior leadership.Administer contracts, change orders, progress claims, purchase orders, and subcontract agreements while ensuring all commercial matters are documented, negotiated, and resolved in accordance with the contract.
  • Work closely with subcontractors, suppliers, and consultants to manage procurement, monitor commitments, and ensure timely delivery of materials and services.

Project Execution and Management:

  • Maintain and update the project schedule, identify potential delays, and assist in implementing recovery plans.Manage project documentation, including RFIs, submittals, shop drawings, project logs, meeting minutes, contracts, correspondence, and closeout documentation, ensuring information is complete, accurate, and current.
  • Provide leadership and ongoing support to the Site Superintendent through regular communication, site meetings, trade coordination, problem solving, and by providing site management coverage during periods of absence when required.

Stakeholder Communication:

  • Serve as the primary day-to-day contact for assigned clients, consultants, subcontractors, and project stakeholders.Lead regular project meetings, provide status updates, resolve issues, and maintain strong working relationships.
  • Prepare and distribute project reports covering schedule, budget, risks, and key decisions.
  • Ensure effective communication channels are in place for team members and stakeholders.

Quality Assurance and Control

  • Ensure all work complies with project documents, building codes, safety regulations, and quality standards.
  • Monitor quality throughout construction, conduct inspections, and lead post-project reviews to identify lessons learned.

Safety Management:

  • Ensure compliance with company safety policies and regulatory requirements.
  • Promote a strong safety culture through inspections, training, and timely resolution of safety issues.

Team Leadership:

  • Coordinate and support the project team while promoting collaboration, accountability, and professional development.

Close Out:

  • Coordinate project closeout activities, including deficiencies, turnover documentation, O&M manuals, and commissioning support.

REQUIREMENTS:

  • Min 2 to 5 years experience in commercial, institutional, or industrial construction, with experience managing projects up to approximately $8M.
  • Post-secondary education in Construction Management, Engineering, Building Science, or a related discipline.
  • Working knowledge of construction methods, contract administration, scheduling, project controls, and safety requirements.
  • Ability to interpret construction drawings, specifications, and contract documents.
  • Demonstrated leadership potential with the ability to manage multiple priorities and develop strong client and trade relationships.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office, MS Project, Procore, and other construction management software.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities while maintaining attention to detail.
  • PMP designation, LEED accreditation, or equivalent certifications are considered assets.
