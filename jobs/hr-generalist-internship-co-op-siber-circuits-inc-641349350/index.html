Full Description

HR Generalist (Internship/Co-op)

Location: Markham, ON
Job Type: Internship / Co-op
Weekly Hours: Approximately 35–40 hours per week (flexible based on program requirements)
On-Site Parking Available

Start date: Immediately

About Us:

Siber Circuits Inc. is a leading manufacturer of high-quality printed circuit boards (PCBs) located in Markham, Ontario. With a strong commitment to innovation, precision, and customer satisfaction, we serve clients across various industries, including Medical, Military and Aerospace. At Siber Circuits, we value teamwork, continuous improvement, and a culture of excellence that empowers employees to learn and grow.

Position Summary:

We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented HR Generalist (Intern/Co-op) to join our Human Resources team. This role offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a professional HR environment while supporting various HR and administrative functions. The ideal candidate will be organized, proactive, and eager to learn about all aspects of HR management and operations within a manufacturing setting.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Assist in maintaining accurate and up-to-date employee files and personnel records.
  • Support onboarding processes, including preparing documentation and orientation materials.
  • Help coordinate employee training and development programs.
  • Perform filing, scheduling, and general administrative tasks to support the HR department.
  • Assist with the development and maintenance of job descriptions and HR forms.
  • Support records management and ensure confidentiality of all documentation.
  • Prepare and update reports and spreadsheets as required.
  • Contribute to employee engagement and communication initiatives.
  • Participate in continuous improvement activities within HR operations.
  • Perform general office administration tasks, including data entry, document preparation, and correspondence.
  • Manage and organize electronic and physical filing systems to ensure easy retrieval of information.
  • Prepare and format reports, forms, and internal communications with accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Assist with document control and record retention in compliance with company policies.

Qualifications:

  • Currently enrolled in a post-secondary program in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • Strong organizational and time management skills with keen attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint) is required.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality and demonstrate professionalism.
  • A positive attitude and willingness to learn in a fast-paced environment.

What’s in It for You:

  • Hands-on HR experience in a professional manufacturing environment.
  • Mentorship and guidance from experienced HR professionals.
  • Skill development in HR operations, documentation, and administration.
  • Career growth opportunity, including potential for future employment based on performance and business needs.
  • On-site parking and a supportive workplace culture that values learning and collaboration.

How to Apply:

Interested candidates are invited to submit their resume and cover letter to Nabeela Mirza at: nabeela@sibercircuits.com.

Please include “HR Generalist (Intern/Co-op) – Your Name” in the subject line.
