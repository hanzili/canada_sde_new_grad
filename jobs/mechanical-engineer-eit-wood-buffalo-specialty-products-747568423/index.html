About the Role

Develop P&ID, piping, and drawings for oil sands

About Wood Buffalo Specialty Products

Oil sands equipment specialist with design focus

Full Description

Overview

We are seeking a motivated Mechanical Engineer (EIT) to join our dynamic engineering team. This entry-level position offers an excellent opportunity for recent graduates or early-career professionals to apply their mechanical engineering knowledge in a collaborative environment. The role involves supporting system design, drafting, and manufacturing processes while gaining valuable industry experience. Candidates should possess strong technical skills, a passion for innovation, and a commitment to continuous learning.

Role Requirements:

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Mechanical Engineering from an accredited Canadian university
  • Registered EIT or recent graduate eligible for registration
  • Experience with P&ID development and piping systems is an asset
  • Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel; familiarity with engineering piping software
  • Experience with SolidWorks and/or AutoCAD is considered an asset
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Demonstrated ability to work effectively with Plant Leads, Sales Director, and cross‑functional teams
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to prioritize multiple projects and tasks

Role Responsibilities:

Project Delivery

  • Perform document reviews, engineering calculations, and design tasks
  • Develop application‑specific P&IDs for tailings hydro and underflow piping systems in Oil Sands operations
  • Apply knowledge of process piping, pipe stress, and related engineering calculations
  • Demonstrate understanding of materials, operational constraints, and interdisciplinary design impacts
  • Participate in drawing reviews and oversee technical aspects of drawing production
  • Develop and maintain Bills of Materials (BOMs)
  • Manage RFIs and document control processes
  • Support fabrication of Issued for Construction (IFC) work packages
  • Assist the Quality Control Manager with ABSA and CWB regulatory compliance

Design Oversight

  • Guide and complete technical designs following standardized drafting and design procedures
  • Apply engineering knowledge to solve design challenges and contribute actively to design development
  • Support the creation and refinement of internal engineering specifications for product design

Professional Practice

  • Demonstrate professional integrity, accountability, and sound engineering judgment
  • Apply knowledge of relevant codes, standards, regulations, and safety practices
  • Identify technical risks and propose effective mitigation strategies
  • Understand safeguards required to protect the public and mitigate adverse impacts
  • Recognize the role and expectations of regulatory bodies in engineering practice

This position provides an engaging environment for aspiring Mechanical Engineers (EIT) to develop their skills while contributing to impactful projects. Candidates should demonstrate enthusiasm for engineering innovation, attention to detail, and a proactive approach to learning.

Role Requirements:

  • A University Degree in a related Mechanical Engineering program from an accredited Canadian University is required.
  • Registered EIT or new Graduate.
  • P&ID and Piping experience is asset.
  • Comprehensive working knowledge of Microsoft Office and Excel, along with knowledge of engineering piping software.
  • Familiarity with Solidworks/AutoCAD software is an asset.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Proven ability to work in a team environment with Plant leads and Sales Director.
  • Demonstrated ability to keep organized and prioritize various projects and tasks.

Role Responsibilities:

Project Delivery

  • Execute document reviews, engineering calculations, and engineering design work.
  • Produce application-specific P&ID for tailing Hydro and underflow lines for the Oil Sands Properties.
  • Knowledge of process piping, pipe stress and their engineering calculations is an asset.
  • Demonstrate knowledge of materials or operations as appropriate, project and product design constraints, design to best fit the purpose or service intended and address inter-disciplinary impacts.
  • Participate in reviews and oversee the technical aspects of drawing production.
  • Development and oversight of Bill of Materials.
  • Manage RFIs and document control.
  • Support fabrication of Issued for Construction work packages as required.
  • Support quality control manager with ABSA and CWB construction regulation requirements.

Design Oversight

  • Guide and complete a technical design according to standardized design and drafting procedures.
  • Applies engineering / technical knowledge to design challenges, including active participation in design.
  • Development of internal engineering specification for product design.

Professional Practice

  • Demonstrates professional integrity and accountability.
  • Proven knowledge of regulations, codes, standards, and safety including local engineering procedures and practices.
  • Analyzes technical risks and offers mitigation solutions.
  • Proven understanding of the safeguards required to protect the public and the methods of mitigating adverse impacts.
  • Understands the role of regulatory bodies on the practice of engineering.

This position offers an engaging environment for aspiring Mechanical Engineers (EIT) to develop their technical skills while contributing to meaningful, industry‑impacting projects. Ideal candidates will bring enthusiasm for engineering innovation, strong attention to detail, and a proactive approach to learning and professional growth.

Pay: $50,000.00-$55,000.00 per year

Benefits:

  • Extended health care
  • Life insurance
  • On-site parking

Work Location: In person
