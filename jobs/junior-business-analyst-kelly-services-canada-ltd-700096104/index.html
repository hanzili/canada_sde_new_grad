Full Description

Business Analyst I

📍 Location: Toronto, ON (Hybrid – 4 days onsite)

This is for a current opening.

*Our recruitment process for this role includes the use of AI-based tools to help evaluate candidate qualifications and screen applications.*

Why This Role?

Long-term contract with potential extension or conversion

Strong training program provided

Exposure to global financial operations and funding processes

About the Team

The Funding and Money Market Middle Office team partners with front office, finance, and risk teams globally to manage liquidity, funding, and transactional risk. This team plays a key role in cash management, trade support, and maintaining efficient financial operations across the bank.

Role Overview

This is a business-as-usual (BAU) analytical role focused on managing and monitoring USD payment flows, funding requirements, and financial transactions within capital markets operations.

Key Responsibilities

Manage and analyze USD payment flows through trading Nostro accounts using internal tools (IDL, LCM)

Investigate and resolve discrepancies in account balances

Manage daily funding requirements and internal funding relationships

Assist in approving and releasing daily transactions

Provide support and guidance to junior analysts

Identify risks, escalate issues, and ensure compliance with internal policies

Support process improvements and operational efficiency initiatives

Collaborate with internal stakeholders and external banking partners

Must-Have Qualifications

Undergraduate degree in Economics, Finance, Math, Statistics, or related field

1–2 years of relevant experience (banking, financial services, Big 4, or co-op experience)

Strong Excel skills (data analysis, formulas, reporting)

Experience working in a large organization environment

Soft Skills

Quick learner with strong adaptability

Excellent communication and collaboration skills

Ability to work under pressure in a fast-paced environment

Strong analytical thinking and attention to detail

Nice-to-Have

Experience in major banking institutions

Exposure to Money Market products or capital markets

Master’s degree

Familiarity with Jira

What We’re Looking For

Strong interest in financial operations and capital markets

Ability to manage ambiguity and prioritize work effectively

Detail-oriented mindset with strong problem-solving abilities

Team-oriented individual with a proactive approach

Performance Expectations

Ability to independently manage workload after training

Maintain accuracy and timeliness in processing transactions

Contribute to process improvements and risk mitigation
