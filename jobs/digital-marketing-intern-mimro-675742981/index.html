Full Description

About Mimro

Mimro is a forward-thinking digital platform focused on helping people make more conscious and informed purchasing decisions. We combine education, storytelling, and data-driven insights to connect users with eco-friendly products and sustainable lifestyle choices.

Based in Toronto, ON, Mimro operates at the intersection of sustainability, digital media, and e-commerce. Our mission is to make sustainability accessible through clear, engaging, and actionable content that empowers everyday consumers to understand the environmental impact of what they buy.

We are building a dynamic, creative, and impact-driven team culture where innovation, collaboration, and learning are at the core of everything we do.

Marketing Intern – Mimro

Role Overview

This is a full-time hybrid Marketing Intern role based in Toronto, ON. The Marketing Intern will support Mimro’s growth by assisting in marketing strategy, content creation, market research, and customer engagement.

You will work closely with the team to understand user behavior, analyze market trends, and contribute to campaigns that increase awareness and adoption of sustainable products. This role is ideal for someone who is curious about marketing, passionate about sustainability, and eager to learn in a fast-paced startup environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct market research to identify trends, customer insights, and competitor activity
  • Support the development and execution of marketing strategies and digital campaigns
  • Assist in creating and distributing content across blog, email, and social media platforms
  • Analyze marketing data and help generate insights for decision-making
  • Support customer engagement and communication initiatives
  • Contribute to sales and growth-related marketing activities
  • Collaborate with the team on branding, storytelling, and campaign ideation
  • Assist with ad-hoc marketing and growth experiments

Qualifications

  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Interest in marketing, digital media, sustainability, or consumer behavior
  • Ability to conduct research and interpret basic data insights
  • Familiarity with or willingness to learn marketing strategy and digital tools
  • Comfortable working in a hybrid (remote + in-office) environment
  • Strong organization skills and ability to manage multiple tasks
  • Proactive, adaptable, and eager to learn in a startup setting
  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree/diploma in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field

What You’ll Gain

  • Hands-on experience in real startup marketing and growth strategy
  • Exposure to sustainability-focused digital product development
  • Opportunity to contribute directly to campaigns and decision-making
  • Skill development in content marketing, research, and analytics
  • Experience working in a collaborative Toronto-based startup environment
