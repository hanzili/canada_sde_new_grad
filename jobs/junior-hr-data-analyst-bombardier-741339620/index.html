Overview

As a Junior HR Data Analyst, you will play a key role in collecting, analyzing, and visualizing data to support teams in their decision-making. You will work closely with technology and business experts to improve the quality of the reporting.

What are your contributions to the team?

  • Collect, clean, and structure data from various sources.
  • Perform analyses and produce clear reports.
  • Create interactive dashboards (Power BI, Excel, etc.).
  • Identify trends and provide data-driven recommendations.
  • Collaborate with business teams to improve data quality.
  • Analyze business requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement system enhancements and upgrades.
  • Monitor system performance and troubleshoot issues to ensure optimal functionality.
  • Develop and maintain documentation for systems, processes, and procedures.
  • Ensure compliance with data security and privacy standards.
  • Provide user support and training for new systems and features.

How to thrive in this role?

  • Bachelor degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Management or business administrative degree or related field.
  • Minimum 0-2 years of relevant experience.
  • Curiosity, eagerness to learn, and genuine interest in working with data.
  • Ability to manage multiple requests and priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Comfortable working on parallel projects with competing deadlines.
  • Strong interest in data, analytics, and continuous improvement.
  • Proactive mindset with a willingness to learn new tools, systems, and business processes.
  • Strong analytical mindset with high attention to detail and data accuracy.
  • Knowledge of analytical tools (Advanced Excel, Power BI).
  • Understanding databases and modeling concepts.
  • Ability to interpret data and communicate insights.
  • Strong communication and collaboration abilities.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in French and English
  • Ability to work collaboratively and meet deadlines.
  • Familiarity with HRIS systems (e.g., SAP SuccessFactors) is a strong asset.
  • Understanding of HR data domains (headcount, hires, terminations, turnover) is an asset.
