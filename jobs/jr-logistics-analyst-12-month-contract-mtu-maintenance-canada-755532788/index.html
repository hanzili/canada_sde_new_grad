About MTU Maintenance Canada

Full Description

MTU Maintenance Canada is the North American member of the MTU Maintenance network of companies, the largest independent provider of commercial engine maintenance services worldwide. Quality, efficiency and innovation are our strengths. You, too, can become part of our dedicated team.

Salary: $25 - $35 an hour Contract Duration: 12 months MTU Maintenance Canada Ltd. Jr. Logistics Analyst (12 month Contract)

  • Location Delta
  • Starter area Logistics
  • Target group Graduates
  • Contract type Full-Time
  • Job ID 1358

The Junior Logistics Analyst will assist with analyzing courier and freight forwarder reports, consolidating transport and customs data, and filing/monitoring customs duty and tax claims.

Tasks:

  • Review commercial invoices and shipment documents to identify incorrect duties and surcharges
  • File dispute claims with couriers for shipments with incorrect charges
  • Communicate and report discrepancies to the Finance team promptly
  • Document all identified discrepancies and dispute actions internally for record-keeping and audit purposes
  • Follow up with couriers on the status of dispute claims until resolution
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams as needed to resolve shipment charge issues
  • Create and maintain KPIs and the necessary tracking spreadsheets relating to transport duties and taxes.

Requirements:

  • Bachelor degree in a Logistics or Procurement related areas
  • Minimum of 1 year experience in Logistics, Procurement, Customs or related areas
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office (particularly Excel) and ERP systems; experience with shipping platforms (e.g., FedEx, DHL) is an asset.
  • Familiarity with SAP transactions, preferably within the MM (Materials Management) module
  • Experience interpreting commercial invoices and bills of lading
  • Familiarity with import/export regulations, tariff classification (HS code), and trade agreements (e.g., CUSMA/USMCA).
  • Strong attention to detail
  • Ability to stay updated on changing tariff regulations and scenarios

Go to our website, to learn more about us. After your application is received, hiring managers will review for potential next steps.
