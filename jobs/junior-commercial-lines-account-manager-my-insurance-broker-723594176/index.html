Full Description

Job Title: Junior Commercial Lines Account Manager

Location: 505 Hwy 7 #311, Thornhill, ON L3T 7T1

Employment Type: Full-time / Permanent / 5 Days in Office

Salary Range: $55,000 - $65,000

Reason for Vacancy: New position

Who we are:
Established in 2008, My Insurance Broker is a proud family-owned and Canadian-operated brokerage. As one of Ontario's fastest-growing online brokerages, we have forged strong partnerships with Canada's leading insurance companies. With multiple offices across Southern Ontario and a dedicated support center in Richmond Hill, we are committed to delivering superior pricing, comprehensive coverage, and exceptional service to our clients.

About the role:
My Insurance Broker Corp. is looking to add a dynamic, energetic, and team-oriented Junior Commercial Lines Account Manager. In this role, you will service a portfolio of commercial accounts by managing renewals, handling daily client requests, and remarketing policies when needed. If you love building long-lasting relationships with clients, brokers, and carriers, we want to hear from you!

What you’ll be doing:
Account Management & Client Service

  • Manage inbound requests from the commercial service queue, processing policy changes across Commercial Property, Liability, Auto, and Specialty Lines for the existing house book of business.
  • Review, underwrite, and process endorsement requests from brokers and house clients within established service standards.
  • Issue Certificates of Insurance (COIs) for house clients and brokers.
  • Complete transactions within EPIC, issue invoices for agency-billed policies, and actively manage related accounts receivable.
  • Act as a trusted resource for brokers and clients. Maintain an accessible presence by promptly returning missed calls, ensuring adherence to call-recording protocols.
  • Process policy cancellations, offering guidance on next steps while proactively attempting to retain the business where possible.

Relationship Management & Collaboration

  • Build and maintain strong working relationships with clients, brokers, insurance carriers, and internal team members.
  • Communicate effectively with insurance carriers regarding risk submissions, endorsements, and specific policy inquiries.
  • Partner with internal teams to ensure seamless processing, meticulous documentation, and timely follow-ups.
  • Work closely with the new business, renewal, and Hamilton teams to identify re-marketing and cross-selling opportunities.
  • Address and resolve carrier, client, or broker concerns in a timely manner.

Compliance & Quality Control

  • Ensure all transactions comply with carrier underwriting guidelines, regulatory requirements, and internal policies.
  • Maintain data accuracy within EPIC, ensuring all applications, files, and client histories are thoroughly documented with detailed notes.
  • Proactively identify potential exposure, coverage gaps, or E&O concerns, escalating issues as required.

Growth & Operational Support

  • Participate in team meetings, ongoing training sessions, and internal process improvement initiatives.
  • Support brokerage goals tied to client retention, growth, and service excellence.
  • Perform other related duties as required.

What we need you to bring:

  • College Diploma, University Degree, or equivalent professional industry experience.
  • RIBO License in good standing.
  • 1 - 3 years of hands-on experience within a general insurance brokerage, specifically focused on servicing commercial lines accounts.
  • Solid understanding of insurance carrier portals and standard commercial underwriting workflows.
  • Experience working with a Broker Management System (EPIC preferred).
  • Proficient in MS Office, specifically Excel, Word, and Outlook.
  • CIP or CAIB Certification or working towards its completion is considered an asset
