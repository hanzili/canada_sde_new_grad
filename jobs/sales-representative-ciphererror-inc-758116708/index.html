About Ciphererror Inc.

Waterdown-based retailer; growth-focused sales role

Full Description

Job Summary

We are seeking a dynamic and motivated Sales Representative to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for engaging with customers, demonstrating products, and driving sales growth across various channels. The ideal candidate will possess excellent communication skills, a customer-focused attitude, and the ability to manage multiple tasks efficiently. This position offers an opportunity to develop your sales expertise in a fast-paced environment while providing exceptional service to our clients.

Responsibilities

  • Engage with customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products or services
  • Conduct product demonstrations and explain features to enhance customer understanding and interest
  • Manage merchandising displays and ensure product availability on the sales floor
  • Handle point-of-sale (POS) transactions accurately, including cash handling and processing payments
  • Upsell additional products or services to maximize sales opportunities
  • Maintain organized and attractive product displays to attract customer attention
  • Provide excellent customer service by addressing inquiries, resolving issues, and ensuring customer satisfaction
  • Assist in inventory management and stock replenishment as needed
  • Utilize strong communication skills to build rapport with customers and team members
  • Manage time effectively to meet sales targets and complete daily tasks efficiently

Skills

  • Multilingual abilities are highly desirable to serve a diverse customer base effectively
  • Previous experience in technology sales or retail sales is preferred, especially involving product demos and technical explanations
  • Bilingual proficiency in English and additional languages is a plus for broader customer engagement
  • Strong merchandising skills with knowledge of POS systems and basic math for transactions
  • Excellent customer service skills with professional phone etiquette and effective communication abilities
  • Proven ability in upselling, cross-selling, and achieving sales goals
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage time efficiently in a fast-paced environment
  • Experience in cashiering, cash handling, and retail operations is advantageous
  • Ability to demonstrate technical products confidently and clearly during demos or presentations

This role is ideal for individuals passionate about sales, eager to develop their skills, and committed to delivering outstanding customer experiences.

Pay: Up to $34,000.00 per year

Work Location: On the road
