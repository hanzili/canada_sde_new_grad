About the Role

Assemble hardware, rack servers, perform basic troubleshooting

About Cloudy4 Web Services Inc.

Edmonton-based bare-metal cloud infrastructure provider

Full Description

About Cloudy4 Web Services:

Cloudy4 is a high-growth, bare-metal public cloud operator headquartered in Edmonton, Alberta. We deploy enterprise-grade server clusters, high-speed fiber optics, and advanced cloud infrastructure to power modern businesses worldwide.

We are looking for an eager, hands-on Junior Data Center Technician to join our team. If you are passionate about computer hardware, enterprise servers, and networking, and want to build a career in cloud infrastructure, this is your ground-floor opportunity to learn from seasoned systems engineers and grow with our business!

Key Responsibilities

  • Hardware Assembly & Staging: Unbox, assemble, and configure enterprise server chassis, RAID controllers, RAM, storage drives, and network expansion cards;
  • Rack & Stack Field Operations: Visit our data center facilities to physically mount servers, top-of-rack switches, and power distribution units (PDUs) into server cabinets;
  • Cable Management: Run, route, label, and manage copper Cat6 and high-speed fiber optic patch cords cleanly according to rack diagrams;
  • Remote Hands Support: Serve as the physical "hands and eyes" on-site for our global systems engineering team, assisting with hardware swaps, rebooting nodes, and basic troubleshooting;
  • Inventory Management: Maintain accurate records of incoming server components, spare optics, drive sleds, and cabling inventory.

Who You Are

  • Hardware Enthusiast: You love building PCs, tinkering with server hardware, or setting up home labs. You know your way around motherboards, RAM, storage arrays, and network cables;
  • Eager to Learn: No prior enterprise data center experience required! We will train you on enterprise server architectures, SAN/vSAN, and data center best practices;
  • Great Communicator: Comfortable collaborating with our remote engineering lead and local management team;
  • Detail-Oriented & Reliable: You take pride in neat cable routing, careful handling of sensitive electronics, and clear documentation.

Requirements

  • High school diploma or equivalent (post-secondary education in IT, Computer Science, or trade certifications like CompTIA A+ / Network+ is a bonus, but not required);
  • Valid Alberta Driver’s License and access to a reliable vehicle for local travel to our Alberta data center facilities;
  • Physical ability to lift up to 50 lbs (rackmount servers and hardware boxes);
  • Basic understanding of computer hardware components and networking concepts.

What We Offer

  • Direct mentorship from experienced global network and cloud engineers.
  • Hands-on experience working with enterprise-grade silicon (Dell, HP, Arista, 40G/10G optical networks).
  • Clear pathway for career advancement as Cloudy4 expands its data center footprint across North America.
  • Flexible working hours and a dynamic startup environment.

Pay: From $15.00 per hour

Ability to commute/relocate:

  • Edmonton, AB: reliably commute or plan to relocate before starting work (required)

Application question(s):

  • Do you have a valid driver's license and reliable transport to travel to local data centers in Alberta?

Work Location: In person
