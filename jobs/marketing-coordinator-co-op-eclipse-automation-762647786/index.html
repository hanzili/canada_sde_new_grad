Full Description

Job Title: Marketing Coordinator Co-op Location: Cambridge, ON | On-site

Job Type: Co-op

Compensation: $22.00 - $26.00/hour Get To Know Us Eclipse Automation delivers cutting-edge custom automated manufacturing solutions across multiple industries. We combine advanced automation expertise with digital innovation to create smart, efficient, and sustainable manufacturing systems. Our global network includes facilities in Canada, the United States and Hungary ensuring regional expertise and global strength.

The Position We are looking for a motivated, organized, and curious Marketing Coordinator (Co-op) to join our marketing team. This is a unique opportunity to gain hands-on B2B marketing experience with a global leader in advanced manufacturing automation. You’ll contribute to projects across content, communications, campaigns, digital marketing, video, and events – while building skills that prepare you for a career in marketing or communications.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Develop and publish social media content (LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram) in alignment with brand guidelines and direction from the Content team
  • Contribute to internal communications activities such as the newsletter, videos, town halls – partnering with the HR and Content teams
  • Assist with digital marketing projects such as website updates, SEO, and digital advertising campaigns
  • Support campaign management processes in HubSpot, including list organization and lead scoring
  • Provide on-site support for events and video shoots, including setup, logistics, and gathering content
  • Conduct research to explore new marketing platforms and opportunities (e.g., TikTok, Reddit, Capterra)
  • Assist with organizing shared marketing files, image libraries, and task management tools
  • Coordinate and facilitate team workshops and brainstorming sessions, including prepping materials and capturing outcomes
  • Participate in meetings, collaborate with the team, and support day-to-day marketing activities
  • Work and collaborate in a team environment, actively participating and engaging in group meetings
  • Ensure deadlines are met, notifying the appropriate departments/stakeholders if milestones are delayed or at risk
  • Maintain a strong corporate citizen role in support of the Eclipse Corporate Vision
  • Ensure that all business activities are performed with the highest ethical standards and in compliance with the Eclipse Automation Inc. Conduct Code
  • Foster the development of a culture reflective of the core values of the business
  • Complete other duties and projects as assigned

What We’re Looking For

  • Currently enrolled in a post-secondary program in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Experience writing for business, social media, or newsletters
  • Organized with strong attention to detail and file management skills
  • Comfortable conducting research and presenting findings
  • Familiarity with MS Office; experience with Hootsuite, HubSpot, or WordPress is a plus
  • Able to manage multiple tasks and deadlines in a fast-paced environment
  • Positive, collaborative, and eager to learn
  • Interest in B2B marketing and advanced manufacturing
  • Students should be enrolled in a post-secondary academic program requiring a co-op/work term

Why Join Us Here at Eclipse Automation, creation and innovation are at our core, and our culture reflects that. Fast-paced and continuously fostering growth, we encourage the entrepreneurial spirit that our teams embody, and leverage each of their strengths to do better for our people, our clients, and our communities.Our teams are made up of innovators, thinkers, and doers, with the know-how and expertise to exceed expectations, and the relentless spirit to create and reinvent.
