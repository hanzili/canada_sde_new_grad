What Is The Opportunity?

Reporting to the Project Manager, Continuous Improvement, the Junior Product Manager supports the delivery and ownership of purpose-built software solutions, platforms, and workflow tools used by project teams and business functions across the Nuclear Continuous Improvement portfolio. These solutions exist to strengthen how Aecon delivers engineering, procurement, and construction work, translating field and operational insight into capabilities that improve safety, predictability, and productivity on our projects.

The role is responsible for managing product initiatives from intake through release, with ownership of defined product areas including backlog management, prioritization, and delivery outcomes. The Junior Product Manager ensures alignment between business needs, user requirements, and technical execution while coordinating cross-functional teams to drive delivery. This position plays a key role in converting project and operational needs into practical tools that crews, engineers, and project leaders rely on, supporting decision-making and ensuring outcomes are measured by their impact on project performance. The role requires strong ownership, structured thinking, and the ability to manage competing priorities within a controlled and regulated environment.

What You'll Do Here

Own assigned tools and solutions from intake through release, ensuring they directly support how project teams plan, execute, and close out work

Engage project teams, site leadership, and business functions to understand execution pain points and translate them into prioritized product improvements

Define and maintain product priorities, roadmaps, and delivery plans anchored to project milestones and operational readiness needs

Translate business needs into structured product requirements, including user stories and acceptance criteria

Manage and prioritize product backlogs to ensure clarity, sequencing, and alignment with program objectives

Coordinate cross-functional teams, including project delivery, business, IS, and vendors, to drive execution and maintain alignment

Facilitate intake, backlog refinement, and working sessions with project and operational stakeholders

Develop and maintain delivery plans, schedules, and milestones to support structured execution

Monitor delivery progress against plan, including timelines, risks, issues, and dependencies that could affect rollout to active projects

Escalate risks, issues, and decisions with clear context and recommended actions, including impact to project users and adoption timing

Track and report on delivery status to ensure transparency and stakeholder alignment

Measure success by adoption and execution outcomes, usage by project teams, reduction in manual effort, quality of data captured, and contribution to safer, more predictable delivery

Drive product decisions based on user needs in the field and office, project execution value, business value, and operational constraints

Support rollout and implementation into live project environments, including change management, training, and post-go-live stabilization

Identify opportunities for product and process improvement through analysis of user workflows and system gaps

Ensure product outputs align with governance, compliance, and security requirements where applicable

Maintain clear and structured documentation to support both product delivery and project execution

Manage day-to-day system administration, including user account creation, access management, and onboarding of new users

Perform minor system configuration without reliance on IS (e.g. field updates, dropdown value additions, light workflow adjustments)

Support data management activities, including import/export of data across systems

Provide ongoing user support to project and business users, including troubleshooting, training, and responding to enhancement requests

Translate existing operational and project execution processes into system design, including defining fields, forms, and workflow structure

Work hands-on within the platform to maintain and evolve the solution post-deployment, rather than relying solely on development teams
