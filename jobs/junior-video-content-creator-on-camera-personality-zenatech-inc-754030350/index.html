Full Description

About ZenaTech

ZenaTech (Nasdaq: ZENA | FSE: 49Q) is a technology company specializing in AI drones, Drone as a Service (DaaS), enterprise SaaS, and quantum computing solutions for mission-critical business applications. Since 2017, the company has leveraged its software development expertise and expanded its drone design and manufacturing capabilities through ZenaDrone to innovate and enhance customer inspection, monitoring, safety, security, compliance, and surveying processes. ZenaTech serves over 100 enterprise software customers in law enforcement, government, and industrial sectors, with drones used in agriculture, defense, and logistics industries. The company has offices in North America, Europe, and the UAE.

Portfolio attached in applications is required

Job Overview

We are looking for a Junior Video Content Creator and On-Camera Personality who is as comfortable in front of a lens as they are behind one. You will conceptualize, create, and appear in video content that brings ZenaTech's products and mission to life — from explainer videos and product spotlights to short-form social content and thought leadership pieces. If you have natural screen presence, a sharp creative instinct, and genuine curiosity about technology, this is the role for you.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conceptualize and develop original video content formats suited to LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.
  • Serve as an on-camera host, narrator, and/or presenter for ZenaTech branded content, product demonstrations, and interview-format videos.
  • Write scripts and outlines for on-camera delivery and collaborate with marketing on messaging alignment.
  • Self-direct and self-shoot content in office, field, and event environments using available equipment.
  • Edit and post-produce own content and assist in editing other team members' footage.
  • Build and manage a content calendar in coordination with the social media and marketing teams.
  • Monitor performance analytics and adapt content approach based on engagement and platform data.
  • Represent ZenaTech authentically and professionally in all on-camera appearances.
  • Stay current with the latest content trends across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
  • Create fast, engaging, platform native video content for social media, including content filmed on iPhones and other mobile devices.
  • Film, shoot, and capture content in office, field, event, and studio environments using available equipment.
  • Edit and post produce video content for social media, marketing, product, and branded storytelling needs.
  • Operate professional video and cinema cameras, including systems such as the Canon C80 and Canon C50.

Qualifications

  • 1–3 years of experience creating video content in a professional, brand, or media context — including work that features you on camera.
  • Natural, confident on-camera presence with the ability to communicate technical concepts in an engaging, accessible way.
  • Proficiency in self-directed filming (camera setup, framing, basic lighting for talking head and field formats).
  • Familiarity with editing skills in Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci resolve.
  • Strong writing ability — comfortable drafting scripts, captions, and content briefs with minimal oversight.
  • A content portfolio or channel demonstrating on-camera work, brand storytelling, or audience engagement is required.
  • Post-secondary education in communications, film, marketing, or a related field is preferred.
