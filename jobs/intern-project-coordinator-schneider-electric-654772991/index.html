About the Role

About Schneider Electric

Global energy management leader; innovative solutions

Full Description

What will you do? The intern will be introduced to project management by performing project management activities and will be exposed to the technical side of the electrical distribution field by doing changes to existing orders.

What qualifications will make you successful for this role?

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed (within the last year) a university degree in electrical engineering or a related field
  • Proficient in MS Office (Excel, Word, Adobe Acrobat)
  • Functional knowledge of English and French is required in order to facilitate collaboration with our teams, clients and stakeholders at the provincial and international levels.
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Effective planning and time management skills
  • Strong communication skills
  • Detail-oriented and able to multi-task
  • Ability to work independently
  • Knowledge in project management and electrical distribution (low and medium voltage) is an asset
