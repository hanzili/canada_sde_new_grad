About the Role

Process AP/AR, assist month-end close

About Kassen Recruitment

Growing real estate firm; hands-on accounting

Full Description

A growing organization in the real estate sector is seeking a Junior Accountant to join their team. This role offers strong exposure across multiple areas of accounting, making it an excellent opportunity for someone looking to broaden their skill set and gain hands-on experience in a collaborative environment.

About the Role

Reporting to a senior finance leader, this position will support day-to-day accounting operations with a primary focus on accounts payable, while also gaining exposure to accounts receivable, reporting, and project-based work. You’ll play a key role in maintaining accurate financial records and supporting ongoing process improvements.

What You’ll Do

  • Process and verify a high volume of invoices, ensuring accurate coding and timely payment execution
  • Match purchase orders to invoices and resolve discrepancies with vendors or internal teams
  • Assist with vendor account reconciliations and support issue resolution
  • Support month-end and year-end close processes, including preparing working papers and investigating variances
  • Contribute to financial reporting by assisting with reconciliations and tracking investment or operational activity
  • Collaborate with senior team members on audits and other key deliverables
  • Participate in ongoing projects, including developing internal documentation and process improvement initiatives
  • Cross-train across different accounting functions to build a well-rounded understanding of the business

What You Bring

  • Bachelors degree in accounting or a related field
  • 2+ years of accounting experience (real estate or property management exposure is an asset)
  • Strong understanding of basic accounting principles
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office
  • High attention to detail with strong organizational and time management skills
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving abilities
  • Comfortable working both independently and as part of a team

Why Join

This is a great opportunity to join a collaborative and growing team where you’ll gain exposure to multiple areas of accounting early in your career. The organization offers a supportive environment with opportunities to learn, contribute to meaningful projects, and develop professionally over time.
