About the Role

Own social media content, on-camera presence.

About Jishang International Ltd.

Toronto-based beauty brand startup building online presence.

Full Description

Job Summary

SEYUNE (operated by Jishang International Ltd.) is a Toronto-based online beauty store curating Korean and Japanese skincare, makeup and haircare, shipping across Canada and the US. We're a small, fast-moving team building a beauty brand from the ground up, and we're looking for a paid Social Media & Video Content Intern who loves making short-form videos and isn't shy on camera. You'll have real ownership of our TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts content, and a path to a longer-term role for the right person.

Responsibilities

  • Plan, shoot and edit short-form vertical videos (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)

  • Appear on camera: product comparisons, routines and "how to choose" guides

  • Write hooks and captions that grab attention in the first second

  • Research beauty trends and turn them into video ideas that fit our brand

  • Post content and reply to comments across our social accounts

  • Help with product photos and occasional in-person shoots in North York

Experience

  • Student or recent grad in media, film, marketing, communications or a related field

  • A portfolio of short-form videos you shot and/or edited yourself (required, please include a link)

  • Comfortable appearing on camera

  • Fluent English; Mandarin is a plus

  • Video editing skills (CapCut, Premiere Pro or similar)

  • Genuine interest in Korean and Japanese beauty

  • Nice to have: your own beauty or lifestyle content account

Details

  • Paid internship: $18–20/hour, 15–20 hours per week, flexible schedule

  • Hybrid: edit remotely, shoot in North York as needed

  • Shortlisted applicants will complete a short paid test (2 videos)

Pay: $18.00-$20.00 per hour

Expected hours: 15.0 – 20.0 per week

Application question(s):

  • Please paste a link to short-form videos you shot or edited yourself (TikTok / Reels / Shorts).
  • Are you comfortable appearing on camera?

Language:

  • English (required)
  • Mandarin (preferred)

Work Location: Hybrid remote in North York, ON (York District)
