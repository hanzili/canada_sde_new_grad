Full Description

SLT Pest Detective — Now Hiring: Technician Trainee & Pest Control Technician

SLT Pest Detective is a locally owned, award-winning pest control company serving Langley, Surrey/Delta and the Tri-Cities. We are hiring for two full-time field positions:

  • Technician Trainee: no pest control experience required
  • Pest Control Technician: about 1–2 years of industry experience preferred

We're looking for reliable, practical people who want a long-term career in pest control and are comfortable working on their own, solving problems and dealing professionally with customers.

Technician Trainee: $24–$26/hour

  • No pest control experience required
  • Experience in construction, trades, maintenance, restoration, landscaping, delivery, field service or other hands-on work is an asset
  • Starting wage depends on relevant experience and transferable skills
  • We pay for your first attempt at the BC Structural Pesticide Applicator exam. Rewrites, if needed, are at your own expense.

Pest Control Technician: $25–$28/hour

  • About 1–2 years of pest control experience preferred
  • Experience with common structural pests, customer communication and independent field work
  • BC Structural Pesticide Applicator Licence preferred
  • Starting wage depends on licensing, technical skill and how independently you work

Compensation & Benefits

  • Take-home company vehicle with fuel included
  • Company phone, so there's no need to pay for your own plan
  • Overtime available during peak season
  • Extended medical and dental benefits after six months
  • Life insurance
  • Paid vacation and paid sick days
  • Wage increases as you gain your licence, skills and independence

Estimated total compensation: about $60,000–$75,000 a year. This includes wages, overtime, the value of the take-home vehicle and fuel, company phone, and medical and dental coverage.

Schedule

  • Monday to Friday, 8:30 am–4:30 pm
  • Longer days and occasional weekend work during peak season

Requirements

  • Valid BC Class 5 driver's licence and a satisfactory driving record
  • Must complete a satisfactory criminal record check, as the role involves entering customers’ homes
  • Able to work in crawlspaces, attics and outdoors in all weather, climb ladders, and lift and carry equipment
  • Professional communication with customers
  • Comfortable working on your own after training
  • Willing to complete the required training and licensing
  • Lives within a reasonable commuting distance of Langley, Surrey/Delta or the Tri-Cities, since the vehicle is take-home

Why Join Us

  • Thorough hands-on field training
  • No sales quotas or pressure selling
  • Stable, manageable service routes
  • A professional, quality-focused work environment
  • Owner-led company with long-term growth opportunities

We welcome requests for accommodation during the hiring process.

How to Apply

Apply through Indeed with your resume. Please say whether you're applying for the Technician Trainee or Pest Control Technician role.

You can also email your resume to lmresume@pestdetective.com.
