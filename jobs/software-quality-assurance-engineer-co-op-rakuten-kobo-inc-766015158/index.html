Full Description

Job Description Here at Rakuten Kobo Inc., our mission is to make reading lives better. We stand for readers. We also stand for our employees – from interns and co-ops to permanent staff – and support their growth and development. Whether you are a student at the start of your career or a professional carving a different path, our early careers opportunities are built with you in mind – with experiences tailored to your individual skills and interests.

We offer a start-up environment, interesting and engaging work within the technology and publishing industries, and a group of friendly and talented individuals. Additionally, our employees rank us highly in terms of commitment to work-life balance and a company culture committed to caring for our employees.

If you’re looking for a company that inspires passion, personal, and professional growth – join Kobo and come help us on our mission of making reading lives better.

The Role Rakuten Kobo Inc. is looking for a Software Engineer Co-op to join our Research and Development International team as a co-op for 8 months starting in September 2025. The team is comprised of many highly talented enthusiastic individuals who ensure the services and offerings from Kobo are available to both internal and external customers.

Reporting to the Manager, Software Engineering, the Software Engineer Co-op will work with the Research and Development International team made up of developers and testers and take an active part in delivering high quality software to Rakuten Kobo’s users.

The Person

  • A passion for software development and delivery
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills
  • Have a desire to work in a team but also be self-organising enough to be able to work on a task independently
  • Constantly learning and self-improving mindset

Required The Skillset:

  • Some previous experience in and Object-Oriented Software Language such as C# or Java
  • An understanding of T-SQL Databases
  • Experience with web technologies (HTML, JavaScript, CSS)
  • Some knowledge/experience with modern UI frameworks e.g. Angular, React, NextJS
  • Knowledge of SDLC
  • Writing and Maintaining Source Code
  • Understanding of the Testing Pyramid
  • Agile methodologies
  • CI/CD Pipeline – Jenkins, GitHub Actions, CircleCI etc.
  • Familiarity with one IDE (VS Code, JetBrains Rider)

Nice To Haves

  • An understanding of server-side web technologies such as Web API and MVC
  • Knowledge of using version control systems such as Git.
  • Experience using an ORM framework (NHibernate, Entity Framework)

The Perks

  • Flexible hours and hybrid remote working environment
  • Subsidized in-office lunches
  • Talent and development training budget
  • Free Kobo device + free weekly e-book or audiobook
  • Weekly Kobo Spark sessions
  • Dog friendly office

About Rakuten Kobo Inc. Owned by Tokyo-based Rakuten and headquartered in Toronto, Rakuten Kobo Inc. is one of the most advanced global ecommerce companies, with the world’s most innovative e-reading services offering more than 6 million eBooks and audiobooks to 30 million + customers in 190 countries. Kobo delivers the best digital reading experience through creative innovation, award-winning e-readers, and top-ranking mobile apps. Kobo is a part of the Rakuten group of companies.

Rakuten Kobo Inc. is an equal opportunity employer. Accessibility accommodations for candidates with disabilities participating in the selection process are available on request. Any information received related to accommodation needs of applicants will be addressed confidentially.
