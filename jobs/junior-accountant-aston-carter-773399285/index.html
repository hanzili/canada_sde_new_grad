About the Role

Process AP/AR, reconcile, assist with audits

About Aston Carter

Global recruiting firm with Fortune 500 clients

Full Description

Description Our client is a leading industrial services organization that supports major infrastructure, construction, energy, mining, and environmental projects across North America. Their operations are fast-paced, project-driven, and focused on safety, operational excellence, environmental stewardship, and customer service. The finance team is looking to add a Junior Accountant who is eager to build a long-term career in accounting and finance while gaining exposure to high-volume transactions and large-scale business operations.

Requirements

  • 1 year accounting related experience (AP/AR/data entry)
  • Undergraduate degree: Accounting, finance, economics, business administration
  • Intermediate excel: VLOOKUP, Pivot Tables
  • Large ERP experience (SAP, D365, Oracle, NetSuite)

Key Responsibilities

  • Process accounts payable and accounts receivable transactions.
  • Perform data entry and maintain accurate financial records.
  • Assist with account reconciliations and month-end activities.
  • Review, validate, and investigate discrepancies in financial data.
  • Generate and maintain Excel-based reports and analysis.
  • Support ERP system maintenance and data integrity initiatives.
  • Assist with financial audits and documentation requests.
  • Collaborate with internal departments to ensure timely and accurate processing of transactions.
  • Participate in process improvement initiatives and system enhancements.
  • Provide administrative and accounting support to the broader finance team.

What's In It For You?

  • Access to benefits and vacation time while on contract
  • Opportunity to join a growing, industry-leading organization supporting large-scale infrastructure, construction, energy, and industrial projects
  • Exposure to a high-volume, fast-paced finance environment that will accelerate your accounting career.
  • Hands-on experience working with large ERP systems and complex business operations.
  • Opportunity to participate in process improvement and system enhancement initiatives, including ERP-related projects.

Job Type & Location This is a Contract position based out of Calgary, AB.

Pay And Benefits The pay range for this position is $24.00 - $27.00/hr.

Workplace Type This is a fully onsite position in Calgary,AB.
