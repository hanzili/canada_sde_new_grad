About the Role

Create marketing materials, draft social content, support campaigns

About Q-Line Trucking

Growing logistics firm with national footprint and team

Full Description

The Marketing Associate will work closely with the Business Development and Marketing team to support Q-Line’s brand presence, customer outreach, recruitment marketing, and event coordination.

This is a hands-on, entry-level role for someone who enjoys creating content, asking good questions, staying organized, and turning ideas into useful marketing materials.

Key Responsibilities & Accountabilities

  • Create marketing materials such as brochures, one-pagers, case studies, sales decks, and customer-facing collateral.
  • Draft social media content, especially for LinkedIn, to support Q-Line’s visibility in key industries.
  • Support blog posts, thought-leadership content, and industry-focused storytelling.
  • Maintain and organize marketing materials, proposal templates, and brand resources.
  • Gather stories and information from across the organization, including operations, drivers, leadership, and customer-facing teams.
  • Support outbound campaigns, email content, LinkedIn outreach, landing pages, and account-based marketing initiatives.
  • Monitor inbound lead activity and share insights with the Business Development team.
  • Contribute to driver recruitment marketing and employer brand content.
  • Support trade shows and industry events, including booth materials, logistics, prospect lists, and follow-up.
  • Help track event results, lead activity, and campaign performance.

Q-Line is a growing transportation and logistics company with a strong reputation, a national footprint, and a team-oriented culture. We move quickly, value initiative, and believe in building from within.

If you’re ready to grow your skills and contribute to meaningful work, we’d like to hear from you.

  • A degree or diploma in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  • Examples of writing, design, content, or campaign work, including school, volunteer, or side projects.
  • Strong written communication skills and an eye for clear, professional presentation.
  • Comfort using tools such as Canva, Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, social media platforms, or scheduling tools.
  • Strong organization skills and attention to detail.
  • A collaborative attitude and confidence working with different teams.
  • Curiosity, initiative, and a willingness to dig deeper, ask questions, and learn the business.

What You’ll Gain

  • Hands-on experience in content creation, campaigns, events, and sales enablement.
  • Exposure to B2B marketing across industries like mining, agriculture, construction, energy, and infrastructure.
  • Mentorship from experienced business development and marketing professionals.
  • A strong foundation in brand building, storytelling, and customer-focused marketing.

Q-Line is from you. a growing transportation and logistics company with a strong reputation, a national footprint, and a team-oriented culture. We move quickly, value initiative, and believe in building from within.

If you’re ready to grow your skills and contribute to meaningful work, we’d like to hear from you.

Candidates must be legally authorized to work in Canada at the time of application. Please note that Q-Line is not able to provide immigration sponsorship or LMIA support for this position.
