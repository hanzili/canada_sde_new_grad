Thinking Alternatively is in Our DNA

With a team of 241 bold thinkers—one-third dedicated solely to investment management—we are specialists, not generalists. Managing over $19.3 billion (June 30, 2026) for institutional and retail clients, we navigate markets with conviction, resilience, and a forward-thinking approach.  

We’re more than investors we’re innovators. We challenge traditional investment mindsets, constantly pushing the boundaries to achieve our mission: To bring greater certainty to investors.  

Our success is built on four guiding principles: 

  • Treat investors’ money like our own.

  • Redefine the way investors invest.

  • Be human, always approachable.

  • Succeed together, one investment at a time.

These aren’t just words—they define how we think, how we invest, and how we work. 

Now, we’re entering our next chapter. We’re transforming our brand and reshaping the way the world invests. If you think alternatively, embrace an entrepreneurial spirit, and thrive in a dynamic, bureaucracy-free environment, join us and be part of what’s next. 

Thriving in Our Entrepreneurial Culture 

At PICTON Investments, you’ll thrive in our creative and dynamic workplace, where collaboration and support are at the core of everything we do. At our firm, you’ll have the opportunity to take on significant responsibilities, work in a flexible environment, and tackle challenging projects from the outset. Our culture is designed for ambitious professionals who want to make an immediate impact while continuing to push boundaries and achieve our greater mission. With competitive total rewards, performance-based bonuses, and a clear path for career growth, you’ll have everything you need to develop both now and over the longer term. 

The Opportunity

We are seeking a candidate to join our Digital Product Management team as a Intern, Business Analyst for an 8 month internship starting this fall. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for providing comprehensive business and product support for Data management. Your primary focus will involve collaborating with various teams, including Investment Management, Portfolio Construction, Trading, Compliance, Sales, Marketing, HR and Operations to support them in data automation needs.

The expected hourly base salary range for this role is $25.00 to $35.00, with final hourly salary determined based on experience, skills, education, and internal equity. 

At PICTON Investments, being employee-owned means we take care of our team. As an intern, you'll be part of a collaborative, entrepreneurial culture where your contributions matter and your development is taken seriously. 

You’ll have the opportunity to learn and lead:

  • Be part of exciting business projects, supporting the team in deploying new data and reporting solutions. You’ll get hands-on experience and learn how business and technology teams work together.
  • Work alongside experienced team members to help define and document business needs and processes.
  • Learn to use helpful tools like flowcharts, activity diagrams, surveys, interviews, and workflow analysis to gather and manage information.
  • Assist in collecting information from multiple sources, making sense of it, and turning high-level requests into actionable tasks.
  • Help identify and document business objectives, current processes, and challenges while learning from subject matter experts.
  • Use templates to create documents such as Business Requirement Documents (BRDs), user stories, and acceptance criteria—and share ideas to make them even better.
  • Support data analysis and reporting, helping transform complex information into clear and simple insights.
  • Collaborate with management and business partners to keep project roadmaps up to date and understand how priorities evolve.
  • Work closely with technology and support teams to see how cross-functional projects come together.
  • Get exposure to Agile projects and contribute throughout

What we’re looking for:

  • Currently pursuing an undergraduate degree in a STEM program such as Mathematics, Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or another related field.
  • Interest in financial concepts, capital markets, wealth management, or asset management.
  • Some exposure to data analysis through coursework, projects, or prior experience is a plus.
  • Familiarity with tools like Excel, SQL, Python, R, Tableau, or PowerBI is helpful but not required.
  • Curiosity about how data is used to make decisions and a desire to learn new skills.
  • Attention to detail and ability to organize information clearly.
  • Comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, and communicating with team members.
  • Eagerness to collaborate, adapt, and contribute in a fast-moving, supportive environment.
  • Motivation to learn, grow, and make an impact while gaining meaningful experience.
  • Experience with AI tools, data analytics platforms, or automation technologies is considered an asset.
