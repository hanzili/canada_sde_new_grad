About the Role

Perform environmental field sampling and data reporting

About Comco Canada LTD

Emerging environmental services firm; strong field opportunities

Full Description

Comco Canada is currently seeking a skilled and diverse Jr. Environmental Technician to join our growing team. We offer a challenging yet rewarding career opportunity where your contribution is highly visible and valued. Comco is committed to fostering a supportive environment that prioritizes safety, teamwork, and professional development.

Core Duties and Responsibilities:

Under the guidance of Project Managers and the Engineering Team, you will assist with:

  • Environmental field activities including soil, groundwater, surface water, and vapour sampling.
  • Environmental monitoring during drilling, excavation, remediation, and construction activities.
  • Supporting Phase I & Phase II Environmental Site Assessments (ESAs).
  • Assisting with installation, monitoring, and decommissioning of groundwater wells.
  • Oversight of soil handling, segregation, and disposal at petroleum and industrial sites.
  • Supporting remediation activities and coordination with contractors.
  • Completing daily field notes, chain-of-custody forms, and health & safety documentation.
  • Data entry and report preparation during office-based periods.
  • Adhere to all health, safety, and environmental requirements.
  • A mix of outdoor fieldwork (60-80%) and indoor laboratory/office environments (20-40%).
  • Multi-site travel with early mornings, late nights, or occasional on-call emergency response shifts.

Education & Experience

  • Diploma or Degree in:
  • Environmental Technician / Technology
  • Environmental Science / Environmental Studies
  • Engineering Technology (Civil, Environmental, or related)
  • Or a related environmental discipline
  • New graduates are encouraged to apply
  • Co-op, summer, or field experience is an asset but not required

Skills Required:

  • High attention to detail.
  • Strong critical-thinking.
  • Good communication skills and a team-oriented attitude.
  • Strong report writing skills.
  • Ability to strictly follow scientific methodologies and safety protocols.
  • Ability to follow procedures, document work accurately, and take direction.
  • Ability to work outdoors in various weather conditions.
  • Valid G Driver’s Licence and ability to travel to project sites across Ontario.

Pay: $25.00-$27.00 per hour
