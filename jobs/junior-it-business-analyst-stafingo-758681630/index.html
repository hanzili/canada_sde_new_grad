Junior IT Business Analyst – Public Sector | Hybrid | Ontario

Stafingo is Hiring!

Stafingo is recruiting on behalf of a confidential Public Sector client in Ontario for a Junior IT Business Analyst to support a portfolio of ongoing and upcoming digital transformation and technology initiatives within a large enterprise environment.

This is an excellent opportunity for an early-career Business Analyst who is looking to build experience working on enterprise IT projects while collaborating with business stakeholders, technical teams, and project leadership in a dynamic Public Sector environment.

If you are passionate about business analysis, requirements gathering, process improvement, and delivering technology solutions that make a real impact, we'd love to hear from you.

Position Details

  • Job Title: Junior IT Business Analyst
  • Employment Type: Contract
  • Industry: Public Sector
  • Contract Duration: August 3, 2026 – August 2, 2027
  • Location: Hybrid (2-3 days in office at the manager's discretion)
  • Work Location: Orillia, Ontario
  • Submission Deadline: Tuesday, July 14th, 2026 – 5:00 PM EST

What You will Do :

As a Junior IT Business Analyst, you will support enterprise technology initiatives by working closely with business stakeholders, subject matter experts, project teams, and technical resources to understand business needs, document requirements, improve business processes, and contribute to successful project delivery.

You will participate throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and help ensure technology solutions align with business objectives.

Key Responsibilities :

  • Assist with business analysis activities across multiple enterprise IT projects.
  • Gather, analyze, document, and validate business and system requirements.
  • Facilitate stakeholder interviews, workshops, JAD sessions, and requirements-gathering meetings.
  • Prepare Business Requirements Documents (BRDs), use cases, process maps, workflows, business rules, and functional specifications.
  • Support business process analysis, business process mapping, and business process improvement initiatives.
  • Participate in business modeling, conceptual and logical data modeling, process flow modeling, and business event modeling.
  • Assist with feasibility studies, business cases, cost-benefit analyses, and strategic recommendations.
  • Develop and maintain traceability matrices and other business analysis deliverables.
  • Collaborate with project managers, developers, architects, QA teams, and business stakeholders throughout project delivery.
  • Monitor project progress, identify issues, and communicate updates to project leadership.
  • Participate in Agile ceremonies and support projects delivered using Agile, structured, and object-oriented methodologies.
  • Prepare presentations, briefing materials, documentation, and project artifacts.
  • Ensure solutions align with organizational standards, enterprise architecture, SDLC, and project governance.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement of business analysis practices and documentation standards.

Required Qualifications :

  • Minimum 2 years of experience as an IT Business Analyst .
  • Experience working throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) .
  • Experience gathering, documenting, analyzing, and validating business requirements.
  • Experience with structured, object-oriented, and Agile methodologies .
  • Experience developing performance measures and supporting business analysis evaluations.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, facilitation, and documentation skills.
  • Experience preparing:
  • Business Requirements Documents (BRDs)
  • Functional Specifications
  • Use Cases
  • Business Process Maps
  • Workflows
  • Traceability Matrices
  • Experience conducting stakeholder engagement sessions and requirements workshops.
  • Ability to translate business requirements into technical specifications.
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines.

Preferred Qualifications :

  • Previous Public Sector experience.
  • Experience preparing business cases, feasibility studies, and cost-benefit analyses.
  • Experience using CASE (Computer-Aided Software Engineering) tools.
  • Experience with:
  • Business Process Modeling
  • Business Functional Modeling
  • Business Event Modeling
  • Data Flow Modeling
  • Information Management principles
  • Familiarity with enterprise architecture and SDLC methodologies.
  • Knowledge of the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and accessibility standards.
  • Awareness of emerging business analysis methodologies and technology trends.

Why Join This Opportunity?

  • Work on meaningful Public Sector digital transformation initiatives.
  • Gain valuable enterprise-level Business Analysis experience.
  • Collaborate with experienced technology and business professionals.
  • Hybrid work environment supporting work-life balance.
  • Competitive hourly compensation.
  • Long-term contract with potential extension.
  • Opportunity to grow your Business Analysis career within a large Public Sector organization.

Apply Today

If you are a motivated Junior IT Business Analyst looking to grow your career while contributing to impactful Public Sector technology initiatives, we'd love to hear from you.

Apply directly through LinkedIn job posting or send your resume to harpreet@stafingo.com for immediate consideration.
