Full Description

At Raymond James, we develop, we collaborate, we decide, we deliver, and we improve together .

Raymond James Ltd. is Canada’s leading independent investment dealers offering high quality investment products and services to Canadians seeking customized solutions to their wealth management needs.

Equity Research Associate Intern (Fall 2026) How does the role impact the organization? The Equity Capital Markets division, a team of analysts covering approximately 250 companies, has a solid commitment to investment research, investment banking, and institutional sales and trading. Associates play a critical role assisting in all tasks required for the Equity Research Analyst to stay abreast of market events with the end-goal of providing sound investment judgement on particular stocks to clients. Individuals in this role are continuously learning and developing their investment analysis and securities evaluation skills in a fast-paced and team-oriented environment. We are looking for an exceptional candidate who has exemplary writing skills and is an excellent communicator.

What will your role be responsible for?

  • Work with analysts to conduct thorough analysis of the industries and targeted companies; and
  • Participate in due diligence requirements analyzing industry and company data, developing and maintaining financial models, writing research, and verbal presentations to equity sales/trading personnel and institutional clients.

What do we expect from you? To qualify for this opportunity you possess:

  • An understanding of accounting and financial analysis principles including financial modelling
  • Financial modelling skills, preferred;
  • The ability to work within deadlines in a high-volume and demanding environment while maintaining an imperative attention to detail;
  • Good organizational skills and the ability to prioritize;
  • Willingness to work long hours when necessary to meet time-sensitive deadlines; and
  • An outgoing personality that will fit into a high-performing, team-oriented environment.

Educational/Previous Experience Requirements

  • Working towards or have an undergraduate/postgraduate degree, preferably in Business, Economics, or Accounting;
  • Intern experience in equity research, corporate finance or relevant industry preferred;

We encourage our associates at all levels to:

  • Grow professionally and inspire others to do the same
  • Work with and through others to achieve desired outcomes
  • Make prompt, pragmatic choices and act with the client in mind
  • Take ownership and hold themselves and others accountable for delivering results that matter
  • Contribute to the continuous evolution of the firm
