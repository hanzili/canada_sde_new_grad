About the Role

About Scotlynn

Multinational produce grower and transportation provider

Full Description

Please note this is an onsite position - eligible candidates must be able to reliably commute to our Corporate Office in Vittoria, ON for this position.

With the continued growth in the industry, Scotlynn is currently hiring a Junior Software Developer at our Corporate Office in Vittoria, ON. This role is for an individual who is passionate about technology, problem-solving, and continuous learning. The ideal candidate is a curious and motivated team player with strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and communication skills.

ABOUT US

Scotlynn is a multinational produce grower and transportation provider specializing in time-sensitive refrigerated products. Every aspect of our operation highlights our passion for doing it better – our attention to detail, our open communication, our state-of-the-art equipment, and our comprehensive farming procedures.

THE ROLE

The Junior Software Developer is responsible for supporting the design, development, testing, and maintenance of software applications and business systems that support operations across the organization. This role will work closely with the IT Team and business stakeholders to develop scalable solutions, enhance existing applications, and assist with technology initiatives that improve business efficiency.

ABOUT YOU

Successful Scotlynn Candidates Have:

  • Post-secondary education in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field
  • Knowledge of one or more programming languages such as C#, Java, Python, or JavaScript
  • Basic understanding of software development principles, databases, SQL, and application development
  • Understanding of software testing, debugging, and troubleshooting techniques
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, and communication skills
  • Ability to learn new technologies quickly and work effectively both independently and as part of a team

HOW YOU’LL SUCCEED WITH SCOTLYNN

  • Assist in the design, development, testing, and deployment of software applications and internal business systems
  • Write, maintain, and troubleshoot application code following development standards
  • Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to develop technical solutions
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to continuous improvement of software development processes
  • Support the maintenance and enhancement of existing applications, integrations, and web-based solutions
  • Investigate, diagnose, and resolve software defects and technical issues
  • Develop and maintain technical documentation for applications, APIs, processes, and system configurations
  • Assist with software testing, quality assurance, and user acceptance testing activities
  • Work with databases and APIs to support application functionality and data integration requirements
  • Stay current with emerging technologies, programming practices, and software development methodologies

WHAT WE OFFER

  • Competitive Benefits Plan and Employee and Family Assistance Plan
  • $55,000 - $70,000, based on experience
  • Full gym access with personal trainer
  • Corporate chef providing healthy meal options
  • Opportunities to strengthen local communities through “Community Volunteer Program” initiatives
  • Rewarding, collaborative and lively workplace culture
  • Paid Time Off

YOUR COMMITMENT

  • This is a full-time, permanent role
  • Work in office from 8am – 5pm, Monday – Friday

HOW TO APPLY

Interested in the role or want to learn more? Submit your application online. Our team will contact applicants that have been selected for an interview.

Accommodations are available on request for candidates taking part in all aspects of the selection process. For any questions or accommodation requests, please reach out to our onboarding team at can.hiring@scotlynn.com or 1-800-263-9117 ext 2535. All candidates are encouraged to apply.
