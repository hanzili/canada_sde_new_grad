Full Description

Entry Level Account Executive

Vancouver, BC

Full-Time | In-Person

Starting your career doesn't have to mean spending your days behind a desk.

If you're looking for a role where you can meet new people, build confidence, develop valuable sales and communication skills, and be rewarded for your hard work, we'd love to hear from you.

Convertiqq is growing, and we're looking for ambitious individuals to join our team as Entry Level Account Executives. Whether you're just finishing school, graduating from college or university, leaving retail or hospitality, or simply looking for a fresh challenge, we'll provide the training and support to help you succeed.

No previous experience is required—we're much more interested in your attitude, work ethic, and willingness to learn.

What You'll Be Doing

As an Entry Level Account Executive, you'll work directly with customers through face-to-face sales campaigns, helping represent well-known brands while developing practical business experience.

Your role will include:

  • Meeting new customers and creating positive first impressions
  • Learning how to build genuine relationships through great communication
  • Introducing products, services, and promotional offers with confidence
  • Helping customers find solutions that best suit their needs
  • Developing sales skills through real customer interactions rather than classroom learning
  • Working closely with experienced team members who will support your development
  • Contributing towards individual and team sales goals while celebrating success together

Every day is different, giving you the opportunity to meet new people, build your confidence, and continuously develop your skills.

Why People Choose Convertiqq

Many of our team started with little or no sales experience.

What they did have was a positive attitude, a willingness to learn, and the ambition to build something bigger than just another job.

From day one, you'll receive coaching and mentorship while gaining experience in:

  • Customer engagement
  • Face-to-face sales
  • Communication and presentation
  • Business development
  • Relationship building
  • Goal setting and personal development
  • Leadership fundamentals

What You'll Receive

  • Full training from experienced mentors
  • Weekly pay plus performance incentives
  • Ongoing coaching and professional development
  • A supportive, social, and ambitious team culture
  • Networking opportunities
  • Business travel opportunities for top performers
  • Career progression based on performance—not time served

We'd Love To Hear From You If You

  • Enjoy working with people
  • Bring positive energy and enthusiasm
  • Want to develop confidence and communication skills
  • Thrive in team environments
  • Are motivated to learn, grow, and challenge yourself

Experience in retail, hospitality, customer service, sport, or sales can be beneficial, but it's certainly not essential.

Ready To Start Something Bigger?

If you're looking for an opportunity where you'll be supported, challenged, and given the chance to build valuable skills while working with great people, we'd love to hear from you.

Apply today to join Convertiqq's growing Vancouver team.

Applications are reviewed regularly, with shortlisted candidates typically contacted within 2–3 business days.
