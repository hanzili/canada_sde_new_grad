About Iko

Full Description

IKO Industries Ltd. is a market leader in the manufacturing of roofing and building materials. IKO is a Canadian owned and operated business with production facilities worldwide and has many years of unparalleled success in the roofing materials industry. Quality, integrity, and trustworthiness are the values that underlie this success, and we have built this company by hiring people who hold these values. People like you!

Job Description

We are currently seeking a Process Engineering Co-Op student to join our process engineering team located at 71 Orenda Road, Brampton, ON beginning in the Fall 2026 term.

When you join us you will;

  • Assist in the creation of process flow charts, work instructions and control plans using the companies Visual Standard work instruction format.
  • Support the Process Engineer in optimizing plant performance
  • Assist in troubleshooting of plant issues and, assist in the development of long and short term solutions utilizing Process Failure Modes and Effects Analysis.
  • Utilize the companies’ tools for handling process data for analysis and report daily on Plant issues.
  • Prepare and present summary reports, performance metrics reports, and graphical representation for presentations as required
  • Collect data for analysis to uncover areas for improvements and innovation
  • Perform other duties as assigned

Our ideal candidate would be:

  • 3rd or 4th year student enrolled in a Mechanical or Industrial Engineering program.
  • Prior Co-Op/Intern experience preferred.
  • Excellent organization and analytical skills
  • Strong communication (written, verbal, and presentation) and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to establish priorities and work effectively in a team
  • Works well independently

Hours/ Days / Schedule

Hours of work will be primarily Monday through Friday {30 – 40 hours, day shift}.
