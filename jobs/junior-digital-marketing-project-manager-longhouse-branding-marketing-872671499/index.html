Full Description

About the Role

Job type: Full-Time, In-Person

Location : Langley, British Columbia

Compensation : $50,545 - $60,645 annually + benefits, based on experience, agency background, and demonstrated ability across Longhouse services

The Junior Digital Marketing Project Manager is the main point of contact for helping our clients Get Seen with SEO, AEO and Digital Advertising. You’ll build and maintain strong client relationships, bridging the gap between clients and our specialists by turning client goals into clear SEO, AEO, and Advertising tasks. You will build trust through organized meetings, task delegation, and timely updates.

"As a Junior Project Manager, your clarity, curiosity, empathy, and proactive initiative helps our busy partners keep Marketing off their to-do lists and help the Longhouse Team by keeping projects clear, organized and effectively delegated. You make your biggest impact by helping partners and the team focus on what needs them most.”

— Keenan Beavis, Founder

What You'll Do

Client Communication & Relationship Management

  • Communicate proactively and build strong, lasting relationships with the clients in your portfolio.
  • As their main point of contact at Longhouse, keep them informed and confident across their SEO, AEO, and Advertising.

Presenting Results & Recommendations

  • Present results to clients with confidence, and back every recommendation with research and data. Lead clients to the next best step so they feel ready to act.

Onboarding and Intake

  • Lead onboarding for new clients and take time to understand their goals, audience, and services. Set expectations so clients see results as the partnership begins.

Strategy & Planning

  • Shape the SEO, AEO, and advertising strategy for each client and adjust it as their needs and results change.

Team Direction & Delegation

  • Provide project briefs and strategy direction to the specialist team, giving them the context they need to do great work.
  • As a dependable member of the team, you’ll delegate work to the right people and support them through delivery.

Quality and Standards

  • Maintain a high standard of quality and review key deliverables before they reach clients.
  • Give helpful feedback and make sure every piece meets the Longhouse standard.

Account Monitoring, Retention, and Growth

  • Monitor the health of every account to protect retention and support growth.
  • Identify risks and expand accounts where added services genuinely help the client.

Internal Project Management

  • Contribute as a project manager internally on Longhouse initiatives that help grow our business. Including all project management phases such as interview (problem identification, research, team coordination, delegation, follow-through and evaluation

Required Experience

  • 0-2 years managing a portfolio of client accounts in a digital marketing agency, with retention results you can speak to
  • Some experience leading client meetings or projects
  • Some SEO and paid advertising knowledge to guide strategy, including campaign structure, keyword strategy, and budget pacing
  • Confidence reading GA4, Microsoft Clarity, Google Ads, and reporting dashboards, then turning the numbers into recommendations
  • Experience briefing and coordinating specialist teams through a project management tool such as Monday, ClickUp, or Asana

Bonus Qualifications

  • Hands-on AEO or GEO work, with a feel for how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews select and cite content
  • A record of growing accounts through expanded scope or added services
  • Familiarity with a CRM and marketing stack such as Pipedrive, HubSpot, or Mailchimp
  • Degree in marketing, business, or communications, or equivalent hands-on experience

Why Longhouse

When you join Longhouse, you join a team of Branding and Marketing experts focused on results for partners across North America. The Longhouse team works from shared values and everyday principles. We prioritize innovation, leadership, passion, and play, proud to create an environment where people can show up as themselves and contribute at a high level.

How to Apply

Send your resume and cover letter to careers@longhouse.co with the subject line: Digital Marketing Project Manager Application. The address ends in .co.

In your cover letter, share:

  • Your experience managing multiple client accounts
  • How you explain performance and next steps to non-technical stakeholders
  • An example of how you’ve improved an internal workflow or made delivery more consistent
  • What “excellent account management” looks like in a SEO, AEO, and advertising context

We'll be in touch with qualified candidates to schedule a phone screen

Our Hiring Process

Here's what to expect after you apply:

  1. Application review. We review every application and reach out to candidates whose experience and working style look like a strong fit.
  2. Phone screen. A short call (15 to 20 minutes) with a member of our team to learn more about you and share more about the role.
  3. Interviews. In-person conversations at our Langley office with the hiring team.
  4. Reference check. We'll ask for two to three professional references before extending an offer.
  5. Offer. If it's a fit on both sides, we'll put together an offer and walk you through the details.

We aim to keep the process straightforward and respectful of your time. If you have questions at any stage, ask anytime.
