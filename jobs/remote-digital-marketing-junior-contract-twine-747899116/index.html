About the Role

About Twine

Premium furniture and heritage workwear brand

Full Description

This role focuses on driving online sales and brand visibility for a premium furniture and men’s heritage workwear brand. The successful candidate will leverage existing high-quality photography and video assets to execute targeted digital marketing campaigns, manage paid advertising, and optimize the e-commerce store. The position is fully remote and ideal for a marketing professional with a strong background in retail, fashion, or home goods. While this is a single project, there is potential for ongoing collaboration based on performance.

Deliverables

  • Develop and implement digital marketing strategies tailored to the furniture and apparel sectors
  • Distribute and promote existing media assets across relevant digital channels
  • Manage and optimize paid advertising campaigns (e.g., Google Ads, Meta Ads)
  • Monitor campaign performance and adjust tactics to maximize ROI and drive sales
  • Optimize the online store for improved user experience and conversion rates
  • Provide regular performance reports and actionable insights

Requirements

  • Proven experience in digital marketing for e-commerce, preferably in retail, fashion, or home goods
  • Strong understanding of paid advertising platforms and campaign management
  • Proficiency in using analytics tools to track and report on campaign performance
  • Ability to work independently and deliver results within tight deadlines
  • Excellent communication and organizational skills
  • Experience with Shopify or similar e-commerce platforms is a plus
  • Availability for remote work and flexible scheduling
