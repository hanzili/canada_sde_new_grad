Administrative Assistant – Small Business Advisory Firm
About Time Risk and Retirement Consulting | Calgary, AB
30 hours/week | Competitive compensation ranges based on experience

Hybrid in office and work from home.

We are seeking a reliable and growth-oriented Administrative Assistant to join our integrated advisory team. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a learning environment and is genuinely curious about financial matters, client service, and helping others to achieve inevitable success.

Successful candidates will demonstrate:

  • Strong curiosity about financial, tax, retirement, and business topics
  • Compassionate, empathetic, and kind approach to clients and colleagues
  • Ability to grow rapidly from novice to mastery in new skills

Prerequisites:

  • Excellent written and verbal English proficiency
  • Clean police background check (RCMP/CPIC + Vulnerable Sector Check)
  • Valid Alberta Driver’s License with clean driver’s abstract
  • Grade 12 education or equivalent
  • Professional office appropriate appearance, dress, and deportment

Key Responsibilities:

  • Coordinate workflow, scheduling, and priorities
  • Maintain accurate records and administrative systems
  • Support consultants, accountants, and advisors
  • Manage time tracking, compliance documentation, and client communications
  • Handle confidential information with the highest integrity

Valued Skills & Experience:

  • Proficient with Google Drive, Outlook/Gmail, Excel, Word, QuickBooks, Canva, Signority, Adobe, and similar tools
  • Strong online research and organizational skills
  • Polite, professional, and clear communication

What Sets You Apart:

  • Adaptive learning mindset and personal accountability
  • Lighthearted optimism and resilience under pressure
  • Integrity, humility, curiosity, courage, and tenacity
  • Ability to manage challenging client situations with grace

We honor time as our most precious resource and build genuine, long-term relationships with our curated clients — Canadian SME businesses, individuals, and families. This is more than an admin role; it’s an opportunity to grow within a values-driven advisory practice that delivers tangible results.

If this resonates with you, please send your resume. Include a note or message sharing how your values align with ours, and why you'd be a good fit. Share what inevitable success means to you and how being on the About Time team enhances your inevitable success. We will reply to every response.

Pay: $20.00-$32.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Extended health care
  • Flexible schedule
  • On-site parking
  • Paid time off
  • RRSP match

Education:

  • Secondary School (required)

Language:

  • English (required)

Licence/Certification:

  • Alberta Driver's License (required)

Work Location: Hybrid remote in Calgary, AB T2Z 3R6
