About Avello Inc

Full Description

We’re looking for a driven and detail-oriented Jr. Recruiter to join our energetic and growing team at Avello ! If you love a fast-paced environment and enjoy a mix of recruiting, admin work, and social media support, this is a great opportunity to grow your career.

⭐ What You’ll Do

Recruiting & Onboarding

  • Source candidates on Indeed, LinkedIn, and other platforms
  • Conduct pre-screening calls and schedule interviews
  • Support and streamline the onboarding process for new hires

Administrative Support

  • Maintain internal databases and filing systems
  • Manage office supplies and general office operations
  • Provide a professional and welcoming environment for guests and staff

Social Media Support

  • Help create and schedule content for company's social media
  • Promote job postings and office events
  • Contribute to building our online presence

What You Bring

  • Excellent communication skills (written + verbal)
  • Strong organizational and multitasking abilities
  • Proficiency with Google Workspace
  • Experience in recruiting, administration, or reception is an asset
  • Familiarity with social platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn
  • Self-starter attitude and comfort working in a high-energy environment

Preferred Qualifications

  • Strong interpersonal skills, outstanding work ethic
  • Impressive and professional image and demeanor
  • Must be extremely organized
  • Intermediate/Advanced MS Excel skills
  • Participate in extra-curricular team events (company paid for)
  • Self-starter who is career driven

What We Offer

  • A supportive and upbeat team culture
  • Conferences, team events, and team-building opportunities
  • Room for growth within the company
  • Travel opportunities
  • Quarterly incentives

If you're excited to bring your recruiting skills to a dynamic, team-focused environment, we’d love to connect!
