Full Description

About Us

Decorama is a custom cabinetry design and build company serving the Greater Toronto Area for over 20 years. We specialize in high-quality kitchens, vanities, and built-ins, providing tailored solutions for homeowners, interior designers, contractors, and builders.
We are expanding and looking for a Kitchen Designer/Project Manager to join our team. This role is ideal for someone who is passionate about cabinetry design and highly organized in managing projects from concept to completion.

Key ResponsibilitiesDesign & Drafting

  • Create detailed and functional kitchen and cabinetry designs using AutoCAD
  • Produce plans, elevations, and shop-ready drawings
  • Prepare Mozaik files and cut lists (Mozaik experience is a strong asset)
  • Collaborate with clients and designers to revise and finalize design packages

Project Management

  • Oversee projects from initial design through production, installation, and final walkthrough
  • Coordinate with shop staff, CNC operators, installers, contractors, and designers
  • Track materials, hardware orders, timelines, and missing items
  • Prepare daily or weekly updates on project progress

Client & Contractor Coordination

  • Communicate professionally with homeowners, designers, and contractors
  • Schedule site visits, follow-ups, and installation dates
  • Ensure clients are always updated on timelines and any changes

Showroom & Administrative Duties

  • Maintain a clean, organized, and professional showroom
  • Answer inquiries, book consultations, and manage client files
  • Assist with quotes, pricing, and order verification
  • Prepare job folders, material lists, and project checklists

Site Visits

  • Conduct accurate site measurements and verify site conditions
  • Ensure installations follow design intent
  • Create deficiency lists and coordinate resolutions

Skills & Experience

  • 1+ year of experience in kitchen design, cabinetry, interior design, or related field
  • AutoCAD proficiency is required
  • Experience with Mozaik Software is a major asset
  • Strong understanding of cabinetry construction, measurements, and layout
  • Excellent organizational and multitasking abilities
  • Strong communication skills and ability to work with clients and industry partners
  • Ability to calculate measurements, pricing, and design requirements accurately
  • Proficiency in MS Office and Excel

Schedule

  • Full-time (30–40 hours per week)
  • 8-hour shifts
  • In-person role at our Toronto showroom

Job Types: Full-time, Part-time

Pay: $18.00-$25.00 per hour

Work Location: In person
