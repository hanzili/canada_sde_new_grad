📢 YouTube Channel Growth & Content Intern

Chair Massage ToGo

Remote (Canada)

About Us

Chair Massage ToGo provides professional on-site chair massage services for workplaces, conferences, trade shows, exhibitions, job fairs, and special events across Canada.

We are seeking a motivated and creative intern to help develop, manage, and grow our YouTube channel while gaining hands-on experience in content marketing, video strategy, and digital brand growth.

Position Title:

YouTube Channel Growth & Content Intern

Responsibilities:

  1. YouTube Channel Management:

  2. Manage and maintain the Chair Massage ToGo YouTube channel

  3. Upload, organize, and optimize videos
  4. Create engaging video titles, descriptions, and thumbnails
  5. Organize playlists and improve channel structure

2. Content Development:

  • Identify content opportunities aligned with company goals
  • Assist in developing video content ideas and content calendars
  • Repurpose existing content into YouTube-friendly formats
  • Coordinate with video editors and marketing team members

3.YouTube Growth Strategy:

  • Research YouTube best practices and industry trends
  • Recommend strategies to increase subscribers, watch time, and engagement
  • Support the implementation of channel growth initiatives
  • Monitor competitor channels and identify opportunities

4. Analytics & Reporting

  • Track channel performance and key metrics
  • Monitor views, engagement, subscribers, and audience behavior
  • Prepare monthly growth reports and recommendations

5. Brand Awareness

  • Help position Chair Massage ToGo as a leading provider of workplace and event chair massage services
  • Support educational and promotional video campaigns

Ideal For Students In:

  • Marketing
  • Digital Marketing
  • Communications
  • Business Administration
  • Media Studies
  • Public Relations
  • Content Creation
  • Related Fields

What You Will Learn:

  • YouTube channel management
  • Video marketing and content strategy
  • SEO for YouTube
  • Audience growth and engagement techniques
  • Digital branding and online marketing
  • Analytics and performance tracking
  • Working directly with a growing wellness company

What You Will Receive:

  • Hands-on experience managing a real business YouTube channel
  • Portfolio-building opportunities
  • Reference letter upon successful completion
  • LinkedIn recommendation
  • Practical experience in digital marketing and content growth

How to Apply

Please apply through LinkedIn or send your resume along with a short message explaining your interest in YouTube marketing and content creation to:

relax@chairmassagetogo.com
