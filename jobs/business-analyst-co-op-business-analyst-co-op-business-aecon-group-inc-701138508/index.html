What is the Opportunity?

Safer. Faster. Smarter. This is how Aecon Utilities is committed to delivering projects. Aecon Utilities is a leading North American utility infrastructure provider with a strong track record delivering safe, reliable and innovative solutions across electrical, telecommunications, pipeline distribution and renewable energy markets. With operations across North America, Aecon Utilities supports critical utility infrastructure that powers and keeps communities connected. Guided by our core value of Safety Always, we partner with clients to build the infrastructure that powers the future.

Reporting to the Senior Analyst, the Business Analyst Co-op Student will assist in performing audits on process compliance within the various operations of the Utilities business unit.

Location: Etobicoke, Head Office

Duration: 12 - 16 months

What You'll Do Here:

  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather and document current business processes
  • Observe and report deviations from standard operating procedures
  • Determine root causes for deviations and perform impact analyses
  • Present safety and utilization metrics on weekly equipment calls to operations teams
  • Create process documentation, including flowcharts and standard operating procedures
  • Identify improvement opportunities; develop and present recommendations to management and monitor their implementation
  • Determine what processes are being followed and if purported benefits have been realized
  • Work with management to develop solutions and provide value-added services for Utilities’ dynamic operational environment

What You Bring to the Team:

  • Must be able to work well within a team environment as well as individually
  • Strong organizational and time management skills with an ability to multi-task
  • Ability to adhere to project schedules and meet deadlines
  • Microsoft Office proficient – with strong Excel, PowerPoint, and Word skills
  • Experience working with SAP is preferred
  • Excellent communication skills, both written and oral, and the confidence and ability to work with all levels of staff and management within a large organization
  • Experience in the utilities industry an asset
  • Methodical and thorough approach to diagnosing problems

Salary: $20 - $25 per hour

Individual pay is determined based on several factors such as work location, education, experience and unique skills.
