Full Description

Industry Research Associate

Health & Wellness Event

Location: Fully Remote  |  Employment: 2 Month Contract, Part-Time | Hours: 20-30 hours/week  |

Start: Immediate

About the Role

We are developing an exciting new premium event focused on health, wellness, longevity, and healthy living.

We are looking for an Industry Research Associate to help us conduct research interviews with professionals, founders, practitioners, and leaders across the health and wellness space.

This is not a sales role. This role is about having thoughtful conversations, asking strong questions, uncovering meaningful insights, and helping shape an event the industry genuinely wants to attend.

What You’ll Do

  • Conduct one-on-one research interviews by phone and Zoom.
  • Speak with professionals across the health and wellness industry.
  • Identify key trends, priorities, and content ideas.
  • Help uncover potential speakers, partners, and advisory board members.
  • Summarize interview insights into clear notes and recommendations.
  • Support the early development of a major new health and wellness event.

Who You’ll Speak With

  • Wellness professionals
  • Healthcare practitioners
  • Fitness and recovery businesses
  • Clinic owners and operators
  • Nutrition and supplement companies
  • Health technology companies
  • Corporate wellness professionals
  • Entrepreneurs, founders, industry experts, and thought leaders

What We’re Looking For

  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Comfortable speaking with people by phone and Zoom.
  • Naturally curious and able to ask thoughtful questions.
  • Excellent listening and note-taking skills.
  • Organized, professional, and detail-oriented.
  • Interested in health, wellness, business, and emerging trends.

Relevant Experience

  • Research, consulting, recruitment, business development, partnerships, journalism, customer success, or sales experience is an asset.
  • Curiosity, professionalism, and strong communication skills matter most.

Why Join Us?

  • Work fully remotely with flexible scheduling.
  • Help launch a major new health and wellness event from the ground up.
  • Speak with senior leaders, founders, practitioners, and industry experts.
  • Gain experience in research, conference development, and market insights.
  • Contribute directly to the strategy and direction of a new event.

How to Apply

Please send your resume and a brief note explaining why this opportunity interests you and what makes you a strong fit for the role.
