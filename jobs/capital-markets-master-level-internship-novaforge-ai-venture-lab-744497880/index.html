About the Role

About NovaForge / AI Venture Lab

AI finance startup with market analytics focus

Full Description

We are looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Capital Markets - Business Development & Strategy Intern to join our team within the Business Development department. This role is perfect for a Master’s level student with a strong interest in financial markets and strategic analysis. As a Capital Markets Intern, you will work closely with our business team to analyze market trends, support investment strategies, assist in building relationships with key stakeholders, and contribute to strategic initiatives that drive business growth and opportunities within the capital markets space.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Conduct market research and analysis on capital markets trends, investment opportunities, and financial instruments.
  • Assist in evaluating market performance data to identify risks, opportunities, and areas for strategic decision-making.
  • Support the development of market insights and financial models to analyze investment opportunities.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to assist in the execution of capital market strategies.
  • Prepare reports and presentations summarizing financial data, market trends, and investment findings for leadership review.
  • Monitor market developments, competitor activity, and regulatory changes to ensure informed decision-making.
  • Support the preparation of investor communications and strategic recommendations related to market opportunities.

About You:

  • Currently enrolled or recently graduated from a Master’s program in Business Administration, Economics, Finance, or a related field. Master’s program enrollment or completion is mandatory.
  • Strong understanding of capital markets, investment strategies, and financial instruments.
  • Experience with data analysis and financial modeling tools (e.g., Excel, Bloomberg, SQL, or Power BI).
  • Familiarity with market research, equity analysis, and portfolio management concepts.
  • Knowledge of financial statements, risk assessment, and market trends.
  • Ability to work both independently and collaboratively.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
  • New graduates are encouraged to apply.

We believe exceptional talent often emerges from diverse paths. If you possess a profound curiosity, a genuine passion for continuous personal and professional growth, and a strong desire to apply your unique abilities to create significant impact within our team, we strongly encourage you to apply—even if your background doesn’t align perfectly with every single qualification.

The Vosyn internship is unique. Remuneration is equivalent to $32.00 CAD per hour payable in common shares of the Corporation at the then fair market value. For greater clarity, NO cash in-hand (in hourly rate or salary) will be paid to the Intern. Instead, Vosyn being an AI start-up, the upside is in potential future value of said shares based on the hours worked, billed and approved.

Pay: $32.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Flexible schedule

Work Location: Remote
