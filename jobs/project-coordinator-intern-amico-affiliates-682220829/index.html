About Amico Affiliates

Full Description

Job Description This role supports the development and coordination of technical proposals, including EOIs, RFIs, RFQs, and RFPs. The successful candidate will collaborate with internal and external teams to create compelling proposal content, coordinate submission materials, maintain proposal databases, and support presentations and post-submission activities.

Responsibilities

  • Work collaboratively with the Proposal Team and external/internal team members in the development of all technical proposal pursuits including Expressions of Interest (EOI); Re-quests for Information (RFI), Requests for Qualifications (RFQ); and Requests for Proposal (RFP)
  • Contribute clearly articulated and persuasive written and visual content that identifies Amico’s value differentiators while complying with technical requirements and marketing specifications
  • Engage in proposal development discussions and activities with internal and external team members coordinating efforts as required.
  • Consult with clients by generating and submitting request for clarifications to inform pro-posals.
  • Research proposal opportunities to understand the client, project team, and project in-sights
  • Coordinate graphic design and layout to ensure a visually appealing and professional submission
  • Manage post-submission activities such as clarifications, presentations, and debriefs.
  • Liaise with internal subject matter experts for their written content or interview them for in-formation to generate content.
  • Maintain various databases for company information, project information, pursuit updates proposal templates, resumes, project case studies, and corporate qualifications.
  • Develop PowerPoint presentations as required
  • Developing and managing project/pursuit-specific SharePoint sites.

Qualifications Post secondary education in engineering, architecture, communications, marketing, or other related field with an avid interest and capability in writing

  • Proven experience in succeeding in a team environment
  • Experience working with teams of individuals with varied professional backgrounds
  • Completion of complex projects with a significant amount of virtual communication
  • Advanced communication skills both written and oral
  • Working knowledge of Adobe InDesign, MS Word, PowerPoint
  • Ability to demonstrate leadership, even in situations without authority
  • Position requires proactive skillsets and a level of self-motivation to achieve a successful completion to complex interactions

About Us Amico is a leading, Canadian-owned and operated construction company specializing in large-scale infrastructure projects. With a strong track record in general contracting, civil engineering and road building, we deliver complex infrastructure solutions that shape the communities of Ontario. Our expertise spans major infrastructure projects, including highways, bridges, and critical public works that require meticulous planning, innovative engineering, and precise execution.

At Amico, we believe in fostering collaboration and empowering our team to tackle ambitious challenges head-on. By leveraging cutting-edge technology and proven project management practices, we consistently deliver high-quality infrastructure projects, regardless of their scale or complexity.

At Amico we don’t just build projects; we build careers, communities, and a lasting legacy. Being part of our team means playing a pivotal role in shaping Ontario’s most impactful infrastructure developments, while gaining hands-on experience. Join us and be part of something bigger.

About The Team At Amico, we are committed to providing an environment of mutual respect where equal opportunities are available to all applicants and teammates. Accommodation is available upon request for applicants throughout the recruitment and selection process. We thank all applicants for their interest. However, only those selected for an interview will be contacted.
