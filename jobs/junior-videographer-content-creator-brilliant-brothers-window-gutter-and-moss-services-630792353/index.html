About the Role

Shoot, edit, and publish short-form content

About | Brilliant Brothers | Window, Gutter and Moss Services

Local service provider with growing video brand

Full Description

$25/hour | 20 to 40 hrs/week | Flexible Contract (1–6 Months)

Victoria, BC | Brilliant Brothers Services

Looking for someone to capture and edit high quality content for META and TikTok Reels

__________________________________________________

Are you fresh out of film school?

Or obsessed with capturing cinematic content?

Do you love turning raw footage into engaging short-form reels?

We are looking for a hungry, creative videographer who wants real-world action footage to build their portfolio.

Not coffee shop interviews.
Not staged indoor shoots.

Real action.

Roofs.
Rain.
Pressure washing.
Moss removal.
Window cleaning at heights.

You’ll be capturing high-energy, gritty, satisfying content that people actually want to watch.

__________________________________________________

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Driving to active job sites around Victoria
  • Filming crews performing roof cleaning, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, and soft washing
  • Capturing content in rain and sunshine
  • Shooting from the ground and sometimes from rooftops
  • Organizing and saving all raw footage properly
  • Editing short-form vertical reels for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
  • Creating engaging hooks and smooth transitions
  • Helping build a dominant local brand through content

__________________________________________________

Who This Is Perfect For

  • Recent film school or videography grads
  • Entry-level creators looking to build a serious portfolio
  • Someone who understands short-form content
  • Someone who studies viral reels and knows what holds attention
  • Someone organized with files and media storage
  • Someone comfortable around ladders and rooftops

You do not need 10 years of experience.

You do need initiative, creativity, and pride in your work.

__________________________________________________

What You’ll Get

  • $25 per hour
  • Flexible schedule (20–40 hours per week, your choice)
  • Contract length flexible (1–6 months)
  • A portfolio full of high-action outdoor footage
  • Real ad creative experience
  • Freedom to experiment and test ideas
  • A team that values creative input

This is not a boring corporate media job.

This is boots-on-the-ground brand building.

Requirements

__________________________________________________

  • Basic knowledge of video editing software (Premiere, Final Cut, CapCut, etc.)
  • Ability to shoot stable, well-framed footage
  • Understanding of vertical content formats
  • Valid driver’s license
  • Reliable transportation
  • Comfortable working outdoors

Bonus if you:

  • Have drone experience
  • Understand Meta ad creative
  • Love before-and-after transformations

__________________________________________________

Send us:

  • Your resume
  • A link to your portfolio or social media work (if you have one)
  • A short paragraph explaining why this job excites you

Show us your creativity.

We are building something big in Victoria and the right person will play a major role in shaping how the city sees us.
