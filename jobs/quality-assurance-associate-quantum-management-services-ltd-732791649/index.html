Full Description

Position: Quality Assurance Assosciate

Location: Brantford, ON (On-site)

Salary: $30 per hour

Job Type: 6 months (with potential for extension)

Posting Type: Open vacancy

Our client, a reputable organization in the manufacturing industry, is seeking a Quality Assurance Associate to support quality and compliance activities across its operations. The successful candidate will play a key role in maintaining quality systems, supporting regulatory compliance, and ensuring adherence to established quality standards and procedures.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Maintain and review controlled quality documentation, including SOPs, forms, and records.

  • Support quality investigations, deviations, CAPAs, and change control activities.

  • Assist with supplier qualification, audit readiness, and compliance documentation.

  • Coordinate training records, quality programs, and data tracking activities.

  • Monitor manufacturing operations to ensure compliance with quality and regulatory requirements.

  • Support internal audits, inspections, product release activities, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Qualifications & Experience:

  • Diploma or degree in Science, Quality, Life Sciences, or a related field.

  • 1-3 years of experience in a Quality Assurance or manufacturing environment.

  • Knowledge of GMP requirements and quality management systems.

  • Experience with investigations, CAPA, change control, or document control processes.

  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office and electronic quality management systems.

  • Strong attention to detail, organizational, and communication skills.
