Full Description

Join the Evoked Crew! Sales & Production Team Member Wanted

Hey you! Yes, you. Evoked is looking for a Production & Sales Team Member to join our growing team. This role sits at the intersection of design execution, production, and customer service. While graphic design skills are important, this is not a conceptual art or creative-direction role—it is a structured, deadline-driven position focused on accuracy, consistency, and efficiency.

If you enjoy using design tools like Photoshop in a real-world production environment and work well in a fast-moving team, this role may be your dream career.

What You’ll Be Doing:

  • Preparing and adjusting customer artwork using Adobe Photoshop for production
  • Executing repeatable design tasks accurately and efficiently
  • Producing physical products in our studio environment
  • Ensuring files and finished products meet quality standards
  • Working closely with the team to meet daily production deadlines
  • Communicating professionally with customers to clarify artwork or order details
  • Maintaining a clean, organized, and production-ready workspace
  • Safely using production tools and equipment (training provided)

You Might Be a Great Fit If You Are:

  • A go-getter with a "let’s do this" attitude
  • Have graphic design experience, particularly with Photoshop
  • Are comfortable working within defined templates, specs, and processes
  • Can balance design tasks with hands-on production work
  • Work well under time constraints and shifting priorities
  • Are detail-oriented and take pride in getting things right
  • Enjoy being part of a collaborative, supportive team
  • Retail experience is a bonus, but not a dealbreaker

What You’ll Learn (and Love):

  • Hands-on training in production, sales, and customer experience
  • Real-life skills in marketing, social media, and entrepreneurship
  • How trends are made and what makes a product pop
  • Experience working with clients, creatives, and business owners
  • Opportunities to grow as we grow!

Perks You’ll Enjoy:

  • Employee discounts on seriously cool stuff
  • A chance to be part of a growing, innovative company
  • A role where no 2 days are the same
  • Real opportunities to level up and grow your career
  • Opportunities to grow with a scaling business

Based in Calgary | Part retail, part production, all heart.

We’re not your average workplace—and we’re not looking for average. We value creativity as a skill, not as an open-ended process. Success in this role comes from applying design knowledge efficiently, meeting deadlines, and contributing reliably to the team.

If that sounds like the kind of design work you enjoy, we’d love to hear from you.… apply now and let’s make cool things together!

Job Types: Full-time, Part-time

Pay: $15.00-$18.00 per hour

Work Location: In person
