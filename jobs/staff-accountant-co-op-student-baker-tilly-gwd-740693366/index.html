Your role: 

The Staff Accountant Co-Op Student plays an important role in delivering an exceptional client experience by assisting with the production of high-quality deliverables in a timely manner, collaborating with colleagues and management, taking ownership of work assigned to them and assuming responsibility for their individual development during their placement.

Client service delivery

  • Complete various Audit, Review, and Compilation engagements individually and as part of a team.
  • Review client accounting and financial records for accuracy and compliance with accounting standards, procedures, and internal controls.
  • Perform technical work in the office or in the field on assigned engagements or portions of engagements, under supervision.
  • Support complex areas of larger audit and review engagements.
  • Maintain responsibility for accuracy and completion of assigned work and related documentation.
  • Prepare individual and corporate tax returns.
  • Cooperate with colleagues and management on file and engagement planning.
  • Address engagement issues by consulting appropriate resources and escalate relevant matters to the engagement supervisor when necessary.
  • Respond to client needs and address concerns proactively.
  • Complete assignments within set timelines, budget, and specifications.

Practice and professional development

  • Contribute to project teams implementing initiatives when requested.
  • Develop positive working relationships with colleagues
  • Identify inefficient processes and suggest improvements.
  • Acquire knowledge of support systems, software applications, computer systems, and firm standards.
  • Build experience in public accounting to support postsecondary studies.
  • Meet individual objectives for career development.
  • Remain open to receiving mentoring, guidance and feedback from team members with more experience
  • Develop communication skills for effective interaction with colleagues and clients.
  • Collaborate across different teams within the firm.

What you bring to the table: 

  • Undertaking postsecondary program in accounting or related fields
  • Interest in pursuing a career in accounting
  • Ability to take initiative for own learning and development
  • Strong organizational and time management skills
  • Problem solving mindset

Job Type: 

  • Start Date: September 8, 2026
  • Temporary for 12 months.
  • 100% in office

Your Schedule: 

  • Standard hours of work are 37.5 per week, busy season hours of work are 44-50 per week.
