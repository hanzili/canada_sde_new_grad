Job Overview Ontario Power Generation (OPG) is looking for a dynamic, strategic and results-driven professional to join our team in the role of Associate Software Engineer Developer – Full Stack . Reporting to the Manager, IT Product Delivery, this position is responsible for delivering functional software by independently developing, testing, and maintaining applications, enhancing system architecture, ensuring system stability, and supporting team growth through code reviews and mentorship. It also includes promoting knowledge sharing through documentation, boosting application efficiency and scalability with performance optimizations, driving innovation by recommending emerging tools or practices, and contributing to team outcomes by fulfilling additional responsibilities as needed.

Key Accountabilities

  • (Develop, test, and maintain high-quality software applications that meet user needs and support business objectives.
  • Contribute to system design and architecture discussions to improve scalability, reliability, and long-term maintainability.
  • Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve software defects efficiently to support system stability and a positive user experience.
  • Participate in code reviews, promote coding best practices, and support knowledge sharing across the development team.
  • Apply security, compliance, data protection, and industry best practices throughout the software development lifecycle.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to gather requirements, align on priorities, and deliver effective software solutions.
  • Create and maintain clear technical documentation for features, design decisions, processes, and system architecture.
  • Support continuous improvement by optimizing application performance, evaluating new tools and technologies, and adapting to evolving team and business needs.

Qualifications

  • Four-year university education in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field.
  • Minimum 4 years of relevant experience in software development.
  • Proficiency in multiple programming languages such as C#, Python, Java, or Angular, React, JavaScript. Experience with Agile software development methodologies.
  • Strong understanding of software design patterns and principles.
  • Hands-on experience with cloud services, APIs, and databases.
  • Effective collaboration and communication skills.

The successful candidate will exhibit uncompromising integrity and commitment to upholding corporate values, and the OPG Code of Business Conduct.

OPG is committed to fostering an inclusive, equitable, and accessible environment. If you require accommodation during the selection process, please contact AODA@opg.com

What makes a career at OPG different? With operations across Ontario, OPG is one of the most diverse power producers in North America. As the largest generator in Ontario, we meet approximately 50% of the province’s electricity needs, largely from low-carbon sources like nuclear and hydro.

As we work to achieve our vision of Electrifying life in one generation, OPG and our family of companies are also helping advance the development of new low-carbon technologies such as Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), refurbishment projects, and electrification initiatives to help power the growing demands of a growing economy. Join OPG and make history.
