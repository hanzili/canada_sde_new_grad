About Oteck Group

Full Description

Position Summary

We are seeking a motivated and reliable Junior Purchaser / Inventory Coordinator to join our team.

This hands-on role is split approximately 70% on the production floor managing and controlling inventory and 30% office-based processing incoming shipments and related paperwork. This position reports directly to the Purchasing Manager and Facilities Manager.

The ideal candidate is organized, detail-oriented, comfortable working in a warehouse/manufacturing environment, and willing to learn. Forklift experience is preferred, but training can be provided.

Key Responsibilities

Inventory & Floor Operations (70%)

  • Monitor and control inventory levels on the production floor
  • Perform inventory counts and cycle counts
  • Receive, move, and organize materials and products
  • Ensure materials are available for production as needed
  • Maintain clean and organized inventory storage areas
  • Communicate inventory shortages or discrepancies to management
  • Operate a forklift safely to unload and move materials and products to storage areas

Shipping & Administrative Duties (30%)

  • Process incoming shipments and receiving documentation
  • Verify packing slips and shipment accuracy
  • Enter inventory and shipment information into company systems
  • Assist with purchase orders and supplier communications
  • Maintain accurate inventory and receiving records
  • Support purchasing and operations teams as required
  • Obtain quotes from multiple suppliers to secure the best pricing for the company

Skills & Qualifications

  • Minimum high school diploma
  • Previous warehouse, inventory, or purchasing experience is preferred
  • Forklift experience preferred; training can be provided
  • Basic computer and data entry skills
  • Strong attention to detail and organizational skills
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication and documentation skills
  • Self-motivated, detail-oriented, and able to process confidential and/or controlled information
  • Positive attitude with an upbeat, enthusiastic personality and strong interpersonal skills
  • Ability to lift and move materials as required
  • Interested in long-term growth and development within the company

What We Offer

  • On-the-job training
  • Opportunity for growth and development
  • Supportive team environment
  • Competitive compensation
  • Stable, full-time position

Schedule

  • Full-time
  • Day shift

If you are dependable, eager to learn, and enjoy a combination of hands-on and administrative work, we encourage you to apply.

Programs for You

  • Group Benefits Plan after 6 months
  • RRSP Matching after 1 year
  • Company Events (Summer BBQ and Christmas Party)
  • 2 Paid Sick Days after 1 year

Pay: $23.00-$25.00 per hour
