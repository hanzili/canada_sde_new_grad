About the Role

Build Power Platform apps and automations

About Intelbyte

Calgary-based tech firm delivering digital solutions

Full Description

Here’s why you should be part of our journey

  • Meaningful Impact – Work on projects that drive real digital transformation for businesses.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life Balance – We understand that life outside of work matters just as much as your career.
  • Growth & Career Development – As we continue to expand, so do opportunities for you to advance and grow with us.
  • Continuous Learning – We believe in investing in our people, offering mentorship, training, and professional development.
  • Inclusive Culture – We are committed to diversity, equity, and inclusion, fostering a workplace where everyone feels valued and empowered.

In this role, you’ll gain

Hands-on experience with Microsoft Power Platform (Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI)

  • Mentorship from industry professionals.
  • Exposure to business-critical projects that shape the future of digital solutions.
  • Opportunities to apply IT project management and digital product management skills in real-world scenarios.

Key Responsibilities

1. Support the Delivery of Power Platform Projects

  • Assist in delivering Power Platform projects
  • Gather and document project requirements
  • Support the development, testing, and deployment of Power Platform solutions
  • Work closely with teams to update and enhance existing digital solutions

2. Product & Project Management Support

  • Provide product and project management services for assigned projects
  • Assist in tracking project schedules, milestones, and deliverables
  • Participate in status meetings and help prepare project reports
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure timely project execution

3. Development of Power Apps & Automations

  • Design, develop, and enhance Canvas Apps to meet business needs
  • Build and optimize Power Automate workflows to improve efficiency
  • Support the integration of Power Platform solutions with Microsoft 365 services (SharePoint, Teams, Dynamics 365)
  • Assist in the maintenance and debugging of Power Apps applications

4. Data Analysis & Reporting

  • Assist in creating dashboards and reports using Power BI
  • Perform basic data analysis to support business decision-making
  • Help develop data models and visualizations to track project success

5. Documentation & Knowledge Sharing

  • Document workflow processes, technical specifications, and solution designs
  • Help create training materials and user guides for end-users and stakeholders

6. Governance & Best Practices

  • Learn and follow Power Platform governance guidelines
  • Ensure security, compliance, and scalability of developed solutions
  • Participate in workshops led by Senior Developers to establish Centers of Excellence (CoE)

7. Collaboration & Communication

  • Work with teams to understand business needs and provide innovative digital solutions
  • Act as a bridge between technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • Communicate effectively to ensure project success

8. AI-Driven Innovation & Development

  • Assist in leveraging Microsoft Copilot Studio and AI Builder to automate intelligent decision-making and enhance application capabilities.

Who We’re Looking For

Education & Experience:

  • Student or Graduate with a Bachelor’s degree in any field (STEM, IT, Business, or related fields preferred)
  • 0-2 years of experience in IT Project Management or Digital Product Management
  • Awareness of Microsoft Power Platform and the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)
  • 0-2 years of experience building Canvas Apps & Power Automate workflows (preferred)

Technical Skills:

  • Basic knowledge of Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI
  • Familiarity with Microsoft 365 services (SharePoint, Teams, Dynamics 365)
  • Exposure to JSON, APIs, or UI/UX principles (a plus!)

Soft Skills:

  • Strong problem-solving & analytical skills
  • Excellent communication & collaboration abilities
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks & meet deadlines

Eager to learn & adapt in a fast-paced environment

NB: This role is based in Calgary (On-site).
