About the Role

Build AI-enabled software, integrate agents and tools

About Huawei Canada

Huawei Canada, AI research arm

Full Description

Huawei Canada has an immediate co-op opening for an Engineer. About the team: Founded in 2012, the Noah’s Ark lab has evolved into a prominent research organization with notable achievements in academia and industry. The lab’s mission focuses on advancing artificial intelligence and related fields to benefit the company and society. Driven by impactful, long-term projects, the aim is to enhance state-of-the-art research while integrating innovations into the company's products and services, including LLMs, RL, NLP, computer vision, AI theory, and Autonomous driving.

About the job:

  • We are seeking a forward-thinking Software Engineer to join our team. Our mission is to integrate cutting-edge AI technologies into the core of our mobile ecosystem. In this role, you will not only build robust systems but also pioneer the use of AI agents and LLM-driven tools to redefine how we develop and maintain mobile software.
  • AI-Enhanced Development: Leverage and build AI-driven code generation tools to accelerate feature delivery and improve codebase maintainability.
  • Intelligent Agent Integration: Research and implement AI agents to automate complex workflows, from automated testing to intelligent system monitoring.
  • Mobile System Innovation: Design and optimize core components for next-generation mobile platforms.
  • Collaborative R&D: Work closely with cross-functional teams to integrate AI solutions into real-world mobile applications.

The total target annual compensation for this position ranges from $58,000 to $104,000 depending on education, experience, and demonstrated expertise.

Job requirements

About the ideal candidate:

  • Currently enrolled in Bachelor in Computer Science or a related technical field.
  • Software Engineering Expertise: 2+ years of professional experience in C/C++, Python, and TypeScript application development, with a deep understanding of their respective programming models and ecosystems.
  • AI & ML Integration: Working knowledge of Machine Learning principles and Large Language Models (LLMs) applied to software engineering workflows.
  • Adaptability & Tooling: Proven ability to rapidly master new frameworks, open-source tools, and programming languages to meet evolving project requirements.
  • Operational Excellence: Demonstrated ability to manage complex, multi-tasking environments under tight deadlines while maintaining high attention to detail.
  • Collaboration: Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with a track record of effective teamwork in collaborative environments.
  • AI-Augmented Engineering: Proven experience applying LLMs to the full software development lifecycle (SDLC), including intelligent code generation, automated debugging, and architectural refactoring. Agentic System Development: Hands-on experience in architecting and deploying autonomous agent systems.
  • Deep understanding of agentic design patterns, including multi-agent orchestration, state management, tool-use integration, and long-term memory implementation to solve complex software engineering challenges. Developer Experience (DevEx) & Tooling: Strong commitment to building custom AI-assisted tools that streamline workflows and measurably boost team productivity. Mobile Ecosystem Expertise: Experience in HarmonyOS or large-scale mobile platform development.

Additional Information: Huawei Canada is committed to a fair, inclusive, and accessible recruitment process. If you require accommodation during any stage of the hiring process, please let us know and we will work with you to meet your needs.

All applications for this position are reviewed directly by our hiring team, we do not use artificial intelligence tools to screen or select candidates.
