About Azimuth Capital Management

Full Description

Azimuth is seeking a Student Analyst for the Calgary office starting September 2026 for an 8-month term.

A career in Private Equity is challenging, fast-paced and rewarding. The successful candidates will work directly with members of the investment team gaining an in-depth understanding of the energy sector as well as the financial and operational learnings of growing energy companies.

Job Description

·      Broad industry analysis and financial modeling

·      Updating and maintaining the company’s energy sector databases

·      Evaluating potential portfolio investments

·     Supporting operations and reporting responsibilities of the investment platform

·      Collecting and analyzing company-specific financial and operating information

·      Monitoring portfolio investments

·      Various ad-hoc initiatives and data gathering

Qualifications

·      Working toward or recently completed a degree in finance, economics, or engineering

·      Demonstrated interest in sustainable energy technologies and infrastructure

·     Prior work experience in energy and/or energy transition would be considered an asset

·      Excellent interpersonal skills and critical thinking abilities

·     Advanced presentation preparation and financial modeling skills – strong command of the Microsoft Office Suite with an emphasis on PowerPoint and Excel

·     Handles diverse responsibilities and meets deadlines

·      Strong work ethic and motivation

Qualified candidates can email their applications (cover letter, resume, and transcript) in one combined PDF document by Monday, July 20th, 2026 , to info@navigatingenergy.com , addressed to the Azimuth Hiring Committee , with “Private Equity Student Analyst” included in the email subject line.
