About the Role

Plan and execute social campaigns; analyze performance data

About Odtutor

Ontario-based online tutoring startup for exam prep

Full Description

Company Description Odtutor is an online platform that connects learners with tutors for a wide range of academic subjects and skills. The company offers access to experienced faculty for competitive exams such as IIT JEE, NEET, and SSC CGL, supporting learners preparing for high-stakes tests. In addition to exam preparation, Odtutor provides courses in programming languages and tools including Python, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, and PHP. The platform is designed to help learners upskill efficiently, whether they are seeking academic support or career-focused technical training.

Role Description The Digital Marketing Intern will support Odtutor’s marketing efforts by assisting with the planning and execution of online campaigns that promote tutoring services and courses. Day-to-day tasks include creating and scheduling social media content, monitoring engagement, and helping optimize Odtutor’s presence across digital channels. The intern will contribute to basic web analytics reporting, track campaign performance, and suggest improvements based on data and learner behavior trends. This is a contract, on-site role located in Ontario, Canada, requiring collaboration with the local team on marketing initiatives, content ideas, and outreach strategies.

Qualifications

  • Candidates should possess skills in Digital Marketing and Online Marketing, with an interest in education and e-learning.
  • Candidates should possess skills in Social Media Marketing to create, publish, and manage content across key platforms.
  • Candidates should possess skills in Web Analytics to interpret basic performance metrics and support data-driven decisions.
  • Candidates should possess strong Communication skills to collaborate with team members and create clear, engaging messages.
  • Basic understanding of SEO principles, content creation, or graphic design tools is beneficial.
  • Currently enrolled in or recently completed a program in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field is preferred.
  • Ability to work on-site in Ontario, Canada, manage time effectively, and adapt to a fast-paced startup environment.
