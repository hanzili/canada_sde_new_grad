About the Role

Assist customers, process sales, manage inventory

About The Main Ingredient

Local specialty foods shop emphasizing sustainability

Full Description

At The Main Ingredient, we believe good food brings people together. We're passionate about helping our community discover new ingredients, cook from scratch, learn new skills, and feel inspired in the kitchen. From everyday staples to unique finds, we're here to make cooking more approachable, enjoyable, and sustainable. We're looking for a friendly, reliable, and detail-oriented Full-Time Sales Associate to join our team and help create an exceptional experience for every customer who walks through our doors.

What You'll Do :

Customer Service

  • Greet and assist customers in a welcoming and professional manner
  • Answer questions about products, ingredients, cooking, baking, and specialty foods
  • Help customers navigate our bulk food system and make recommendations
  • Process transactions accurately through our point-of-sale system
  • Maintain a positive shopping experience for all customers

Product Knowledge & Sales

  • Learn and maintain knowledge of our bulk foods, herbs, spices, teas, baking ingredients, nuts, seeds, snacks, and specialty products
  • Assist customers with ingredient substitutions and product suggestions
  • Support in-store promotions and merchandising initiatives
  • Restock shelves, bins, and displays throughout the day

Inventory & Store Operations

  • Receive and organize deliveries
  • Refill bulk bins following proper rotation procedures
  • Monitor product quality, freshness, and inventory levels
  • Assist with inventory counts and stock management
  • Label products and maintain accurate pricing and signage

Cleaning & Food Safety

  • Follow food handling and sanitation procedures
  • Maintain cleanliness of bins, scoops, shelving, and work areas
  • Assist with regular store cleaning and maintenance tasks
  • Ensure the store remains organized, safe, and presentable

Sustainability & Community

  • Support and promote environmentally responsible practices
  • Encourage reusable container programs and waste reduction initiatives
  • Represent the values and mission of The Main Ingredient in daily interactions

What We're Looking ForRequired Qualifications

  • Previous experience in a retail environment
  • Strong customer service skills
  • Ability to work independently and confidently when scheduled alone
  • Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills
  • Strong communication and problem-solving abilities
  • Reliable, punctual, and self-motivated
  • Ability to multitask and prioritize in a fast-paced environment
  • Ability to lift and carry up to 50 lbs regularly

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience working with food, grocery, bulk foods, specialty foods, or hospitality
  • Knowledge of cooking, baking, herbs, spices, teas, and ingredients
  • Passion for sustainability, local food systems, and environmental responsibility
  • Experience with inventory management or receiving shipments

Schedule & Availability

  • Full-time position
  • Must be available for a combination of weekdays and Saturdays (every other)
  • Occasional flexibility for special events, workshops, and seasonal busy periods

What You'll Love About Working Here

  • Starting wage of $20/hour
  • Supportive small-business environment
  • Opportunity to learn about food, ingredients, and sustainable living
  • Meaningful work that supports local food systems and waste reduction
  • A workplace where initiative, curiosity, and community matter
  • Lots of room to grow in company

To apply, please send your resume and a brief introduction telling us why you'd be a great fit for The Main Ingredient.

Email: themainhire@gmail.com

Pay: From $20.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Casual dress
  • Discounted or free food
  • Flexible schedule
  • Store discount

Work Location: In person
