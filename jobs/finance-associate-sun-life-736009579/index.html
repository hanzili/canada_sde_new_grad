About Sun Life

Full Description

You are as unique as your background, experience and point of view. Here, you’ll be encouraged, empowered and challenged to be your best self. You'll work with dynamic colleagues - experts in their fields - who are eager to share their knowledge with you. Your leaders will inspire and help you reach your potential and soar to new heights. Every day, you'll have new and exciting opportunities to make life brighter for our Clients - who are at the heart of everything we do. Discover how you can make a difference in the lives of individuals, families and communities around the world.

Job Description: The Sun Life Health (SLH) business within Sun Life Canada is a leading provider of Group Benefits and Health Solutions. A market leader in the advancement of workplace mental health across Canada, through the introduction of new tools such as Virtual Care and Stress Management and Well-Being program providing members access to mental health and medical specialists 24/7. SLH is seeking a Finance Associate to join its fast-paced and energetic Finance team.

Reporting to the Manager, Audit & Risk, the Finance Associate will support audit and risk activities critical to the Sun Life Health business.

What will you do?

  • Manage internal and external audits, including report preparation.
  • Coordinate and attend internal control walkthrough meetings with relevant individuals from within the business unit and the external auditors.
  • Coordinate and support the quarterly attestation process.
  • Coordinate the write-off process and support reporting.
  • Business continuity planning and disaster recovery.
  • Coordinate access reviews.
  • Support the Financial Controls team as needed.

What do you need to succeed?

  • Bachelor’s degree. Major/specialization in accounting, finance, or commerce is preferred.
  • Suitable for a new or recent graduate.
  • Understanding of IFRS and SOX controls preferred.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication, interpersonal and collaboration skills.
  • Self-motivated, results-oriented and flexible to changing priorities and timelines.
  • Proven technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office tools, including Word, Excel, Access, and PowerPoint.
  • This role requires Reliability Status Clearance. In addition to a law enforcement inquiry and a credit check, as part of your application, the Government of Canada will ask if you lived or travelled outside of Canada for 6-consecutive months during the last 5 years, and you must account for all activities during this time.

What's in it for you?

  • Time off giving you the flexibility to focus on the moments that matter including 15 vacation days per year
  • Flexible Benefits from the day you join to meet the needs of you and your family
  • Pension Plan participation starts immediately, and you are automatically enrolled. We help you save more with Sun Life automatic and matching contributions and by including both your salary and bonus when making
  • contributions.
  • The opportunity to move along a variety of career paths with amazing networking potential
  • Flexible hybrid work model
  • Work and professional development that is united by our Purpose: to help Clients and Employees achieve lifetime financial security and live healthier lives
  • A friendly, collaborative, and inclusive culture
  • We’re honoured to be recognized as a 2026 Best Workplace in Canada by Great Place to Work® Canada
