About the Role

Lead communications campaigns and community outreach

About Health Out Loud

Public-health nonprofit focused on accessible mental health

Full Description

WE ARE RECRUITING FOR COMMUNICATIONS SPECIALISTS!

You will lead impactful campaigns that share updates, stories, and ways to access our supports. Make a meaningful difference by:

  • Developing and promoting routine content (e.g. posts, blogs, stories, newsletters, etc.) through online mediums
  • Creating appealing visual designs, using our branding guide and graphical assets, working collaboratively with our designers
  • Acting as a community organizer to nurture and strengthen relationships with online communities
  • Acting as a storyteller that gives elevates lived experience
  • Overseeing marketing cycles, including brainstorming, planning, execution, and reflection
  • Contributing to high-level organizational goals, including in relation to diversity, engagement, and reach

You may also assist with:

  • Coordinating advisory groups, administrative duties (i.e. communication for programs and evaluation processes), report creation, as well as presenting at public forums and conferences

We're recruiting full-time interns and employees (35 hours/week), at an hourly wage range of $17.75–$30.00/hours.

Grow with us!

  • Next Step, Apply Here: https://healthoutloud.org/intern-comms-apply-2026

We’re not just about working hard; we’re about building friendships, pursuing personal wellness goals together, and personal development.

We’re looking for people who really care – about our mission, our work, and the communities we serve.

What are we looking for?

1) LEADERSHIP

  • Demonstrable history of advocacy, engagement, and forged relationships as an active leader (or leadership potential) across multiple social media, digital OR communications platforms
  • Motivated self-started that is proactive, accountable, and empowers the people around them to be their best

2) PROJECT MANAGEMENT SKILLS

  • Experience with innovating and following systems for working collaboratively in diverse teams, including for tracking progress, posting drafts, sharing feedback, and visualizing timelines
  • Ability to prioritize multiple projects in parallel and manage time-sensitive deadlines

3) PEOPLE SKILLS

  • Skilled capacity to initiate, strengthen and build diverse relationships in a way that is authentic, intentional, and meaningful
  • Enjoys learning new perspectives, having thoughts challenged, and composing thoughtful replies to content made by others
  • Personal lived experience of mental health and skills for recovery

4) CREATIVITY

  • Inventiveness to transform personal lived experience into original equity-seeking initiatives that are relatable, fun, and have measurable outcomes
