Intern - Human Resources **Mississauga, ON / Full-time (Hybrid)* Pay rate $20.90 - $24.20 Your responsibilities*** The Human Resources Intern supports the Human Resources department in a coordinator capacity while participating in a variety of HR functions and special projects, while gaining practical experience related to their studies.

  • Recruiting: Assists with recruiting activities
  • Leave Management: Assist with Leave Management (Files claims online and coordinates initial care, etc.)
  • Training: Assists with training activities (coordinate trainings, send communications, answer general inquiries etc.)
  • Drafts and sends out offer letters, termination letters, and other employee correspondence
  • Conducts New Hire Survey, and New Employee Follow-Up Survey and compiles/reviews data and identifies trends
  • Participates in special projects as assigned
  • Performs other duties as needed

**Your Profile****

  • High-School Diploma – Required
  • Four-Year College (Business Administration) – Required
  • 0-2 Years experience (Human Resources) - Required
  • Excellent interpersonal, computer and organizational skills.
  • Able to multitask, work in a fast-paced team environment and adhere to strict deadlines.
  • Knowledge Microsoft Office skills
  • Travel Percentage – 10%
