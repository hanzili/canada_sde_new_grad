About the Role

Assist audit engagements; prepare tax work papers

About DMCL Chartered Professional Accountants

Canadian CPA firm with mentorship and growth

Full Description

About The Role At DMCL our teams are known for its exceptional mentorship and commitment to guiding junior staff who are eager to learn, grow, and gain meaningful experience in the field of audit or private enterprise. Joining our Vancouver or Surrey office as an Articling Student, you'll have the opportunity to work closely with a variety of managers, senior managers, and partners across different client engagements.

From mining to health sciences, manufacturing to not-for-profits—and much more—you'll build your audit skills across a versatile client base that gives you exposure to a wide range of industries, file types, and working styles. This means no two weeks are ever the same, and you'll develop a well-rounded, practical skillset that sets you up for long-term success in the profession.

At DMCL, we believe in hands-on learning, open-door mentorship, and empowering our students to take ownership of their careers. Our supportive team culture, structured training, and strong track record of student development make this a great place to start your CPA journey.

How You’ll Make a Difference

  • Support our clients by preparing accurate tax returns, working papers, and other accounting assignments that help them navigate both personal and business finances.
  • Be an active member of a collaborative team, delivering assurance engagements that give our clients confidence in their financial information.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships by communicating with clients and third parties to gather the information needed to deliver exceptional service.
  • Help shape and strengthen DMCL’s culture through your teamwork, fresh ideas, and active engagement.

Who You Are

  • You have completed an undergraduate degree with a focus in accounting.
  • You will have completed all the required prerequisites for entry into the CPA program before your start date.
  • You’re proficient in Microsoft Office, with solid working knowledge of Excel.

Our students benefit from

  • A CPA mentor to support you throughout your journey to CPA designation;
  • An extensive in-house CFE training program facilitated by experts, experienced with marking the CFE;
  • Both informal and formal mentorship programs;
  • Comprehensive training to cover all aspects of your development;
  • On-the-job learning that will provide you with continued technical skills development and diverse business experience;
  • Opportunity to work on a broad range of engagements from start to finish in a variety of industries;
  • A professional working environment, with a culture that focuses on work-life balance and flexibility.

What DMCL Offers We’re offering more than just a place to article—we’re offering an exceptional opportunity to grow your skills through diverse assignments in a flexible, supportive environment. Here’s what you can expect:

  • A standard 35-hour work week
  • Competitive salary and benefits
  • Paid overtime or the option to take time off in lieu
  • Full financial support toward obtaining your CPA designation—including exam fees, prep courses, and annual dues
  • A flexible work schedule and personal days throughout the year
  • Ongoing learning, training, and development opportunities to support your personal and career growth
  • Mental and physical wellness initiatives, including a wellness allowance
  • Firm-sponsored social events to stay connected and have fun
  • Meal allowance for overtime worked
  • You’ll gain hands-on experience while being part of a team that truly values your development, well-being, and future.

Your next step To apply, submit your resume, cover letter, and transcript through our website by clicking the “Apply for This Job” button.

About DMCL DMCL LLP is a full-service accounting firm dedicated to helping our clients achieve and exceed their business goals, no matter how ambitious. As a leading Canadian firm with global reach, we continue to remain agile and responsive, providing clients with personalized and strategic solutions specific to their industry.

With over 300 team members spread across Vancouver, Surrey, Tri-Cities (Port Coquitlam) and Victoria, working at DMCL means being part of a diversified team of expert advisors that view every day as a new opportunity to contribute to the success of our clients: solving costly problems, building long-lasting relationships and always striving for excellence.
