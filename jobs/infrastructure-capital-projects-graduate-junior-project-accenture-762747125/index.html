About Accenture

Full Description

You’ve Never Been Satisfied with “Good Enough.”

You want to make an impact, not just manage projects, but change how the world gets built. At Accenture Infrastructure & Capital Projects, you’ll do exactly that. You’ll help develop and deliver the factories, grids, transit systems, and public infrastructure that keep communities moving - and do it smarter, safer, and more sustainably than ever before.

You’ll work alongside people who think big and act bold - project managers, engineers, technologists, and strategists who blend real-world experience with digital innovation and AI. Together, we’re transforming how capital projects are planned, managed, and executed, creating a better way to build for the future.

Because “good enough” builds the past. You’re here to build what’s next, on a team that outperforms every norm.

Visit us here to learn more about Accenture Infrastructure & Capital Projects

  • (Internal Title: Project Management I)
  • Support the Signaling Project Management team, providing coordination, reporting, and document control support across Stage Gate delivery and early construction activities.
  • Support action tracking, schedules, and risk registers
  • Assist with Stage Gate documentation and governance materials
  • Coordinate meetings, minutes, and follow‑ups with contractors and consultants
  • Maintain project controls, trackers, and reporting dashboards
  • Support interface management across signalling projects, ETCS, GTCS, and Onboard teams
  • Support the coordination and management of project-related tasks
  • Contribute to the planning and development of projects
  • Perform administrative tasks such as preparing and reviewing invoices, estimates, scheduling meetings, etc.
  • Keep track of and report on project progress
  • Conduct research to support the project team and company operations as needed
  • Complete any tasks assigned by Project Management II or higher in an efficient and timely manner
  • Working Conditions

  • Office-based

  • Degree in Engineering, Project Management, or related discipline

  • Strong organizational and communication skills
  • Interest in rail, signaling, or transit infrastructure
  • Previous internship or co‑op experience in infrastructure or construction
  • Exposure to signaling, rail systems, or major capital projects

BONUS POINTS IF YOU HAVE:

  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Willingness to learn other project management software
  • Ability to arrange priorities and escalate competing risks to meet deadlines with minimum input from leadership
  • Keen eye for detail and adept problem-solving abilities
  • Must have demonstrated experience relaying and receiving information (written and verbal)
  • Equipped to work independently and as part of a team
