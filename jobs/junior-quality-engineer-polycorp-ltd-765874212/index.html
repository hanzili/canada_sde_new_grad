About POLYCORP LTD

Full Description

Polycorp Ltd. is a leader in the design and manufacture of engineered polymer products serving customers around the world in Transportation, Mining, Protective Linings, and Defense. As we continue to grow, we’re looking for skilled Dual Ticket Maintenance professionals to join our Maintenance team.

Based in Elora, Ontario, Polycorp offers competitive compensation, generous shift premiums, excellent benefits, and a supportive team environment where your skills make a real impact. We’re conveniently located less than 30 minutes from Guelph and Kitchener-Waterloo.

Junior Quality Engineer

JOB PURPOSE: The Junior Quality Engineer is responsible for ensuring the quality and accuracy of products by maintaining the standards of measurement tools and processes.

DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES:

Quality and Safety Advocate Responsibilities:

  • Accountable - Advocate for safety by ensuring all safety procedures and policies comply with provincial standards.
  • Perform calibration of Internal gauges according to Polycorp established procedures.
  • Review existing calibration methods and create new WI for new gauges when needed.
  • Review conditions of the existing gauges and replace as necessary.
  • Review calibration log daily, locating any missing gauges
  • Fixing/maintenance of internal gauges (Snap gauges, filler templates etc.)
  • Gemba walk for all internal/external gauges once every 4 months
  • Assist with Internal Non-conformance report (NCR) after any Non-conformance identification
  • Review quality data, sending regular updates to cross functional team
  • Check Warehouse 88 and 89 Inventory and send weekly ‘Status Update’
  • Monitor access database inventory, sending regular updates for aging material
  • Perform layered audits on regular basis for each process and machinery
  • Assist in the Management of the Corrective Action System
  • Strong shop floor presence
  • Investigate and collect data on day to day for non-conforming materials and corrective actions.
  • Inspection of Mining, lining and rail products as required
  • Other duties as assigned

EDUCATION & EXPERIENCE REQUIRED:

  • Bachelor or Master Degree in engineering with 3-5 years manufacturing experience
  • Good oral and written communication skills
  • Computer literacy – reports, charting, etc
  • Proven ability to problem solve
  • Excellent communication and organizational skills
  • Understanding and respect for confidential information
  • Knowledge of Microsoft office (Word, Excel, Outlook specifically)

SKILLS REQUIRED – COMPUTER PROGRAMS OR OTHER SKILLS: - Good oral and written communication skills - Computer literacy – reports, charting, etc - Proven ability to problem solve

ADDITIONAL DESIRABLES:

  • Knowledge of formulating, mixing, processing and testing of rubber compounds
  • Knowledge of metals would be an asset
  • Lean Six Sigma training is an asset
