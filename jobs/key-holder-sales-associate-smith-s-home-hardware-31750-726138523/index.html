About Smith's Home Hardware, 31750

Full Description

Overview
We are seeking a dynamic and customer-focused Key Holder/Sales Associate to join our retail team. This role is vital in delivering exceptional service, maintaining store operations, and supporting sales growth. The ideal candidate will possess strong communication skills, leadership qualities, and a passion for retail. Experience in retail sales and customer service. This position offers an opportunity to develop your skills in merchandising, POS systems, and organizational management within a vibrant retail environment.

Responsibilities

  • Greet customers warmly and provide outstanding customer service to enhance shopping experiences
  • Assist customers with product inquiries, upselling, and recommending merchandise to meet their needs
  • Operate POS systems efficiently for cashiering, cash handling, and transaction processing
  • Maintain visual merchandising standards and organize products to attract customer attention
  • Manage store opening and closing procedures, including security checks and cash reconciliation
  • Support inventory management through filing, stock replenishment, and stock organization
  • Lead by example in demonstrating excellent communication skills and teamwork
  • Handle customer complaints professionally and resolve issues promptly
  • Assist with training new team members and support leadership in daily store operations

Experience

  • Previous experience in retail sales or customer service is preferred
  • Familiarity with Microsoft Office applications such as Excel and Word is advantageous
  • Experience operating POS systems and computer operation skills are required
  • Ability to perform heavy lifting when necessary and stand for extended periods during shifts
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage time effectively
  • Demonstrated leadership capabilities and the ability to motivate team members
  • Knowledge of merchandising techniques, upselling strategies, and basic math for cash handling
  • Excellent communication skills to engage with diverse customers and team members effectively

Pay: $17.00-$20.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Casual dress
  • On-site parking
  • Store discount

Work Location: In person
