About the Role

Support planning, coordination, and client management.

About CastleMoore Inc.

Asset-management firm serving institutional clients.

Full Description

Company Description CastleMoore Inc. provides security asset management services to private and institutional clients, focusing on both absolute and relative return expectations. The company specializes in structured, disciplined investment approaches designed to manage risk while pursuing performance objectives. Team members work with a diverse client base and a range of security types, gaining exposure to institutional-grade portfolio practices. CastleMoore values analytical rigor, accountability, and transparent communication in all client interactions. The organization supports a collaborative environment where emerging professionals can grow their skills in asset management and project execution.

Role Description As an Associate PM at CastleMoore Inc., you will support the planning, coordination, and execution of client management. There will be some portfolio management committee participation that can grow over time.

This full-time hybrid role is based in Mississauga, ON, with the flexibility to work from home for part of the week.

Qualifications

  • Associate Portfolio Manager designation.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with attention to detail and accuracy in documentation.
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills for client and team interaction.
  • Proficiency with common office and project management tools (e.g., spreadsheets, presentation software, collaboration platforms).
  • Post-secondary education in business, finance, project management, or a related field; relevant certifications (e.g., CAPM, PMP in progress) are an asset.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities, work independently in a hybrid environment, and maintain a professional, client-focused approach.
