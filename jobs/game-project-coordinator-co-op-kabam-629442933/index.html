Full Description

At Kabam, we’re constantly raising the bar of excellence in free-to-play gaming. We hire for passion and diversity so that we can triumph in our collective skill. Our teams are made up of a fusion of personalities and interests bound together by a collaborative nature, fun-loving culture, and a drive to do what it takes to make great games. And this is where you come in…

We are hiring two game project coordinator co-ops one for Marvel Contest Of Champions and one for an unreleased project! The Game Project Coordinator Co-op supports the Games team in coordinating day-to-day operations for small-medium size and moderate complexity projects within the team and company. This role involves assisting in project planning and execution while providing administrative support to help achieve department, team, and organizational goals.

This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Downtown Vancouver studio.

In this role, you can expect to:

  • Project Support:
  • Assist with task tracking, scheduling, and coordination for projects within the Game team.
  • Monitor project tasks and alert senior staff to potential risks or conflicts as they arise.
  • Support team members with the identification of potential conflicts in schedules, staffing, or resource allocation.

  • Help maintain and support clear goals, timelines, and basic project deliverables for projects overseen by the Game team.

  • Reporting and Documentation:

  • Support the creation of project documents, such as schedules, task lists, design documents, and risk management notes.
  • Help maintain a well-established documentation repository, to allow for easy onboarding and knowledge sharing across stakeholders.
  • Assist in preparing basic project status reports and collecting data from team members and stakeholders.

  • Compile notes and actions from project meetings.

  • Stakeholder Coordination:

  • Engage with internal stakeholders as needed, including managers and senior team members.
  • Ensure effective communication and coordinate information between project teams and other departments.

To be successful in this role, your background includes:

  • Working towards a Bachelor’s degree.
  • Basic understanding of spreadsheet applications (Excel, Google Sheets).
  • Strong communication skills and ability to interact professionally with team members across the organization.
  • Experience with project management software or organizational applications (e.g., Asana, JIRA) is beneficial.
  • Passion in the tech and gaming space is preferred.

Perks of being a Kabam intern:

  • Our success is due to the hard work of our people. We share our success with our people through flexible benefits and reward programs, sweet swag, snacks, and more.

The typical hiring range listed above is specific to candidates located in Vancouver. The salary will be determined based on your knowledge, skills and competencies, and you’ll have room to grow after you join!

If you’re interested in the position but your expectations don’t fully align with the provided range, we still encourage you to apply! Considerations may be made based on your experience level.

Excited by this opportunity? Kabam is dedicated to growing our diverse and inclusive workforce, so if your past experience doesn’t perfectly match the listed requirements we encourage you to apply anyways - you could be a great fit for this or other positions.

We invite you to apply now and start the conversation with us. Together, we can create and support some of the best games ever made and entertain the world!

About Kabam

Kabam is a world leader in developing entertaining, immersive, and highly social multiplayer games, bringing high-quality graphics, next-generation technology, and revolutionary gameplay to players around the world.

Kabam’s games, Marvel Contest of Champions, Shop Titans, and more have generated hundreds of millions of downloads and have received multiple awards including Apple’s Editor’s Choice and Google Play’s Best Game of the Year.

Founded in 2006, Kabam has studios and offices in Vancouver & Montreal in Canada, and Los Angeles & San Francisco in the United States. Kabam is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Netmarble Games.
