About Company 58 - Manulife Bank of Canada

Full Description

At Manulife John Hancock, we believe in investing in the future – starting with you. Our Co-op Program is your launchpad to success, offering the support, structure, and exciting opportunities you need to thrive and make your mark.

As a Technology Strategy & AI Enablement Co-op, you'll gain hands-on experience at the intersection of business strategy, emerging technologies, artificial intelligence, and innovation. You'll work with technology and business leaders to evaluate technology opportunities, support strategic initiatives, and develop solutions that help drive business outcomes. Join a collaborative team where you'll gain exposure to technology strategy, AI enablement, executive communications, and innovation initiatives while building skills that can accelerate your future career.

We are currently looking for highly motivated students to join us in our Toronto, Ontario, Canadaoffice for a co-op running from September 1, 2026 – December 18, 2026. This is a hybrid position, where you are expected to be in the office Tuesday–Thursday, with the opportunity to work remotely on Mondays and Fridays.

Position Responsibilities:

  • Assess emerging technologies, AI solutions, and vendor capabilities to identify opportunities that help solve business challenges and support strategic objectives.

  • Conduct research on industry trends, market landscapes, and technology innovations, translating findings into actionable recommendations for business and technology stakeholders.

  • Develop executive-quality presentations, business cases, and visual materials that communicate complex business and technology concepts clearly and effectively.

  • Partner with stakeholders to define use cases, document requirements, evaluate potential solutions, and support technology strategy initiatives.

  • Build and prototype AI-powered solutions using tools such as Microsoft Copilot Studio and Power Automate to demonstrate opportunities for automation and innovation.

Required Qualifications:

  • Currently pursuing an undergraduate degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, Engineering, Business Technology Management, or a related field.

  • Excellent communication, research, and analytical skills, with the ability to translate complex information into clear recommendations and presentations.

  • Strong PowerPoint and storytelling skills, including the ability to create polished, executive-level presentations and visual content.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Familiarity with artificial intelligence, Generative AI, Agentic AI, or automation technologies.

  • Experience with Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power Automate, process mapping, technology strategy, consulting, or business analysis projects.

When You Join Our Team:

  • We’ll empower you to learn and grow the career you want.

  • We’ll recognize and support you in a flexible environment where well-being and inclusion are more than just words.

  • As part of our global team, we’ll support you in shaping the future you want to see.

  • As part of our co-op program, you will gain early access to Manulife John Hancock’s premier early talent program, GRO, tailored to graduating students! This program is designed to launch your career with the support, structure, and opportunities you need to thrive.

Please note the salary range is expected to be between $40,950 USD to $56,550 CAD.

The actual salary will vary depending on local market conditions, geography and relevant job-related factors such as knowledge, skills, qualifications, experience, and education/training. The annual salary will be pro-rated based on the length of the work term.
