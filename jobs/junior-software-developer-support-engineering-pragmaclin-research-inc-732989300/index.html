About the Role

Develop support tools, diagnose device connectivity issues

About PragmaClin Research Inc.

Healthcare tech for Parkinson's disease management

Full Description

Company Description

PragmaClin Research Inc. is transforming Parkinson’s Disease management with PRIMS, a remote monitoring system that uses 3D camera technology andML to track symptoms with precision. The company focuses on delivering innovative digital health solutions that support more accurate assessments and timely interventions. By combining advanced technology with clinical insight, PragmaClin aims to improve quality of life and outcomes for individuals living with Parkinson’s Disease. Team members contribute to meaningful work that impacts patients, caregivers, and healthcare providers around the world.

What You'll Be Working On

  • Support tooling — Build the internal tools used to diagnose and resolve issues across deployed sites
  • Remote device pipeline — Develop a two-way communication system for interacting with deployed medical devices (monitors, sensors, peripherals)
  • Hardware integration — Write and upgrade software that interfaces with clinical hardware, handling device communication, data, and errors
  • Logging and diagnostics — Build systems to collect and read logs from live environments to help with troubleshooting

What you'll do day to day

  • Reading logs and helping triage bug reports from deployed clinical sites
  • Building and improving support tools based on issues that come up in the field
  • Working with clinical hardware to diagnose connectivity and communication problems
  • Documenting issues, fixes, and workarounds
  • Collaborating with developers to escalate bugs and confirm fixes

What We're Looking For

Required

  • Graduated within the last two years in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a similar field
  • Working knowledge of full-stack development — React, C#/.NET, and SQL
  • Methodical approach to debugging and problem solving
  • Clear written and verbal communication

Nice to Have

  • Familiarity with hardware communication protocols (serial, USB, Bluetooth, HL7, or similar)
  • Any exposure to medical device software, IoT, or embedded systems
  • Experience with remote access or device management tools
  • Interest in healthcare software
