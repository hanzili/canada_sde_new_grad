Full Description

Who We Are:

ATS is the industry leader in using technology to revolutionize engineering and design processes. We create tools to help engineers and architects integrate complex systems quickly and accurately for North America’s most exciting building projects.

ATS is first and foremost about delighting our clients, and we are deeply committed to providing them with exceptional service. We are recognized for our technical expertise and experience, which we apply to help our clients succeed. We have a strong work ethic and will do all that we can to assist our clients in completing their projects efficiently. Our thriving and motivated culture is the heartbeat of our organization. We hope you will become a part of it!

See more at www.atssoftware.com and our promotional video: http://youtu.be/MPyk3BdN-8o

Please note: This is a 100% in-office position.

As our JR QA Automation Engineer, you will:

  • Ensure the quality of the different products within ATS’ Solutions is appropriately quality assurance tested.
  • Create and automate test cases.
  • Test new features as they are developed, working alongside Product Owners and developers.
  • Prepare and execute functional and regression testing.
  • Create and execute test cases, log and assign defects, and keep track of the defect status.
  • Participate in the test reports and analysis of test results.
  • Ensure the high quality of the application releases.
  • Own your areas of responsibility (be a self-starter, don't rely on others to direct you).
  • Be a quick learner and an analytical thinker.
  • Grasp new techniques and technologies to continuously improve the performance of our platforms.
  • Be comfortable working under pressure in a fast-paced environment.

As our JR QA Automation Engineer, you must have:

  • A Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or a related discipline.
  • 2+ years of experience as a QA Tester with manual and automation testing.
  • Proficiency with XPath and CSS selectors.
  • Hands-on experience with front-end automated testing tools (Playwright and Selenium).
  • Strong knowledge of software QA methodologies, tools, and processes.
  • Experience in writing clear, concise, and comprehensive test plans and test cases.
  • Solid knowledge of SQL.
  • The ability to prioritize and execute tasks in a high-pressure environment.
  • Critical thinking skills, with good analytical and problem-solving abilities.

ATS is dynamic, industrious, innovative and collaborative. We believe diverse and inclusive organizations create work environments that are inventive and open-minded, where people spark new ideas and explore alternatives.   • Gym facilities
  • Epic year-round employee events!
