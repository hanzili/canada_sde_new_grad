Full Description

About Bells Of Steel Inc. At Bells of Steel, our mission is to make fitness and strength training approachable, affordable, and fun. We design and manufacture high-quality strength and exercise equipment for home gym owners and fitness professionals, helping people of all experience levels achieve their goals.

We’re not your typical fitness equipment brand. We’re down-to-earth, a little cheeky, and focused on making fitness more rewarding and less intimidating. If you enjoy a fast-paced, no-BS environment and want to help shape the future of strength training, you’ll fit right in.

*Our Core Values: A.C.T. Adaptability – Raise the Bar* We challenge the status quo, learn quickly, and continuously improve. We embrace change, seek better ways of working, and take initiative to drive results.

*Customer Centricity – Always Spot the Customer* Everything we do is focused on helping our customers succeed. We lead with empathy, build trust through action, and create value for both external and internal customers.

*Teamwork – We Win Together* We operate with honesty, transparency, trust, and respect. We collaborate openly, take shared ownership of outcomes, and succeed as a team.

We’re looking for an early-career engineer who’s ready to learn fast, get their hands dirty, and own real product work from day one. You won’t be buried on a big team — you’ll be the engineer on the project, with support from product, ops, and factory partners as you ramp up.

This is an in-person role out of our Calgary office. The best designs happen when you can walk into the warehouse, drop a sample on the platform, and load it up yourself.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Taking a concept, refining it, and making it manufacturable — owning the design from first sketch through tooling and full production
  • Designing innovative, multi-use, space-saving equipment that punches well above its price tag
  • Producing the deliverables that move a product forward: 3D models, 2D concept drawings, occasional DXFs, and engineering reports (FEA and the like)
  • Designing to ISO 20957 compliance so the gear is safe, certifiable, and built to standard
  • Living in Autodesk Inventor day to day (SolidWorks experience is great too) — turning ideas into clean, manufacturable drawings and BOMs
  • Working hand-in-hand with our overseas factories — including the occasional trip (1–2 per year) for tooling sign-off, sample reviews, and supplier audits, plus the odd industry trade show
  • Pressure-testing your own designs IRL — assemble the sample, load up some plates find the wobble before the customer does
  • Collaborating across product, marketing, ops, and customer service so the gear works in real garages, basements, and spare bedrooms — not just on paper
  • Owning the process, but staying nimble when priorities shift mid-project (and they will)
  • Understanding the voice of the customer through reviews and customer feedback, and incorporating that feedback into designs for new products and product revisions

What You Bring

  • A Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related field
  • EIT registration/P.Eng designation or eligibility in Alberta
  • 2+ years of mechanical design experience — internships and co-ops count
  • Proficiency in Autodesk Inventor or SolidWorks — Inventor is what you’ll primarily be working in
  • Working knowledge of steel fabrication (welding, tube bending, machining, finishing)
  • Bonus points for any exposure to plastic and iron castings
  • Some experience working with overseas manufacturing partners is a plus, but not required — we’ll get you up to speed
  • A weightlifting or strength training background doesn’t hurt — at minimum, a real willingness to put hands on the gear and to learn strength training and learn about our customer base
  • Strong written and verbal communication — you can ask the right questions and flag issues early
  • Process-oriented, but not thrown off by a last-minute pivot

What’s In It For You

  • $70,000–$85,000 base salary, depending on experience
  • Health Spending Account
  • Hybrid schedule based out of a Calgary office you’ll actually want to come into
  • Employee discounts on all our gear
  • Access to our staff gym
  • A team that actually likes what they do
