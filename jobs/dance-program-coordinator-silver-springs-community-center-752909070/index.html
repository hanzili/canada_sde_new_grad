Full Description

Our community based, recreational dance program is looking for an enthusiastic coordinator to lead our dance program. Classes run on Wednesdays & Thursdays 4:30-7pm.

You will be given the opportunity to help us build a new program.

Qualifications:

Knowledge of various types of dance

Enjoy working with children

Excellent communication skills

Ability to supervise other instructors

Ability to plan, manage and coordinate classes
