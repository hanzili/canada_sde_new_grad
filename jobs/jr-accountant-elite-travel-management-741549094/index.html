About Elite Travel Management

Fast-growing, award-winning travel finance company

Full Description

At Elite Travel Management, we live and breathe culture.

Welcome to our story! We are three years in a row winners of one of the Globe and Mail's Fastest Growing Companies in Canada! Are you ready to jump into a finance role that builds your skills of a Canadian success, entrepreneur story. The right candidate is ready to make s BIG impact on a company that is going place.

We are looking for a Junior Accountant to join the growing team at Elite Travel Management.

Your desire to go above and beyond will drive your success in this role. The ideal candidate for this position will be responsible for maintaining records, reconciling accounts, and contributing to new initiatives to help streamline our accounting and finance operations.

More importantly, the successful candidate will play a part in Business Central (our new ERP system), virtual credit card technology, and the ever evolving industry of corporate travel.

Who are our clients? Think NHL Hockey Teams, Oil and Gas, Tech, Mining, and more.

Want an office you can feel proud to come to work at each day? The Ampersand is where you will find us complete with (included) golf simulators, commercial gym (and towel service!), basketball courts, and more.

We mean it - Elite means culture and we want YOU if you're the right fit.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Manage Elite Travel’s virtual payments program
  • Reconcile incoming and outgoing transfers
  • Create and deliver weekly client reports
  • Day to day involvement with Microsoft Business Central
  • Assist with client reports and reconciliation
  • Complete various ad hoc tasks
  • Respond to accounting and reporting inquiries from internal and external stakeholders
  • Provide additional support and complete ad hoc tasks and initiatives as requires

Key Qualifications:

  • Post-secondary education in Accounting, Finance, or related field required
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including V-lookups and Pivot Tables
  • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to effectively perform in a fast-paced, start-up environment
  • Demonstrated ability to build strong relationships

What you will benefit from:

  • Limitless growth possibilities
  • Being part of a collaborative and engaging team that prioritizes employee development
  • Partnering with, and learning from, an experienced network of professionals
  • A competitive total rewards package with an employee benefits program that includes extended health coverage, a health spending account and an employee & family assistance program

Education and Additional Considerations

  • Post-Secondary education in accounting or finance
  • Experience in the travel industry is an asset, though not required

Compensation, Benefits, and Perks!

  • Health Spending Account
  • 3 weeks holidays to start
  • An RRSP matching program
  • We all are shareholders at Elite Travel – shares in the company provided at year two!
  • Industry discounts with airlines
  • Industry discounts with hotels
  • Company conferences (Past trips include Banff, Seattle, Palm Springs, Cancun)
  • Team events and incentives
  • A new office space complete with commercial grade gym, basketball courts, golf simulator, food trucks

We offer unlimited career potential, and a competitive compensation package will be negotiated based on experience and qualifications.

Additionally, you will access IATA industry travel benefits including airlines and hotel rates.

We are an equal opportunity employer and believe in diversity! All are encouraged to apply.
