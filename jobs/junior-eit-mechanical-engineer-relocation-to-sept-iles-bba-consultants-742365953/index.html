About the Role

About BBA Consultants

Canada's Best Employer, engineering consulting firm

Full Description

Position type: Regular

Your future role on our team To support project and client development in the Sept-Îles region, BBA is seeking a candidate to the engineering profession in mechanical engineering to bolster its local multidisciplinary team. This team works closely with our offices in Labrador City, Montréal and Mont-Saint-Hilaire and contributes to regional development and delivering projects in the mining and metals and energy sectors. By joining this small, collaborative team focused on proximity, you'll benefit from BBA's network of recognized experts, who’ll support you in your mandates and your professional growth. You'll also have the opportunity to be actively involved on project sites and client work sites.

With a strategically located office close to our clients, you’ll have several opportunities to perform site surveys, provide technical assistance to local clients and participate in commissioning new equipment and systems while working on projects of all sizes.

Sept-Îles offers a vibrant social and community life, as well as access to a host of sports and outdoor activities such as snowmobiling, camping, fishing and water sports. You’ll also enjoy kilometres of beautiful beaches. It's an excellent opportunity to step away from big cities while still having access to the services and amenities of a dynamic town of over 25,000 people.

With us, you’ll get the opportunity to...

  • Prepare technical documents for mandates related to heavy mechanics, conveyors, pumping systems, grinding mills and metallurgical processes as well as engineering for mechanical services
  • Perform detailed calculations under the supervision of experienced engineers
  • Perform onsite technical information surveys
  • Prepare material lists and bills of lading in accordance with codes, standards, drawings and other established procedures and within BBA’s quality system requirements
  • Assist more experienced personnel with the technical aspects of complex tasks
  • Occasionally coordinate the work of drafters or others, at the request of a senior engineer
  • Help write technical reports
  • Assist the team with technical audits at work sites
  • Help supervise the work and commissioning of designed systems
  • Help prepare proposals
  • Participate in various project phases, from studies and detailed engineering to site support and commissioning

Do your qualities and values match our corporate culture?

  • Results-driven
  • Constantly seeking sustainable and agile solutions
  • Willingness to work together while having fun
  • Entrepreneurial spirit
  • Ability to look beyond established standards
  • Rigorous and ethical

Overview Of Certifications And Job Requirements

  • University degree in mechanical engineering and a member of the Ordre des ingénieurs du Québec
  • Three years of experience or less
  • Available for short-term work assignments between Sept-Îles and our Labrador City office, depending on business needs
  • Available for short-term (up to 50 % of the time) and long-term (asset) assignments with our clients in Québec and the rest of Canada
  • ASP Construction card
  • Excellent knowledge of MS Office Suite, like Excel, and other applications, databases and interfaces
  • Knowledge of AutoCAD, SolidWorks and Navisworks applications
  • Asset: experience in the heavy industry sector (mining, petrochemicals, energy, etc.)
  • Asset: knowledge of SmartPlant 3D, CADWorx, ANSYS and Fathom applications
  • Asset: Bilingual (English and French)

An Overview Of BBA’s Total Rewards

  • Annual base salary established using a 37.5-hour week
  • An annual premium program for all regular employees
  • Onsite mobility premium
  • Access to a time bank
  • Cellphone program
  • A group insurance plan that starts on Day 1, including short-term and long-term disability insurance and a telemedicine program
  • Retirement savings plan
  • Vacation and sick leave
  • A premium through the Employee Referral program

With Us, You’ll Get Many Benefits

  • Access to many experts and coaching
  • A collaborative team that shares its ideas and knowledge
  • Custom training and development plans
  • A vibrant social club with something for everyone
  • A caring environment where everyone’s ideas are listened to and there is no perception of hierarchy
  • Friendly, eco-mindful and high-tech workspaces

About BBA For over 40 years, we’ve been pushing the boundaries of engineering to serve our clients in the Energy and Natural Resources sector. At BBA, we develop innovative, flexible and sustainable solutions, from strategy to execution.

BBA is one of Canada’s leading private consulting engineering firms, focused on practice (PCB), with a network of 18 offices. Our teams work closely to deliver projects that shape tomorrow’s industry at the local, national and international levels.

Our people’s passion and excellence make BBA a Canada’s Best Employer and one of Canada’s Best Managed Companies.
