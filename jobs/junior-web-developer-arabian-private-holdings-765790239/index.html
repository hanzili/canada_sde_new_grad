About Arabian Private Holdings

Full Description

About The Role The Junior Web Developer provides essential technical support for our engagement with a major Canadian financial client. This position exists to maintain the digital tools and interfaces that allow our principals to present complex strategy data with clarity. You will ensure that our internal reporting systems remain reliable and functional as we manage various corporate mandates.

About Arabian Private Holdings Arabian Private Holdings was founded on the principle that critical business problems do not require the traditional large-firm consulting model. We believe that a board facing a succession question or a family group weighing a capital injection does not need a team of twelve associates and a hundred-page methodology deck. Instead, they need experienced partners who can sit with a problem, form a rigorous view, and defend it. We take on a limited number of mandates to ensure that every client receives direct attention from the principals who are actually accountable for the outcome. Our work covers corporate strategy, governance, and operating model efficiency for significant family groups, listed corporations, and government entities. We focus on clarity over complexity and operate as a fully remote firm, prioritizing the quality of our analysis over traditional office overhead.

What You Will Will Do

  • Build and maintain functional web interfaces that support our advisory mandates.
  • Update and refine digital dashboards used by our partners to present findings to our Canadian financial client.
  • Write clean, documented code using HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript to ensure site reliability.
  • Identify and resolve technical bugs within our internal digital toolset.
  • Assist in the organization of mandate-related data into clear, accessible digital formats.
  • Ensure all web assets align with the firm's standards for professional presentation and logical structure.
  • Collaborate with the principals to translate complex strategy requirements into simple technical solutions.
  • Monitor the performance and accessibility of our web-based tools to ensure a consistent experience.

What We Are Looking For

  • A foundational knowledge of web development principles and modern coding standards.
  • The ability to think logically and break down complex problems into manageable steps.
  • Strong written communication skills, with an emphasis on clarity and precision.
  • A disciplined approach to working independently in a fully remote environment.
  • Genuine curiosity about how corporate governance and holding companies are structured.
  • Attentiveness to detail in both functional code and visual appearance.
  • A commitment to maintaining professional standards without the need for constant supervision.

How We Work We operate as a fully remote firm, which allows us to focus entirely on the quality of our analysis and the strength of our client relationships. Our approach to governance and mentoring is direct. Junior members of the team work closely with principals, gaining insight into how high-level decisions are made and defended. We value clarity of thought and the ability to form a view based on evidence. Because we do not have a traditional office structure, we rely on clear, asynchronous communication and a shared commitment to accountability. This model ensures that our work remains focused on the needs of the client rather than internal firm politics or growth targets.

What The Role Offers

  • A permanent, full-time position that is fully remote.
  • Competitive pay that is attractive for an entry-level professional in the current market.
  • Direct exposure to significant mandates within the global financial sector.
  • Mentorship from experienced firm principals who value rigorous analysis and clear thinking.
  • A professional environment free from the overhead and bureaucracy of traditional consulting firms.
  • The opportunity to build a career supported by a focus on corporate strategy and governance.
