Full Description

Job Description Co-op, Human Resources The Co-op, HR Coordinator acts as a member of the HR Generalist team, and assists with HR initiatives and special projects in the areas of employee relations, performance management, and new hire on-boarding and orientation.

Responsibilities

  • Provide HR support for various initiatives and projects;
  • Conduct new-hire orientation sessions;
  • Schedule and conduct exit interviews;
  • Act in compliance with all applicable employment legislation across Canada and Ricoh Canada Inc. policies;
  • Other duties as assigned by manager.

Education & Experience

  • University or College level Human Resource Management Degree or Diploma
  • Minimum of 1 year experience in an office environment is an asset

Skills

  • Ability to learn new skills quickly
  • Ability to work in a fast paced environment with demonstrated ability to prioritize and handle multiple tasks
  • Good time management and organizational skills
  • Excellent verbal and written communications
  • Comfortable working independently and in a team
  • Proficiency in the use of MS Office applications , Lotus Notes an asset
