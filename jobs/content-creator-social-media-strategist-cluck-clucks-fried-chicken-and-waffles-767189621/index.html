Full Description

Content Creator & Social Media Strategist

Job Overview
We just opened our doors at Sage Hill Passage NW recently, and we are looking for a creative, highly ambitious individual to build our local TikTok and Reels presence from scratch.

We don't require a university degree or years of corporate experience. Instead, we are looking for pure passion, a deep understanding of short-form video trends, and a desire to learn how to scale a local audience.

If you love fast-casual food marketing, this is your opportunity to own the creative process for a growing brand.

Duties

  • Concept & Script: Brainstorm engaging skits, satisfying food ASMR, and quick-turnaround trend videos tailored to our menu.
  • Film & Edit: Show up to our Sage Hill location for regular batch-filming sessions and edit the footage into polished, high-retention vertical videos.
  • Analyze & Grow: Monitor algorithm performance, study audience retention, and learn what drives real-world foot traffic.

Requirements

  • Local to Calgary: Must be able to easily commute to our Sage Hill location in NW Calgary for filming sessions.
  • Fluent in Platform Trends: A deep understanding of what makes a video go viral on TikTok and Instagram Reels (hook writing, pacing, trending sounds, and SEO keywords).
  • Equipment: Must own a modern smartphone (iPhone 13 Pro/14 or equivalent Android) with a high-quality camera.
  • Food Safe & Clean: Since you will be filming close up in our active kitchen, you must be professional, and adhere to our kitchen safety guidelines while capturing footage.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience or background in Digital Marketing, Communications, or Film/Media
  • Experience capturing Food ASMR
  • A Strong Personal Portfolio: A portfolio of short-form videos (personal TikTok/Reels accounts, client work, or school projects) that demonstrate clean camera work and modern editing skills.

The Compensation:

  1. Hourly base pay for all filming and editing hours.

  2. A structured view-based incentive program—meaning you earn direct cash bonuses as your videos achieve major organic view milestones.

How to Apply: To show us your creative style, please don't just send a resume. Instead, send us a short 15-to-30 second vertical video introducing yourself, showing a sample of your video editing work, or sharing a quick concept for our "Cluck Yeah" waffle sandwich.

Send the video link or file to sagehill@cluckclucks.ca.

Benefits:

  • Flexible schedule
  • Store discount

Work Location: Hybrid remote in Calgary, AB
