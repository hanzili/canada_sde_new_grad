About the Role

Analyze data, format proposals, automate workflows

About Framatome

Global nuclear energy technology and services leader

Full Description

Why This Role Is Critical As the need for nuclear energy grows, the future couldn’t be brighter. Join our vital mission to create lasting solutions for our planet’s greatest challenges. In this role, you are ready to shape the future of clean energy and make a long-lasting global impact. With us, anticipate engaging and challenging projects that deliver innovative solutions and value-added technologies helping power more than 38 million North American homes.

What You’ll Do Day-To-Day

  • Learn and support the proposal development process by analyzing historical proposal data to identify compliance trends, common gaps, and improvement opportunities.
  • Assist in formatting, organizing, and preparing data-driven materials required for each proposal, including requirements matrices and compliance checklists.
  • Support process automation initiatives by helping document workflows, testing automated tools, and maintaining structured proposal data repositories.
  • Schedule and coordinate meetings related to proposal development, requirements analysis, and process improvement activities.

What You'll Bring

  • Rising junior or senior pursuing a degree in Business, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field.
  • Strong analytical and organizational skills with the ability to structure and interpret complex datasets.
  • Interested in process automation, data-driven decision-making, and workflow optimization.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively, demonstrating initiative and a willingness to learn new tools and methods.
  • High attention to detail, especially when reviewing requirements or checking proposal compliance.
  • Comfortable working with spreadsheets, databases, and digital collaboration tools; familiarity with basic automation or scripting concepts is a plus.
  • Clear and effective communication skills for working with team members, subject matter experts, and various stakeholders.

Total Rewards Package

  • Salary: $22.00-$24.00 hourly, with the opportunity to receive performance bonuses.
  • Additional Benefits: Additionally, you may receive 401k, Business Travel Insurance, Employee Assistance Program.

About Framatome With roots dating back to 1964—just two years after Ontario’s first power-generating reactor began operation—Framatome Canada has become a leading provider of services and engineering expertise for the Canadian and global CANDU market. Built on a strong reputation and a steadfast commitment to excellence, our teams deliver outage services, advanced nuclear fuel technologies, and next‑generation reactor solutions that support safe and efficient operations.

At Framatome, you will join a global team committed to creating reliable, carbon-free energy. Here, you can:

  • Solve complex nuclear challenges that directly impact the climate.
  • Build your career through technical fellowships, leadership roles, and global opportunities.
  • Do your best work in a culture that values safety, innovation, and well-being.
