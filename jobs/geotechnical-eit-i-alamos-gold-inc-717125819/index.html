Full Description

Job Opportunity Alamos Gold Inc. Young-Davidson is currently recruiting the following challenging position to join our operations team:

Geotechnical EIT I Alamos Gold is a Canadian-based gold producer with a long-term track record of creating value for all stakeholders through solid financial performance, low-cost production growth, and a company-wide commitment to social responsibility and environmental stewardship. We currently operate three mines – two in Canada and one in Mexico – and we have a strong portfolio of development stage projects, including the Lynn Lake Project and Island Gold Phase 3+ Expansion in Canada, and Puerto Del Aire Project in Mexico. Alamos also has an early-stage exploration project in Canada, the Qiqavik Gold Project. Our core values of safety, teamwork, environmental sustainability, integrity, and commitment allow our more than 1,900 dedicated team members to thrive in their careers and to create a lasting legacy that benefits all Alamos stakeholders.

Alamos Gold’s Young-Davidson is a low-cost, long-life operation and one of Canada’s largest underground gold mines. It is located near the town of Matachewan centrally located between Timmins, Kirkland Lake, North Bay and Sudbury, Ontario. The 2020 completion of a highly automated, lower mine expansion has resulted in Young-Davidson increasing production and generating strong free cash flow. With a large Mineral Reserve base, it will serve as Alamos’ foundation for growth for many years to come. This is a very exciting time at Young-Davidson, and we are searching for highly motivated and committed people to join our team!

If you are willing to be part of a team that is dedicated to growth, high performance and a culture of safety and accountability, we would like to hear from you!

Join Us in Advancing Safe and Sustainable Mining! Are you passionate about geotechnical engineering and underground mining? Do you thrive in a safety-driven, technical, and collaborative work environment? If you are an early-career geotechnical professional who excels in analysis, problem-solving, and field-based work, we want you on our team!

Role Overview As Geotechnical Engineer-in-Training (EIT) I, you will play a strategic role in supporting ground stability and safe mining operations. Reporting to the Geotechnical Superintendent, you will be responsible for implementing and managing key aspects of the Ground Control Management Plan. Working closely with geotechnical and operations teams, you will drive continuous improvement in underground safety and ground support practices while contributing to overall mine performance.

Key Responsibilities

  • Ground Inspections: Conduct underground mine inspections to assess ground conditions and identify potential risks.
  • Ground Support Design: Assist in the design and implementation of secondary ground support systems, including cable bolting.
  • Instrumentation Management: Support the installation, monitoring, and maintenance of geotechnical instrumentation.
  • Data Analysis & Reporting: Analyze geotechnical data and provide recommendations to improve ground control performance.
  • Compliance & Safety: Ensure adherence to the Ground Control Management Plan and applicable safety standards.

What You Bring To The Table

  • 0–3 years of experience in mining or geotechnical engineering, with expertise in inspection, analysis, or ground support.
  • Proven ability to work effectively in field-based, safety-critical environments.
  • Bachelor’s in Mine Engineering or Geological Engineering.
  • Eligible for registration with a Professional Engineering Organization (EIT preferred).
  • Strong communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Experience with Deswik, Vulcan, or other mining software is an asset.
  • Accreditation in Underground Common Core modules is an asset.
  • Passion for underground mining safety and geotechnical excellence.

Why Join Us?

  • Be part of an organization that values safety, teamwork, and commitment.
  • Opportunity to drive meaningful change in underground ground control practices.
  • Work alongside dynamic leaders and technical experts committed to development and mentorship.
  • Competitive salary and benefits package.

Working Conditions

  • 7 days on, 7 days off – 11-hour shifts
