Full Description

Position Scope We are seeking a Co-op, Project Coordinators to support our North American Construction Group team in Fort McMurray. This is a great opportunity for an ambitious student looking to gain valuable experience in a dynamic and innovative company.

(8- 12 month p lacements are available)

Here at NACG, we are dedicated to being a part of your professional growth and pushing your future forward. Our people are what make North American Construction Group an industry leader, it’s our people that keep the largest fleet of equipment in Western Canada running and it's our people that keep our job sites safe.

What’s In It for You?

  • Health and safety are our top priorities, with a commitment to 3-6-5.
  • Competitive rate for the duration of the co-op term with scheduled wage increases.
  • Valuable experience in the construction and mining industry.
  • Opportunity to work with an experienced engineers and supportive team.
  • Exposure to a variety of projects, tasks, and machinery.
  • Networking opportunities and mentorship.
  • Potential for future career growth within NACG.

Location: Fort McMurray

Site: Suncor or Syncrude sites (Fort Hills, Suncor Baseplant, etc.)

Shift: 14x14 or 14x7- 11.5 hours

Accommodations: $1000/ month LOA or Camp Accommodations (site dependent)

Travel: Transportation provided to and from Fort McMurray to site or Client flights provided out of Edmonton/Calgary only (site dependent).

The Engineering Coop Program within North American Construction Group will provide you with valuable exposure to actual operational conditions and the daily processes essential to effective project management. Successful applicants will be matched to specific projects based upon their areas of interest, individual abilities, and operational requirements.

The position available involves working from a field office on site. You will work under the supervision of project managers and coordinators (generally engineers) in conjunction with field supervisors, surveyors, and equipment operators. Day-to-day tasks include tracking operating conditions and changes, providing technical support for field supervisors, collecting operational data and the timely reporting of project details to clients and management.

Outside of daily tasks, the Co-op, Project Coordinator, will work under the supervision of project managers or senior engineers to monitor, interpret, analyze, and report on performance indicators; manage resources and execution schedules; and interact with operational personnel and clients. Tasks will be determined based on specific experience, interest, and skill sets.

Responsibilities What You’ll Be Doing As a Co-op, Project Coordinator, You Will

  • Work in a field office on-site, Fort McMurray.
  • Collaborate with project managers, engineers, field supervisors, surveyors, maintenance personnel and equipment operators.
  • Collect, interpret, and analyze operational data.
  • Interact with staff and operational personnel.
  • Report project details in a timely manner.
  • Perform tasks based on your specific experience and skill sets.

Qualifications What Makes You the ONE

  • Highly motivated individual with excellent communication, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
  • Pursuing a degree in Industrial, Civil, or Mining Engineering (other engineering disciplines considered).
  • Valid Class 5 driver’s license (required).

Summary What’s Next After you submit your resume, our Recruitment team will review your application. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted for an interview.

If you're a dedicated and motivated student looking to gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced construction and mining company, we encourage you to apply. We're confident that your contribution will be valued, and we look forward to being part of your educational and professional journey. Join North American Construction Group today and be a part of our shared success story!

Overview NACG in a Nutshell North American Construction Group (NACG) has served as an industry leader in the heavy construction and mining industries for over 65 years. In that time, we’ve proven ourselves as a safe and reliable contractor who can complete projects of any size and scope. We’ve also taken a number of bold steps forward to broaden our footprint, expanding to mines outside our provincial borders, and providing third-party maintenance services to clients and customers.

In addition to providing employees with rewarding careers, North American has a whole lot more to offer:

  • Significant Growth –As a company, NACG is rapidly expanding, and has practically doubled in size over the past year alone.
  • Long Term Contracts – With over $1 billion dollars in contracted work booked through 2023, NACG can provide long term employment solutions and numerous opportunities for advancement.
  • Commitment To Safety – With a top tier record for safety performance, NACG is dedicated to ensuring that everyone gets home safe, and constantly strives for a goal zero performance.
  • Competitive Compensation – NACG provides competitive compensation packages for all employees.
  • Attractive Benefits Package – With a substantial benefits package provided to all employees, NACG ensures that you are covered for life’s many surprises.
  • GRRSP Matching – As you begin your new career, you can also start planning for the future thanks to NACG’s GRRSP matching.
  • Safety focused – An exceptional leader in committing to a safe work environment by promoting personal safety amongst the workforce and continually improving our HSE systems. Everybody gets home safe!
