Role Overview

We are looking for a detail-oriented Junior Quality Assurance Engineer to join our QA team supporting financial applications on the IBM iSeries (AS/400) platform. This role is ideal for someone passionate about software quality and eager to grow in a fast-paced, regulated financial environment.

Key Qualifications

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Finance, or a related field.
  • 0–2 years of experience in software testing or Quality Assurance, preferably in a financial services environment.
  • Familiarity with IBM iSeries (AS/400) tools and environments (e.g., SEU, PDM, DB2/400).
  • Understanding of QA methodologies and financial data validation.
  • Basic knowledge of SQL and financial reporting systems.
  • Strong attention to detail and analytical skills.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.

Nice-to-Have Qualifications:

  • Exposure to financial systems such as core banking, payment processing, or investment platforms.
  • Experience with RPG, ILE RPG, or CL programming.
  • Familiarity with financial regulations and audit processes.
  • Knowledge of test automation tools or scripting languages.

The expected base salary range for this position is $60 – $70 per hour, depending on experience, skills, and internal equity.
