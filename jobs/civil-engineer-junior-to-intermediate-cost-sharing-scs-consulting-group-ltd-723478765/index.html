About the Role

Prepare cost sharing schedules and certifications

About SCS Consulting Group Ltd.

Local regional civil consulting firm; client-focused infrastructure projects.

Full Description

What you’ll be doing:

Reporting to both Senior & Intermediate Project Managers, you will be a Project Engineer who is highly motivated and eager to learn the discipline of administering cost sharing for large development and infrastructure projects. Cost Sharing is a great opportunity to leverage existing engineering & land development skills and be involved with the legal & business aspects of the industry while maintaining a strong technical base. We pride ourselves in being able to undertake detailed analysis of subdivision systems but with a constant eye on the big picture and how everything fits together.

The Junior Intermediate Cost Sharing Engineer is required to perform a range of duties including, but not limited to:

  • Work on the preparation of cost sharing schedules, cost sharing figures and engineers certification/release letters;
  • Assist with general civil engineering studies;
  • Liaise with clients and developer groups; and
  • Complete related tasks under the guidance of a more senior staff member.

What we’re looking for from you:

  • Civil Engineering or Civil Technology Degree;
  • High attention to detail and enjoy problem-solving & working with detailed Excel models;
  • A working knowledge of AutoCAD;
  • 1- 2 years of experience in land development approvals and/or municipal review processes.
  • Previous experience in administration of Cost Sharing schedules and/or knowledge of residential subdivisions; master & functional servicing studies; stormwater management plans, site grading and servicing plans is an asset;
  • Ability to work in a team environment; and
  • Must be self-motivated possessing strong interpersonal & communication skills; and have the ability to work independently & manage multiple projects / clients

Why SCS?

SCS is a company that believes in building long lasting relationships with our clients, community partners, employees and their families. We are a preferred employer where we work as hard as we play and always celebrate as a team! We frequently host companywide events, participate in community and industry events and our multiple sports teams encourage a healthy lifestyle, good fun and friendly competition between co-workers.

We encourage employees to be part of the communities that they live and work in. SCS contributes to a variety of community-based charities.

We believe our fundamental strength lies within our staff. Our employees are the engine of our company and are empowered and encouraged to take ownership of their projects. Staff are rewarded with challenging work, a focus on job satisfaction and the opportunity for career advancement. We offer competitive salaries and health benefits, as well as a wellness allowance.

SCS Consulting Group Ltd. welcomes and encourages applications from people with disabilities. Accommodations are available on request for candidates taking part in the aspects of the selection process.

This posting reflects a current vacancy, not a general expression of interest. No artificial intelligence (AI) tools are used in the screening, assessment, or selection of candidates for this position. All stages of our recruitment process are conducted by human personnel.

Thank you for your interest in SCS Consulting Group Ltd. Your resume will be carefully reviewed and we will contact you should your skills and experience match our requirements.
