Full Description

Student Project Coordinator AtkinsRéalis is one of Canada's largest private sector nuclear engineering groups. We have been providing a wide range of services to the nuclear industry in Canada and around the world since for more than 60 years. We are proud to be the steward of Canadian CANDU nuclear technology.

Location – Mississauga, ON. The position of Student Project Coordinator will be a junior contributing role to assist Project Managers or Senior Technical Leads in the execution of periodic reactor inspection programs, major nuclear refurbishment projects, or new-nuclear projects. The successful candidate will join a mature team of project and engineering personnel and will be assigned tasks in the areas of engineering, engineering administration and/or project administration.

The employee will be required to obtain security clearance for CANDU Energy Inc. facilities and possibly for our customer sites. This activity typically requires extended time for someone who is not a citizen of Canada, so Canadian citizenship is an advantage.

The employee may be assigned to short-term field service assignments on CANDU Field Services projects, typically during customers' outages and at their sites. During these assignments, the employee may be requested to perform work in a radiological work environment such as set-up and operation activities of certain tooling. In this event, the employee will be required to Nuclear Energy Worker qualified and trained to CANDU Energy's RP program as "Group 3" worker and with our customer's'onboarding process, as well as receiving practical training related to the project. Physical fitness and hands-on experience with tools and equipment will be an advantage.

The employee will be expected to perform tasks and activities commensurate with their level of experience and familiarity with the technology, while supervised by senior staff.

Your Role Within The Team

  • Organizing, hosting, minuting regular project and/or customer meetings for senior staff.
  • Collecting data and status for the purpose of regular schedule updates.
  • Updating project Key Performance Indexes.
  • Maintaining a running log of project lessons learned.
  • Minor document revisions, including learning to navigate the various administrative systems (e.g. ProAct and MyTrak, Aconex).
  • Performing any other administrative or analytical review or investigation as directed.
  • Self-study of the requisite quality procedures required to perform typical engineering and/or project administration tasks.

Why choose AtkinsRéalis as an employer? Because We Offer

  • The opportunity to work on an innovative and award-winning project.
  • An exciting environment where work-life balance is important.
  • A wide array of learning and development opportunities.
  • Competitive compensation package.
  • A work environment focused on health and safety.

The Ideal Candidate

  • Experience supporting various type of projects including construction, manufacturing or engineering services projects considered an asset.
  • An appreciation of the importance of Quality Assurance Programs and their significance to the nuclear industry considered an asset.
  • Excellent organizational, communication and customer relationship skills.
  • Demonstrated oral and written communication skills.
  • Progression within a recognized course of college or university study – preference will be given to engineering and/or engineering technology students.
  • Commitment to Safety.
  • Problem Solving.
  • Integrity and Trust.
  • Customer Focus.
  • Team Player.
  • Shares Knowledge.
  • Self-starter/self-motivated.

Additional Job Requirement/Information –

  • Duration: 4, 8,12 or 16 months depending on candidates' availability.
  • Currently enrolled in a University in the field of Science or Business Administration
  • Alternatively, will consider students enrolled in related fields
