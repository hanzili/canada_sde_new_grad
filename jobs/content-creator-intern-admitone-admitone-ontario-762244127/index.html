Full Description

Content Creator Intern

The MRG Group

Toronto, ON

We're hiring a summer intern to help us launch a new content engine across our portfolio of nightlife, events, and ticketing brands. This isn't a role you're inheriting from someone else. It's one you'll invent, execute, and grow.

You'll own both written blogs and short-form video. You'll research, write, and publish SEO-driven blog posts that pull organic traffic from Google and AI search across our domains. You'll create and star in short-form video for Instagram. And you'll help produce and appear in episodes of our podcast covering Canadian nightlife, music, and live events.

You're genuinely comfortable on camera. You write cleanly, without sounding like AI. And you have a sharp instinct for what our audience clicks on, watches, and shares. You'll be the founding member of our content team. You'll post daily. You'll be encouraged to experiment, fail, and try new things.

About Our Internship Program

The MRG Group Internship Program is designed to provide you with practical skills, valuable experiences, and exposure to real-world scenarios in the Experience industry.

The program is structured to ensure that 80% of your time is dedicated to performing activities specific to their role, while the remaining 20% is allocated to job shadowing, skill development, and team projects across various businesses within the MRG Group.

About The MRG Group

Founded in 2008, The MRG Group is Canada's largest independent entertainment company and a leader in experience creation. Our mission is to bring experiences to life for creators, fans, brands, and partners.

The MRG Group is an integrated network of companies operating across the hospitality, venue, media, ticketing (including the ticketing service Admit One), and insurance sectors. With a portfolio of 18 hospitality properties across Canada, The MRG Group also owns and operates an events business, a travel company, and a digital publication.

Key Responsibilities

  • Pitch, script, star in, and edit short-form video for social media
  • Research keywords, then write, edit, and publish blog posts
  • Pitch concepts for, appear in, edit, and produce podcast episodes

Knowledge, Skills, Abilities

  • An active blogger and vlogger already: you have a body of work, whether that's a personal blog or a YouTube/TikTok/Instagram presence.
  • Comfortable having your real name appear as the bylined author on published blog posts
  • Comfortable being the face of our content, on camera, with the understanding that the goal is for this content to travel widely
  • Strong writer who can produce clean, engaging copy without an AI tell
  • Able to carry a video on your own energy and presence
  • Working knowledge of SEO basics: keywords, search intent.
  • Familiar with short-form video editing (CapCut or similar)
  • An interest in nightlife, music, and live events
  • Self-directed: can take a rough idea to a published post or posted video without supervision
  • Organized enough to hit a daily posting cadence

Internship Program Term

Start Date: August 4, 2026

End Date: December 18, 2026

What’s In It For You?

  • Paid Internship (Minimum Wage)
  • A fun and casual environment. You will be joining a close-knit, innovative team of creative and energetic go-getters
  • Employee discounts to our concerts, shows and hospitality venues
  • A welcoming, fun and safe work environment where everyone can show up authentically to work each day and feel respected
  • Career development opportunities with a company that encourages individuals to learn and grow

Terms & Expectations

  • This internship is a full-time position and requires in-office attendance 5 days a week (40 hours).

Thank you for your interest in The MRG Group. Given the anticipated interest in this role, only those selected for an interview will be contacted.
