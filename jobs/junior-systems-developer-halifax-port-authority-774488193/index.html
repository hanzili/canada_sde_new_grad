About Halifax Port Authority

Full Description

When you join the Halifax Port Authority (HPA), you’re stepping into a role with impact, purpose, and the opportunity to make a difference on a global scale. Guided by our values of accountability, collaboration, inclusion, integrity and resilience, we work together to connect Canada to the world.

The world’s largest shipping lines call here, connecting the port to more than 150 countries. Every day, our team supports a leading international gateway that drives lasting prosperity for our communities.

Our core mandate is to facilitate the movement of goods and people to support economic development while ensuring safe, efficient, and sustainable operations. Our business includes three distinct lines of activity – cargo, cruise and real estate – supported by our dedicated and talented employees.

We are ISO45001 certified and deeply committed to a culture of safety, ensuring the well-being of every team member is our top priority.

At the Halifax Port Authority, you'll be part of something bigger, joining an inspiring team that is helping shape the future for the region, the province and Canada.

Currently, the Halifax Port Authority (“HPA”) requires a

Junior Systems Developer

As a Junior Systems Developer, you will contribute to the design, development, testing, and support of data integration and enterprise applications, with a focus on Microsoft Azure technologies. Working closely with business stakeholders, technical teams, and external partners, you'll help create reliable, innovative solutions that improve how data is managed and shared across the organization. This is an excellent opportunity for an early-career developer who is passionate about technology, data, continuous learning, and delivering business value through modern digital solutions. This is a term position with a duration of three (3) months.

What You'll Do:

  • Design, develop, test, and implement applications and integration solutions using Microsoft Azure technologies.
  • Collaborate with developers, systems analysts, service providers, and business stakeholders to deliver technology solutions.
  • Analyze existing systems, data flows, and processes to identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Enhance and support enterprise applications to meet evolving business requirements.
  • Create and execute test plans, troubleshoot issues, and support quality assurance activities.
  • Develop technical documentation and user guides for new and existing systems.
  • Provide second- and third-level technical support for business applications and data solutions.
  • Develop scripts and automation solutions that improve application functionality and performance.
  • Monitor, test, and optimize software performance and reliability.
  • Work with project teams and stakeholders to resolve integration challenges and achieve project objectives.
  • Stay current with emerging technologies and recommend improvements that support HPA's digital transformation goals.

What You Bring:

  • Post-secondary education in Computer Science, Software Development, Data Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • One to three years of experience in software development, systems development, data integration, application support, or a similar technical role; recent graduates with relevant co-op, internship, or project experience are encouraged to apply.
  • Knowledge of programming and software development best practices.
  • Experience or exposure to Microsoft Azure, cloud-based applications, or data integration platforms.
  • Familiarity with database development, SQL, application testing, scripting, and technical documentation.
  • Exposure to Python, data analytics concepts, Apache Spark, or modern data architecture is considered an asset.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with exceptional attention to detail.
  • Ability to communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • A collaborative approach and commitment to teamwork, continuous improvement, and learning.
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment.

Why you’ll love working here:

  • Dynamic workplace that embraces professional development
  • Benefits that make a difference
  • Commitment to sustainability – HPA Sustainability Strategy
  • Cutting-edge technology and innovation
  • Inclusive and supportive culture
  • Community partnership
  • …and of course, the stunning Halifax Harbour view from our doorstep
