Job Opportunity

Smurfit Westrock is looking for a summer HR Intern to work on a data focused project. The intern will be responsible for the completion of the project and as time permits, will be exposed to other areas of HR. The summer term will be June – August inclusive.

What you need to succeed:

  • Current enrollment or recent completion of a bachelor's degree in HR, Business, Organizational Psychology, or another related field
  • Clear communication skills, both written and verbal
  • Self-driven, organized and detail oriented
  • Excellent time management skills
  • Excellent computer and technology skills (e.g., PowerPoint, Excel, Word, etc.)
