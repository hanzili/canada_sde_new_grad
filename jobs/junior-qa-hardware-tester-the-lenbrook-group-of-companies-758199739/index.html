Full Description

JUNIOR QA HARDWARE TESTER

For more than 50 years, Lenbrook International has been dedicated to bringing high-resolution audio into homes around the world through meaningful technological innovation and thoughtfully designed products. Our brands, NAD and PSB, are recognized as leaders in high-performance audio, while Bluesound and Bluesound Professional, powered by our proprietary BluOS platform, continue to redefine the wireless, multi-room audio streaming experience.

We are seeking a highly motivated Junior QA Hardware Tester to join our team in Pickering. The ideal candidate has a background in Electronics Technology and enjoys hands-on testing, troubleshooting, and problem-solving. This role is well-suited for someone who is detail-oriented, takes initiative, and is eager to learn while working with a wide range of consumer electronics.

A Day in the Life:

·       Run test cases to verify new firmware before public release.

·       Conduct incoming quality inspections on consumer electronic products and spare parts.

·       Perform hardware validation on products prior to their release to manufacturing.

·       Create rework instructions. Rework or modify products and document activities.

·       Perform defect analysis on product returns.

·       Build and test prototypes received from the factories and report findings.

·       Repair and adjust electronic equipment using soldering tools.

·       Read and follow product manuals and schematics.

·       Provide support to sales administration and engineering.

·       Assist with inventory movement through the warehouse.

·       Other related duties as required.

You Bring to the Team:

·       Post secondary education in an Electronics Technology program.

·       Basic electronic hardware troubleshooting.

·       User experience with iOS, Android, and Windows operating systems.

·       Knowledge of computer networking, including setting up WiFi routers and configuring NAS storage.

·       Logical and analytical thinker.

·       Detail-oriented, accurate, with strong attention to detail.

·       Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

·       Ability to work independently with minimal supervision.

·       Knowledge of analog and digital audio concepts is an asset.

Total Compensation:

$45,000 to $47,000 annually (inclusive of base salary and variable sales incentive)

Additional benefits include:

  • Paid vacation and sick leave
  • Health and dental benefits
  • Group RRSP program

In accordance with the Ontario Human Rights Code, the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 and Lenbrook’s Accommodation Policy, accommodation will be provided in all parts of the hiring process. Please make any such needs known in advance.
