Full Description

We are Hiring

Junior IT recruiter

At YDCPro, we’re proud to be a Great Place to Work certified company, built on a culture of collaboration, growth, and purpose. We value people who are curious, committed, and ready to contribute in a meaningful way.

With that in mind, we’re excited to share an opportunity to join our team and contribute to a role that supports our continued growth.

We are located right on the union station 2 minutes’ walk from Union station to our office

ASAP to start – Remote – Once a month in Downtown

3 months contract

Education and qualifications:

Graduate degree in any discipline

1 to 2 years in IT staffing

Must be authorized to work in Canada

Excellent communication skills

Job responsibilities

  • Drive the recruitment cycle from end to end, including sourcing, screening, interviewing and placement.
  • Working on assigned job orders and submitting suitable candidates on time with right qualification and experience
  • Sourcing and screening candidates based on job requirements through various job portals including LinkedIn etc.
  • Submitting qualified and potential candidates on time to meet the submission deadlines and also meet the competitive targets.
  • Devise and implement sourcing strategies and plans to identify potential candidates from referrals, cold calls, social media, conferences and trade shows, referrals, professional organizations, market research and other related channels
  • Post positions on job portals, social media and other relevant places
  • Build strong database network and pool of candidates
