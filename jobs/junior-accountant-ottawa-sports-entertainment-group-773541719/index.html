About the Role

Process AP/AR, reconciliations, and audit support

About OTTAWA SPORTS & ENTERTAINMENT GROUP

Ottawa-based sports & entertainment group; large employer

Full Description

Reporting to the Controller, the Junior Accountant is an exciting opportunity to launch your career in a fast-paced sports and entertainment environment. As a member of the Finance team, you'll play a role in supporting the day-to-day financial operations by processing and monitoring a high volume of transactions while gaining valuable hands-on experience. This role offers continuous learning and the opportunity to develop your skills in a dynamic and collaborative environment.

This full-time, permanent role has an annual salary range of $45,000 to $48,800, with 100% employer-paid benefits. The final compensation will be determined based on the successful candidate's experience and qualifications. This is a replacement position and offers flexibility to work in a hybrid work arrangement.

What you’ll do:

  • Record daily bank transactions and complete bank reconciliations for multiple entities.
  • Complete various accounts payable tasks including processes invoices, expense reports, purchase requisitions, and coordinating approvals.
  • Manage the accounts payable and accounting inboxes by responding to inquiries, uploading invoices and examining customer statements.
  • Process credit card transactions, authorizations and analyze statements.
  • Verify that G/L transactions are accurate, in line with budget, and comply with financial policies and procedures.
  • Provide timely responses to and cultivate positive relationships with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Assist with year-end external audits.
  • Perform other duties as required.

What’s Needed:

  • A degree or college diploma in accounting or related field.
  • 0-2 years of accounting experience with experience in bank reconciliations and accounts payable considered an asset.
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, Outlook).
  • Experience working with Microsoft Great-Plains and NetSuite is considered an asset.
  • Strong ability to work within a team environment.
  • Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written.
  • Highly organized with outstanding administrative and time management skills.
  • Strong attention to detail.
  • Working towards CPA designation considered an asset.
  • Bilingualism in English and French is an asset.

Why OSEG/ TD Place?

You can make a meaningful and powerful impact on the entertainment industry in Ottawa. Working with Ottawa Sports and Entertainment Group (OSEG) / TD Place will provide you with a competitive health benefits plan, dynamic team environment and the ability to continuously learn and grow.

Located at Lansdowne Park, OSEG / TD Place is the largest sports and entertainment company in the capital. We are proud owners of the Ottawa REDBLACKS, and Ottawa 67’s. We are also home to Atletico Ottawa, Ottawa Blackjacks, and Ottawa Rapid FC, and live events at TD Place. OSEG / TD Place and its related Foundation were founded on the vision of its partners - to create year-round world-class guest experiences and give back to the Ottawa community.

OSEG / TD Place is an advocate for equity, diversity, and inclusion and invites and encourages all candidates to apply for this role, including women, Indigenous peoples, members of racialized communities, persons with disabilities and persons of minority sexual orientations and gender identities. Additionally, we are happy to accommodate any individual needs in keeping with the Ontario Human Rights Code and the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act. If you require accommodation to participate in the hiring process, please contact us to make your needs known in advance.

Please note this role will require interacting with the public, including in crowded spaces. TD Place is committed to providing a safe and enjoyable experience for all employees and guests. We encourage you receive a full dose of the COVID-19 vaccination (subject to applicable exceptions).

We appreciate the time and effort it takes to apply and thank all applicants for their interest. Our selection process utilizes AI to compliment application screening. We’ll reach out to candidates whose applications best match the role's requirements.
