About the Role

About Worley

Global energy, chemicals services leader; strong EIT program

Full Description

Building on our past. Ready for the future Worley is a global professional services company of energy, chemicals and resources experts. We partner with customers to deliver projects and create value over the life of their assets. We’re bridging two worlds, moving towards more sustainable energy sources, while helping to provide the energy, chemicals and resources needed now.

Purpose As part of the Worley Graduate Community and under the guidance of experienced process engineers, the Graduate Process Engineer-in-Training (EIT) supports the delivery of safe, high-quality process engineering services to Worley and its customers. This role provides structured mentorship, hands-on project exposure, and a strong technical foundation to support professional development toward registration as a Professional Engineer.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver process engineering services that meet Worley, client, and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements, on time and within budget.
  • You will support the process engineering team by:

  • Assisting with the preparation of process engineering deliverables, including:

  • Process flow diagrams (PFDs) and piping & instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs)

  • Heat and material balances

  • Process datasheets, calculations, and technical reports

  • Contributing to engineering studies, technical evaluations, and design reviews under supervision.

  • Supporting the development of process models and simulations as required.
  • Participating in hazard identification and risk assessment activities (e.g., HAZID, HAZOP) as part of multidisciplinary teams.
  • Collaborating with other disciplines (mechanical, piping, electrical, instrumentation, and project controls) to support integrated project delivery.
  • Supporting procurement, construction, and commissioning activities by assisting with technical clarifications and document reviews.
  • Identifying technical issues, proposing solutions, and seeking guidance when appropriate.
  • Maintaining strong awareness of health, safety, and environmental (HSE) requirements and contributing to Worley’s safety culture.

What You Will Bring Technical & Industry Experience

  • Co-op, internship, or vacation student experience in an engineering or industrial environment is an asset.
  • Foundational knowledge of chemical/process engineering principles, including thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat and mass transfer, and reaction engineering.
  • Developing skills with process engineering tools and software (e.g., Aspen HYSYS, Aspen Plus, UniSim, or similar).
  • Basic proficiency with common productivity tools (e.g., MS Office Suite).
  • Curiosity, strong problem-solving skills, and a willingness to learn in a project-based environment.

Required Education, Qualifications & Training

  • A recognized and relevant engineering degree in Chemical Engineering or a related discipline that is eligible for registration as an Engineer-in-Training and progression toward Professional Engineer status.
