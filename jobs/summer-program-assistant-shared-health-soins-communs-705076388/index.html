NORWEST SUMMER STUDENT JOB POSTING Summer Program Assistant Paid Term Position | 15 hours/week | Term for 8 weeks Department: Baby Steps and Bright Start Program

Position: Summer Program Assistant

Responsible To: Community Development Coordinator

Approximate Start Date : July 6, 2026

Salary: $16.00/hour

Anticipated Shift: 15 hours per week (Tuesday and Thursday 9:00 AM–12:30 PM; Wednesday 9:00 AM–5:00 PM)

Summary Of Job Description The Summer Program Assistant will support early years programming for infants, young children, and families across program sites. Responsibilities include assisting with program setup and cleanup, supporting program delivery and family engagement, helping with light food preparation, maintaining cleanliness and sanitation, and following organizational policies and confidentiality requirements.

Employment is subject to eligibility under the Manitoba Green Team Program, including confirmation that the applicant is between 15–29 years of age, legally entitled to work in Canada, and possesses a valid Social Insurance Number.

Qualifications:

  • High school diploma preferred or currently enrolled in high school or post-secondary education
  • Energetic, friendly, and enthusiastic attitude
  • Willingness to take initiative and work as part of a team
  • Interest in working with infants, young children, and families
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills
  • Ability to work in a respectful, inclusive, and non-judgmental manner
  • Subject to successful criminal records and child abuse registry checks
  • Food Handler’s Certificate considered an asset

Please submit your application in confidence to: Rupsi Parajuli, HR Assistant

NorWest Co-op Community Health

785 Keewatin Street, Winnipeg MB

Email: rparajuli@norwestcoop.ca

Application deadline: June 17, 2026 We thank everyone who expressed interest but only those selected for an interview will be contacted.
