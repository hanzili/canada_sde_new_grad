About the Role

Full Description

About The Role The Junior BI Analyst helps provide clarity for our primary mandates by translating complex information into actionable insights. This position specifically supports a significant project for a major North American financial organization, focusing on the precision of data reported to senior leadership. You will ensure that every piece of analysis we provide is grounded in logic and clear evidence.

About Arabian Private Holdings Arabian Private Holdings operates on the principle that most critical business problems do not require a massive team of consultants or overly long methodology decks. Instead, we provide our clients with direct access to experienced partners who can sit with a problem, form a rigorous view, and defend it. Our work centers on corporate strategy, governance, and operating model efficiency for large family groups and government entities. We are a fully remote firm, prioritizing the quality of our analysis and the strength of our relationships over traditional office hierarchy or aggressive growth targets. If a project does not fit our specific expertise, we are direct about it, and if it does, we stay involved until results are delivered.

What You Will Do

  • Gather and clean data from various sources to support internal mandates and client reviews.
  • Build and maintain dashboards that track the performance of financial portfolios for our North American financial services client.
  • Assist principals in identifying trends within a holding company to support portfolio clarity.
  • Create simple, clear visualizations that explain complex trends to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Perform routine quality checks on data to ensure accuracy and consistency across all strategy reports.
  • Research industry benchmarks to provide context for the performance of specific client investments.
  • Draft brief summaries of findings that focus on plain English rather than technical jargon.
  • Support the firm in building efficient internal systems for tracking project progress and resource allocation.

What We Are Looking For

  • A curious mindset and a genuine interest in how large organizations are governed and structured.
  • Basic familiarity with data visualization software such as Power BI or Tableau.
  • Logical thinking skills with the ability to spot errors or outliers in a dataset.
  • Strong writing skills and the ability to present a clear argument based on data.
  • A basic understanding of how spreadsheets work for organizing and calculating information.
  • The ability to work independently in a remote setting while staying organized.

How We Work Our firm functions through a remote operating model that values autonomy and directness. We avoid unnecessary meetings and instead focus on the quality of our written communication and the clarity of our logic. In this role, you will work closely with our principals, receiving direct mentorship and feedback on your analysis. We operate with a high level of accountability, meaning every team member is responsible for the accuracy and integrity of their work. Because we are small and focused, your contributions will be directly visible and highly relevant to the decisions our clients make.

What The Role Offers

  • Direct involvement in high stakes mandates for prominent international financial organizations.
  • Mentorship from experienced firm principals who prioritize clarity and rigor.
  • A fully remote work environment that offers flexibility in your location and workspace.
  • Daily exposure to complex problems involving corporate governance and portfolio strategy.
  • Participation in a firm that values professional honesty and independent thought.
  • Attractive and competitive pay for an entry level position within the current market.
