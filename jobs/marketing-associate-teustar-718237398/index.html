Full Description

TEUSTAR Inc. is an innovative Canadian startup dedicated to redefining daily convenience through its upcoming everyday utility app. Beginning with food delivery and expanding into a range of essential services, TEUSTAR aims to simplify everyday tasks with efficiency and ease. Represented by its modern and approachable mascot, TEU, the company is committed to delivering practical, user-friendly solutions that enhance everyday life.

Position: Marketing Associate

Location: Scarborough & North York, Ontario (On-Site)

TEUSTAR is seeking energetic, outgoing, and motivated Marketing Associate looking to gain real-world startup experience in branding, field marketing, customer acquisition, and community engagement.

As a Marketing Associate, you will play a key role in helping TEUSTAR grow its customer base through grassroots marketing campaigns, brand awareness initiatives, and direct interaction with local communities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Promote the TEUSTAR app through field marketing and community engagement initiatives
  • Support in B2B marketing, customer acquisition efforts and help increase app downloads
  • Conduct flyer distribution campaigns and public outreach activities
  • Represent the TEUSTAR brand professionally at events and promotional activations
  • Assist with launch campaigns, promotions, and awareness drives
  • Educate potential customers about TEUSTAR's services and features
  • Gather customer feedback and market insights
  • Support social media content creation during marketing campaigns
  • Collaborate with the marketing team on growth strategies and campaign ideas
  • Participate in team meetings and brainstorming sessions

Requirements

  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Outgoing, energetic, and confident personality
  • Interest in marketing, startups, branding, or sales
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively
  • Comfortable engaging with the public
  • Students and recent graduates are encouraged to apply
  • No prior experience required; training will be provided

What You'll Gain

  • Hands-on startup marketing experience
  • Performance Based Incentives
  • Experience in customer acquisition and B2B marketing
  • Networking opportunities within a growing startup environment
  • Direct exposure to leadership and marketing teams
  • Experience supporting real product launch campaigns and field activations
  • Potential future growth opportunities within TEUSTAR

Preferred Areas

  • Scarborough
  • North York
