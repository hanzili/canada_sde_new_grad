Full Description

The Opportunity:

Company, Department: Emera Energy, Corporate Finance

Location: Halifax, NS

Closing Date: June 11th, 2026

Emera Energy is currently seeking a Co-op, Corporate Accounting student for the Fall 2026 term (September 7 – December 18) within the Corporate Finance team. This 16-week opportunity provides hands-on experience in financial reporting, settlement processes, and accounting operations within a dynamic business environment.

In this role, you will gain valuable exposure to month-end close processes, financial analysis, and corporate accounting activities while working closely with professionals across the organization.

What you’ll do:

As a co-op student, you will contribute to key accounting processes and support financial operations within the Corporate Finance Team. This includes:

  • Prepare monthly journal entries and support general ledger monitoring, including full working paper and audit file preparation, account reconciliations, and analysis
  • Assist with settlement activities, including invoice reconciliation, payment processing, and monitoring bank account activity
  • Work closely with internal teams to resolve settlement discrepancies and ensure accuracy
  • Prepare government and regulatory reports related to business activities
  • Provide customer service support through telephone and email communications
  • Assist with routine and ad hoc financial reporting and analysis

Work term outcomes:

By the end of your work term, you will have:

  • Gained a strong understanding of the month-end close cycle and corporate accounting processes
  • Developed a foundational understanding of the marketing and trading business
  • Strengthened your analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills
  • Developed the ability to work independently in a professional accounting environment
  • Built experience delivering effective customer service in a finance setting

Who you are:

You’re a motivated and detail-oriented student in your 3rd or 4th year of a Bachelor of Commerce (Accounting) program, looking to develop your accounting and financial reporting skills in a collaborative environment.

You bring:

  • Strong quantitative, analytical, and organizational skills with high attention to detail
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, deadline-driven, team-focused environment
  • Solid business acumen and a customer-focused mindset
  • Ability to handle confidential information with professionalism
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, along with experience using financial reporting systems

Who we are:

Emera Inc., a North American Energy Leader, is one of Atlantic Canada’s largest publicly traded companies and is ranked one of Canada’s Top 100 Employers and Top Employer for Young People.

Across Emera, we know our success is driven by our strong & dedicated teams. We support our teams by developing the skills and attributes they need to become our leaders of tomorrow. We do this in a number of ways including on the job training, work experiences and mentorship opportunities. We are proud to offer hands-on work experience to post-secondary students through student opportunities across our operating companies. By offering more than 125 student employment opportunities each year.

To learn more about us, please visit; www.emera.com

What we offer:

Facing the beautiful waterfront, you can find us located at 5151 Terminal Road in Downtown Halifax. We believe in caring for our employees and are proud to offer our students:

  • Perks in the office; Free Coffee Machine, Specialty Teas, Sparkling Water Machine, Access to our onsite Gym, Rooftop Patio and Private Meeting rooms and Collaboration Spaces;
  • Networking and Growth; Our Student Social Committee offers students a way to connect with their peers with biweekly events like Keynote Speaker Series, (Virtual) Coffee Chats and Games;
  • Comprehensive and Fun Onboarding Program including facilitated group orientation sessions for all students joining us each term, and a handy Student Handbook and Technology Guide.
