AI Co-op, Brampton, ON

Are you passionate about Artificial Intelligence and excited to build solutions that make a real business impact? Join our team as an AI Engineering Co-op and work alongside experienced engineers to design, build, and deploy AI-powered applications using the latest technologies, including Large Language Models (LLMs), machine learning, and automation. This is a hands-on opportunity to gain real-world experience while contributing to innovative projects that improve customer and employee experiences.

What You’ll Do

  • Design, develop, and test AI-powered applications and automation solutions using Large Language Models (LLMs), Python, and modern AI frameworks.
  • Build and optimize prompts, AI workflows, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) solutions to solve real business challenges.
  • Collaborate with software engineers, product teams, and business stakeholders to prototype, evaluate, and deploy AI features.
  • Analyze datasets, evaluate AI model performance, and recommend improvements based on experimentation and user feedback.
  • Document technical solutions, share learnings with the team, and contribute to continuous improvement of AI development practices.

What You Bring Currently enrolled in a Computer Science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or related post-secondary program.

  • Experience programming in Python, Java, JavaScript, or another modern programming language through coursework, personal projects, or internships.
  • Familiarity with AI/ML concepts, including machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), or Large Language Models (LLMs).
  • Experience with Git, APIs, SQL, cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP), or AI frameworks such as LangChain, OpenAI, Hugging Face, or similar is an asset.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with a willingness to learn new technologies and work collaboratively in an Agile environment.
