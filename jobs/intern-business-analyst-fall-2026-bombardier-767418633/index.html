About the Role

Support budgeting, forecasting, and reporting.

About Bombardier

Global aerospace leader; strong learning opportunities

Full Description

Innovation is in our DNA… is it in yours?
Bombardier is a global leader in aviation, creating innovative and game-changing planes. Our products and services provide world-class experiences that set new standards in passenger comfort, energy efficiency, reliability and safety. We are a global organization focused on working together with a team spirit.

Your boarding pass will include…

Several conferences, including:

  • Meet An Executive
  • Women Taking Flight

Learning more about Bombardier, including:

  • Bombardier Products conference
  • Visits of the Bombardier sites
  • Bombardier Academy of Learning

Many social/networking opportunities, including:

  • Volunteering
  • Networking for Success
  • 5 à 7, Potluck, and much more!

What are your contributions to the team?

Work with the financial analysts in their monthly tasks

Participate to the annual budget process and to the quarterly forecast exercise

Produce various financial reports

Develop new financial analysis templates

Answer ad hoc demands from the finance team or different business partners

Participate in the implementation of process improvement initiatives

Produce monthly invoices

How to thrive in this role?

You are currently completing a bachelor's degree in accounting or finance

You have a strong team spirit and a positive attitude

You are organized and able to meet tight deadlines

You have a good knowledge of the Microsoft Office suite, especially Excel

You have good communication skills in French and English

You have an excellent analytical mind, you demonstrate commitment and perseverance.

Boarding Information:

  • Location: Marcel-Laurin Plant 1
  • Duration: 4 months
  • Flexible workplace-Hybrid

It is important to note that our internship opportunities are open to students only, not new graduates. All our interns may be required to occasionally travel outside of Canada for training/work purpose.
