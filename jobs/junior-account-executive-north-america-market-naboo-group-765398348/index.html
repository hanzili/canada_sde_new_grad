About the Role

About NABOO GROUP

Montreal startup with inbound sales focus

Full Description

Naboo is the smart, all-in-one concierge for corporate events — helping companies book everything from venues and catering to transportation and team-building activities. Whether it’s a business lunch or a full-blown annual retreat, Naboo makes it happen smoothly and in style.

Behind the scenes, our platform uses AI-powered automation to eliminate the tedious admin and repetitive tasks no one wants to deal with. The result? Less time chasing invoices and emails, more time focusing on what really matters: creating unforgettable moments.

After closing our third funding round in 2024, we’re scaling fast — because let’s face it: the corporate event space is long overdue for a shake-up. Budgets are growing, but tools and workflows haven’t evolved in decades. We’re here to change that.

And in a world transformed by remote and hybrid work, real human connection has become a business imperative. From fostering a sense of belonging to building loyalty and culture, in-person events are now essential touchpoints — not just perks.

With intelligent planning, full financial transparency, and a sleek user experience, Naboo is rewriting the rules of corporate event management. We’re not just making events easier to organize — we’re making them matter more than ever.

Your role:

As an Account Executive for the North American Market, you’ll be a key player in Naboo’s international expansion — helping teams in the US & Canada plan unforgettable events.

Your mission: Turn qualified leads into happy customers. You’ll do this by understanding your clients’ unique needs, creating trust-based relationships, and guiding them smoothly through the sales journey - all the way to a successful close.

  • Support clients (inbound leads via marketing or returning customers) in planning their seminars, team retreats, or workshops through the Naboo platform and curated venue collection
  • Qualify and manage leads from our marketing and SDR teams, present tailored solutions, close deals, and build long-term relationships with key accounts
  • Contribute to internal improvements - from process suggestions to product feedback
  • Participate in strategy discussions shaping Naboo’s roadmap
  • Mentor and onboard new team members as we grow

No cold calling - all leads are warm and inbound.

You’re the ideal fit if:

  • You have experience as an SDR or Account Executive, ideally in a startup or scale-up
  • You are fluent in English
  • You enjoy working towards clear goals and love closing deals
  • French is a plus, but not required

Naboo is the ideal company for you if:

  • You’re looking for ownership, impact, and growth
  • You love results and are energized by challenges
  • You’re excited about joining a fast-moving startup and shaping its future
  • You want to go beyond your job description and contribute your ideas
  • You value a supportive, inclusive, and people-first environment

What we offer:

  • Beautiful offices in the center of Montreal
  • Coverage of transportation costs up to £200 per month
  • 100% coverage by a mutual insurance company (Canada Life)
  • A Classpass subscription to get cool perks for sports

Recruitment process:

Step 1: Intro call with the Country Manager (15min)

Step 2: Business case with the Sales Manager (30min)

Step 3: Speed dating with 4-5 Naboo team members from different departments, 15 minutes each, for an informal discussion and insights into the company’s culture

Pay: $35,000.00-$80,000.00 per year

Benefits:

  • Company events
  • Extended health care
  • On-site parking
  • Paid time off
  • Wellness program

Work Location: In person
