About the Role

About Schneider Electric

Global energy management leader; sustainability focused and innovative

Full Description

Kickstart your technical career with a role that offers real responsibility and real learning. As a Technical Intern, you'll work alongside experienced engineers on projects that design, develop, and optimize innovative products and solutions. This isn't just observation—it's hands-on experience that builds the foundation for your future.

What will you do:

  • Assist engineering teams by conducting analyses, documenting findings, and supporting product development initiatives
  • Contribute to the design, testing, and optimization of products and solutions under expert guidance
  • Learn industry standards, engineering methods, and technical tools used throughout the product lifecycle
  • Collaborate with team members on active projects while building practical skills and professional competencies

What qualifications will make you successful:

  • Genuine enthusiasm for engineering and a strong desire to learn and grow
  • Detail-oriented approach to documentation and technical tasks
  • Team-oriented mindset with openness to feedback and supervision
  • Problem-solving attitude and willingness to take initiative in a learning environment

What's in it for me:

  • Real-world engineering experience on projects that make a difference
  • Mentorship and guidance from professionals who care about your success
  • Exposure to advanced tools, technologies, and industry best practices
  • A collaborative culture that values curiosity, questions, and continuous learning
  • A launchpad for building a rewarding technical career
