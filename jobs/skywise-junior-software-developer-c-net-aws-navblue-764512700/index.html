Full Description

Job Description:

Skywise is a leading digital services company, wholly owned by Airbus. We deliver a first-of-its-kind offer that seamlessly integrates Planning & Operations Control, Flight, Technical, and Ground Operations. By providing interoperable digital solutions for end-to-end operations, Skywise combines aircraft manufacturer expertise and digital know-how to transform operational complexity into predictable and profitable performance, ensuring resilient and more sustainable operations.

As a Software Engineer at Skywise, you will play a pivotal role in shaping our digital solutions. We have organized the core responsibilities to give you a clearer picture of your day-to-day impact:

Development & Architecture

  • Build & Scale: Assemble and edit source code repositories to develop a high-quality software stack tailored to product needs.
  • Architectural Alignment: Develop design specifications and parameters that strictly comply with existing architectural blueprints, standards, and environment structures.
  • System Integration: Determine how existing applications, legacy systems, databases, web interfaces, and hardware logic across multiple platforms work together to meet emerging enterprise requirements.

Full Lifecycle & Quality Assurance

  • End-to-End Ownership: Participate in all phases of the product development lifecycle—from initial analysis and design through to testing, integration, and live operations.
  • Test Strategy: Define comprehensive test strategies, scripts, and scenarios, while actively monitoring test execution to ensure system reliability.
  • Deployment & Monitoring: Deploy, test, and integrate highly available, scalable, maintainable, and thoroughly monitored systems and services.
  • Functional Coordination: Lead and coordinate both functional and technical integrations across teams.

Continuous Improvement & Support

  • Innovation: Research, evaluate, and develop new tools to continuously improve product development efficiency.
  • Documentation: Assist in the development of clear, user-friendly product manuals to support our end-users.
