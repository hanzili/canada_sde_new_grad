Full Description

Job Overview

Inkaer is a Canadian talent platform that connects international students at top Canadian universities and colleges with Canadian startups and growing companies. We handle sourcing, matching, and support so our clients focus on building — and we stay involved throughout the placement to make sure both the student and the company get the most out of the internship.

We're placing a Full Stack Developer Intern with one of our clients, NewPath Consulting — a Toronto-based technology consultancy that helps membership organizations, non-profits, clubs, and associations run their operations more efficiently. NewPath is the #1 service partner for WildApricot (Membership Management Software) and Make.com automations, and builds custom integrations that hundreds of client organizations rely on to manage members, events, websites, and finances. This is a paid, 4-month internship. You'll work on real integrations between WildApricot and third-party services, build custom Make.com scenarios and Node.js backend logic, and contribute to AccountBridge, NewPath's QuickBooks-for-WildApricot product.

Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain React / Vite / Node.js backend integrations for the AccountBridge product
  • Design Make.com automation scenarios that connect WildApricot with external services (CRM, email marketing, payment gateways)
  • Develop custom Make.com modules or use webhooks and API calls from Make.com to Node.js when needed
  • Write clean, well-documented, and testable code
  • Collaborate with client success and project managers to understand client needs
  • Troubleshoot integration and front-end issues

Requirements

  • Currently studying computer science, software engineering, or a related field at a Canadian university or college
  • Familiarity with React, Node.js, and JavaScript
  • Understanding of RESTful API principles
  • Comfort with Git version control
  • Strong problem-solving skills and curiosity about integration work
  • Clear communicator — you can explain technical decisions to non-technical teammates

Nice to have

  • Prior experience with automation platforms (Make.com, Zapier, n8n)
  • Familiarity with WildApricot API
  • Experience with PostgreSQL or MySQL
  • Exposure to AWS, Google Cloud, or Docker

Terms

  • Paid position
  • 40 hours per week
  • 4-month term
  • Hybrid — NewPath's office is at 5000 Yonge Street, Suite 1901 Toronto
