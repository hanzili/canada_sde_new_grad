About the Role

About Canada Engineering Services Inc

Toronto-based civil engineering firm; diverse infrastructure projects

Full Description

Company Description Canada Engineering Services Inc is an engineering firm based in Toronto, Ontario, serving clients from its office at 39 Davisbrook Blvd. The company is focused on delivering practical, high-quality engineering solutions to support infrastructure and development projects. Team members collaborate closely with clients and project partners to provide reliable technical expertise and timely service. Working at Canada Engineering Services Inc offers exposure to local projects, opportunities to grow technical skills, and a professional environment that supports career development.

Role Description The Junior Geotechnical Engineer will support geotechnical investigations and design activities for a variety of civil and infrastructure projects. Day-to-day responsibilities include assisting with site investigations, collecting and logging soil samples, performing basic geotechnical analyses, and preparing technical reports under the guidance of senior engineers. The role involves coordinating with field staff, contributing to foundation design recommendations, and helping interpret subsurface conditions for project planning and construction. This is a full-time, on-site role based in Toronto, ON, requiring regular presence at the office and visits to project sites.

Qualifications

  • Ability to perform and support site investigation activities, including planning, fieldwork, and documentation.
  • Knowledge of soil behavior, classification, and testing methods relevant to geotechnical projects.
  • Foundational skills in geotechnical engineering, including basic analysis, report preparation, and design support.
  • Understanding of engineering geology principles for interpreting subsurface conditions and project risks.
  • Skills related to foundation design, including assisting with shallow and deep foundation recommendations.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering, or a closely related discipline.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with attention to detail and accuracy in calculations and records.
  • Effective written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to prepare clear field logs and technical summaries.
  • Willingness to work outdoors in varying weather conditions and to travel to local job sites as needed.
  • Eligibility for or interest in obtaining professional engineering licensure in Ontario (EIT status is an asset).
