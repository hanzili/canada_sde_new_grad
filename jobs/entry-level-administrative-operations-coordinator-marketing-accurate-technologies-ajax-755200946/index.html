Full Description

Job Summary

We are seeking a motivated Entry Level Administrative & Operations Coordinator & Marketing Coordinator to join our dynamic team. This versatile role offers the opportunity to develop skills across administrative operations, digital marketing, and content creation. The ideal candidate will be eager to learn, detail-oriented, and passionate about e-commerce and digital marketing strategies. This position is available on a full-time or part-time basis, providing flexible scheduling for candidates interested in gaining comprehensive experience in a fast-paced environment.

Responsibilities

  • Assist with day-to-day administrative and operational tasks to ensure smooth business functions
  • Support marketing initiatives including social media management, email campaigns, and content creation
  • Conduct keyword research and implement SEO strategies to improve online visibility
  • Manage and update website content using WordPress, HTML, CSS, and related tools
  • Design graphics and visual content utilizing Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Creative Suite for marketing materials
  • Coordinate digital advertising campaigns across platforms such as Google Ads, Facebook Advertising, and Google AdWords
  • Monitor website analytics using Google Analytics and other tools to assess campaign performance and optimize strategies
  • Support e-commerce activities by updating product listings, managing inventory data with SQL, and ensuring website functionality
  • Assist in developing and executing email marketing campaigns to engage target audiences
  • Collaborate with team members on project planning, content scheduling, and analytics reporting

Requirements

  • Basic understanding of digital marketing concepts including SEO, Google Ads, Facebook Advertising, and Google Analytics
  • Familiarity with website management platforms such as WordPress; knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript is a plus
  • Experience with graphic design tools like Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Creative Suite for creating visual content
  • Knowledge of e-commerce platforms and SQL database management is advantageous
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Eagerness to learn new skills in digital marketing, analytics, and administrative operations
  • Ability to work independently as well as part of a team in a fast-paced environment

This role offers an excellent opportunity for individuals looking to build a career in digital marketing, e-commerce, or administrative operations within a supportive team environment.
