About the Role

Fulfill orders; manage shipping and inventory

About Plantish Future

Small eco-friendly Vancouver brand; logistics internship

Full Description

Plantish is founded on zero waste, plastic-free & vegan principles. We desire to create a community while sharing inter-generational wisdom and a passion for plant-based products.

We a small business here in Vancouver and we are growing our shipping and dispatching team! You will be exposed to the fast-growing e-commerce industry and learn more about eco-friendly/zero waste products. You will work directly with us to manage the logistics of product packaging, shipping and dispatching.

Roles and Responsibilities:

  • Fulfill and organize orders to ensure the customer delivery process is efficient and accurate.
  • Create accurate shipping documentation for domestic shipments.
  • Perform inventory check and quality assurance of products;
  • Organize and tidy warehouse and update inventory quantity;
  • Tasks as assigned
  • Driver's license and access to a vehicle (Preferred not Mandatory)

Education: open to various education backgrounds. Students registered in a post-secondary school preferred. If not, please indicate educational background in your resume.

Qualifications

  • Any experience in postal logistisc, shelfing, product packaging, ecommerce order fulfillment, warehouse management preferred
  • Desire to work in a fast-paced, organized and positive environment.
  • Ability to frequently lift packages that may weigh up to 70 lbs.
  • Attention to detail and good time management skills.

Hours Per Week: 16-37.5
