Full Description

At S&C Electric Canada, Co-Op students are valued contributors to our mission of building a safer, more reliable, and more resilient electrical grid. With over 70 years of innovation in Canada, we continue this legacy as a trusted, forward-thinking leader in the power industry. As a co-op student, you will work on meaningful projects that support our operations and give you hands-on experience in a dynamic, safety-focused environment.

Join S&C to make an impact on tomorrow’s energy challenges!

Work Arrangement: Hybrid (3 days onsite, 2 days remote)

Work Term: Winter 2027 (4 Months: Jan. - Apr. 2027)

Compensation

At S&C, we are dedicated to providing competitive and equitable compensation for all our team members, with a commitment to transparency in our pay practices. The hourly pay range for this position is $22.70 - $36.40.Individual pay within this range is determined by several compensable factors, including the year of study, type of academic program, and the number of prior co-op work terms completed.

Join Our Team as a Technical Sales Associate (Co-op)!

The Technical Sales Associate (Co-op) is responsible for the support, quotation, order write up and application for S&C products, under minimal supervision. They are also the main contact for customers and provide assistance to team members in such areas as product knowledge, production process, departmental procedures and process.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Respond to and provide information to internal clients by telephone, written communication, or in person.
  • Basic Electrical & Protection Engineering performing various calculations, including available fault current analysis and fuse curve coordination
  • Learn and recommend protection for substation transformers, busses and feeders
  • Assist by collaborating and contributing to high-voltage protection projects
  • Learn and develop single line diagrams for electrical utility and industrial customers high-voltage circuits
  • Summarizing transmission and distribution level system protection specifications and applying S&C protection devices
  • Additional duties as assigned

What you’ll Need To Succeed:

  • Currently pursuing a post-secondary degree in Electrical engineering
  • In a registered in an eligible co-op program and is eligible to work 4 months starting in the Winter 2027 work term
  • Knowledge of basic engineering concepts
  • Basic understanding of electrical systems
  • Ability to comprehend electric schematic drawings and specifications
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving skills
  • Working knowledge of Microsoft Office software

Physical Effort:

Normal or frequent minor physical effort required for the day-to-day activity in the workplace. May involve standing, sitting, walking, climbing stairs and lifting/moving/using objects or equipment up to 20lbs.

Work Environment:

Acceptable working environment with minor or almost no disagreeable or challenging working conditions as part of everyday expected levels (all jobs will occasionally experience undesirable or disagreeable events).

About Us

In 1909, S&C Electric Company transformed the delivery of safe, reliable electricity with the invention of the Liquid Power Fuse. Today, as the world faces extreme weather events and the demand for electricity grows, S&C continues to innovate and advance the electrical grid, ensuring reliable and resilient power for homes, communities, and critical infrastructure around the world.

With a diverse, global workforce and core values around integrity, safety, and quality, S&C is a trusted industry leader and top workplace that offers meaningful careers to more than 3,500 team members. As a people-first organization, S&C is committed to fostering an inclusive and collaborative workplace where team members advance their careers through robust talent-development programs and involved leadership.

S&C’s deeply rooted belief diversity fosters greater creativity, innovation, and success guides the company to advance and sustain a diverse, equitable, and inclusive workplace culture.
