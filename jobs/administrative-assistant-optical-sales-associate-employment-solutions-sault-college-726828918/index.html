Job Description Administrative Assistant / Optical Sales Associate Station Tower Optometry – Sault Ste. Marie, ON Are you looking for a rewarding career where you can make a difference in people's lives every day? Station Tower Optometry is seeking a friendly, motivated, and customer-focused individual to join our fabulous team as an Administrative Assistant / Optical Sales Associate.

We are proud to provide exceptional eye care in a welcoming and supportive environment. Our team values collaboration, professionalism, and creating outstanding experiences for every patient who walks through our doors.

Hours: 22.5–37.5 hours per week (depending on clinic needs and candidate availability)

Compensation: Competitive wage based on experience and qualifications.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Welcome and greet patients with a positive attitude
  • Manage patient check-in and check-out processes
  • Communicate with patients in person, over the phone, and through email
  • Perform pre-testing and assist our doctors in delivering comprehensive eye care
  • Schedule appointments and coordinate patient visits
  • Process billing, payments, and insurance-related transactions
  • Assist patients with eyewear selections and optical sales
  • Maintain accurate patient records and support daily clinic operations
  • Contribute to a positive team environment and assist with additional duties as needed

Why Join Station Tower Optometry?

  • Work alongside a fun, supportive, and experienced team
  • Enjoy a positive workplace culture built on teamwork and respect
  • Gain valuable experience in both healthcare and optical retail
  • Make a meaningful impact on patients' vision and eye health every day
  • Receive compensation that reflects your experience, skills, and qualifications

If you're enthusiastic, dependable, and excited to join a clinic that truly values its team members, we'd love to hear from you!

Required Skills

  • Excellent customer service and communication skills
  • Strong organizational and time-management abilities
  • Professional, friendly, and patient-focused attitude
  • Ability to multitask and work efficiently in a fast-paced environment
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy
  • Comfortable using computers, email, and scheduling software
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • Reliable, dependable, and punctual
  • Willingness to learn new skills and take initiative
  • Problem-solving skills and adaptability

Requirements Preferred Qualifications

  • Previous experience in customer service, retail sales, administration, or healthcare
  • Optical, optometric, or medical office experience
  • Experience with insurance billing and direct billing programs
  • Experience with electronic medical records (EMR) software
  • Optometric Assistant certification or Optician experience

How To Apply Please submit your resume and cover letter to Drs. Alicia Mlynarczyk & Dustin Diotte at diottemlynarczykopc@gmail.com

Applications will be reviewed as they are received, and interviews will be scheduled on a rolling basis.

Thank you!
