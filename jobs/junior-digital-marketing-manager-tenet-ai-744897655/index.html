Full Description

The role

Yes, we built an AI marketing agent — the irony isn't lost on us. But the best products are still powered by great people. Tenet handles the strategy and execution. We're looking for someone who wakes up thinking about our digital presence and drives it forward.

We're looking for a Junior Digital Marketing Manager to grow Tenet's digital footprint. You'll be responsible for growing our audience across LinkedIn, Instagram, and other channels, bringing our content to life visually, and building a presence that makes people stop scrolling.

We already have a solid content foundation and a clear point of view. This is an early role, so you'll have the space to set things up the right way from the start. You won't be on your own either — Tenet's AI agent is here to guide your strategic direction day-to-day. But the creative execution, the judgment, and the community building are yours to run with.

We're a small, fast-moving team. You'll report directly to the founder and work largely independently. If you want ownership and direct access to decision-making from day one, this role gives you that.

What you'll own

  • Multi-channel strategy and execution across LinkedIn, Instagram, X, and wherever our audience is. This includes owning the content calendar, publishing, tracking what works, and doubling down on it to grow our digital footprint
  • Follower and engagement growth: Set targets, track progress, and iterate on what's working
  • Short-form video creation and editing: Reels, LinkedIn video, and platform-native formats
  • Visual content creation: Carousels, graphics, and designed assets
  • Community engagement: Managing comments and engaging in authentic conversations
  • Reporting: A clear weekly snapshot of what's moving and what isn't
  • Email marketing: growing and managing our newsletter and nurture campaigns, keeping our audience engaged beyond social
  • Paid social (LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads): if you have the experience, you'll eventually own this too

What we're looking for

  • 1–2 years of hands-on digital marketing experience across multiple platforms
  • A portfolio that shows what you've built and grown (business or personal accounts, both are welcome) — we want to see your work
  • Strong storytelling and visual design instincts; you know what resonates, looks good, and why
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Comfortable with short-form video creation and editing (CapCut, Instagram native, or similar)
  • Experience with social scheduling tools (Buffer, Later, Hootsuite, or similar)
  • Experience with email marketing tools (Loops, Mailchimp, or similar)
  • Comfortable with AI tools as part of your daily workflow
  • Familiarity with platform-native analytics (LinkedIn, Instagram Insights) and Google Analytics basics
  • Growth mindset: you care about the numbers and want to move them
  • Comfortable with ambiguity — we're building as we go and not everything will be defined upfront
  • Async-first work style, with availability to overlap with Toronto (EST) for check-ins

The right person for this role doesn't wait to be told what to do; they see what needs to happen and does it. They're detail-oriented, take pride in their work, and back their instincts with action. If you see this as an opportunity to build something great from the ground up and care about the results as much as we do, we'd love to hear from you.

Not required, but nice to have

  • Experience with paid social (LinkedIn Ads or Meta Ads)
  • Experience with marketing for a B2B audience

The culture

We're async-first and autonomy-driven. There's no micromanagement here; we’d like to hire people we trust and get out of their way. You'll have ownership over your work from day one, and we love seeing people grow into more as the company does. We move quickly, communicate clearly, and care more about outcomes than hours. If you do your best work when you have space to think and run with things, you'll fit right in here.

We're a small, global team and still early, which means the person who joins now has the opportunity to shape the role and grow with it.

Compensation & benefits

Salary is based on experience and location. We believe in paying fairly for great work — please include your salary expectations in your application. We welcome applicants globally.

We're a small team, and we take care of our people.

  • All the tools you need to do your best work
  • Monthly wellness stipend
  • Learning budget to grow your skills (courses, conferences, or subscriptions)
  • Tenet merch of course
  • Local public holidays and paid time off in line with your country's standards

How to apply

Send your resume, portfolio, and salary expectations to hello@yourtenet.com . Subject line: Junior Digital Marketing Manager. You don’t have to send us a lengthy cover letter; just a summary of your experience would be great.

About Tenet

Great products deserve great marketing. But for many businesses, that's always been out of reach: they're understaffed, or it's too expensive, too slow, or too dependent on a team they can't afford. We built Tenet to change that.

Tenet is the AI marketing agent for lean teams. It runs the full marketing function — strategy to execution — so lean businesses can compete on the same level as companies ten times their size, without the expertise, budget, headcount, or the agency retainer.
