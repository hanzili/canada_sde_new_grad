Full Description

Job Summary

We are looking for a motivated and technology-focused Junior IT Support & Web Specialist to join our team.

This is an excellent opportunity for a junior IT professional with 1–2 years of experience who wants to grow their skills in IT support, website management, SEO, and digital operations.

The role includes approximately 60% IT support and 40% website, SEO, and digital marketing support.

The ideal candidate is a quick learner who enjoys solving problems, researching solutions independently, and taking on new technology challenges.

Responsibilities

IT Support (Approximately 60%)

  • Provide first-level IT support to employees and customers.
  • Troubleshoot Windows computers, Microsoft 365, printers, software, and basic network issues.
  • Set up, configure, and maintain computers, laptops, and peripherals.
  • Assist with user accounts, passwords, and access management.
  • Provide remote and occasional on-site technical support.
  • Manage support tickets and document solutions.
  • Research technical issues and implement solutions with guidance when needed.

Website, SEO & Digital Support (Approximately 40%)

  • Maintain and update the company website using WordPress.
  • Update website content, products, and service information.
  • Perform basic SEO tasks, including keyword optimization, metadata updates, and content improvements.
  • Monitor website performance using Google Analytics and Google Search Console.
  • Assist with company social media updates, including LinkedIn and Facebook.
  • Support digital marketing activities and online business initiatives.

Qualifications

Required

  • 1–2 years of experience in IT support, help desk, technical support, or a related role.
  • Basic knowledge of Windows 10/11 and Microsoft 365.
  • Basic understanding of networking concepts:TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VPN,Wi-Fi
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Good English communication skills.
  • Ability to learn quickly and work independently.
  • Strong interest in technology and willingness to explore new tools and systems.

Preferred

  • Experience with WordPress website management.
  • Basic understanding of SEO principles.
  • Experience with Google Analytics or Google Search Console.
  • Basic HTML/CSS knowledge.
  • Experience with social media platforms.
  • Knowledge of Canva or similar design tools.

Ideal Candidate

  • A recent IT graduate or junior technology professional.
  • Someone with strong curiosity and a passion for learning.
  • Comfortable researching solutions and learning new technologies independently.
  • Able to manage multiple tasks and adapt to changing business needs.
  • Interested in developing a broad skill set across IT and digital operations.
