About the Role

Assist with financial modelling and investment analysis

About Maverix Private Equity

Toronto-based private equity firm focused on growth tech

Full Description

Who we are:

Maverix Private Equity is a Toronto-based private equity firm led by an experienced and talented team with the background, network, and track record necessary to successfully execute a technology-enabled growth and disruption investment strategy. We closed our inaugural fund in 2022 and are raising Maverix Growth Equity Fund II. Maverix targets North American businesses with rapidly growing revenues and evidence of a clear, profitable business model. The average position size is US$50M for meaningful minority positions in our portfolio companies.

Our mission is to build a globally recognized, industry-leading, Canadian-based Private Equity platform that invests in market-changing businesses and exploits the disruptive opportunities inherent to the accelerating pace of transformation. We aim to transform legacy businesses and industries to unlock substantial financial opportunity with appropriately mitigated levels of risk. Our founding principles include: We are builders, and Entrepreneurs are our partners; Opportunity is generated by innovation; Challenging the status quo with a bias for action; Respect, transparency and freedom from judgement; and Having fun! For further details on the Fund and Team visit maverixpe.com.

Responsibilities:

  • Assist with industry research and the screening and assessment of potential investment opportunities.
  • Support the investment team in investment thesis development, market mapping, and deal sourcing initiatives.
  • Assist with financial modelling and valuation analysis, including comparable company analysis, precedent transactions, discounted cash flow models, and return analysis.
  • Prepare research materials, presentations, and investment memoranda for internal discussions and Investment Committee review.
  • Attend management meetings, diligence meetings, site visits, and Investment Committee meetings, and provide concise summaries of key findings and discussions.
  • Maintain and enhance CRM data quality, ensuring investment activity, contacts, and research insights remain current and accurate.
  • Conduct market and competitive landscape research across sectors of interest to the firm.
  • Leverage AI-enabled research and productivity tools, where appropriate, to support market research, company analysis, and knowledge management activities while applying sound judgment, validating outputs, and adhering to Maverix's standards for confidentiality, accuracy, and responsible AI use.

Qualifications:

  • Entering year two or three of an undergraduate program in an analytical field (e.g., business, computer science, mathematics, etc.)
  • Strong academic standing
  • Demonstrated interest in pursuing a career in investing, finance, consulting, or venture capital
  • Positive attitude, eagerness to learn, and willingness to “roll up the sleeves”
  • Ability to succinctly communicate detailed information, both written and verbal
  • A passion for innovation, technology and growth equity investing
  • Curiosity about emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, and an understanding of the importance of responsible, ethical, and secure use of AI tools in a professional environment.
  • Eligibility to work in Canada without sponsorship

Location: this role is located Downtown, Toronto. We are in office Monday - Thursday and are work from home on Fridays.

Dates: May 2027 until August 2027

Due to the volume of interest, we are unable to accommodate informational interviews, networking conversations, coffee chats, or application-related discussions outside of the formal recruitment process. Direct outreach will not influence candidacy and team members will not be responding to individual inquiries. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.
