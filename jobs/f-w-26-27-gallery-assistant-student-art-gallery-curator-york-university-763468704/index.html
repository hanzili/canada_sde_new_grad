About the Role

Assist gallery curation, exhibitions, and events operations

About York University

York University gallery enabling student-curated, contemporary exhibitions on-campus

Full Description

Purpose:

The Student Art Gallery Curator is responsible for providing administrative support to the Manager with gallery space curation, exhibitions, and events.

Education:

Must be enrolled as a York student. Completion of relevant visual arts courses preferred.

Experience:

Previous work experience may be considered.

Skills:

  1. Ability to communicate with others to explain and share information.2. Ability to use social and digital media to effectively communicate or respond to others.3. Ability to work collaboratively and cooperatively in a team environment.4. Ability to demonstrate responsible, ethical, and professional conduct.5. Ability to demonstrate organization skills to carry out a variety of tasks.6. Ability to exercise judgment and decision making.

Additional Notes:

Ability to lift/move/carry equipment or material of varying weights, with assistance if needed.
