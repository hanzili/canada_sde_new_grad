About the Role

Assist QA, maintain ISO 17025 compliance docs

About ViVitro Labs Inc.

ISO 17025 accredited QA lab for cardiovascular devices

Full Description

ViVitro Labs is an ISO 17025 accredited testing laboratory and a designer and manufacturer of advanced test systems used by leading cardiovascular device companies worldwide. ViVitro provides engineered solutions for cardiovascular testing by combining scalable and modular test systems with advanced and validated test methods. We elevate patient safety at the pre-clinical level while enabling seamless scalability throughout the entire development process.

We are looking for a motivated Quality Assurance Co-op Student who is eager to apply academic knowledge to real-world quality and regulatory challenges while working within a multidisciplinary engineering and laboratory environment.

Term Details:

  • Expected term length: 8 months (January - August 2027)
  • Full-time, on-site (40 hours/week)
  • Location: Victoria, BC

Salary:

  • Hourly rate for 3rd-year students: $24.99
  • Hourly rate for 4th-year students: $26.14

Key Responsibilities:

  • Support the management and maintenance of ViVitro’s ISO 17025 Quality Management System
  • Provide quality assurance support for cardiovascular testing activities and equipment development projects
  • Review test protocols and test reports from the Laboratory Department to support compliance with ISO 17025 and regulatory requirements
  • Review, author, and update Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), Work Instructions, and quality forms with guidance from the team
  • Participate in internal audits, including preparation of audit documentation, reports, and follow-up activities
  • Track and support resolution of Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA), Non-Conformance Reports (NCR) and Engineering Change Requests (ECR)
  • Assist in the development, review, and implementation of quality manuals, procedures, standards, and engineering and laboratory work instructions
  • Perform document control activities, ensuring documents are reviewed, approved, released, and maintained in accordance with QMS requirements
  • Perform document archiving and ensure record retention requirements are met
  • Communicate procedural changes and updates across departments, including Engineering Services, Product Development, and Operations
  • Support other QA initiatives and related duties as assigned

Qualifications:

  • Currently completing a post-secondary program in engineering, science, or a related technical discipline, with graduation expected in the near term.
  • Working knowledge or academic exposure to Quality Management Systems
  • Familiarity with ISO 17025:2017 requirements is a strong advantage
  • Experience or coursework related to mechanical testing is an asset
  • Enrollment in or completion of a Quality Assurance certificate program is an asset
  • Demonstrated willingness to learn and take on new responsibilities
  • Interest in quality, compliance, and continuous improvement

Job Requirements:

  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy
  • Highly organized with the ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities
  • Ability to analyze information and present data clearly
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Comfortable working with documentation and technical procedures
  • Proficient with Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Outlook)
  • Ability to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team'

What You’ll Learn:

  • How QMS works and how ISO 17025 is applied in a real laboratory
  • How audits, CAPA, and document control work in practice
  • Exposure to quality practices in a regulated testing environment
  • Technical insight on the medical device industry

About ViVitro Labs:

ViVitro Labs is an ISO 17025 accredited testing laboratory and a designer and manufacturer of advanced test systems used by leading cardiovascular device companies worldwide. ViVitro provides engineered solutions for cardiovascular testing by combining scalable and modular test systems with advanced and validated test methods. We elevate patient safety at the pre-clinical level while enabling seamless scalability throughout the entire development process.
