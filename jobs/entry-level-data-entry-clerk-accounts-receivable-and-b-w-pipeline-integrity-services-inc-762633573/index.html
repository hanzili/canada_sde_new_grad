Full Description

Overview

We are seeking a detail-oriented and organized Entry-Level Data Entry Clerk & Accounts Receivable and Customer Service Administrator to join our team. This role offers an excellent opportunity for individuals interested in financial services, bookkeeping, and customer support to develop their skills in a professional environment. The ideal candidate will be proactive, possess strong analytical abilities, and demonstrate excellent communication skills. Prior experience with accounting software such as QuickBooks, Sage, or PeopleSoft is a plus but not required, as training will be provided. This position is paid and suitable for candidates eager to grow within the finance and customer service sectors.

Duties

  • Enter and maintain accurate data within various accounting and customer management systems
  • Manage accounts receivable processes, including invoice processing and account reconciliation
  • Assist with accounts payable tasks and ensure timely payments
  • Use accounting software such as QuickBooks, Sage, or PeopleSoft to support bookkeeping activities
  • Perform detailed account analysis to identify discrepancies or irregularities
  • Support customer service efforts by addressing inquiries via phone and email with professional phone etiquette
  • Collaborate with team members to ensure accurate record-keeping and efficient workflow
  • Conduct regular data audits to ensure integrity and compliance with company policies
  • Assist in financial reporting and analysis tasks as needed

Skills

  • Proficiency in data entry with high accuracy and attention to detail
  • Familiarity with accounting software such as QuickBooks, Sage, or PeopleSoft; experience with accounting or bookkeeping is preferred
  • Strong understanding of accounts receivable, accounts payable, and account reconciliation processes
  • Basic knowledge of financial analysis, account analysis, and general ledger management
  • Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks efficiently
  • Effective communication skills, including professional phone etiquette for customer interactions
  • Ability to perform mathematical calculations accurately for bookkeeping and financial analysis purposes
  • Experience with negotiation or problem-solving in financial contexts is a plus
  • Strong analysis skills to interpret financial data and identify trends or issues

This position offers a valuable entry point into the finance industry, providing opportunities for professional development in accounting practices, customer service excellence, and financial analysis. The successful candidate will be integral to maintaining accurate financial records while delivering exceptional support to clients and internal teams.

Pay: $17.20-$27.48 per hour

  • Flexible schedule

  • Tuition reimbursement
  • VRSP

Work Location: In person
