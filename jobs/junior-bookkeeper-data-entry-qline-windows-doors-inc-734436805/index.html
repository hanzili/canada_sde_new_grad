Full Description

Bookkeeper / Order Entry Administrator

Company: QLine Windows & Doors Inc.
Location: 589 Barton St, Stoney Creek
Job Type: Full-Time, Permanent

About QLine Windows & Doors Inc.

QLine Windows & Doors Inc. is a growing supplier of quality windows and doors, committed to providing exceptional products and customer service. We are seeking a reliable, detail-oriented Bookkeeper / Order Entry Administrator to join our team. This role combines bookkeeping responsibilities with customer order processing and administrative support in a fast-paced office environment.

Responsibilities

Bookkeeping Duties

  • Maintain accurate financial records using QuickBooks
  • Process accounts payable and accounts receivable
  • Reconcile bank and credit card statements
  • Prepare and maintain financial reports
  • Assist with month-end and year-end procedures
  • Support payroll administration and related record keeping

Order Entry & Administration

  • Enter and process customer orders accurately and efficiently
  • Verify pricing, product specifications, and order details
  • Coordinate with sales, production, and customer service teams
  • Maintain customer accounts and records
  • Respond to customer inquiries regarding orders and account information
  • Perform general administrative and office support duties as required

Qualifications

  • Experience with QuickBooks is required
  • Previous bookkeeping experience preferred
  • Strong data entry skills with a high level of accuracy
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, Word, and Outlook
  • Excellent organizational and time-management skills
  • Strong communication and customer service abilities
  • Ability to multitask and work independently
  • Experience in the construction, building products, or manufacturing industry is an asset

What We Offer

  • Competitive compensation based on experience
  • Full-time, permanent position
  • Supportive and collaborative work environment
  • Opportunity for professional growth and advancement
  • Benefits package (if applicable)

Schedule

  • Monday to Friday
  • Full-time hours

How to Apply

Interested candidates are invited to submit their resume along with a brief cover letter outlining their bookkeeping and QuickBooks experience.
