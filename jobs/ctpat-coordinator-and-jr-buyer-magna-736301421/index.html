Group Summary:

The Magna Exteriors portfolio of products includes access systems such as liftgates, exterior trim, modular systems, front-end modules including fascia, active aerodynamic systems and other lightweight structural components for automotive, commercial truck and other industrial markets.

Recognized globally as an innovator in all aspects of vehicle exteriors, Magna provides everything needed, from materials development and design through manufacturing and assembly, to help automakers create sleek, state-of-the-art vehicles across the world.

Job Responsibilities:

Immediate open position

QUALIFICATIONS

  • Post Secondary Education
  • 1-3 years of experience in purchasing, procurement, or supply chain (automotive/manufacturing preferred)
  • Exposure to ERP/MRP systems (SAP preferred)
  • Understanding of BOMs, production environment, and supplier manufacturing processes is an asset
  • Experience with RFQ processes, cost breakdowns, and supplier quoting is an advantage
  • Occasional travel to visit Vendors may be required

DESIRABLE ADDITIONAL QUALIFICATIONS

(Optional) – Preferred but not required

  • Automotive manufacturing experience

MAIN TASKS & RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Compensation: $75,000 - $85,000 annually
  • Create and manage purchase orders in accordance with Magna procurement procedures
  • Track supplier deliveries to ensure on-time performance and support issue resolution (shortages, delays, premium freight)
  • Support sourcing activities including RFQs, supplier comparisons, and cost analysis (piece price, tooling, logistics)
  • Assist in supplier negotiations focused on cost reduction, payment terms, and contract compliance
  • Maintain accurate pricing, supplier data, and purchase records within ERP/MRP systems
  • Monitor inventory levels and support production requirements to prevent line disruptions
  • Collaborate with engineering and program teams on part changes, new launches, and supplier transitions
  • Support supplier performance tracking (delivery, quality, cost) and escalate issues as needed
  • Analyze market trends and raw material fluctuations (e.g., aluminum, steel) to support cost management initiatives
  • Ensure compliance with Magna’s procurement policies, customer requirements, and automotive standards (IATF, ISO)
  • Act as the primary security subject matter expert for CTPAT in the division
  • Implement and manage the CTPAT program within their assigned division
  • Ensure local processes comply with CBP minimum security criteria
  • Coordinate team members to ensure the correct implementation and follow-up of the program
  • Schedule and coordinate internal audits
  • Ensure proper document control with respect to CTPAT requirements
  • Update the required information on the CTPAT portal in a timely manner
  • Send the required ASPR documents for review and approval to the Group Trade Compliance Manager & Magna's Director of Border Security and Asset Protection
  • Participate in cross-divisional initiatives and standardization efforts
  • Escalate risks, gaps, and resource needs through the functional reporting line
