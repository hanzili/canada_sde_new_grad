Full Description

WHO ARE WE?

We've been serving Canadian clients for over 75 years. The companies that began independently and now form Dexterra Group have an outstanding record of supporting the infrastructure and built assets that play a vital role in our society and helping our clients achieve higher performance and productivity. Powered by passionate people, we bring the right teams with the right skills together – offering both experience and regional expertise so companies can operate their day to day, confidently and successfully.

About the Role

We are seeking an innovative and detail-oriented Co-op Student to join our iLab team for the Fall co-op term (September - December 2026). This role is ideal for a student with a strong technical background in Artificial Intelligence (AI), data management, and system integration. You will play a key role in improving access to information, enhancing system documentation, and supporting large-scale data entry initiatives to ensure accuracy, usability, and impact.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assist in improving access to internal systems and ensuring data/documentation is well-organized, accurate, and easy to retrieve.
  • Support the integration of AI-driven tools and techniques to enhance data processing, analysis, and workflow efficiency.
  • Conduct large-scale data entry with high attention to detail, ensuring information is clean, structured, and consistent.
  • Collaborate with the iLab team to design and implement process improvements for data management and system accessibility.
  • Contribute to documentation efforts, making technical systems more user-friendly and impactful for diverse stakeholders.
  • Perform ad hoc research and technical tasks to support ongoing innovation projects within the lab.

WHO ARE WE LOOKING FOR?

  • Currently enrolled in a post-secondary program in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Foundational knowledge of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or related AI applications.
  • Strong technical and analytical skills with experience in data entry, data management, or system documentation.
  • Familiarity with databases, data visualization tools, or workflow automation is considered an asset.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and ability to work independently or in a collaborative team environment.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.

What You’ll Gain

  • Hands-on experience applying AI and data skills in a practical, innovation-focused environment.
  • Exposure to system integration, process improvement, and large-scale data management.
  • The opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects that enhance organizational efficiency and impact.
  • Mentorship and guidance from experienced professionals in technology and operations

WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU?

  • A place where people matter, not only at work but in the community.
  • Be #1 on day 1 by joining an industry leader.
  • Job security as we are in growth mode.
  • Benefits coverage starts on your first day.
