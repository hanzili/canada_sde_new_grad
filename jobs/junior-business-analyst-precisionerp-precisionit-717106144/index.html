About PrecisionERP / PrecisionIT

ERP analytics firm with strong data focus

Full Description

PrecisionERP is seeking a Junior Business/Data Analyst for a 9+ month, hybrid contract role in Burnaby, BC.

Academic and/or 1-2 years of experience is OK for this role.

MUST HAVES:

  • Hands-on experience with Google Analytics (GA4), including analysis of web and campaign performance data.
  • Experience building dashboards and reports in Looker Studio.
  • Experience with Google Tag Manager for web tracking setup, QA, and troubleshooting.
  • Experience working with app analytics and mobile measurement platforms (e.g., Firebase, app engagement/conversion tracking, mobile attribution platforms)

NICE TO HAVES:

  • Familiarity with Power BI is an asset.
  • Experience with app analytics tools such as Firebase, mobile attribution platforms, or customer data platforms (CDPs).
  • Some experience with statistical modelling, forecasting, or advanced analytics techniques.
  • Experience working in the utility space would be an asset.
