About GenPark

AI-powered discovery platform for brands and consumers.

Full Description

Company Description

GenPark is an innovative discovery platform transforming global commerce with agent-first marketing. By leveraging Intelligent Brand Agents and User Agents, GenPark personalizes content, products, and experiences to connect brands with consumers effectively. With a focus on Gen Z audiences, the platform combines AI-powered insights, interactive agents, and immersive video to drive engagement and conversions. GenPark enables brands to adapt to evolving consumer behaviors while improving efficiency and outreach.

Role Description

This is a hybrid internship role for a Backend Developer Intern based in Vancouver, BC, with the flexibility to work remotely on occasion. The intern will contribute to backend system architecture, design, and development, ensuring scalability and optimizing performance. Day-to-day tasks include writing clean and efficient code, collaborating with cross-functional teams, implementing APIs, and assisting in debugging and troubleshooting issues to support a seamless experience for users.

Qualifications

  • Proficiency in backend development, including experience with programming languages such as Python, Java, or Node.js.
  • Understanding of database technologies (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB) and familiarity with data modeling and management.
  • Knowledge of API development, RESTful services, and third-party integrations.
  • Basic understanding of cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • Ability to work in agile teams, effective communication, and problem-solving skills.
  • Knowledge of version control systems like Git and basic CI/CD workflows.
  • Enthusiasm for learning, attention to detail, and an interest in commerce technology.
  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
