Your Role at Manion

The Finance Coordinator (Associate) supports the daily financial operations of our business, including processing claims payments, reconciling drug card claims, managing tracer requests, and depositing cheques electronically. Reporting to the Manager, Financial Reporting, this role is well-suited for someone who thrives under pressure, takes pride in accuracy, and can confidently manage multiple deadlines in a fast-paced environment. No formal finance education is required, just a sharp eye for detail and 1 or more years of relevant experience. This is a full-time, on-site position based in our Etobicoke, ON office, Monday through Friday.

What You Will Do

  • Print and distribute cheques
  • Submit and confirm direct deposit files from various banks, and void any rejected transactions
  • Void and trace claims payments as needed
  • Reconcile drug card claims paid by third party partner
  • Deposit cheques through remote deposit capture, or manually by completing the deposit form
  • Monitor the Finance JIRA queue and action tracer requests from the Contact Centre on a daily basis
  • Manage multiple projects and meet various established deadlines

What You Bring to the Team

  • 1 or more years of relevant work experience
  • A degree or diploma in Accounting, Finance, or a related field is an asset but not required
  • The ability to spend focused hours reviewing and entering financial data with a high degree of accuracy
  • Comfort working under pressure and managing multiple priorities simultaneously
  • An understanding that this role carries multiple critical monthly deadlines, and a commitment to meeting them consistently

Core Competencies for the Role

  • Effective Communicator: Communicates clearly, in a positive and respectful manner. Interacts with others to build relationships through tact and diplomacy, and can navigate difficult situations and conversations with ease.
  • Client Centric: Prioritizes and takes action based on the needs of both internal and external clients. Exhibits excitement, a positive, can-do attitude, and takes ownership, while striving to consistently meet or exceed client expectations.
  • Embrace Change: Thrives in a constantly changing environment by adjusting thinking and behavior to be resilient, using experience to fuel growth, and is unafraid to challenge the status quo.
  • Team Player: Works with others to achieve individual, team, department, and organizational goals. Values diverse perspectives and is committed to the team's success.
  • Organized: Invests time in upfront planning to achieve goals and objectives. Plans, organizes, and schedules work efficiently, focusing on key priorities to stay on track and meet deadlines.
  • Innovator: Thinks beyond the confines of tradition and seeks opportunities for new and better ways of doing things, encouraging experimentation along the way.
  • Continuous Learner: Continuously seeks opportunities to learn and grow in current role, taking ownership for career development through experiential, social, and formal learning opportunities.
