Full Description

Sales & Communications Associate

Vancouver, BC | Full-Time | In-Person

Communication creates opportunities.

Whether it's building relationships, presenting ideas, influencing decisions, or creating memorable customer experiences, strong communication skills are at the heart of successful businesses.

At Convertiqq, we're looking for individuals who enjoy working with people and want to develop valuable communication and sales skills while helping brands connect with customers through face-to-face campaigns.

As a Sales & Communications Associate, you'll become an important part of our customer engagement team, supporting sales initiatives while representing brands professionally and confidently.

A Day In The Role

No two days are exactly the same.

You'll spend your time engaging directly with customers, building relationships, and helping deliver sales and customer acquisition campaigns across the Vancouver area.

Responsibilities include:

  • Creating positive customer experiences through face-to-face engagement
  • Communicating products, services, and promotional offers clearly and confidently
  • Building rapport with new customers and strengthening brand awareness
  • Supporting customer acquisition and sales objectives
  • Answering questions and providing helpful information
  • Gathering customer feedback and market insights
  • Working collaboratively with team leaders and colleagues

Skills You'll Develop

This role offers practical experience in:

  • Sales and customer acquisition
  • Communication and presentation
  • Relationship-building
  • Customer engagement
  • Goal setting and performance tracking
  • Team collaboration
  • Professional confidence

Why People Enjoy Working Here

Our team environment is energetic, supportive, and focused on development.

We believe in recognising effort, celebrating achievements, and creating opportunities for people who are committed to improving themselves professionally.

Benefits include:

  • Full training provided
  • Weekly pay and performance incentives
  • Ongoing mentorship and coaching
  • Networking opportunities
  • Business travel opportunities
  • Advancement opportunities based on results

Who We're Looking For

We're interested in meeting people who:

  • Enjoy working with others
  • Bring positive energy and enthusiasm
  • Are eager to learn and develop
  • Have strong communication skills or want to improve them
  • Thrive in team-oriented environments

Experience in retail, hospitality, customer service, events, sport, or sales can be helpful but isn't essential.

Ready To Apply?

If you're looking for a role where you can build confidence, develop communication skills, and gain valuable sales experience in a supportive environment, we'd love to hear from you.

Applications are reviewed regularly, and shortlisted candidates are typically contacted within 1–3 business days.
