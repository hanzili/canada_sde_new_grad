About the Role

About Compass Group

Global hospitality and facilities services leader

Full Description

At Compass Digital, our core product engineering team builds innovative technology solutions for the hospitality industry. We develop a range of products, including food ordering apps, kitchen display systems, and operator management tools. Our back-end services are shared along the guest's hospitality journey: Join - Verify - Search - Order - Pay - Pick-up/Delivery - Enjoy.

As a Mobile Developer Intern, you'll have the opportunity to work alongside talented professionals to create impactful solutions and gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

What You'll Do
Now, if you were to come on board as our Mobile Developer Intern, your experience would include:

  • Collaborate with developers and designers to build user-friendly, high-performance interfaces.
  • Write clean, maintainable code while learning about best practices for code quality and testing.
  • Take part in the full software development lifecycle, including agile methodologies.
  • Learn about and apply principles of scalability, reliability, and accessibility in development.
  • Approach work with curiosity and challenge the status quo.

What we like to use: Flutter, Dart, Firebase, State Management (Riverpod), RESTful API's, version control systems, Feature flagging

What You'll Gain

  • Professional Growth: Work on impactful projects that enhance both your technical skills and your ability to collaborate and communicate effectively.
  • Hands-On Experience: Gain exposure to the full development lifecycle and modern development practices in a real-world setting.
  • Support System: Receive guidance and support from experienced developers and a dedicated manager, with regular check-ins throughout your internship.
  • Career Insights: Learn about long-term career possibilities at Compass Digital and gain valuable insights into the path ahead.
