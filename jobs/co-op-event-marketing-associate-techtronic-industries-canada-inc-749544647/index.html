About the Role

About Techtronic Industries Canada Inc.

Global tools brands Milwaukee and Ryobi; strong co-op

Full Description

At TTI, people come for the opportunity and stay for the culture!

Job Description:

TTI is where culture meets opportunity. We are fast paced, highly energetic and we reward innovative thinking. We provide a multifaceted training program and hands-on field experience that will stimulate, challenge, and reward you! TTI is a place to explore your potential, where wins are celebrated, and your development is our priority. This is your opportunity to work with amazing people and support brands such as Milwaukee® and RYOBI®.

Job Description

This co-op role sits at the center of how Milwaukee brings products to life in the field. You’ll support the planning and execution of product-focused events, trade shows, and showcases, helping turn product strategy into real-world experiences. Working closely with the Product and Event Marketing teams, you’ll play a key role in keeping teams aligned and events running smoothly, while also leading exciting special projects that help shape the future of Milwaukee’s event marketing strategy.

What You Will Do

  • Support the coordination and execution of product launches, roadshows, national showcases, and trade shows
  • Work closely with Product and Event Marketing teams to organize event-related activities, including internal contests, SPIF programs, trade show activations, and team communications
  • Help ensure teams are aligned, informed, and prepared leading up to key events
  • Lead high-impact special projects focused on researching and evaluating trade shows and industry events across Canada, assessing audience quality, product fit, and return on investment
  • Research emerging trends in product-focused event marketing, including new technologies, merchandising approaches, and engagement tools
  • Create leadership-ready reports and presentations to support future event strategy and investment decisions
  • Develop hands-on skills in market research, data analysis, product storytelling, presentation development, and cross-functional communication
  • Gain exposure to how product strategy, marketing, and field execution come together within a national brand

What You Will Bring:

  • Currently enrolled as a student in a post-secondary co-op program.
  • Role requires a Driver’s license and access to a vehicle for the length of the term.
  • Occasional travel is required (local).
  • Must have proficient computer skills and Microsoft applications (Excel, Power Point, Word).
  • Comfortable managing multiple tasks and priorities in a fast-paced environment
  • A strong communicator who enjoys collaborating with different teams
  • Proactive, reliable, and eager to learn
  • Able to work independently while knowing when to ask questions

TTI is proud to be an equal opportunity employer, where we celebrate, support, and thrive on diversity.

Accommodations are available for applicants with disabilities throughout the recruitment process. If you require accommodations for interviews or other meetings, please advise when submitting your application.

About TTi Canada

TTI is a world-class leader in design, manufacturing, and marketing of power tools, outdoor power equipment, and floorcare products, as well as hand tools and accessories. We cover a wide range of industries, and our customers vary from consumers to professionals. Our unrelenting strategic focus on powerful brands, innovative products, operational excellence, and exceptional people drives our culture. This focus provides TTI with a platform for sustainable leadership and growth.

Our brands and products are recognized worldwide for their deep heritage, superior quality, outstanding performance, and compelling innovation. We have partnered with some of Canada’s largest retailers to bring our customers brands such as Milwaukee®, RYOBI®, Hoover®, and HART®
