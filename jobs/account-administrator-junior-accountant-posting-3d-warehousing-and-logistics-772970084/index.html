Full Description

Account Administrator / Junior Accountant

Location: Hamilton, ON
Job Type: Full-Time, Permanent

About Us

We are a growing, family-owned warehousing and logistics company committed to providing exceptional service to our customers. We're looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Account Administrator / Junior Accountant to join our accounting team. This is an excellent opportunity for someone looking to begin or grow their career in accounting.

Position Summary

The Account Administrator / Junior Accountant will assist with a variety of accounting and administrative functions that support the day-to-day financial operations of the company. We are willing to train the right candidate, so this position is ideal for a recent graduate or someone with limited accounting experience who has a strong willingness to learn.

Responsibilities

  • Process accounts payable invoices and prepare payments.
  • Assist with accounts receivable, including applying customer payments and following up on outstanding balances.
  • Perform bank and credit card reconciliations.
  • Prepare and post journal entries.
  • Assist with month-end and year-end closing activities.
  • Maintain accurate financial records and supporting documentation.
  • Reconcile general ledger accounts.
  • Assist with HST filings and other government remittances.
  • Prepare reports and spreadsheets using Microsoft Excel.
  • Respond to internal and external accounting inquiries.
  • Provide administrative support to the accounting department.
  • Assist with special projects and other duties as assigned.

Qualifications

  • College diploma or university degree in Accounting, Business, Finance, or a related field.
  • New graduates are encouraged to apply.
  • Previous accounting or office experience is considered an asset but is not required.
  • Basic understanding of accounting principles.
  • Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to prioritize tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Positive attitude with a willingness to learn.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

Preferred Qualifications (Assets)

  • Experience with accounting software (such as Microsoft Dynamics GP, Sage, QuickBooks, or similar).
  • Experience working in logistics, transportation, or warehousing.
  • Familiarity with Excel functions such as VLOOKUP, PivotTables, or XLOOKUP.

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary.
  • Comprehensive health and dental benefits.
  • Paid vacation.
  • Employer-supported training and professional development.
  • Stable, full-time employment.
  • Friendly, team-oriented work environment.
  • Opportunities for career growth and advancement.

How to Apply

If you are organized, eager to learn, and looking to build a career in accounting, we'd love to hear from you. Please submit your resume along with a brief cover letter outlining your interest in the position.

We thank all applicants for their interest; however, only those selected for an interview will be contacted.

Pay: $45,000.00-$50,000.00 per year

Benefits:

  • Casual dress
  • On-site parking

Work Location: In person
