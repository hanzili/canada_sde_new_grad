Full Description

Twisted Goods Part-Time Sales Associate, The Centre Mall, Saskatoon.

About us: We opened our doors in Saskatoon in 2001, and since then we have grown to five stores (plus an online store) in Saskatchewan and Alberta! We are a Saskatoon based company and - whether you are a store manager, an assistant manager, a team lead, or a sales associate – we rely on your direct input to make us successful. Our store teams are the lucky ones that get to touch our products, wear them, explain them, and promote them! It’s your passion that puts us on the map!

What does a Twisted Goods Part-Time Sales Associate position entail?

As a Twisted Goods Sales Associate you would get to use your extraordinary customer service talents to serve our fabulous customers and ensure they are cared for during their visit to Twisted Goods. Twisted Goods is all about Giving: giving the best customer service out there; giving awesome gift suggestions and ideas; and giving quality home décor and personal accessory items that are always trendy, always changing, and always fun. We also love finding ways to be involved in our community through donation and volunteer opportunities. Our people reflect our spirit, in knowing the trends, thriving on change and having fun while respecting others.

What we are looking for:

  • fun, high-energy, positive attitude

  • goal and sales driven

  • customer service driven

  • able to work a variety of shifts including evenings/weekends

  • able to stand for extended periods and lift up to 40lbs

  • excellent listening skills

What we can offer:

  • competitive wages

  • a fabulous working environment where your input makes a difference

  • amazing discounts

  • special sales incentives and perks!

Everyone at Twisted Goods is fun, creative, and hands on. No job is too small!

If you want to be part of a company that is local, growing, and where you can make a real difference, send your resume and cover letter to Operations Manager, Lisa!
