About the Role

Create content, optimize SEO, support campaigns

About ShipFusion

Ecommerce logistics innovator, strong growth potential

Full Description

Please note: This opportunity is exclusively available to students who are enrolled in a recognized post-secondary co-op program and are seeking a co-op work term in the Fall of 2026.

Shipfusion gives brands the best tools possible for building a successful eCommerce operation. We were founded by a team of long-time eCommerce and logistics professionals who believe retailers should be able to trust their fulfillment partners as much as their in-house team.

At Shipfusion, we pride ourselves on our commitment to entrepreneurship and innovation. We are always looking for individuals who share these values and who are looking to further their careers in a unique and rapidly growing organization!

The Opportunity:

We are looking for a Marketing Coordinator Co-op Student to join our marketing team! Reporting to our Brand & Editorial Content Strategist and Growth Marketing Manager, you'll be responsible for assisting the Marketing team and their diverse range of activities across growth, content, events, ABM, and partnerships.

This role requires strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and the ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines. You'll get exposure to many facets of marketing, including the opportunity to put forward or own initiatives when possible.

In this role you will…

  • Conduct market research and present findings on competitors, target audiences and prospects, and marketing opportunities
  • Draft, update, edit, or post content across Shipfusion's blog and social channels
  • Apply SEO/AEO best practices to blog, website, and landing page content
  • Provide event logistics and procurement support, including researching vendors, ordering event equipment or promotional materials, and vetting events for potential attendance
  • Support the development of digital campaigns and assets, including building or updating landing pages, drafting content, developing and editing videos, creating or updating email workflows, working with vendors, and conducting research
  • Assist with marketing budget tracking and monthly reporting
  • Identify and support the build of sales enablement, client storytelling, or client lifecycle assets
  • Support special projects and other administrative duties as assigned

What You'll Bring to the Team (Required Skills):

  • A strong interest in all aspects of marketing, with a particular focus on content and digital campaigns
  • Strong writing and editing skills, with the ability to adapt Shipfusion's brand voice to different audiences and mediums
  • Familiarity with SEO and AEO best practices and agentic tools such as ChatGPT and Claude
  • Comfortable with video creation, editing, and posting
  • Collaborative and curious - eager to test new ideas, challenge assumptions, and grow your skill set
  • Willingness to take on new challenges and campaigns as they arise
  • Strong proficiency in Google Workspace tools (bonus if you're also familiar with HubSpot, SEMRush, Google Analytics, and Google Search Console)
  • Proven organizational and time management skills with the ability to manage deadlines

Why join Shipfusion?

Attractive Salary and Lucrative Compensation Plan: Earn what you deserve with our competitive and rewarding salary package.

Generous Vacation Time: Recharge and explore with 5 paid wellness days.

Cutting-Edge Tech Setup: Receive a brand-new MacBook shipped to you, plus a budget to personalize your workspace with peripherals of your choice.

Vibrant Company Social Events: Connect and celebrate with exciting social events that bring our team together.

Remote Work Opportunities: Enjoy the flexibility and convenience of working from the comfort of your home.

Positive Company Culture: Join a workplace known for its supportive, inclusive, and dynamic culture.

High Employee Retention Rates: Be part of a company where employees are valued and tend to stay, reflecting our commitment to employee satisfaction and growth.

Shipfusion is an equal opportunity employer committed to creating an inclusive and accessible workplace. We welcome and encourage applications from all qualified candidates. Accommodations are available upon request for candidates taking part in all aspects of the recruitment process.
