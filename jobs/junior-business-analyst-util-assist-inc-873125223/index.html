About Util-Assist Inc.

Full Description

⚡Who We Are:

Util-Assist is a leading consulting and services partner to electric, water, and gas utilities across North America. We help utilities navigate digital transformation—empowering them with smart technology, data-driven solutions, and customer-first services. From outage communications to billing support and grid security, we’re reshaping how utilities serve their communities.

At Util-Assist, our people are our power. We foster a flexible, supportive workplace grounded in four core values: Deliver Excellence, Put People First, Do the Right Thing, and Be Humble. Recognized as a Great Place to Work®, we offer rewarding, purpose-driven careers where you can grow, innovate, and make a real impact.

Learn more at www.util-assist.com.

🛠️The Opportunity:

We are seeking a skilled Junior Business Analyst to join our growing Consulting team! This role is ideal for an early-career professional who enjoys solving problems, working with clients, analyzing data, and collaborating with experienced consultants to deliver meaningful business outcomes. You will gain hands-on exposure to utility operations, business transformation, project delivery, and consulting while building a foundation for a long-term career in the utility industry.

📋What You’ll be Doing:

  • Participate in stakeholder interviews, workshops, and discovery sessions.
  • Translate business needs into clear functional and non-functional requirements.
  • Analyze business challenges and identify opportunities for process, operational, and technology improvements.
  • Develop process maps, workflows, use cases, and other business analysis documentation.
  • Support business case development and recommendations that help clients achieve measurable business outcomes.
  • Contribute to utility transformation engagements involving areas such as AMI, MDM, CIS, customer experience and digital transformation, grid modernization, operational excellence, and strategic advisory services.
  • Conduct gap analyses, research industry trends and emerging technologies, and support solution evaluations.
  • Prepare presentations, reports, executive summaries, and other client-facing materials.
  • Participate in client meetings and help coordinate activities across clients, vendors, and project teams.
  • Support testing, validation, training, and business readiness activities.
  • Collect and analyze business and operational data to identify trends, issues, and opportunities for improvement.
  • Support the development of dashboards, KPIs, and performance reporting to inform client decision-making.
  • Build strong relationships with clients and internal teams and contribute to knowledge sharing, continuous improvement, and innovation initiatives.

🧠What You Bring:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Commerce, Engineering, Economics, Information Technology, Public Administration, or a related discipline
  • 1–3 years of relevant experience
  • Experience in utilities, energy, municipal services, engineering consulting, or technology projects is considered an asset
  • Experience gathering and documenting business requirements is considered an asset
  • Experience with Power BI, data analysis, or reporting tools is considered an asset
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Word, and Teams
  • Familiarity with process mapping tools such as Visio or Blueworks is considered an asset
  • Understanding of Agile, Waterfall, or hybrid project delivery methodologies is considered an asset
  • Understanding of AI and its potential applications and impacts within large organizations is considered an asset
  • Ability to transform information into clear, actionable recommendations
  • Ability to travel to client sites within North America

🌟Who You Are:

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical audiences
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
  • Conscientious and collaborative working style
  • Ability to work effectively both independently and collaboratively
  • Demonstrates initiative, curiosity, accountability, and a willingness to learn
  • Comfortable working directly with clients and stakeholders

💙 Why You’ll Love Working at Util-Assist

Work-Life Balance: Enjoy a truly flexible work environment with hybrid work arrangements and adaptable hours.

Summer Hours Program: Work a compressed workweek to enjoy extra days off from June to August

Upfront Vacation & Birthday Day Off: Get your full vacation balance at the start of the year and celebrate your birthday your way with a paid day off during the month of your birthday

Award-Winning Culture: Be part of a collaborative, inclusive, and growth-focused team that’s proud to be certified as a Great Place to Work® four years running.

Comprehensive Wellness Support: Access a full Employee Assistance Program with mental health resources, Nutritional Health Coaching, and recognition as one of the Best Places to Work for Mental Wellness in 2026

Career Growth: Thrive in a fast-growing company where your ambitions are supported and your development matters.

Innovative Environment: Work at the forefront of utility transformation, surrounded by forward-thinking teammates and impactful projects.

Competitive Compensation: Receive a pay package that recognizes your expertise and contributions.

Comprehensive Benefits: Access generous health and dental coverage, and RRSP matching to support your long-term goals.

Parental Leave Top-Up: Growing your family? We provide enhanced support through our parental leave top-up program.

To work with us, successful candidates must complete a SIN and passport verification and pass a police vulnerability check.
