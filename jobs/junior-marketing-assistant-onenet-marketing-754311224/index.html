About the Role

Build CMS pages; QA; EA support

About OneNet Marketing

Small, fast-growing digital marketing agency

Full Description

Location: Victoria, BC (in office)

‍

‍Type: Full-time

‍

We’re looking for a junior marketing assistant that wants to get their foot in the door of a fast-growing digital marketing agency in a multi-faceted role that includes webpage building (not coding, not design), QA testing and executive assistant functions.

‍

What you'll do

‍

  • Page building within the Sanity CMS
  • QA testing (websites and AI chatbots)
  • Bug reporting
  • Support executives with EA tasks including video conference setup, call scheduling, ticket writing, travel bookings and expense tracking.
    Assist on video shoots

‍

What you'll bring

‍

This is a junior role so emphasis will be on coachability, willingness to learn and overall enthusiasm opposed to experience.
‍

  • An undergraduate degree in business, computer science or design
  • Previous experience creating and editing webpages in a recognizable CMS (Wordpress, Webflow, Squarespace, etc)
  • Ability to follow written and verbal instructions
  • Attention to detail
  • Proactive communication
    ‍

‍ About One Net

‍

At One Net, you'll work alongside global tech brands (SteelSeries, Lenovo) and ambitious startups, plus tourism brands (Destination Vancouver, Visit Haywood) reimagining how people discover a place. We're a small, agile team with a high bar built on curiosity, craft, teamwork, accountability and results.

‍

We offer

‍

  • Competitive compensation + profit sharing + ESPP
  • Comprehensive health & dental coverage
  • Paid holiday closure (in addition to vacation) + flex hours
  • Education and wellness allowances
  • Team outings
  • Dog-friendly downtown Victoria office
  • Parking and transit allowance

‍

How to apply

‍

Remote work will not be considered. All candidates must hold a Canadian SIN. ‍

Send your resume and cover letter to hr@onenetinc.com with the subject line "Junior Marketing Assistant." Emails with different subject lines risk not being seen. ‍

We look forward to hearing from you.
