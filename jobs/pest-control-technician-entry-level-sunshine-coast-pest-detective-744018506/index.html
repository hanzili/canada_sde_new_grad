Full Description

Job Overview

If you're looking for more than just another job—one that combines practical problem-solving, customer service, and the opportunity to earn increasing responsibility and independence—we'd like to hear from you.

Sunshine Coast Pest Detective is looking for a full-time Pest Control Technician to join our growing team.

We're a locally owned company that believes in doing the job properly, treating customers with respect, and building long-term relationships through professional service.

We believe in training people properly, giving them the support they need, and then trusting them to take ownership of their work, equipment, and customer relationships.

This is not a sales position. Your success will be measured by the quality of your work, your professionalism, your judgement, your attention to detail, and the relationships you build with customers.

No previous pest control experience is required. We provide structured training, licensing support, and ongoing mentorship. We're looking for someone with integrity, good judgement, and a willingness to learn. We'll provide the training, support, and experience needed to build the technical skills.

Every property and every customer is different. Successful technicians investigate the situation, identify the cause of the problem, and implement effective solutions that protect our customers, their homes, and their businesses.

As your training progresses and you demonstrate good judgement, you'll earn increasing responsibility and independence while always working within established company procedures, product labels, and provincial regulations. There are opportunities to grow your career as the company continues to expand.

Responsibilities

  • Perform scheduled pest control inspections, monitoring, and treatment services.
  • Inspect residential and light commercial properties for pest activity and contributing conditions.
  • Select and apply treatments in accordance with product labels, regulations, and company procedures.
  • Document inspections, findings, and completed services accurately using company software.
  • Communicate professionally with customers and explain findings and solutions clearly.
  • Identify situations that require additional attention and escalate them appropriately.
  • Maintain assigned equipment, tools, and company vehicle in a clean, safe, and service-ready condition.
  • Represent Sunshine Coast Pest Detective professionally at every customer interaction.

What We're Looking For

You'll likely enjoy this role if you:

  • Enjoy working independently while being part of a supportive team.
  • Take pride in doing quality work rather than simply finishing as quickly as possible.
  • Have strong problem-solving skills and good attention to detail.
  • Communicate confidently and professionally with customers.
  • Take ownership of your work, equipment, and responsibilities.
  • Are dependable, punctual, and willing to learn.

Requirements

  • Valid driver's licence with a clean driving abstract.
  • Comfortable working outdoors in a variety of weather conditions.
  • Comfortable accessing attics, crawlspaces, and other confined areas where safe to do so.
  • Able to follow established procedures and work safely with regulated products.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Willingness to obtain a BC Pesticide Applicator Licence within 90 days of employment (training provided).

Employment is conditional upon a background check appropriate for working in private residences.

Benefits

  • Company vehicle during working hours.
  • Paid training and licensing support.
  • Paid time off.
  • Flexible scheduling.
  • Structured career progression with opportunities for increased responsibility as the company grows.
  • Small, supportive team where your work makes a real difference.
