Reports To: Director / Manager, Operations

About the Role

The Supply Chain Engineer & Analyst Intern will support operational excellence initiatives within McKesson Canada’s distribution centre network. This role provides hands-on experience in warehouse operations, supply chain analytics, and process improvement, contributing directly to performance, quality, and efficiency outcomes in a fast-paced pharmaceutical distribution environment.

McKesson Canada operates a national distribution network delivering critical medications daily, where accuracy, reliability, and compliance are essential to patient care.

What You’ll Do (Key Responsibilities)

  • Analyze operational performance data

Support the tracking and reporting of key performance indicators (KPIs) such as productivity, service levels, quality, and inventory accuracy to identify improvement opportunities. - Support process improvement initiatives (PDCA / Lean)

Participate in continuous improvement projects focused on warehouse flow, picking efficiency, inventory management, and automation optimization. - Assist with workforce and labour analysis

Evaluate staffing models, shift patterns, and labour utilization to improve operational efficiency and throughput. - Contribute to supply chain optimization efforts

Analyze inbound/outbound flows, inventory positioning, and order profiles to support network efficiency and customer service levels. - Develop dashboards and reporting tools

Build and maintain Excel, Power BI, or other reporting tools to provide visibility into operational performance and trends. - Conduct root cause analysis

Investigate operational issues (e.g., service disruptions, inventory discrepancies, safety incidents) and support corrective action planning. - Collaborate cross-functionally

Work with operations, quality, transportation, and inventory teams to support key business initiatives and projects.

Learning & Exposure

  • End-to-end pharmaceutical distribution operations
  • Warehouse management systems (WMS) and automation
  • KPI management aligned to operational excellence frameworks
  • Exposure to real-world supply chain constraints including compliance, quality, and service delivery

Qualifications

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Engineering (Industrial, Mechanical, Systems), Supply Chain, Operations, or related field
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Proficiency in Excel (required); Power BI or data visualization tools, and SQL considered an asset
  • Interest in logistics, distribution, and process improvement
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills

Key Competencies

  • Data-driven decision making
  • Continuous improvement mindset
  • Attention to detail and quality
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced operational environment

Working Conditions

  • Primarily based in an operational warehouse environment
  • May require time on the floor observing and analyzing processes
  • Standard business hours with flexibility depending on operational needs

Why Join McKesson Canada

  • Work in a critical healthcare supply chain supporting patients across Canada
  • Gain hands-on experience in a leading, large-scale distribution environment
  • Contribute to meaningful projects with real operational impact
