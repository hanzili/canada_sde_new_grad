Full Description

AI Enablement Consultant (Intern/Co-op/Student)

Remote / Hybrid - Canada (Eastern preferred) - Part-Time / Co-op

About the Role

Walk into an organisation, understand how people work, and help them work differently with AI. You will deliver foundational AI training, run masterclasses, lead workshops, and grow into a full engagement lead over time. This role combines deep AI-productivity expertise with the ability to build trust with client teams and the judgment to know what will help versus what merely sounds impressive.

Responsibilities

  • Lead discovery sessions to understand client needs, pain points, and opportunities

  • Deliver foundational AI training programmes to client teams, from executives to practitioners

  • Run AI masterclasses and workshops tailored to each client's context

  • Support change management as clients adopt AI-driven workflows

  • Build relationships with client stakeholders and become a trusted advisor

  • Contribute to SoftHouse's training materials and delivery methodology

  • Partner with senior consultants on account growth and scoping follow-on work

Must Have

  • Bachelor's degree (in progress or completed)

  • Understanding of AI productivity tools and how they change the way people work

  • Strong facilitation skills: you can run a room, read an audience, and adapt on the fly

  • Highly personable; clients need to trust you before they will change how they work

  • Comfort with AI tools across the landscape (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini)

  • Ability to translate technical concepts into business language

Nice to Have

  • Experience in consulting or professional services

  • Background in change management or organisational development

  • Experience building training programmes or curricula

  • Understanding of AI governance, ethics, and responsible adoption

  • Experience with enterprise environments (financial services, manufacturing)

How to Apply

Send your CV and a short note about why this role interests you to careers@shsadvisory.com

Pay: $18.00-$25.00 per hour

Benefits:

  • Flexible schedule

Work Location: Hybrid remote in Toronto, ON (Toronto District)
