About NE Analytics & Consulting

Full Description

Company Description

NE Analytics & Consulting empowers organizations to make informed financial and strategic decisions through advanced analytics, data-driven insights, and expert financial planning. Our services are designed to support businesses in evaluating investments, optimizing operations, and creating scalable data infrastructures. We specialize in delivering clarity and actionable insights for critical business decisions.

Role Description

This is a full-time, on-site role for a Deal Analytics / Power BI Associate with a focus on finance, based in Toronto, ON. The role includes working with financial data to create dashboards and visualizations using Power BI, conducting data and business analytics, and providing actionable insights to inform investment and operational decisions. This is not generic dashboard work and involves high speed analytics for turning financial data into investment-grade insights across:

  • Deal evaluation & due diligence
  • Portfolio performance tracking
  • EBITDA / revenue driver analysis
  • KPI frameworks for decision-making

What we’re looking for:

  • Power BI experience (strong preference)
  • Understanding of financial statements / KPIs
  • FP&A / consulting / deal advisory exposure
  • Ability to structure messy financial data into insights

Qualifications

  • Strong foundational skills in Data Analytics, Business Analytics, and general Analytics
  • Advanced Analytical Skills and expertise in applying Statistics to solve complex problems
  • Experience with Microsoft Fabric and Power BI in creating data pipelines, visualizations and dashboards
  • Detail-oriented with strong problem-solving capabilities
  • Knowledge of financial models and an understanding of investment analysis
  • Ability to work collaboratively in cross-functional teams
  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Data Science, Business, Economics, or related field
  • Experience in consulting or a data-focused role, especially in finance, is a plus
