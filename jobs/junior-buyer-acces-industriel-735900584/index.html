About the Role

Assist procurement, process orders, monitor inventory

About ACCES INDUSTRIEL

Growing industrial supplier of equipment and service

Full Description

BUILD A JOB THAT SUITS YOU, CHOOSE ACCESS INDUSTRIAL!

Access Industrial is a growing and dynamic company providing equipment, parts, and service solutions to customers across Ontario and Quebec. We take pride in our strong reputation for delivering reliable products, responsive service, and long-term value to our customers.

We are currently seeking a motivated and skilled Junior Buyer.

POSITION OVERVIEW :

  • Assist buyers and purchasing managers with daily procurement activities;
  • Create and process purchase orders for raw materials, components, and supplies;
  • Monitor inventory levels and help prevent shortages or overstock situations;
  • Communicate with suppliers regarding pricing, delivery schedules, and order status;
  • Track shipments and resolving delivery or quality issues;
  • Maintain accurate purchasing records and supplier databases;
  • Compare vendor quotes and help negotiate pricing when needed;
  • Ensure compliance with company purchasing policies and procedures.

YOU ARE THE PERSON WE ARE LOOKING FOR IF :

  • High school diploma required; diploma or degree in business, supply chain, or procurement preferred;
  • 1–2 years of purchasing, inventory, or administrative experience is an asset;
  • Experience in manufacturing or industrial environments preferred;
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and ERP/MRP systems;
  • Strong organizational and communication skills;
  • Attention to detail and ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment.

WHY JOIN OUR TEAM?

  • Competitive wages based on experience.
  • Comprehensive benefits package.
  • Ongoing training and career development opportunities.
  • A supportive team environment with long-term stability.

This job posting is for an existing vacancy.
