Full Description

Junior Accountant (Entry-Level | Growth Opportunity)
Location: Richmond, BC (Hybrid, one-day remote)

Are you an entry-level accounting professional ready to launch your career with a large, established organization? We’re partnering with a leading North American company in the food manufacturing and distribution space to find a Junior Accountant who is eager to learn, grow, and make an impact.

This is an excellent opportunity for candidates with 2+ years of work experience — especially those looking to gain exposure to a dynamic, multi-entity environment within a collaborative finance team.

Why This Role Stands Out

  • Join a large publicly traded, industry-leading organization with employees across North America
  • Be part of a large finance team while learning and growing in your career
  • Gain hands-on experience across accounts payable and corporate accounting processes
  • Work in a fast-paced environment that values learning, initiative, and continuous improvement

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Process and verify vendor invoices with accuracy and attention to detail
  • Support accounts payable operations, including weekly payments (EFT, cheques, tax payments)
  • Respond to vendor and internal inquiries with professionalism and timeliness
  • Assist with invoice coding, approvals, and tracking across departments
  • Contribute to intercompany transactions, reconciliations, and journal entries
  • Support month-end and quarter-end processes, including working papers and financial reporting
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure smooth financial operations

What We’re Looking For

  • 2+ years of experience in high-volume accounts payable or accounting
  • Degree or diploma in Accounting or related field (or currently enrolled in this)
  • Strong Excel and MS Office skills
  • Detail-oriented with strong organizational and multitasking abilities
  • Eager to learn, adaptable, and comfortable reprioritizing in a fast-paced setting
  • Strong communication skills and a team-first mindset

Bonus (Nice to Have)

  • Exposure to accounts payable or general accounting processes
  • Experience working in multi-entity corporate environment
  • Experience with ERP, accounting software experience would be beneficial

If you’re looking to build a strong foundation in accounting with a company that offers real growth potential, we’d love to hear from you. Apply today!
