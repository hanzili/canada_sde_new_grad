Position Summary

Thales Canada, Defence & Security, is at the forefront of supporting Canada's maritime security through the development and implementation of the Interdepartmental Maritime Integrated Command, Control, and Communications (IMIC3) system. This state-of-the-art technology will revolutionize how Canada's Navy and Coast Guard commanders collaborate, providing them with advanced tools to protect the nation's coastline. By enabling the secure exchange of real-time data among vessels and onshore operations centers, IMIC3 ensures that all stakeholders have access to critical information simultaneously, leading to more coordinated and effective maritime operations.

Thales has a vacancy for an IMIC3 Junior Systems Engineer. The IMIC3 project encompasses various areas of expertise, and this opportunity is within our Systems Engineering group at our Ottawa office. This Junior Systems Engineer to support the deployment, configuration, and maintenance of core system components as part of a multi-disciplinary team. This role includes IT responsibilities such as managing COTS software applications, maintaining Windows operating systems, power shell scripting, and maintenance of firewalls and server racks. Additionally this role will involve management of customer documentation, ensuring concise and accurate information.

Key Areas of Responsibility

In their efforts to develop and sustain naval systems, Systems Engineers are called upon to integrate a variety of sensors, software, firmware, and apps on commercial and military platforms. The essential functions and responsibilities of a Systems Engineer include:

  • Tailor the system engineering process, practices and tools;
  • Provide timely and accurate status updates;
  • Develop the preliminary and detailed designs for system and subsystem elements;
  • Identify, characterize, and describe issues in the system or its design through problem reports and document and design reviews;
  • Author technical documentation to capture the system and subsystem designs including the architecture, interfaces, and installation elements;
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to ensure the designs are consistent with needs, desires, and concerns of the end-users;
  • Collaborate with other functions during the implementation and integration of the system and subsystem designs;
  • Resolution of technical issues arising during the implementation, integration and testing of the system and subsystem elements; and/or
  • Support the installation and/or integration testing of the systems remotely or on customer site.

For this specific project, typical duties will include:

  • Installing and configuring servers and software;
  • Managing hardware and software obsolescence;
  • Authoring documentation;
  • Creation of tools for customers to upgrade their configurations;
  • Answering technical questions for customers and product management;
  • Supporting the configuration & maintenance of a test lab for the team;
  • Writing power shell scripts; and
  • Contributing to quotes and estimates for work on upcoming tasks.
