Full Description

Staff Accountant

Summer Intern Position

Sussex, New Brunswick

Mrs. Dunster’s has been a family tradition throughout the Maritimes and Maine for 50 years. We are a local, family owned and operated fresh bakery, with bakery operations in Sussex, NB, and Moncton, NB. We are known for our unique products, our great customer service, our talented teams and our passion for community involvement.

We are currently looking for an Accounting Student to join our team in the role of Summer Staff accountant. This position will run for 8 weeks and will allow the right candidate to improve their skills, while gaining hands-on experience.

Job Summary

Reporting to the Director of Financial Reporting, the staff accountant will be responsible for entry-level tasks within the accounting department.

Primary Duties:

·         Develop an understanding of our AP and AR roles

·         Handle data-entry for various systems

·         Create ad-hoc reports on projects that may include sales report analysis, cost accounting, expense analysis, etc

·         Other duties as assigned.

Qualifications

·         Candidates must be enrolled in an accredited Accounting or Business Administration program.

·         Experience with Microsoft Excel

·         Must be able to follow verbal and written instructions and communicate with individuals and teams.

·         Perform all work in a safe manner, following all health and safety operating procedures.

The ideal candidate is reliable, has a strong work ethic. This is a chance for you to take the knowledge and skills you have developed and have a real impact on a great company. If you think this sounds like you and would like to join our family-owned company, please forward a copy of your resume in confidence to:

Qualified applicants are asked to forward a copy of their confidential resume to:

Mrs. Dunster’s Inc., Human Resources Manager, 30 Leonard Drive, Sussex, N.B., E4E 5T5

E-Mail: employment@mrs-dunsters.com

*Please quote the job title in the e-mail subject line

We thank you for your interest in our company. All qualified applicants will be considered, however only those chosen for an interview will be contacted.
