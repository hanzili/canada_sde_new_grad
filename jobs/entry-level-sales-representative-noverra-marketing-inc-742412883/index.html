Learn from the best. Compete with the best. Earn based on your results.

You’re not built for an ordinary environment.

At Noverra Marketing Inc., we believe the best results come from people who thrive on competition, personal development, and constant self-improvement.

Our culture is similar to a high-performance team: ambitious, coachable individuals who want to grow quickly, take on challenges, and be recognized for their results.

Founded in Toronto in 2025 and now expanding into Montreal as of 2026, Noverra Marketing Inc. is building a new team of high performers focused on developing B2B and B2C sales campaigns for recognized clients.

We are currently partnered with TELUS to help customers improve their services through an approach built on relationships, communication, and trust.

Why top performers choose Noverra

  • Performance-driven environment: You’ll be surrounded by ambitious people who enjoy learning, competing, and improving. This environment is designed for those who want to push themselves and achieve high-level goals.
  • Coaching and continuous development: You’ll have access to a proven sales system, hands-on coaching, and leaders who will help you develop your communication, sales, and leadership skills.
  • Earning potential based on performance: Your income grows directly with your performance. Representatives who follow the system and develop their skills regularly earn between $800 – $1,200+ per week.
  • A culture that rewards performance: Team challenges, recognition, ambitious targets, and an energetic environment designed to keep motivation high and drive consistent growth.

What you will do

  • Represent TELUS in sales campaigns across Montreal
  • Develop your communication, persuasion, and client acquisition skills
  • Learn sales strategies used by top performers
  • Build strong relationships with customers
  • Develop confidence, leadership, and independence
  • Participate in ongoing coaching and team development sessions

Ideal Candidate

We are looking for individuals driven by performance and growth.

  • 1–2 years of experience in sales, customer service, or competitive sports is an asset but not required
  • Competitive, energetic, and results-oriented
  • Driven by personal growth and financial independence
  • Coachable, open to feedback, and eager to improve quickly
  • Entrepreneurial mindset with a positive attitude
  • Strong communication skills and ability to build rapport quickly
  • Bilingual (English & French) – required
  • Full-time availability
  • Based in Montreal and able to work in person (this is not a remote role)
