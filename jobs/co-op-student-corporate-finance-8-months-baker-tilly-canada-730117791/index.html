Be part of something bigger. Build with us today — Now, for tomorrow.

Baker Tilly Canada Capital ("BTCC") creates value for clients that seek a merger, acquisition, divestiture, or capital raise. Our team is growing and has successfully closed many deals in all industries across Canada. We value client service excellence and having FUN while supporting the career growth and the development of each other.

Our team at BTCC is looking for two Analyst-Interns for 8‑month terms commencing in September 2026 and January 2027, respectively. The roles will support both business development and deal execution, with the first four months serving as a probationary period.

What Can You Expect?

  • Business development: generate cold prospects (in generating new deals) from outside the Baker Tilly network
  • Outreach: assist in organizing webinars + other public outreach opportunities
  • Preliminary valuations: learn what drives value in private businesses
  • Market research: used in creating industry reports and confidential information memorandums, which are utilized on live deals
  • Presentation development: this will primarily include industry research and data visualization
  • Buyer due diligence: when bringing a business to market, a tailored list of sophisticated strategic and private equity investors needs to be compiled based on research
  • Financial Modelling: assist in creating forecasts of income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements in leveraged buyout transaction models
  • Capital advisory: assist in creating pitches used in raising debt capital
  • Quality of Earnings: assist in creating QoE reports used by acquirers in their financial due diligence

Qualifications:

You are an ambitious Accounting/Finance student and have:

  • Can-do attitude
  • Relevant 3rd and 4th year course work in accounting, corporate finance, marketing, and economics
  • Attention to detail and strong computer skills (e.g. Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Excellent interpersonal relationship and communication skills, and demonstrated ability to work effectively with teammates
  • Superior verbal and written communication skills, and demonstrated ability to create presentations
  • Demonstrated analytical ability, such as in case competitions

Why BTCC?

BTCC leads the corporate finance practice at Baker Tilly Canada, a midsized, national accounting and advisory network of 50+ offices across Canada. We foster a meaningful career and the opportunity to influence tomorrow for your clients, your profession, and your community. Our professionals are encouraged to Be True to their values and ambitions, alongside our shared commitment to building great relationships, great conversations, and great futures.
