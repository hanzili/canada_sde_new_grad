About the Role

About NETGEAR

Global networking hardware leader

Full Description

Role Overview We are looking for a motivated co-op student to join our R&D Mobile team as a Hardware Engineer Co-op. In this role you will be able to gain hands-on experience with lab equipment and get the chance to learn from industry professionals while working on our Mobile Hotspot products. You will be working in a temporary full-time position at our Richmond, BC office. This role requires working on-site five days a week.

Key Responsibilities

  • Validate and troubleshoot hardware circuits including power supplies, power management, memory interfaces, inter-processor interfaces, serial interfaces, USB, Ethernet, and other high-speed standards
  • Assist in validating Power Adapter, Battery Pack, and battery charging systems
  • Follow design verification test plans and document reports for wireless devices
  • Assist with assembly, rework, and debug of various wireless products

Required Qualifications

  • Strong interest and knowledge in wireless data communication hardware development
  • Solid experience with lab equipment (power supply, oscilloscope, function generator, multimeter, etc.)
  • Experience with reworking PCB (i.e., soldering fine wires and SMT components, etc.)
  • Proficient experience with MS Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)
  • Strong written and oral communication skills
  • Ability to multi-task, and work in a fast paced and dynamic environment
