About the Role

Procure materials, manage supplier orders, optimize inventory

About Vaco by Highspring

Global recruiting firm with diversified client opportunities

Full Description

About the Opportunity

  • Create and process purchase orders for materials, supplies, equipment, and services.
  • Follow up with suppliers on order confirmations, deliveries, and shipment status; expedite orders as needed.
  • Maintain purchasing records, pricing, supplier data, and receiving documentation within the ERP system.
  • Review requisitions for accuracy and support inventory replenishment to prevent shortages and overstock.
  • Coordinate with internal departments and suppliers to ensure timely procurement and information flow.
  • Reconcile purchase orders and invoices, resolving discrepancies related to pricing, quantities, and terms.
  • Source and manage suppliers for materials, services, contracts, and capital equipment.
  • Resolve supplier-related issues and support quotation reviews, pricing analysis, and supplier evaluations.
  • Procure operational materials and services to support business needs.
  • Support urgent purchasing requests, monitor inventory levels, and assist with stock management.
  • Maintain strong supplier relationships to ensure quality, service, and delivery expectations are met.
  • Participate in supplier performance tracking and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Ensure procurement activities and documentation comply with company policies and procedures.
  • Provide backup support to the Senior Buyer during absences and peak workload periods.

About You

  • 1+ year of experience in a buyer/purchasing/procurement role
  • Post secondary in procurement, business administration, supply chain or related
  • Experience within the Steel manufacturing industry is a strong asset
  • Proficiency with MS Outlook, specifically MS Excel
  • Must have strong attention to detail

$60,000 - $68,000/year
