What finance jobs are best for Canadian new grads?

Common entry points include financial analyst, commercial banking analyst, audit associate, risk analyst, actuarial analyst, wealth management associate, and rotational analyst programs at banks, accounting firms, insurers, pension funds, and fintech companies.

When should students apply for finance internships in Canada?

Many finance internships and co-op roles recruit months ahead of the work term, especially banks and accounting firms. Hanzilla keeps daily-updated finance listings so students can catch both formal programs and off-cycle postings.
