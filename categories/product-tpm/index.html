9 entry-level positions available

Product Management and Technical Program Management roles bridge business and engineering teams. These positions are highly competitive for new grads but offer excellent career growth. Entry-level APM (Associate Product Manager) and TPM roles exist at larger tech companies. Salaries range from $70,000 to $100,000 CAD, with strong communication and technical skills being essential.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can new grads get Product Manager roles?

It's competitive but possible through Associate Product Manager (APM) programs at companies like Google, Microsoft, and Meta. Strong internship experience or a technical background helps.

What's the difference between PM and TPM?

Product Managers own product strategy and roadmap. Technical Program Managers coordinate complex technical projects across teams. Both require technical understanding.
