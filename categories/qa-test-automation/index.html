17 entry-level positions available (13 full-time, 4 internships)

Quality Assurance and Test Automation engineering ensures software reliability and quality. Modern QA roles are highly technical, requiring coding skills for test automation frameworks. Entry-level QA engineers in Canada earn $55,000-$80,000 CAD, with SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test) roles paying higher. This is an excellent entry point into tech for those who enjoy systematic thinking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is QA a good entry point into tech?

Yes! QA is often easier to break into than development, and many QA engineers transition to development roles later. Focus on automation rather than manual testing.

What's the difference between QA Engineer and SDET?

SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test) is a more senior, development-focused role. SDETs build test frameworks and tools. QA Engineers execute tests and may do some automation.
