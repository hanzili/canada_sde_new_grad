Jobs in Remote

4 positions available (3 full-time, 1 internships)

Work from anywhere in Canada with remote-first companies offering flexible tech positions.

Remote work has transformed the Canadian tech landscape, allowing new grads to access opportunities at companies across Canada and globally while living anywhere. Remote positions often pay Toronto or Vancouver salaries regardless of where you live, creating significant financial advantages for those in lower cost-of-living areas. Companies like Shopify, Automattic, and many startups are fully remote. Entry-level remote salaries range from $60,000-$120,000 CAD depending on the employer's location policy.

Frequently Asked Questions about Jobs in Remote

Do remote jobs pay the same as in-office jobs?

It varies. Some companies pay the same regardless of location, while others adjust for local cost of living. Shopify and many tech companies pay Toronto-equivalent salaries for remote workers anywhere in Canada.

How do I find remote-first companies?

Look for companies explicitly advertising 'remote-first' or 'distributed' cultures. Shopify, Automattic (WordPress), GitLab, and many startups are fully remote. Also check job boards like WeWorkRemotely and RemoteOK.
